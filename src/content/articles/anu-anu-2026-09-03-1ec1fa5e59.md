## Required Documents for ANU Undergraduate Applications

When applying for an undergraduate program at the Australian National University (ANU), you need to prepare a set of core documents. These include your academic transcripts, English language test results, a personal statement, and reference letters. Each document serves a specific purpose in the application review.

![ANU campus building with students walking](https://img.ulec.com.cn/留学-单校wiki/anu-anu-2026-09-03-1ec1fa5e59-image-02-2026-1679x1300.jpg)


- **Academic transcripts**: Official records of your previous studies, showing subjects taken and grades achieved.
- **English language test results**: Such as IELTS or TOEFL, to demonstrate your proficiency in English.
- **Personal statement**: A written statement explaining your motivation, interests, and suitability for the program.
- **Reference letters**: Letters from teachers or other referees who can attest to your academic abilities and character.

## Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Applicants often encounter issues related to document formats, submission deadlines, and credential verification processes. Here are some frequent mistakes:

![A checklist and documents on a desk](https://img.ulec.com.cn/留学-单校wiki/anu-anu-2026-09-03-1ec1fa5e59-image-03-2026-1733x1300.jpg)


- **Incorrect document format**: Ensure each document meets ANU's specified format requirements. For example, transcripts may need to be certified copies.
- **Missing deadlines**: Be aware of application deadlines for your program and submit all documents on time.
- **Unverified credentials**: If your qualifications are from overseas, you may need to have them verified or assessed by an official body.

To avoid these mistakes, carefully check the requirements for each document, keep track of deadlines, and plan ahead for any verification processes.

## Preparing Your Application Package

Organize your documents neatly and double-check that you have included everything required. It is advisable to have all documents ready before you begin your application to avoid last-minute issues.

- Start early: Gather transcripts and test results well in advance.
- Follow instructions: Use the exact formats and submission methods outlined by ANU.
- Seek clarification: If you are unsure about any requirement, contact the admissions office for guidance.

By following this checklist and being mindful of common pitfalls, you can increase your chances of a smooth application experience.
