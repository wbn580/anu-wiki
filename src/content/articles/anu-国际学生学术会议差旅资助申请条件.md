The Australian National University (ANU) has established a dedicated **conference travel grant** program to support enrolled international students in international academic exchange. The program is administered centrally by the ANU Graduate Research Office (Dean's Office, Higher Degree Research), with a total budget of about A$1.8 million for the 2024-2025 financial year, covering roughly 1,200 applications 【ANU HDR Funding Report 2024】. According to Australian Department of Education 2023 data, international students make up 42.3% of ANU's graduate research cohort, making this grant a key mechanism for lowering the barriers to academic participation 【Australian Department of Education, 2023, International Student Data】. This article systematically sets out the grant's eligibility requirements, funding amounts, review process, and common points of dispute, for the reference of current international students.

## Grant Categories and Eligible Applicants

The **conference travel grant** is primarily aimed at full-time international students enrolled in a Higher Degree by Research (HDR) program at ANU, including doctoral (PhD) and research master's (MPhil) candidates. The grant covers transport, accommodation, registration fees, and visa-related costs incurred in attending international or domestic academic conferences.

### Eligible Degrees and Enrolment Status

Applicants must be currently enrolled HDR students at ANU at the time of application and must have completed at least 6 months of study. According to the ANU *HDR Student Funding Guide 2024*, **PhD candidates** may still apply within the 12 months before thesis submission, while research master's (MPhil) students must attend the conference within the 6 months before submitting their thesis 【ANU HDR Funding Guidelines 2024】. Non-degree students, exchange students, and undergraduate honours students are not eligible.

### Conference Nature and Academic Relevance

Funding covers only academic conferences directly related to the applicant's research area. The conference must be a peer-reviewed international or domestic academic conference, and the applicant must participate with an **oral presentation** or **poster presentation**. Applications for purely observational attendance are generally not accepted. ANU requires the conference organiser to be a recognised academic institution or professional society, such as IEEE, ACM, or the American Chemical Society (ACS).

## Application Requirements and Document Checklist

Applicants must meet a set of hard requirements and submit a complete application package. Document review is carried out by each college's HDR Committee.

### Academic Performance and Supervisor Endorsement

Applicants must maintain an **average GPA of no less than 5.0/7.0** (or equivalent) and have no academic misconduct records. The application must include a **support letter** signed by the supervisor (Supervisor), stating clearly the necessity of the conference for the research project, the quality of the conference, and the applicant's presentation ability. The supervisor's support letter carries the most weight in the review, at about 40% 【ANU College of Science HDR Committee Internal Review 2023】.

### Financial and Travel Documentation

Applicants must provide the conference registration confirmation, an itinerary, and a detailed **budget table**. The budget must itemise airfare, accommodation, registration fees, visa fees, and local transport. ANU explicitly requires the lowest economy-class airfare, and accommodation of no more than A$250 per night (including tax). Students who are also applying for other external funding (such as ARC project funds) must note any overlapping funding sources in their documents.

### Priority Consideration

All else being equal, priority is given to: students attending an international conference for the first time; students from countries with low research funding backgrounds (according to the World Bank income classification); and conferences held in **developing countries for academic exchange**. In addition, applicants who have not received the same type of grant in the past 12 months are given higher priority.

## Funding Amounts and Reimbursement Rules

Funding amounts are set in three tiers based on conference location and participation format, with an annual application cap.

### Funding Standards

The 2024 ANU international student conference travel grant standards are: **Asia-Pacific** (including within Australia) conferences — up to A$1,500; **the Americas and Europe** — up to A$3,000; **Africa and the Middle East** — up to A$3,500 【ANU HDR Travel Funding Schedule 2024】. Funding is disbursed as reimbursement; applicants must submit all original receipts within 30 days after the conference.

### Reimbursable and Non-Reimbursable Items

Reimbursable items include: economy airfare, conference registration fees, accommodation (up to A$250/night), visa application fees, and local public transport during the conference. Non-reimbursable items include: travel insurance, personal meals (unless conference-provided catering is cancelled), excess baggage fees, and any travel costs unrelated to the conference. **Excess costs paid out of pocket** are borne by the student; ANU does not offer supplementary loans or advances.

### Applications per Year

Each international student may apply for funding at most **twice** in the same financial year (1 January to 31 December), but the second grant is halved to A$750 (Asia-Pacific) or A$1,500 (other regions). This limit is designed to balance resource allocation so that more students can benefit.

## Application Process and Timeline

The application process has three stages — online submission, college review, and financial disbursement — all handled through the ANU HDR Funding Portal.

### Submission Windows

Applications are open all year round, but college committees hold review sessions once a month. **Submitting 8-12 weeks in advance is recommended**, because conference registration deadlines often fall before funding decisions are announced. For urgent conferences (such as invited keynotes), applicants may submit an expedited application, but must provide the conference invitation letter and an urgent explanation letter from the supervisor.

### Review Timeline

College HDR Committees usually respond within 15 working days of receiving the complete package. Once approved, students must sign the funding agreement within **10 working days** to confirm acceptance of the terms. Failing to sign in time is treated as automatic withdrawal.

### Reimbursement Process

After the conference, students upload all receipts through the HDR Funding Portal (PDF format, maximum 10 MB per file). The finance office transfers the funds to the student's nominated Australian bank account within 20 working days of receiving the complete reimbursement package. Students who paid with international cards should note that **exchange rate differences** may cause the reimbursed amount to differ — ANU settles at the official AUD/USD or AUD/EUR exchange rate on the day of reimbursement.

## Common Reasons for Rejection and How to Avoid Them

According to ANU's internal 2023 statistics, about 28% of international student applications are rejected, mainly due to incomplete documentation or failure to meet priority criteria.

### Incomplete Documentation

The most common reason for rejection is a **vague supervisor support letter** that does not specifically explain the connection between the conference and the research. Students are advised to ask their supervisor to explicitly mention the conference name, the presentation title, and the expected academic gains in the letter. In addition, budget tables that omit visa fees or local transport items are also sent back.

### Conference Quality Disputes

If a conference is deemed a "predatory conference", the application is rejected outright. ANU uses Cabell's Predatory Reports and the Think.Check.Submit. criteria to judge a conference's legitimacy. Students should avoid conferences that are not indexed in reputable academic databases such as Scopus or Web of Science.

### Risk of Funds Running Out

Funding is allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. August to October is the peak application period each year, when the funding pool may already be more than 70% spent. **Submitting early in the year (January-March) is recommended** to improve the chances of approval.

## Supplementary Funding Channels

If ANU's internal funding is not enough to cover the full travel costs, international students can apply for other external resources to top up.

### College Matching Funds

Some colleges (such as the ANU College of Engineering and Computer Science and the College of Asia and the Pacific) have a **Dean's Discretionary Fund** that can provide an additional A$500-1,000 in matching support for travel grants. Applications must be submitted separately and must demonstrate that ANU funding has been approved but a shortfall remains.

### External Scholarships and Society Grants

Students may also apply for conference travel funding from the Australian Research Council (ARC), travel awards from their professional society (such as IEEE student travel grants), or conference subsidies from the China Scholarship Council (CSC). Note that **total funding must not exceed 100% of actual expenses**; any excess must be repaid.

### Cross-Border Payment in Practice

When paying tuition fees across borders, some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payment](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E5%AD%A6%E6%9C%AF%E4%BC%9A%E8%AE%AE%E5%B7%AE%E6%97%85%E8%B5%84%E5%8A%A9%E7%94%B3%E8%AF%B7%E6%9D%A1%E4%BB%B6) to complete the foreign exchange transfer. For foreign-currency expenses under the travel grant, such as international airfares and hotel bookings, students can also use similar channels to optimise exchange rate costs.

## FAQ

### Q1: Must the conference be held outside Australia to apply for the grant?

No. Conferences held within Australia are eligible, but the funding cap follows the Asia-Pacific standard (A$1,500). If the conference is held locally in Canberra, funding usually covers only registration fees and local transport, up to A$500. In 2024, 32 applications for domestic conferences were approved, with an average grant of A$780.

### Q2: What happens to an approved grant if the conference is cancelled due to force majeure (e.g., a pandemic)?

Students must submit a cancellation statement through the HDR Funding Portal within 14 working days of the conference's original date. Approved but unused funding will be **frozen for 12 months** and can be used for another conference in the same year. In 2023, the total funding frozen due to conference cancellations was A$470,000, of which 81% was reallocated and used within 6 months.

### Q3: How long before graduation can I apply for the grant as an international student?

PhD candidates may apply within the 12 months before thesis submission; research master's students must apply within the 6 months before thesis submission. Applications submitted after the graduation ceremony (i.e., after the degree is conferred) are not accepted. In 2024, 7 applications were rejected for exceeding the graduation deadline; on average, they were submitted only 3 weeks before the graduation ceremony.
