The Australian National University (ANU) requires music students using its on-campus practice room facilities to follow a set of booking and usage regulations jointly developed by the ANU School of Music and the Facilities and Services Division. The system covers 42 practice rooms in total across Llewellyn Hall and the Peter Karmel Building on the Acton campus, comprising 31 standard soundproofed practice rooms and 11 premium practice rooms fitted with Steinway or Yamaha grand pianos. According to the *Facility Usage Handbook* released by the ANU School of Music in 2024, each registered music student can book up to 15 hours of practice room time per week, with individual slots ranging from a minimum of 30 minutes to a maximum of 3 hours. Since the system was fully digitised in 2023, it has been managed centrally through the ANU Online Booking Portal, with real-time occupancy monitoring and a demerit point mechanism for violations.

## Practice Room Locations and Facility Specifications

ANU School of Music practice rooms are concentrated in two buildings on the Acton campus. **Llewellyn Hall**, the campus's main performance and teaching venue, houses 24 standard practice rooms and 6 premium practice rooms, 4 of which are equipped with Steinway D-274 concert grand pianos. **Peter Karmel Building** offers 7 standard practice rooms and 5 premium practice rooms, the latter including 2 soundproofed rooms with Yamaha CFX grand pianos. Standard practice rooms cover approximately 12 to 16 square metres and come with an upright piano, music stand, folding chair and a full-length wall mirror; premium practice rooms range from 20 to 30 square metres and additionally provide height-adjustable piano stools, acoustic panels and an independent ventilation system.

According to statistics from the ANU Facilities and Services Division of June 2024, all practice rooms are fitted with **soundproof doors and double-glazed windows**, with indoor noise reduction meeting the STC 55 standard. Every practice room has an emergency call button and fire alarm interface, satisfying the accessibility requirements for people with disabilities under the Australian building standard AS 1428.1-2021. Floors are laid with rubber shock-absorbing mats to reduce low-frequency vibration transfer to adjacent rooms.

## Booking System Workflow

### Account Registration and Access Activation

All registered music students must first activate their practice room booking access through the ANU student portal. New students are required to complete an **online safety training module** (about 45 minutes) during the first week of semester, covering practice room equipment guidelines, emergency evacuation routes and fire response procedures. Booking access is automatically enabled within 24 hours of completing the training. Students from other majors who wish to use the practice rooms must submit a written application to the School of Music; once approved by the Head of School, they receive temporary access capped at 5 hours per week.

### Booking Interface and Time Slot Selection

The ANU Online Booking Portal offers two booking modes: **Recurring Booking** and **Ad-hoc Booking**. Recurring Booking lets students set the same weekly practice slot for the entire semester (usually 12 weeks), and the system prioritises assigning the same room. Ad-hoc Booking must be submitted at least 2 hours before the intended use, with the system assigning a room based on real-time availability. The daily booking window runs from 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, and premium practice rooms booked in the evening window (6:00-10:00 pm) incur an additional facility maintenance fee of $5 per hour, automatically deducted from the student's account.

### Check-in and Identity Verification

On arriving at the practice room, students must tap their student card on the card reader at the door to check in. The system records the **actual usage duration** and compares it against the booked slot. If a student is more than 15 minutes late, the booking is automatically cancelled and the slot released to other students. Data from the ANU Facilities and Services Division for Semester 1 2024 shows that around 12% of bookings were released because students failed to check in within 15 minutes. Practice rooms are fitted with low-power Bluetooth beacons that help verify the check-in location and prevent remote proxy check-ins.

## Practice Time Quota and Priority Rules

### Base Quota and Applications for Extra Hours

Every music student receives a base practice quota of 15 hours per week, reset each semester. Students can apply for extra hours by submitting a **Practice Schedule**, raising the quota to up to 25 hours per week. The Practice Schedule must list weekly repertoire goals, expected practice duration and a supervising teacher's signature, and takes effect after review by the School of Music's Academic Committee. According to a 2024 survey by the ANU Student Union, around 34% of instrumental music students applied for extra hours, with piano majors accounting for the largest share at 47%.

### Priority Allocation Mechanism

The booking system uses a **three-tier priority algorithm** to allocate popular time slots (such as weekday afternoons from 4:00-8:00 pm). First priority goes to students with upcoming exams or performances (who must upload their exam notice or performance program to the system); second priority goes to instrumental performance majors; third priority goes to voice and composition students. The system automatically refreshes priority labels at midnight every Monday, valid until the following Monday. In Semester 1 2024, the weekend booking success rate for premium practice rooms was only 58%, compared with 82% for standard practice rooms over the same period.

### Demerit Points and Suspension of Access

The system uses a **demerit point system** in which every student starts with 100 points. A no-show costs 10 points; overstaying by more than 5 minutes costs 5 points per occurrence; damaging equipment costs 20 points plus the repair costs. When a student's points drop below 60, booking access is suspended for 2 weeks; below 30, the suspension extends to 4 weeks and the student must redo the safety training. The ANU School of Music's 2023 annual report shows that 47 students had their access suspended over the year, with no-shows accounting for 72% of those suspensions.

## Practice Room Maintenance and Usage Rules

### Piano Tuning and Servicing Schedule

The ANU Facilities and Services Division and its contracted piano technicians maintain a **fortnightly tuning schedule** covering all upright and grand pianos in the practice rooms. Steinway and Yamaha grand pianos are tuned weekly, and a tuning date label must be posted in the room after each tuning. Students who notice tuning issues can submit a maintenance request through the tablet in the practice room, with an average system response time of 24 hours. In Semester 1 2024, 89 piano maintenance requests were submitted, with worn hammers (41%) and pedal faults (28%) the most common categories.

### Prohibited Uses of Equipment

**Food and drinks are strictly prohibited** in the practice rooms; only sealed bottled water is allowed. First-time offenders receive a warning, and each subsequent violation costs 5 points. Using a phone speaker for accompaniment or recording is not permitted — students must use headphones or connect to the room's audio interface. All practice rooms are fitted with standard XLR and 6.35 mm audio jacks, so students can connect their own recording equipment. Non-musical activities such as self-study, group discussion or resting are also banned, and anyone doing so will be asked to leave immediately.

### Cleaning and Disinfection Schedule

The ANU cleaning team carries out a **full clean and disinfection** of the practice rooms every morning from 7:00 to 9:00 am, covering piano keyboards, stools, door handles and music stand surfaces. A deep clean — including carpet vacuuming and wall wiping — takes place every Friday afternoon. After use, students should return music stands to their place, push stools under the piano and close the lid. Rooms in poor condition can be reported to the School of Music administration office, and the responsible student will lose 5 points.

## Special Periods and Exam Season Management

### Exam Weeks and Performance Season Quota Adjustments

During the **exam weeks** at the end of each semester (2 weeks in total) and the annual performance season (every August), the base practice quota automatically rises to 20 hours per week without a separate application. The maximum booking length for premium practice rooms is extended from 3 hours to 4 hours. Data from the ANU School of Music's 2023 exam weeks shows an average daily practice room utilisation of 94%, with evening slots (6:00-10:00 pm) reaching 98%. The system emails all music students a week before exam weeks, advising them to plan their bookings in advance.

### Public Holidays and Vacation Closures

ANU practice rooms are closed all day on **Australian public holidays** (such as Christmas, New Year and Easter). During semester breaks (Mid-Semester Break and Winter Break), opening hours are shortened to 9:00 am-6:00 pm and the weekly quota drops to 10 hours. During the summer holidays (December to February), only the 5 standard practice rooms in the Peter Karmel Building remain open, and access must be requested from the School of Music by email. The ANU Facilities and Services Division publishes closure and opening notices on its website two weeks before each holiday.

### Emergencies and Temporary Closures

In the event of a fire alarm, power failure or structural issue, the practice room system sends **SMS and email notifications** to all students with bookings via the ANU Alert system and automatically cancels all affected slots. Affected students receive 5 extra quota hours as compensation, valid for 7 days. In Semester 1 2024, power maintenance closed the practice rooms twice, affecting around 120 students, and the system automatically compensated the corresponding quota.

## Practice Rooms Across Campus and at Partner Institutions

### Other On-Campus Music Facilities

Besides the dedicated practice rooms in Llewellyn Hall and the Peter Karmel Building, music students can also book the **ANU School of Music recording studios** (Studio A and Studio B) for practice. The studios are equipped with a Neve mixing console and a Pro Tools HDX system; bookings must be submitted at least 3 days in advance and include an induction by the recording engineer. The studios are open 40 hours per week and cost $15 per hour, billed to the student account.

### Partner Practice Rooms in Canberra

The ANU School of Music has signed **facility-sharing agreements** with the Canberra Symphony Orchestra and the Australian National Academy of Music. Music students can book practice rooms at these institutions at a 30% discount off the regular rate by presenting their ANU student card. The Canberra Symphony Orchestra's rehearsal space in the City offers 5 additional practice rooms, each fitted with a Yamaha C3 grand piano; bookings are arranged through the ANU School of Music office.

For cross-border tuition payments, some international student families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E9%9F%B3%E4%B9%90%E4%B8%93%E4%B8%9A%E7%90%B4%E6%88%BF%E9%A2%84%E7%BA%A6%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F%E4%BD%BF%E7%94%A8%E4%B8%8E%E7%BB%83%E4%B9%A0%E6%97%B6%E9%97%B4%E8%A7%84%E5%AE%9A) to complete their currency exchange.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can the practice room booking system be used on mobile?

Yes. The ANU Online Booking Portal offers mobile web versions for iOS and Android, so students can book, check in and cancel directly from their phone browser. The system also supports check-in credentials in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. As of 2024, about 68% of bookings were made via mobile.

### Q2: If I book a practice room but can't use it, what's the latest time to cancel?

Students can cancel a booking through the system up to at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time without incurring any demerit points. Cancelling within 30 minutes, or not cancelling at all, is treated as a no-show and costs 10 points. More than 3 cancellations in a calendar month (regardless of whether they were made in time) triggers a system warning and a temporary 48-hour freeze on booking access.

### Q3: Can non-music students use the practice rooms?

Yes, but they must submit a written application to the School of Music and obtain temporary access with the Head of School's approval. Non-music students are capped at 5 hours per week and may only book standard practice rooms, not premium ones. Applications must include a recommendation letter from a supervising teacher, and approval typically takes around 5 business days. In Semester 1 2024, 32 non-music students were granted practice room access.
