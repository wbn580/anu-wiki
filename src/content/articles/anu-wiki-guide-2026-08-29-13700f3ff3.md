## Why Teaching Is a Popular Pathway

Teaching is a popular study and migration pathway in Australia. It is often chosen by international students and prospective migrants because it combines a rewarding career with a route to permanent residency.

## What Qualifications Do You Need?

To work as a teacher in Australia, you typically need a bachelor's degree in education or a master's degree in teaching. These qualifications are offered by many Australian universities and are designed to prepare you for the classroom.

## Professional Registration Requirements

In Australia, teachers must be registered with the relevant state or territory authority. Registration ensures that teachers meet professional standards, including qualifications and background checks. Each state has its own registration body, and the requirements may vary.

## How Teaching Leads to Migration

Teaching is on Australia's skilled occupation list, which means that qualified teachers may be eligible for skilled migration visas. This pathway requires you to have your qualifications assessed, meet English language standards, and obtain a job offer or meet other criteria depending on the visa type.

## Steps to Start Your Journey

If you are considering teaching as a study and migration pathway, here are the key steps:

- Choose a teaching qualification that is recognized by the Australian state or territory where you plan to work.
- Enroll in an Australian university that offers that qualification.
- Complete your studies and meet the requirements for teacher registration.
- Apply for a skills assessment and look into visa options that suit your situation.

It is important to research the specific requirements for your chosen state and visa, as they can change and may include additional conditions.
