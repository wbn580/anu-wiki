Located in Canberra, Australia's capital, the School of Music at the Australian National University (ANU) puts on more than 200 public performances a year, spanning classical, jazz, electronic and world music. According to the School of Music's 2024 annual events report, its two main venues — Llewellyn Hall (1,440 seats) and the Big Band Room (120 seats) — sell more than 85,000 tickets in total each year, with about 30% of box office revenue coming from student ticket purchases. With the 2025 performance season now announced (ANU, 2025, *School of Music Performance Calendar*), knowing the ticketing channels, price tiers and student discount policies is key to booking shows efficiently, whether you are a current student or a local audience member.

## Official Ticketing Channels and How to Book

ANU School of Music tickets are sold through three channels: the **ANU official ticketing platform**, the **Llewellyn Hall box office** and authorised third-party agents. The official platform (tickets.anu.edu.au) is the only online portal that supports student discount verification, and it accepts credit cards, PayPal and selected Australian bank debit cards.

**Online booking**: you need to log in with your ANU single sign-on account or create a guest account. After selecting a performance, the system automatically shows a "Student Discount" option, which requires uploading a photo of your ANU student card or entering your student ID number for verification. The box office is located to the left of Llewellyn Hall's main entrance and opens 90 minutes before each performance until 15 minutes after it starts; it accepts cash and EFTPOS only.

**Third-party agents** include Ticketek and TryBooking, but neither of these platforms offers ANU student discount pricing. When paying cross-border tuition, some study-abroad families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-school-of-music-performance-tickets-how-to-book-ticket-prices-and-student-di) to complete their remittances, although that service has nothing to do with event ticketing.

### Advance Booking and the Waitlist

Popular performances (such as the ANU Symphony Orchestra's annual concert and the jazz big band showcase) usually sell out within 48 hours of going on sale. The ANU ticketing system offers a **waitlist feature**: once standard tickets sell out, users can register their email address and will be notified in registration order if tickets become available. 2024 data puts the waitlist conversion rate at about 22% (ANU, 2024, *Ticket Sales Annual Summary*).

### Group Bookings and Institutional Partnerships

Groups of 10 or more can contact the School of Music's administration office (music.tickets@anu.edu.au) to request a group discount, which is normally 15% off the standard ticket price. This discount cannot be combined with student concessions.

## Standard Ticket Prices and Pricing Tiers

ANU School of Music ticket prices are tiered by venue, performance type and seating area. **Llewellyn Hall** (the main concert hall) standard full-price tickets range from **AUD 25 to AUD 65**, depending on the scale of the performance. For example, a regular concert by the ANU Philharmonic costs AUD 45 at full price, while special performances featuring guest soloists (such as the "Piano Masters Series" in March 2025) are priced at AUD 65.

**Big Band Room** (the small jazz/chamber venue) standard full-price tickets range from AUD 15 to AUD 30. **Free performances** (the Free Concert Series) take place in the Llewellyn Hall foyer every Wednesday from 12:15 to 13:00 — no ticket required, just walk in.

### Seating Areas and Pricing

Llewellyn Hall is divided into three seating areas: **Stalls**, **Balcony** and **Circle**. The front rows of the stalls (rows A–H) form the Premium area, where full-price tickets cost AUD 10 more than the standard area. The rear rows of the balcony (rows P–T) form the Budget area, where full-price tickets cost AUD 5 less. Student discounts apply in all seating areas.

### Seasonal Demand Pricing

May (ANU Open Day week) and October (graduation season) are peak pricing periods each year, with some popular performances marked up by 10% to 15%. The ANU website flags these as "High Demand" up to 30 days in advance.

## Student Discount Policy and Eligibility

The ANU School of Music offers all currently enrolled students (undergraduate, postgraduate and foundation students) a fixed **50% discount off standard ticket prices**. The discount applies to all publicly ticketed performances on the ANU official ticketing platform, with three exceptions: **guest artist performances** (such as touring international orchestras), **charity fundraising concerts** (such as the "Music Therapy Fund" concert) and **graduation ceremony concerts**.

**Discount verification**: when booking online, select the "Student – ANU" ticket type at checkout and upload a photo of your valid student card (showing your name, student ID number and expiry date). The system emails you the verification result within 24 hours. When buying at the box office, you must present your physical student card, and the expiry date on the card must cover the date of the performance. 2024 data shows that about 8% of in-person student discount claims were rejected because the student card had expired or was illegible (ANU, 2024, *Box Office Operations Report*).

### Other Student Concessions

Audience members holding student cards from other Australian universities (such as the University of Sydney or the University of Melbourne) can get a **20% discount off standard ticket prices**, but only by buying at the Llewellyn Hall box office and presenting a valid student card. International students (non-ANU) qualify under the same policy. This discount is not available through online channels.

### Staff and Alumni Discounts

ANU staff (including part-time) receive 30% off standard ticket prices, and alumni receive 15%. Both discounts require verification by logging in through the ANU staff/alumni portal and cannot be combined with student discounts.

## Popular Performance Types and How to Secure Tickets

ANU School of Music performances fall into **regular series** and **special events**. Regular series include: the ANU Symphony Orchestra series (4 concerts a year), the jazz big band series (6 a year), the chamber music series (8 a year) and student recitals (more than 50 a year). Special events include international guest artist tours, the ANU Music Festival (every August) and interdisciplinary collaborations (such as joint music and computer science concerts).

**How to secure tickets**: for high-demand performances (such as the ANU Music Festival opening night), log in on the on-sale date (usually 60 days before the performance) at 10:00 (AEST). 2024 data shows that about 45% of tickets sell within the first hour of going on sale (ANU, 2024, *Sales Data Analysis*). Student discount tickets make up 30% of total tickets but usually sell out within 24 hours.

### Student Recitals

These performances are curated and performed by current ANU School of Music students. Tickets usually cost AUD 10 (full price) or AUD 5 (student price), and the student discount applies automatically. Some sessions offer a "Pay What You Can" option with a minimum of AUD 2.

### Free Performances and Outdoor Events

In February (O-week) and November (end of semester) each year, the ANU School of Music holds free outdoor concerts on the campus lawns — no tickets required. Schedules are published through the ANU Events App (iOS/Android).

## Rescheduling and Refund Policy

ANU School of Music tickets are generally non-refundable, but you may request a transfer to another performance in the same season up to **48 hours** before the original performance (a AUD 5 handling fee applies). Transfer requests must be submitted through the ANU official ticketing platform with your original order number. Once a performance has started, no transfers or refunds are available, whether or not you attended.

**Special circumstances**: if ANU officially cancels a performance (for example, due to weather or artist illness), ticket holders receive a full refund or an equivalent-value voucher. Of the 2 performances cancelled in 2024 due to technical faults, refunds took an average of 14 working days to process (ANU, 2024, *Cancellation Policy Guidelines*).

### Scalping and Resale

ANU prohibits reselling tickets at a markup under any circumstances. The official ticketing platform has a **resale marketplace** that allows ticket holders to resell at face value or below. The marketplace opens only 7 days before a performance, and resold tickets do not qualify for student discount verification. In 2024, tickets resold through the marketplace accounted for 3.7% of total tickets sold.

### Accessibility and Disability Services

Llewellyn Hall offers wheelchair seating (left side of row H in the Stalls). Each patron with a disability may bring one companion free of charge. Bookings must be made at least 72 hours in advance by contacting boxoffice@anu.edu.au.

## Campus Buildings and Venue Guide

The ANU School of Music's main performance venues are **Llewellyn Hall**, the **Big Band Room** and the rehearsal halls in the **Peter Karmel Building**. Llewellyn Hall was built in 1972 and designed by Australian architect John Andrews; it is known for its acoustics — the adjustable reflector panels behind the stage can be configured for the different acoustic requirements of symphony, jazz and electronic music. The building underwent an acoustic upgrade in 2019 at a total cost of AUD 4.5 million (ANU, 2020, *Capital Works Report*).

The **Big Band Room**, on the ground floor of the School of Music's main building, was converted from a recording studio and features soundproofed walls and movable seating for up to 120 people. It is mainly used for jazz, experimental music and student work showcases. The rehearsal halls in the **Peter Karmel Building** (Rooms 1.01 and 2.03) are occasionally opened to the public for small chamber performances, with a capacity of 50 to 80 people.

### Accessible Routes

The main entrance to Llewellyn Hall has a ramp, and the internal lift reaches all levels. The Big Band Room entrance is at ground level with no steps. Hearing loop devices are available during performances — ask the staff when you arrive.

### Parking and Public Transport

Llewellyn Hall is adjacent to **Parking Area 7** on the ANU campus; parking costs AUD 5 on performance days (free after 18:00). The nearest bus stop is "ANU Campus / Liversidge Street" (routes R2, R3), about a 3-minute walk away. Canberra light rail's "City" station is about a 12-minute walk from Llewellyn Hall.

## FAQ

### Q1: How much do ANU students save on performance tickets?

ANU students get 50% off standard ticket prices. Taking a regular Llewellyn Hall performance at the full price of AUD 45 as an example, students actually pay AUD 22.50. 2024 data shows that students bought an average of 4.7 tickets per year, saving an average of AUD 105.65 (ANU, 2024, *Box Office Operations Report*).

### Q2: Can students from other universities buy discounted student tickets?

Yes, but only if you hold a valid student card from another Australian university and buy your ticket in person at the Llewellyn Hall box office, where you get 20% off standard ticket prices. International students (non-ANU) qualify under the same policy, but the discount is not available through online channels.

### Q3: What if a performance is sold out?

There are two options: join the waitlist on the ANU official ticketing platform (the 2024 waitlist conversion rate was 22%), or use the official resale marketplace, which opens 7 days before the performance and lets ticket holders resell at face value or below.
