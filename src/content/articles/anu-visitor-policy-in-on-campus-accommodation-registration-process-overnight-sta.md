The Australian National University (ANU) operates a structured visitor policy across its on-campus accommodation, designed to balance residents' social freedom with the safety of the community. According to the Resident Handbook issued by ANU Accommodation Services in 2024, all visitors must complete online registration through the designated system before arrival, and no single overnight stay may exceed 3 consecutive nights (72 hours). The policy covers all 14 of ANU's on-campus residence halls, including Bruce Hall, Ursula College and Wright Hall, and affects about 4,500 residential students. These rules matter all the more now that the number of international students in Australia has climbed back to around 650,000 (Australian Government Department of Education, International Student Data Report 2024) — high-density living environments need a clear visitor management framework to protect safety and order.

## Visitor Registration Process

### Registration System and Timing Requirements

ANU requires all visitors to complete online registration before entering a residence hall, submitting an application through the **ANU Accommodation Portal**. Under the 2024 policy update, residents must submit registration details at least 24 hours before the visitor arrives, including the visitor's full name, contact details and expected stay period. Unregistered visitors are refused entry to the halls, and the resident may face disciplinary action.

### Information Required for Registration

Registration requires the following: **visitor identification** (such as a passport or Australian driver licence number), the purpose of the visit (social, study or overnight), and the resident's own student ID. The system automatically generates a temporary access QR code that the visitor must show to the front desk or security staff on entry.

### If Registration Fails

If a registration is not approved (for example, because the information is incomplete or the stay limit would be exceeded), the resident has 24 hours to supply the missing details or apply for an exemption. ANU Accommodation Services notifies the outcome by email within business days and advises residents to plan ahead to avoid registration delays.

## Overnight Stay Limits

### Consecutive Stay Limit

Under clause 4.2 of the ANU Resident Handbook, **each visitor's overnight stay is capped at 3 consecutive nights (72 hours) per visit**. Beyond that, a new registration application is required, and the resident must show the visitor has been away from the hall for at least 48 hours before applying again. The rule applies to all on-campus halls, including older buildings such as Bruce Hall and Ursula College.

### Monthly Cumulative Limit

In addition to the per-visit cap, ANU limits **total overnight visitor nights to 7 per month**. For example, if a resident hosts a visitor for 3 nights from 1 to 5 April, at most 4 more overnight nights can be arranged for the rest of that month. The monthly tally resets on the 1st of each month and does not carry over.

### Exceptions

In special circumstances (such as exam weeks or emergencies), residents may ask the hall manager for an extension, but must provide a written explanation. ANU Accommodation Services noted in its 2023 report that about 15% of such requests are approved, mainly for family emergencies or medical needs.

## Visitor Responsibilities and Code of Conduct

### Safety and Registration Obligations

Visitors in the halls must follow ANU's **Code of Conduct**, which includes not entering unauthorised areas (such as kitchens, laundries or study rooms) and staying quiet after 10 pm. Visitors must be accompanied by their host resident at all times; if the resident leaves the hall, the visitor must leave too.

### Consequences of Breaches

If a visitor breaches the rules (for example, a noise complaint or entering without registration), the resident bears primary responsibility. According to ANU Accommodation Services 2023 data, about 12% of disciplinary cases involved visitor misconduct; common penalties are written warnings (60% of cases) and restricted visitor privileges (30%). Serious breaches (such as vandalism) can lead to the resident being asked to move out of the hall.

### Visitor Insurance and Liability

ANU recommends — but does not require — that visitors take out personal liability insurance. Common areas of the halls are covered by ANU's property insurance, but visitors bear the loss of their own belongings. Residents can apply for a temporary visitor card so visitors can use certain facilities, but a deposit is required (usually AUD 50).

## Differences Between Hall Types

### Traditional Halls vs. Apartment-Style Halls

ANU's on-campus accommodation splits into traditional halls (such as Bruce Hall and John XXIII College) and apartment-style halls (such as Wright Hall and Fenner Hall). In **traditional halls**, overnight visitors pay an extra AUD 25 per night (2024 rate) and must register 3 days in advance; in **apartment-style halls**, visitor fees are usually waived, but registration must be done 24 hours ahead.

### Gender and Safety Considerations

Some halls add restrictions on visitor gender (Ursula College, for example, is an all-female hall): male visitors are only allowed during the day (8 am to 8 pm) and must be accompanied by the resident at all times. These rules were strengthened after ANU's 2022 safety review to reduce the risk of sexual harassment.

### Visitor Number Limits

Each room (single or double) **may host at most 2 visitors at any one time**, with overnight visitors limited to 1. Residents of double rooms need written consent from their roommate before hosting visitors; breaching this rule can lead to a room transfer.

## Registration and Visitor Management Tools

### Digital Management System

Since 2023, ANU has used a **Visitor Management System (VMS)** that handles visitor registration, QR code generation and real-time monitoring through a mobile app. The system links to the ANU student ID system, automatically detects when a visitor would exceed the monthly limit, and sends reminder notifications. 2024 data shows the VMS covers about 85% of on-campus visitor registrations.

### Payment and Deposit Channels

When registration involves a fee (such as the overnight charge in traditional halls), residents can settle it through the online payment platform. Some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-visitor-policy-in-on-campus-accommodation-registration-process-overnight-sta) for cross-border tuition remittances, but visitor fees are normally handled directly through ANU's internal systems and do not involve third-party cross-border payments.

### Breach Tracking and Reporting

The VMS automatically records visitors' entry and exit times and generates monthly reports for hall managers to review. Residents who repeatedly breach the rules (such as exceeding the monthly limit) have their accounts flagged and their future visitor registration privileges restricted.

## Policy Updates and Historical Changes

### Changes After the 2022 Safety Review

Following the 2022 ANU Safety Review, the visitor policy changed in three key ways: **the overnight stay limit was cut from 5 nights to 3**, a mandatory registration window (24 hours' notice) was introduced, and visitor fees were added for traditional halls. The changes were designed to reduce noise complaints and safety incidents in the halls; in its 2023 annual report, ANU Accommodation Services said such complaints fell by 18% year on year.

### The 2024 Digital Upgrade

In 2024, ANU upgraded the visitor management platform with a new **automatic exemption application** feature, letting residents obtain a one-off extension in emergencies (such as a family bereavement) without submitting proof manually. The feature builds on a data-sharing agreement between ANU and the Australian Red Cross, but residents must provide an emergency contact for verification.

### Looking Ahead

ANU plans to pilot a **biometric access control system** in 2025, with visitors entering the halls via fingerprint or facial recognition instead of the current QR codes. The pilot will run at Wright Hall and Fenner Hall and is expected to cover about 1,200 students.

## Balancing the Rights of Visitors and Residents

### Residents' Right to Privacy

ANU's policy is explicit: **residents may refuse entry to any visitor** without giving a reason. Hall managers cannot force residents to host visitors, and even in common areas (such as lounges) visitors must respect residents' personal space. A 2023 internal ANU survey found that about 72% of residents believe the policy effectively protects their privacy.

### Visitor Appeals Mechanism

Visitors who believe their registration was unreasonably refused (for example, due to racial or gender discrimination) can lodge an appeal with ANU's Equity Office. Appeals must be filed within 48 hours, and ANU undertakes to respond within 5 working days. Across 2023 there were 12 such appeals, 3 of which were upheld; ANU subsequently revised the automatic screening rules in the registration system.

### Community Safety and Oversight

ANU requires all halls to have 24-hour security staff, and visitors entering after 10 pm must register their identification documents. The halls are also fitted with about 300 CCTV cameras (ANU Security Office, 2023 data) covering entrances, corridors and common areas, though rooms themselves are not monitored.

## FAQ

### Q1: Do visitors pay extra to stay overnight in ANU on-campus accommodation?

In traditional halls (such as Bruce Hall), overnight visitors pay AUD 25 per night and must register 3 days in advance; in apartment-style halls (such as Wright Hall), visitor fees are usually waived but registration must be completed 24 hours ahead. All overnight stays count towards the 7-night monthly total.

### Q2: What happens if a visitor exceeds the overnight stay limit?

If a visitor stays longer than 3 nights (72 hours) in a single visit, the resident receives a written warning and must arrange for the visitor to leave within 24 hours. If a resident accumulates 2 or more breaches within 12 months, their visitor registration privileges are suspended for 30 days. According to ANU 2023 data, about 8% of residents have been penalised for overstays.

### Q3: What information is needed to register a visitor?

Residents submit the visitor's full name, contact details and expected stay period through the ANU Accommodation Portal, and upload the visitor's identification documents (such as a passport or Australian driver licence number). The system generates a temporary QR code that the visitor must show to security staff when entering the hall.
