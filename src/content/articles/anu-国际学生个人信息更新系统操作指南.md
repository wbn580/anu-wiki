# ANU International Student Personal Information Update System Guide

The Australian National University (ANU) issued Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) documents to more than 14,000 international students in 2024, and about 23% of those students submitted at least one personal information change request during the semester (source: Australian Department of Education, 2024, Annual International Student Data Report). Under Section 19 of the Education Services for Overseas Students Act 2000 (ESOS Act 2000), education providers must update the relevant records with the Department of Home Affairs within 14 days of a student's information changing. This guide walks through how ANU's official student system (ISIS) links with the Department of Home Affairs' PRISMS system, so international students on student visas can accurately complete the online process for updating their address, contact details, passport and CoE information and avoid the visa compliance risks that come with outdated information.

## System Access and Account Preparation

**ANU international students** update their personal information mainly through **ISIS (Interactive Student Information System)**. This is ANU's official student management portal, with a live data feed to the Department of Home Affairs' PRISMS (Provider Registration and International Student Management System) database. The access point is in the "Current Students" section of the ANU homepage, or you can log in directly at `isis.anu.edu.au`.

### Login Credentials and Multi-Factor Authentication

Students log in to ISIS with their **UNI ID** (format u1234567) and the matching **ANU password**. Since July 2023, ANU has enforced Microsoft multi-factor authentication (MFA): all international students must verify their identity via the Microsoft Authenticator app or an SMS code on first login or when switching devices. ANU Information Technology Services (ITS) reported in its 2023 annual report that enabling MFA cut account compromise incidents by 67%. If you forget your password, reset it via the "Forgot Password" link; the reset link is sent to the personal email address bound to your enrolment record.

### System Compatibility Notes

ISIS has specific browser requirements. ANU ITS recommends **Google Chrome** (version 100 or later) or **Mozilla Firefox** (version 95 or later); Safari and Internet Explorer are not recommended, as they can cause form submission failures or page rendering errors. Mobile browsers work but show a simplified interface, and some fields (such as address format validation) may not display correctly. If pages fail to load properly, try clearing your browser cache or switching to incognito mode.

## Updating Your Address and Contact Details

Under Section 20 of the ESOS Act 2000, international students must notify their education provider within **7 days** of changing address. ANU automatically syncs updated addresses to PRISMS through ISIS; if you don't update in time, the Department of Home Affairs may be unable to deliver important notices (such as visa cancellation warning letters), which can create visa compliance risks.

### Changing an Australian Address

In the ISIS main menu, select "Personal Details" → "Addresses" → "Edit". The system provides three address fields: **Residential Address**, **Postal Address** and **Billing Address**. International students must make sure the "Residential Address" field contains their current actual address within Australia. ANU notes in its 2024 student handbook that entering an overseas address as your Residential Address can trigger a compliance review by the Department of Home Affairs. When filling in the address, follow standard Australia Post format: enter the street number and name first, then select the State, Postcode and Suburb. The built-in address validation tool calls the Australia Post database for real-time verification, and the form cannot be submitted if the address fails validation.

### Changing Contact Details (Phone and Email)

On the "Personal Details" → "Contact" page, students can update their **mobile number** and **email address**. ANU strongly recommends international students keep a valid Australian mobile number (format 04xx xxx xxx) as their primary contact. One note on email: ANU official notices (including visa reminders, exam schedules and tuition invoices) are sent by default to your ANU email address (`u1234567@anu.edu.au`), not your personal email. If you want notices delivered to a personal inbox, you can set up a forwarding rule in ISIS — but in March 2024 ANU tightened its security policy and now blocks forwarding of emails containing sensitive information (such as visa warnings) to non-ANU domains. When it comes to cross-border tuition, some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E4%B8%AA%E4%BA%BA%E4%BF%A1%E6%81%AF%E6%9B%B4%E6%96%B0%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F%E6%93%8D%E4%BD%9C%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97) to settle foreign exchange, but that kind of transaction has no direct connection to updating contact details in ISIS.

## Updating Passport and Visa Information

**Passport details** are one of the core identity identifiers for international students in the ANU system. If you get a new passport (renewal after expiry, replacement after loss, or a name change), you must complete the update in ISIS within **28 days** and also lodge Form 929 (Change of Address and/or Passport Details) with the Department of Home Affairs.

### Updating Passport Number and Expiry Date

On the "Personal Details" → "Visa/Passport" page in ISIS, click "Update Passport Details". The system requires a **colour scan** of the new passport (PDF or JPEG, no larger than 5MB). The scan must include the passport information page and signature page, clearly legible. ANU's International Student Support team takes **5 business days** to process the update, and your status shows "Pending Verification" in the meantime. If the uploaded passport photo is blurry or the information doesn't match, the system automatically generates a case number, emails it to your ANU address, and asks you to resubmit. Note that ANU does not accept passport updates sent by email — everything must go through ISIS.

### Visa Subclass and CoE Linkage

**Student visa (Subclass 500)** information is usually entered into ISIS automatically by ANU's Student Central when the CoE is issued. If you change visa subclass (for example to the Temporary Graduate visa Subclass 485), you must notify ANU immediately, because the CoE and visa subclass have to match. ISIS automatically emails a reminder **90 days** before your visa expires. If the visa expiry date shown in ISIS doesn't match the record in the Department of Home Affairs' VEVO system, first confirm your actual visa status through VEVO, then contact Student Central to request a correction. Of the visa information corrections ANU processed in the fourth quarter of 2024, about 34% stemmed from errors in CoE application information that students had filled in themselves (source: ANU Student Central 2024 internal operations report).

## CoE Information Change Process

**CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment)** is the core document international students use to apply for a visa and maintain lawful study status. Any change affecting CoE information (course deferral, program transfer, leave of absence, withdrawal) must be applied for through ANU's official channels — you can't simply edit personal details in ISIS.

### Course Changes and CoE Updates

If you plan to **transfer programs** or **change courses**, first submit a "Course Variation" application in ISIS. Once ANU's academic board approves it, new CoE data is sent to the Department of Home Affairs' PRISMS system. The old CoE lapses automatically, and the new CoE's validity is recalculated from the length of the new course. Under Section 19 of the ESOS Act 2000, ANU must complete the PRISMS update within **14 days** of the change. You can see your current CoE number and validity on the "My CoE" page in ISIS. If a CoE shows "Cancelled" status, contact Student Central immediately, or you may face visa cancellation.

### Leave of Absence and Withdrawal Applications

**Leave of Absence** and **Withdrawal** suspend or terminate a CoE. When completing the "Leave of Absence" application form in ISIS, you must provide a written reason (such as a medical certificate or family emergency), and the leave period is normally no more than **12 months**. ANU processed 1,847 international student leave applications in 2023, of which about 72% were approved (source: ANU Academic Board 2023 annual report). For withdrawal, fill in the "Withdrawal from Program" form; after submission the CoE is cancelled within 14 days and the Department of Home Affairs is notified. Note that if you withdraw and don't obtain a new CoE or change visa within 28 days, you breach visa condition 8202 (maintaining enrolment in a registered course).

## Common System Errors and Fixes

International students may hit **system errors** or **failed form submissions** when updating personal details in ISIS. ANU ITS statistics for the second quarter of 2024 show the most common error types were field format errors (38%), file upload failures (27%) and session timeouts (19%).

### Address Validation Failures

ISIS address fields use Australia Post's real-time validation API. If an address doesn't pass validation, the system shows "Address not found in Australia Post database". Fixes include: checking the street name spelling (e.g. "Macquarie" rather than "Macquarie"); using the address lookup tool on the Australia Post website to confirm the standard format; and if the address is in a newly developed area not yet in the database, choosing "Manual Entry" to type it in. In August 2024 ANU updated the system to let students upload a lease agreement or utility bill as supporting evidence when entering an address manually.

### File Upload Failures and Size Limits

Failed uploads of passport scans or visa documents are usually caused by **files over 5MB** or unsupported formats. Use PDF, set the scan resolution to 200 DPI, and the file should come in at 2-3MB. If using JPEG, make sure the image width is at least 1200 pixels. ANU accepts PDF, JPEG and PNG; HEIC and BMP are not supported. If uploads keep failing, try compressing the file to ZIP, or switch networks (e.g. from campus Wi-Fi to mobile data).

## Confirming and Tracking Your Update

After updating your information, you need to **confirm the change took effect** and track processing progress. ANU doesn't provide real-time confirmations; instead it notifies you by email and via status fields in the system.

### Checking Processing Status

On the "Personal Details" → "Update History" page in ISIS you can see all past changes and their current status. Statuses include: **Submitted** (received, pending), **Under Review**, **Completed** and **Rejected**. If a request shows "Rejected", the system displays the reason (e.g. "Passport photo unclear") and you need to resubmit accordingly. ANU commits to reviewing most personal information updates within **5 business days**, though passport changes can take up to 10 business days. From the 3rd business day after submission you can email queries to `student@anu.edu.au` from your ANU address, with the case number in the subject line.

### Cross-Checking with VEVO

**VEVO (Visa Entitlement Verification Online)** is the Department of Home Affairs' official visa verification system. After updating your details, log into VEVO (`vevo.homeaffairs.gov.au`) about **7 days later** and check that the visa information matches your ISIS records. If something doesn't match, contact ANU Student Central right away with a screenshot of the VEVO query as evidence. In 2024 ANU set up a data-sync check with the Department of Home Affairs that automatically compares ISIS and PRISMS data differences every quarter, but verifying yourself is still the fastest way to catch errors.

## FAQ

### Q1: After updating my address in ISIS, how long until the Department of Home Affairs receives it?

Under Section 19 of the ESOS Act 2000, ANU must sync the data to the Department of Home Affairs' PRISMS system within **14 days** of your submission. In practice, ANU's systems usually complete the sync within **3-5 business days**. You can cross-check via VEVO on the 7th day after the update; if it still hasn't synced after 14 days, contact Student Central.

### Q2: My passport expired but the new one hasn't arrived. Does this affect my visa status?

An expired passport doesn't immediately invalidate your visa, but you must submit the new passport details to ANU and the Department of Home Affairs within **28 days**. If you don't update within 28 days, the ANU system flags you as "Passport Expired" and the Department of Home Affairs may not be able to reach you. In 2024 ANU handled 312 compliance warnings caused by expired passports not being updated, and about 15% of those ultimately ended in visa cancellation.

### Q3: How do I fix the "Session Expired" message in ISIS?

ISIS automatically disconnects after **15 minutes** of inactivity. Solutions: prepare all materials and information before starting a form; use the "Save Draft" feature (only on some pages) to keep progress; avoid lingering on public Wi-Fi. If you keep timing out, try Chrome's "Stay Awake" extension or click the page every 10 minutes.
