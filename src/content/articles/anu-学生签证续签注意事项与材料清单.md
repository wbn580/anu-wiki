As of 2025, data from the Australian Department of Home Affairs shows that the global average refusal rate for student visa (Subclass 500) applications is approximately 9.8%, while for Chinese applicants it is around 3.5% [Department of Home Affairs, 2025, Student Visa Processing Report]. For international students at the Australian National University (ANU), visa renewal is far more than a matter of filling in forms — it is a compliance process that involves proving academic progress, demonstrating financial capacity and keeping health insurance coverage aligned. Following the 2024 Migration Strategy reforms, which strengthened the Genuine Student (GS) requirement, renewal applications now need to reflect a student's study trajectory and intentions in Australia more precisely. Based on Australian migration regulations and ANU's official policies, this article sets out the key considerations and the essential document checklist for renewing an ANU student visa.

## Renewal Timeframe and Visa Conditions

The best time to apply for a **visa renewal** is 6 to 8 weeks before your current visa expires. Under the processing-time standards updated by the Department of Home Affairs in 2024, 75% of student visa applications are finalised within 29 days and 90% within 58 days [Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Visa Processing Times]. If you lodge your renewal application before your visa expires, you will automatically be granted a Bridging Visa A, which allows you to remain in Australia lawfully while your application is being processed.

**Key visa conditions** include: Condition 8105 (work limitation) restricts work to no more than 48 hours per fortnight; Condition 8202 (study requirement) requires you to maintain full-time enrolment in a registered course and achieve academic progress to the standard the institution considers satisfactory. If an ANU student's CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment) is cancelled because of failed courses, they must obtain a new CoE or leave Australia within 28 days, otherwise they will be in breach of Condition 8516.

**Timing** should also take the academic calendar into account. ANU has two main intake periods each year, in February and July, and the peak renewal season usually runs from November to the following February. It is advisable to avoid lodging your application during this window to steer clear of delays caused by processing backlogs.

## Core Document Checklist and Preparation Tips

### Passport and Identity Documents

You will need to provide scanned copies of the **biographical page of your valid passport** and every page containing a visa (including old visa labels or eVisa records). The passport must remain valid for your expected period of stay after renewal; if it has less than 12 months of validity left, it is recommended that you renew it before applying for the visa. You will also need a passport-style photo taken within the last 6 months (45mm x 35mm, white background).

### Academic Documents

The **CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment)** is the core document for a renewal application. ANU students need to apply for a new CoE through the ISIS system; it shows the course name, start and end dates, and whether tuition fees have been paid. If your study period is extended because of failed courses or a change of program, you will need to provide an **Academic Progress Report** and a **Support Letter** from ANU Student Central to demonstrate that the extended study is justified. Under the Department of Home Affairs' new 2024 rules, if academic progress falls below 50%, an additional explanation letter is required.

### Evidence of Financial Capacity

Since October 2024, the Australian Government has raised the **annual living cost threshold** to AUD 29,710 [Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Migration Regulations]. Applicants must provide evidence of funds covering the first year of tuition, living expenses and return airfare. Acceptable documents include: bank statements (last 3 months), certificates of deposit (fixed deposits must be frozen for at least 3 months), scholarship letters or tuition receipts issued by ANU. If you are sponsored by your parents, you will need to provide a birth certificate and a statement of financial support.

## Strategies for Meeting the Genuine Student (GS) Requirement

From 1 July 2024, Australia replaced the previous genuine student requirement with the **Genuine Student Requirement (GS)**. Under GS, applicants no longer need to prove an "intention to stay temporarily"; instead, their status as a genuine student is assessed. Applicants must answer three questions in the visa application: their personal circumstances and motivation for study, their reasons for choosing Australia and ANU, and the value of the course for their future career.

The **GS statement** should avoid template language and be specific about: why you chose a particular ANU program (for example, by citing ANU's position in the QS World University Rankings or a specific professor's research field), how you will apply what you learn after completing your studies (such as the industry or company you plan to join back home), and your economic and social ties to your home country (such as family assets and career plans). ANU International Student Services offers a free review service for GS statements.

**Academic continuity** is a key focus of the GS assessment. If you transferred to ANU from another institution, you need to explain the reason for the transfer (such as course fit or differences in academic resources). If there was a gap in your studies (for example, a leave of absence or withdrawal), you need to provide medical certificates or an explanation of family circumstances.

## Keeping Your OSHC Coverage Aligned

**OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover)** is a mandatory requirement for visa renewal. ANU recommends Allianz Care Australia or Medibank. The policy must cover the entire duration of the new CoE plus an extra 2 months after the course ends. If your current OSHC is about to expire, you need to purchase a new policy before renewing your visa and make sure there is no gap between the old and new policies.

**Policy coverage** includes: doctor consultations, hospital treatment, some prescription medicines and emergency ambulance services. Note that OSHC does not cover dental, optical or physiotherapy services. ANU students can renew their cover directly through the university's partner Allianz Care website; the cost is roughly AUD 600–800 per year depending on the level of cover. You need to upload your OSHC Certificate to ImmiAccount.

**Cancelling or transferring cover**: if you leave Australia early after finishing your studies, you can apply for a refund of the unused portion of your OSHC. Students who transfer to another institution must make sure their new policy covers the new course period, otherwise they will need to pay the difference.

## The Visa Application Process and Common Mistakes

### Online Application Steps

Renewal applications must be lodged through **ImmiAccount**. The process involves: creating or logging into an account, selecting the Subclass 500 visa, completing the application form, uploading documents, paying the application fee (AUD 2,500 as of 2025) and submitting. Once payment is complete, the system generates a TRN (Transaction Reference Number) for future enquiries.

**Biometrics**: after lodging the application, you need to book an appointment at an Australian Visa Application Centre (AVAC) to have your fingerprints and photo taken. Applicants in China can do this at AVACs in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Chengdu and other cities. Biometric information is valid for 10 years.

### Common Reasons for Refusal

**Incomplete documentation** is the leading cause of refusal, accounting for 47% of refusals in 2024 [Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Visa Refusal Trends]. Common omissions include: failing to upload the CoE, an expired OSHC certificate, and insufficient funds in the financial evidence. **Insufficient academic progress** is the second most common reason; if ANU marks a CoE with "Academic Risk", it may trigger further scrutiny from the Department of Home Affairs. In addition, **false information** (such as forged bank statements or employment certificates) can result in a 3-year ban from entering Australia.

**What you can do**: if you receive a refusal notice, you can appeal to the Administrative Appeals Tribunal (AAT) within 21 days, or lodge a new, complete application. ANU Legal Service offers free immigration legal advice.

## Rights and Obligations After Renewal

### Work and Travel Rights

Once the renewal is granted, your **work rights** are restored immediately. Under Condition 8105, you must not work before your course begins, you may work no more than 48 hours per fortnight while your course is in session, and there is no limit during holidays. Applicants holding a Bridging Visa A whose original visa has expired must wait until the bridging visa takes effect before they can work.

**Travel rights**: a student visa allows multiple entries into and exits from Australia. If you need to travel overseas while the renewal is being processed, you must apply for a Bridging Visa B, which permits one or multiple re-entries and is usually valid for 3 months. Leaving Australia without a Bridging Visa B will cause your renewal application to lapse automatically.

### Maintaining Your Studies and Visa

**Academic progress requirements**: ANU requires international students to pass at least 50% of their course load each semester, otherwise the Academic Intervention Process is triggered. If you fail to meet the standard for two consecutive semesters, the university may cancel your CoE, which would lead to the cancellation of your visa. **Attendance**: although there is no hard 80% attendance requirement, excessive absences can affect the assessment of your academic progress.

**Changing visa conditions**: if you plan to transfer to another institution or downgrade your course level (for example, from a master's degree to a bachelor's degree), you must first obtain approval from the Department of Home Affairs. ANU students who transfer to another institution while their CoE is still valid must notify the Department within 28 days and apply for a new CoE.

## Handling Special Circumstances

### Extended Study and Failed Courses

If you need to extend your studies because of failed courses, ANU will issue a **shortened CoE** (covering only the additional semesters required). When renewing your visa, you must provide an **explanation letter about your academic progress**, outlining the reasons for the failures (such as health issues or family circumstances) and your improvement plan (such as attending academic support sessions or adjusting your course selection). The Department of Home Affairs usually requires supporting evidence such as medical certificates or counselling records.

**GS supplementary materials**: an extended study period may be assessed as a sign of "insufficient study capacity", so the explanation letter should emphasise the student's academic potential, for example by citing ANU's academic support resources (such as the Academic Skills Centre).

### Transitioning to a Post-Graduation Visa

The **Temporary Graduate visa (Subclass 485)**: after completing a CRICOS-registered course, ANU students can apply for a Subclass 485 visa within 6 months. This visa allows you to work in Australia for 2–4 years (depending on your qualification level). Note: the Subclass 485 visa must be applied for separately and cannot be combined with a student visa renewal. When applying, you will need to provide your **IELTS results** (overall 6.5, with no band below 6.0) and a **police clearance certificate**.

**Switching visas**: if you plan to move from a Subclass 485 visa to a student visa (for example, to pursue a PhD), you must first obtain a new CoE and demonstrate continuity between your academic and career plans. ANU Graduate Research can provide support with doctoral applications.

## FAQ

### Q1: How far in advance should ANU students start preparing for a visa renewal?

It is recommended that you lodge your application **6–8 weeks** before your current visa expires. Department of Home Affairs processing times for 2024 show that 75% of applications are finalised within 29 days, but during the peak season (November to February) this can extend to 58 days. Incomplete documentation can add further delays.

### Q2: How much money do I need to show for the financial evidence?

The 2024 standard is **AUD 29,710 per year** for living costs, plus first-year tuition fees (around AUD 40,000–50,000 for ANU undergraduate programs) and return airfare (about AUD 2,000). In total, you need to demonstrate available funds of at least **AUD 72,000–82,000**. Bank statements must cover the last 3 months, and fixed deposits must be frozen for at least 3 months.

### Q3: How do I renew my visa after failing courses?

You will need a **new CoE** issued by ANU (covering the additional semesters), an **explanation letter about your academic progress** (outlining the reasons for the failures and your improvement plan) and **supporting documents** (such as medical certificates). If your CoE was cancelled because of failed courses, you must obtain a new CoE or leave Australia within 28 days. It is advisable to contact ANU Student Central early for academic support.
