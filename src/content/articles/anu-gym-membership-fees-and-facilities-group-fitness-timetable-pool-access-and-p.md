ANU Sport & Recreation, the Australian National University's main provider of fitness and sporting services, operates the **ANU Gym** at the Acton campus. According to ANU Sport's official 2024-2025 fee schedule, the standard full-time student membership costs **AUD 299** per semester (about 20 weeks), while the Off-Peak membership costs **AUD 199** — roughly 25% below the average price of commercial gyms in Canberra (comparison source: Australian Sports Commission, 2023, *AusPlay Survey*). The centre's facilities include a 25-metre indoor heated pool, 8 standard basketball/volleyball courts, a free weights area, and a cardio equipment zone, with more than **50 group fitness classes** offered each week. With the 2024 ANU Student Life Survey showing that **67%** of student respondents rank "accessibility of on-campus fitness facilities" as a key factor in choosing accommodation and lifestyle amenities, the membership structure and service options are worth examining systematically.

## Membership Fee Structure and Payment Options

ANU Gym memberships fall into four categories based on **usage hours** and **contract length**. The standard membership for full-time students allows unlimited entry during opening hours (Monday to Friday 06:00-22:00, weekends 08:00-20:00): a Semester Pass costs AUD 299 and an Annual Pass costs AUD 549. The Off-Peak membership is limited to Monday-Friday 10:00-16:00 plus all day on weekends: AUD 199 for a semester and AUD 379 for a year. Staff and community rates are approximately 30% and 60% higher respectively.

### Short-Term Trials and Day Passes
For students unsure about committing long term, ANU Gym offers casual visits at AUD 12 (students) and AUD 16 (public). A 10-Pass costs AUD 99 and is valid for 6 months. All payments can be made with your Campus Card or through the online portal; some international students use cross-border payment channels such as **Flywire tuition payments** to handle related fees and avoid foreign exchange conversion charges.

### Refund and Freeze Policies
Membership contracts can be cancelled for a full refund within 7 days of commencement (minus an AUD 25 administration fee). Semester passes are non-refundable after week 4 of the semester, but can be frozen once for up to 4 weeks with medical documentation or exchange-semester paperwork. Annual pass holders can apply for two freezes per 12-month period.

## Group Fitness Class Timetable

ANU Gym runs **50-55 group fitness classes** each week across three time slots: morning (06:30-08:00), midday (12:00-13:30), and evening (17:00-19:00). Class types include **high-intensity interval training (HIIT)**, yoga, Pilates, Spinning, and Boxing Fit. Classes are free for standard members; Off-Peak members pay an extra AUD 5 per class.

### Popular Classes and Capacity
**BodyPump** and **Yoga Flow** are the two most-booked class types, with an average attendance rate of **82%** (ANU Sport internal statistics, 2024 Semester 1). Each class holds 20-30 people and can be booked through the ANU Sport App or at the front desk, with places lockable 48 hours in advance. Unbooked students can join a standby queue 5 minutes before class starts, but seats are not guaranteed.

### Semester and Holiday Adjustments
During academic breaks (Mid-Semester Break and Summer Break), the class schedule is reduced by about 40%, retaining 4-5 core classes per day (such as yoga and HIIT). In the first week of semester and the two weeks before final exams, the timetable is adjusted to fit the exam period: fewer morning classes and more midday relaxation classes (such as meditation and stretching).

## Pool Facilities and Usage Rules

ANU Gym's indoor heated pool is **25 metres** long with 6 lanes, ranging from 1.2 to 1.8 metres deep, with the water temperature maintained at **27°C-28°C**. The pool is free for standard members; Off-Peak members can use it during off-peak hours. Pool hours match the gym's opening hours, but 12:00-13:00 and 17:00-18:00 daily are **lane-only sessions**, during which water walking and play are not permitted.

### Pool Safety and Hygiene
The pool is equipped with an automatic water quality monitoring system that tests residual chlorine (1.0-1.5 ppm) and pH (7.2-7.6) every 2 hours. The changing rooms provide lockers (bring your own padlock) and showers. Glass containers are prohibited in the pool area, and all users must wear a swim cap. A deep clean is carried out twice a year (July and December), each lasting 3 days.

### Swimming Classes and Clubs
The ANU Swim Club organises three training sessions per week (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday) open to members of all levels, for AUD 50 per year (gym membership not included). During semester, the pool also runs **adult beginner swimming classes** (6-week course, AUD 120), taught by coaches accredited by the Australian Swimming Coaches and Teachers Association (ASCTA).

## Personal Training Services

ANU Gym offers one-on-one and small-group Personal Training (PT). All PT coaches hold **Fitness Australia** or **Registered Exercise Professional** accreditation at level 3 or above. A single one-on-one session (60 minutes) costs AUD 65 (student price), a 5-session package costs AUD 290, and a 10-session package costs AUD 540.

### Training Programs and Assessments
The first PT session includes a **Functional Movement Screen** lasting 45 minutes, at a cost of AUD 45. Based on the results, coaches develop a 12-week periodised training plan. Small-group training (2-4 people) costs AUD 35 per person per session, and you form your own group. All PT sessions must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance, otherwise the session fee is forfeited.

### Specialised Training Options
Beyond regular strength and fat-loss training, the ANU PT department offers **sport-specific training** (such as basketball, football, and athletics), **postnatal recovery training**, and **posture correction training** (for conditions such as scoliosis and rounded shoulders). These specialised programs must be noted when booking, and coaches hold the relevant qualifications (such as pre- and post-natal training certification).

## Other Facilities and Additional Services

ANU Gym's facilities go beyond the cardio and strength zones. The **multi-purpose Sports Hall** contains 8 badminton courts, 4 basketball/volleyball courts, and 2 indoor tennis courts, bookable free of charge by standard members (2 hours per booking). The centre also has **squash courts** (2) and a **climbing wall** (9 metres high, with a bouldering area); the climbing wall is charged separately at AUD 8 per visit or AUD 60 per semester.

### Lockers and Towel Service
Seasonal locker rental costs AUD 45 per semester and includes a padlock. Day-use lockers are free. The Towel Service requires an add-on pack at AUD 30 per semester, providing one bath towel and one hand towel per visit.

### Sports Massage and Physiotherapy
The centre houses a Sports Therapy Clinic run by sports science interns from the University of Canberra, offering sports massage (AUD 50/30 minutes) and kinesiology taping (AUD 15 per application). Bookings require 3 days' notice, and slots near the end of semester may be scheduled up to 2 weeks out.

## Membership Benefits and Usage Rules

All members must comply with the ANU Sport & Recreation constitution. **Basic rules** include: you must bring your Campus Card or membership card to enter the gym; phone calls are prohibited in the equipment area (filming exercise form is allowed); cardio equipment is limited to 30 minutes per use during peak hours; and barbell plates must be re-racked in the free weights area. Three violations result in a 30-day membership suspension.

### Guest and Trial Policies
Members may bring one guest per month; guest entry costs AUD 12 (students) or AUD 16 (public). Guests must be accompanied by the member at all times, and the same guest may use the facility at most twice per month. New students can enjoy a **Free Trial Week** during the first two weeks of semester — no membership purchase needed, just register on the ANU Sport App.

### Insurance and Liability
All members are automatically covered by ANU Sport's **Public Liability Insurance**, but loss of personal belongings is not compensated. A liability waiver must be signed before using the pool, climbing wall, and squash courts. Members under 18 must have a consent form signed by a parent or guardian.

## FAQ

### Q1: Does the ANU Gym semester pass cover the summer and winter holidays?
No. A Semester Pass covers only that semester (about 20 weeks) and does not include holidays. During the summer break (November to February) and winter break (June to July), students need to buy a **Holiday Pass** (AUD 99/month) or upgrade to an annual pass. Annual pass holders can keep using the gym during holidays with no extra charge.

### Q2: Can Off-Peak members use the pool on weekends?
Yes. The Off-Peak membership's time restriction applies only to Monday-Friday (unavailable outside 10:00-16:00); **all day on weekends** (08:00-20:00) is open to Off-Peak members, including the pool, gym, and group fitness classes. However, weekend group classes (such as Sunday morning yoga) cost an extra AUD 5 per class.

### Q3: Can I get a refund if I take a leave of absence mid-semester?
Yes, but with restrictions. Semester passes can be refunded in full (minus the AUD 25 administration fee) **before week 4 of the semester**. After week 4, you can request a pro-rata refund (calculated by remaining weeks) for medical reasons (doctor's certificate required) or a formal leave of absence (ANU academic office documentation required), or switch the pass to **frozen** status (up to 4 weeks). Annual pass holders can freeze twice per year.
