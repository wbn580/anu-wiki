ANU Student Accommodation Laundry Guide: Top-Up Methods and Equipment Repairs

The laundry rooms in the Australian National University's (ANU) on-campus residences and partner apartments are mainly equipped with coin-operated or card-operated washing machines and dryers from **Speed Queen** and **Electrolux**. According to the Resident Handbook published by ANU Accommodation in 2024, more than 90% of on-campus laundry equipment has been upgraded to **CardPay** or **Coinless** electronic payment systems, and students need to top up via the **LaundryPay** mobile app or their campus card before use. A single laundry cycle (wash plus dry) costs about AUD 4.50 to 6.00 on average, depending on the machine model and the residence building. This guide systematically covers the top-up channels, operating procedures and equipment repair routes, based on data from the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 operations manual and the official LaundryPay support documentation.

## Payment Systems and Top-Up Methods

ANU's on-campus laundry rooms currently use two main payment systems: the **LaundryPay mobile app** (available on iOS and Android) and **direct deduction from the campus card**. According to the Laundry Facilities Update Notice issued by ANU Accommodation Services in February 2024, all newly built residences such as **Wright Hall** and **Fenner Hall** have fully switched to the LaundryPay system, while historic buildings such as **Bruce Hall** and **Ursula Hall** still retain some machines that accept campus card payments.

### Topping Up via the LaundryPay App

Users need to search for "LaundryPay" in the app store and download the official app (developed by **Speed Queen**). When registering an account, they must link a Visa or Mastercard credit/debit card. The minimum top-up amount is AUD 10.00 and the maximum single top-up is AUD 100.00. The app shows the number of available machines and their remaining time in the selected residence building in real time. After topping up, users can start a machine by scanning the QR code on the washer or dryer with the app. According to the official LaundryPay User Guide published in 2023, each transaction incurs a service fee of AUD 0.30.

### Topping Up the Campus Card

For machines that accept campus card payments, students can top up their "laundry wallet" through the **ANU online payment portal** (log in to ISIS and open the "Campus Card" module), or visit the campus card service centre at **Kambri** (opening hours: Monday to Friday 9:00–17:00) to add cash. Each top-up must be a multiple of AUD 5.00, with a daily maximum of AUD 50.00. Some residences, such as **Burgmann College**, also have self-service top-up kiosks that accept AUD 10 and AUD 20 banknotes.

## Operating the Equipment

Every laundry room is equipped with **front-loading washing machines** (8–12 kg capacity) and **tumble dryers** (8–10 kg capacity). The machines are operated via touch panels or physical buttons, and all prompts are displayed in English.

### Using a Washing Machine

Step 1: open the door, load the clothes and close the door lock. Step 2: scan the machine's QR code in the LaundryPay app, or tap your topped-up campus card on the card reader. Step 3: select the wash cycle (standard, quick, heavy duty, etc.); the standard cycle takes about 45 minutes and the quick cycle 30 minutes. Step 4: the machine starts automatically once payment is confirmed. According to the Speed Queen Commercial Washer Operation Manual published in 2022, a single standard wash costs AUD 3.50 to 4.00, and a quick wash AUD 2.80.

### Using a Dryer

The dryer works in a similar way, but note: a single drying cycle defaults to 60 minutes and costs AUD 2.00 to 2.50. The machines are fitted with a **moisture sensor** that stops the cycle automatically when the clothes are dry; the remaining time is not refunded. We recommend loading no more than 75% of the drum capacity to avoid uneven drying. According to the Electrolux Dryer User Manual (2023), overloading increases energy consumption by 20% to 30%.

## Common Faults and Repair Channels

Equipment faults fall into two categories: **payment faults** and **mechanical faults**. Payment faults include the machine not starting after payment is deducted and error codes shown in the app; mechanical faults include the washer not draining, the dryer not heating, and stuck door locks.

### Repair Channels

- **Report via the LaundryPay app**: select the "Report Issue" function in the app and upload the faulty machine's number (located on the side or top of the machine, in the format "SN-XXXXXX") along with photos. According to the Service Response Standards published by the LaundryPay support team in January 2024, fault reports submitted during business hours receive a response within 2 hours, and reports submitted outside business hours by 10:00 the next morning.
- **Report at the residence front desk**: every residence front desk keeps paper fault-report forms; once filled in, staff forward them to ANU Facilities Management. The processing time is usually 1 to 3 business days.
- **Report by phone**: the ANU Facilities Management 24-hour hotline is +61 2 6125 2222, for emergencies such as leaks or smoke. According to the ANU 2024 Emergency Response Procedure, a technician must be dispatched to the site within 30 minutes for such reports.

### Refund Mechanism

If a charge is deducted but the service is not completed due to an equipment fault, students can submit a refund request through the LaundryPay app, or bring their campus card to the Kambri service centre and fill in a refund form. According to the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 Refund Policy, the review takes 5 to 10 business days, and the refund is returned to the original payment account or credited to the campus card laundry wallet.

## Laundry Room Rules

ANU residences have clear **time restrictions** and **code of conduct** rules for the laundry rooms. Under Section 8.3 of the ANU Accommodation 2024 Resident Handbook, laundry rooms are open daily from 6:00 to 22:00; some residences, such as **Lena Karmel Lodge**, are open 24 hours but require campus card access through the door security system.

### Prohibited Behaviour

- Using laundry bags or buckets with a capacity over 5 kg (may damage the machines)
- Putting shoes, rugs or doonas in washing machines or dryers (per the Speed Queen 2022 Prohibited Items List)
- Hanging clothes to dry in the laundry room (a breach of fire safety regulations, with fines starting at AUD 200)
- Leaving clothes in a machine for more than 15 minutes after the cycle ends (staff are entitled to remove them and place them in the lost and found area)

### Cleaning and Maintenance

Every laundry room is equipped with **bins** and **disinfectant wipes**. Students must clean the lint filter (inside the dryer door) and remove lint after use. According to the ANU Facilities Management 2024 Cleaning Standards, professional cleaners deep-disinfect the laundry rooms every Wednesday and Sunday, including machine surfaces and floors.

## Laundry Room Configurations Across Different Residences

ANU has 17 officially certified residences on campus and nearby, and laundry room configurations vary with building age and renovation progress. According to the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 Residence Facilities Comparison Table, the typical differences are as follows.

### Newer Residences (built after 2015)

Represented by **Wright Hall** and **Fenner Hall**: there is one laundry room per floor, each with 4 washing machines and 4 dryers, all supporting LaundryPay app payments. The machines feature an **automatic self-cleaning function** that runs a self-clean cycle after every 50 loads. According to the ANU 2023 New Residence Facilities Report, the laundry room fault rate in these buildings is below 3%.

### Historic Buildings

Represented by **Bruce Hall** (built in 1960) and **Ursula Hall** (built in 1970): the laundry rooms are concentrated on the ground floor, with only 2 per building, each with 6 washing machines and 6 dryers. Some machines still use **coin payment** (accepting AUD 1 and AUD 2 coins only), but they are gradually being converted to the campus card payment system. According to the ANU 2024 Historic Building Renovation Plan, all machines are expected to be upgraded to LaundryPay by the end of 2025.

### Off-Campus Certified Apartments

At properties such as **Davey Lodge** and **Kinselas**, the laundry rooms are operated by third-party management companies and use the **Coinless system** (a dedicated top-up card is required, available from the front desk with denominations starting at AUD 20). A single wash costs AUD 4.50 and a dry AUD 2.50 — slightly higher than in on-campus residences.

## Energy Saving and Environmental Tips

In its 2023 Sustainable Campus Action Plan, ANU set a target of reducing the **energy consumption per unit** of on-campus laundry facilities by 15% by 2028. Students can help in the following ways.

### Wash in Cold Water

According to the Speed Queen 2022 Energy Efficiency Guide, washing in cold water (below 30°C) reduces electricity consumption by 60% to 70% and does not damage most synthetic fabrics. All ANU washing machines offer a cold-water cycle by default.

### Run Full Loads

One full load (8 kg) uses about 30% less water and 25% less electricity than two half loads (4 kg each). According to the Electrolux 2023 Commercial Equipment Energy Efficiency Data, running full loads also extends equipment life by about 20%.

### Clean the Lint Filter

Cleaning the lint filter after every dryer use improves drying efficiency by 15% to 20% and cuts about 10 minutes off each drying cycle. According to the ANU Facilities Management 2024 Maintenance Guide, an uncleaned lint filter is the leading cause of dryer faults, accounting for 35% of all repair cases.

## FAQ

### Q1: The washing machine does not respond after payment is deducted in the LaundryPay app. What should I do?

First check that your phone's network connection is working, then try tapping "Resend Signal" in the app. If there is still no response, note the machine number, take a screenshot of the payment deduction, and submit a fault report via "Report Issue" in the app. According to LaundryPay support team 2024 data, 85% of such issues are resolved within 30 minutes by resending the signal. If there is no response after more than 2 hours, call the ANU Facilities Management hotline on +61 2 6125 2222 to request a refund.

### Q2: Can I get a refund for the balance in my campus card laundry wallet?

Yes, but only when you graduate or move out of the residence. According to the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 Refund Policy, students must bring their campus card to the Kambri service centre and fill in a Laundry Wallet Refund Application Form. The refund is transferred to a nominated bank account within 10 to 15 business days after a AUD 2.50 handling fee is deducted. Balances below AUD 5.00 are not refundable, so we recommend using up the balance before moving out.

### Q3: When are the laundry rooms busiest, and how can I avoid the peak?

According to the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 Laundry Usage Data Report, peak times are **Sunday 14:00–18:00** and **Monday 7:00–9:00**, when laundry room usage exceeds 90%. Off-peak times are **Tuesday to Thursday 10:00–14:00** and **after 22:00 on Saturday**. The LaundryPay app shows the number of free machines in each laundry room in real time, so we recommend going during off-peak hours.
