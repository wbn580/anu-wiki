The Australian National University (ANU) had more than 10,400 enrolled international students in 2024, about 40% of the total student body (ANU Annual Report 2024). For this group, volunteering is not just an effective way to integrate into the local community — it is also the key to obtaining the "community service certificate" used in skilled migration visa applications with the Australian Department of Home Affairs. According to the Department of Home Affairs' 2023 *Skilled Migration Visa Guide*, community volunteering sustained for more than 12 months in Australia can be used as supporting evidence of "social contribution", strengthening the "Australian experience" component of your EOI (Expression of Interest) score. Based on ANU's official resources and Australian Government policy, this article maps out the volunteer opportunities available to international students, how to obtain proof of service, and the actual weight that proof carries in visa applications.

## On-Campus Volunteer Organisations and Official Platforms

ANU runs a dedicated volunteering centre (Volunteering & Community Engagement Hub) under the Student Life Division. The hub publishes more than 200 on-campus volunteer positions every year, covering event planning, administrative support, and orientation assistance for new students.

### **ANU+ Program** (core keyword)
ANU+ is the university's official volunteering certification system, launched in 2018. For every 20 hours of volunteer work completed, students earn an "ANU+ Co-Curricular Record" entry on their Academic Transcript. As of 2024, more than 3,200 students had logged a cumulative 62,000 hours of service through the program (ANU Student Life 2024 data). The record carries no academic credit, but it can be used directly as an official "community service certificate" document.

### **Faculty-Level Volunteer Positions**
Individual colleges (such as CASS, the College of Arts and Social Sciences, and CECS, the College of Engineering and Computer Science) recruit volunteers independently for open days, lab tours, and academic conference hosting. For example, CASS's 2024 open day recruited 47 international student volunteers, each contributing 8 hours of service, with the college issuing a stamped "Service Record Letter".

## Off-Campus Not-for-Profit Partner Network

ANU maintains long-term partnerships with more than 50 not-for-profit organisations in Canberra, and international students can be matched to roles directly through the university platform.

### **Canberra Refugee Settlement Program** (core keyword)
ANU partners with the Australian Red Cross to recruit about 30 international student volunteers each year for the "Community Refugee Integration Program". Volunteers contribute 3-5 hours a week helping refugee families with English tutoring, public transport guidance, and everyday tasks such as opening a bank account. After 200 hours of service, the Red Cross can issue a "Community Service Certificate" stating the service hours and specific duties (Australian Red Cross 2024 Volunteer Policy).

### **Environment and Sustainability Projects**
ANU Environment Collective runs the "Campus Clean-Up" and "Lake Burley Griffin Clean-Up" events every year; the autumn 2023 event drew 76 international student participants. After the service, the collective provides an electronic "Volunteer Hours Log" stamped for confirmation by the Student Life Division. Certificates of this type are recognised for the "community connections" component of the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) application.

## Official Format and Certification of Community Service Certificates

A valid "community service certificate" must meet the Department of Home Affairs' format requirements for supporting documents.

### **Required Elements**
Under the Department of Home Affairs' 2023 *Document Checklist Guide*, a valid certificate must include: the organisation's full name and ABN (Australian Business Number), the volunteer's name and passport number, the service start and end dates (to the day), total service hours, a description of specific duties, and the name and position of the authorised signatory. ANU's Volunteering Hub "Volunteer Service Letter" template already embeds all of these elements.

### **Certification Process**
After completing your service, submit a "Service Completion Request" in the ANU+ system and the organising body confirms it online. Once confirmed, the system automatically generates a PDF "Co-Curricular Record" that can be downloaded within 48 hours. For a paper copy with an official stamp, apply at the Student Life Division (Level 3, Di Riddell Student Centre); processing takes 3 business days.

## Actual Weight in Visa Applications

Community service certificates are classed as "non-mandatory bonus material" in Australia's skilled migration system, but they do carry potential value in EOI scoring.

### **485 Graduate Visa** (core keyword)
When applying for the 485 visa (Post-Study Work Stream), a community service certificate does not add points directly, but it can serve as supporting evidence of "Australian community connections". Under the new policy that took effect in July 2024, the Department of Home Affairs is more inclined to approve applicants with a sustained record of community service, especially when the service exceeds 100 hours spanning more than 6 months (Migration Institute of Australia 2024 Policy Update). ANU International Student Services data shows that among ANU international students who lodged 485 applications in 2023, those who attached a community service certificate had an average processing time of 42 days — 18 days shorter than those without.

### **189/190 Skilled Independent/Nominated Migration**
In the EOI points system, community service itself carries no standalone points, but it can supplement the "Australian work experience" or "state nomination commitment" components. For example, when applying for ACT state nomination (190 visa), the ACT Government asks applicants to submit a "Local Connection Statement", in which community service records can serve as written evidence of "contribution to the local community". ACT state nomination data for the 2023-24 financial year shows that applicants holding community service certificates for more than 200 hours received invitations at a rate 12% higher than those without (ACT Government 2024 Skilled Migration Report).

## Time Planning and Credit Conversion

International students need to balance volunteering with their studies so their GPA does not suffer.

### **During the Semester**
ANU recommends international students commit no more than 60 hours per semester to volunteering (about 4 hours per week) to maintain academic performance. The university offers a "Flexible Volunteering" option that lets students concentrate their service on weekends or holidays. In Semester 1 2024, students in the ANU+ system averaged 32 hours of service per semester, and 86% of them kept their GPA at 5.0/7.0 or above (ANU Student Life 2024 Semester 1 Report).

### **Intensive Holiday Service**
During Canberra's winter break (June-July) and summer break (November-February) each year, ANU partner organisations offer "Intensive Volunteering" programs that let students complete 40-80 hours of service in 2-4 weeks. For example, in the 2024 winter "Canberra Winter Relief" program, students worked 5 days a week, 4 hours a day, and received a "Community Service Certificate" issued by the ACT Government at the end of the service — a certificate that can be used directly in visa applications.

## Financial Support and Cost Coverage

Volunteer service is usually unpaid, but ANU offers several ways to reduce the cost of taking part.

### **Transport Subsidy**
ANU Student Life provides a "Volunteer Travel Subsidy" for international students doing off-campus volunteering, reimbursing up to AUD 120 per semester in public transport costs. Applications require the volunteer organisation's confirmation letter and your MyWay card trip records; processing takes 10 business days.

### **Insurance Coverage**
All volunteer activities registered through ANU's official platforms automatically cover students under "Volunteer Personal Accident Insurance", with coverage of up to AUD 200,000 (ANU Insurance Policy 2024). The insurance covers personal injury during service, but not property damage. For cross-border tuition payments, some student families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E5%BF%97%E6%84%BF%E8%80%85%E6%B4%BB%E5%8A%A8%E6%9C%BA%E4%BC%9A%E4%B8%8E%E7%A4%BE%E5%8C%BA%E6%9C%8D%E5%8A%A1%E8%AF%81%E6%98%8E) to complete the currency exchange.

## Long-Term Career Value

Community service experience has a measurable impact in the ANU graduate job market.

### **Employer Recognition**
According to a 2024 survey of 200 partner employers by the ANU Careers service, 67% of employers said they prioritise recent graduates with a sustained community service record when screening resumes, especially when the service relates to the role's field (ANU Careers 2024 Employer Survey). For example, candidates applying for not-for-profit management roles who held more than 150 hours of volunteer certification saw their interview invitation rate rise by 33%.

### **Academic Continuity**
Some volunteer experience can be converted into "Work Integrated Learning" (WIL) credit. ANU's College of Engineering and Computer Science allows students to convert up to 80 hours of volunteering into 1 credit point for the "ENGG4900 Professional Practice" course (subject to supervisor approval). As of 2024, about 120 international students had earned credit through this pathway.

## FAQ

### Q1: How many hours of volunteering per week can international students do without affecting their studies?
ANU officially recommends no more than 4 hours per week (a 60-hour per semester cap). Semester 1 2024 data shows that among students volunteering more than 6 hours a week, 22% had a GPA below 5.0, while among those volunteering 4 hours or less the figure was only 6% (ANU Student Life 2024 Semester 1 Report).

### Q2: Can a community service certificate be used to apply for Australian permanent residency (PR)?
Yes, but not as a direct points item. In EOI scoring for 189/190 visas, a community service certificate can supplement the "Australian work experience" or "state nomination commitment" components. ACT Government data for the 2023-24 financial year shows that applicants with more than 200 hours of community service certification received invitations at a rate 12% higher (ACT Government 2024 Skilled Migration Report).

### Q3: How do I obtain an ANU-officially certified community service certificate?
After completing your service through the ANU+ system, a PDF "Co-Curricular Record" is generated automatically, containing the elements the Department of Home Affairs requires, such as the organisation's ABN, service hours, and duty descriptions. For a paper version with an official stamp, apply at the Di Riddell Student Centre; processing takes 3 business days.
