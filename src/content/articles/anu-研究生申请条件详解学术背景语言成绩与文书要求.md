Competition for postgraduate places at the Australian National University (ANU) has kept intensifying in 2025, and its admission criteria set explicit quantitative thresholds across three dimensions: academic background, English language scores and application documents. According to the Annual Report on International Student Data released by the Australian Department of Education in 2024, international applications to ANU postgraduate programs grew by 37% compared with 2020, with applicants of Chinese nationality accounting for more than 45%. Meanwhile, the 2025 edition of the QS World University Rankings places ANU 30th globally, with postgraduate admissions generally requiring a weighted average mark (WAM) of 75 to 85 for graduates of 985/211 universities and 85 to 90 for graduates of non-985/211 (shuangfei) institutions. Drawing on ANU's official admission brochures and publicly available admissions data, this article breaks down the specific requirements and practical points for each of the three core admission criteria.

## Academic Background Requirements: Degree Class and Institution Type

ANU assesses postgraduate applicants' academic backgrounds primarily on the level of their undergraduate institution and the class of their degree. Its admissions review applies a **tiered standard** that classifies Chinese universities into 985/211 institutions and non-985/211 (shuangfei) institutions, each with its own minimum weighted average mark (WAM) threshold.

### Applicants from 985/211 universities

For applicants who graduated from a Chinese university in the 985 Project or 211 Project, most ANU coursework master's programs require a **weighted average mark** of 75 to 80 (on a 100-point scale). In the College of Business and Economics, for example, the Master of Finance requires a WAM of at least 85 for 985/211 graduates, while the Master of Management sets the bar at 75. Most programs in the College of Engineering require a WAM of at least 75, while related programs in the College of Computing require 80 or above. ANU updated its admissions guide in 2023 to state clearly that rounded-up marks are no longer accepted — only the actual score calculated by the system counts 【ANU 2023, Postgraduate Admission Guide】.

### Applicants from non-985/211 (shuangfei) universities

Applicants from non-985/211 universities face a higher bar. ANU generally requires shuangfei graduates to score 5 to 10 points higher on the WAM than 985/211 graduates. For example, the Master of Computing requires a WAM of at least 85 for shuangfei applicants, while the Master of International Relations requires at least 80. Some highly competitive programs, such as the Master of Applied Economics, may require as much as 90. Applicants should note that ANU uses a **7-point GPA conversion system**: Chinese percentage marks must first be converted to a 7-point GPA, and the conversion table is available on the "International Qualifications" page of the ANU website 【ANU 2024, International Qualifications Assessment】.

## English Language Requirements: Standardised Tests and Exemptions

ANU accepts a range of standardised English language test results, all of which must still be valid at the time the application is submitted (validity is usually 2 years). The university sets uniform minimum scores for **IELTS**, TOEFL and PTE Academic.

### IELTS and TOEFL score requirements

Most coursework master's programs require an overall IELTS score of at least 6.5, with no band below 6.0. Some humanities and social sciences programs, such as the Master of Arts (Advanced) and the Master of Laws (LLM), require an overall IELTS score of 7.0, with a minimum of 7.0 in Writing and 6.5 in all other bands. The TOEFL iBT requirement is typically an overall score of 80, with at least 20 in Reading and Writing and 18 in Listening and Speaking; for programs requiring an IELTS of 7.0, the TOEFL iBT requirement rises to an overall score of 100 with at least 25 in Writing 【ANU 2025, English Language Requirements】.

### Language test exemption conditions

Applicants who have completed at least 2 years of full-time undergraduate or postgraduate study in an English-speaking country may apply for a **language requirement exemption**. The specific conditions are: the program must have been taught in English, and graduation must have occurred within 2 years of the application date. ANU does not accept exemption requests based on "using English as a working language" or "having worked in an English-speaking country". In addition, some programs — such as the Master of Finance and the Master of Applied Economics — may require GMAT or GRE scores, but these are usually treated as a supplement to the academic background rather than a substitute for the language requirement.

## Application Documents: Personal Statement and References

Application documents are a key part of how ANU's admissions committee assesses an applicant's academic motivation and potential. Unlike some US institutions, ANU places more weight on **academic relevance** than on personal narrative.

### Statement of Purpose

Most coursework master's programs require a personal statement of 500 to 1,000 words. It should clearly answer three core questions: the specific academic motivation for applying to the program, the relevance of your undergraduate studies or research experience to the program, and your plans after graduation in academia or your profession. ANU officially advises applicants not to make vague statements about "loving Australia" and instead to focus on specific course structures, professors' research interests, or academic resources unique to ANU, such as collaborative projects with the "College of Asia and the Pacific" or the "Australian Academy of Science" 【ANU 2024, How to Write a Statement of Purpose】.

### Reference letters and CV

Some programs require two academic reference letters, written by professors who taught you or research supervisors from your undergraduate studies and submitted from official university email addresses. The letters should focus on your academic ability, research potential and classroom performance. Some programs, such as the Master of Public Policy, also require a CV that highlights academic achievements, research projects and internships relevant to the program. ## Application Process and Key Dates

ANU postgraduate admissions run on a **rolling basis**, but with clearly defined priority rounds and deadlines. Applications for Semester 1 2025 (February intake) opened in March 2024, with a priority-round deadline of 31 August 2024 and a regular-round deadline of 15 December 2024. For Semester 2 (July intake), the priority-round deadline is 15 March 2025 and the regular-round deadline is 31 May 2025.

### Checklist of application documents

A complete application requires the following documents: undergraduate graduation certificate and degree certificate (notarised in both Chinese and English), full academic transcripts (including a GPA conversion note), English language test results (if applicable), a personal statement, reference letters (for some programs), a copy of your passport, and the application fee (AUD 100). ANU scrapped the application fee waiver for some programs in 2024, so all international applicants must now pay.

### Offers and how to accept

Admission outcomes are usually released within 4 to 8 weeks of submission. ANU issues two types of offers: Conditional Offer and Unconditional Offer. Conditional offers are typically made to applicants whose language scores are not yet met or who are still in their final semester, and require the outstanding documents to be submitted by the deadline. To accept an offer you must pay a tuition deposit equal to 50% of the first-semester tuition fee or AUD 5,000, whichever is higher. Once paid, the university issues an electronic Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), which is used to apply for a student visa 【Australian Department of Home Affairs 2024, Student Visa Processing Guidelines】.

## Special Program Admission Requirements

Some ANU postgraduate programs impose additional admission hurdles, involving interviews, portfolios or work experience. These programs typically fall into **professionally accredited** or **research-intensive** fields.

### Research master's and PhD programs

Master of Philosophy and PhD programs require applicants to submit a Research Proposal, usually 1,500 to 3,000 words in length, and to obtain written agreement from at least one ANU supervisor willing to act as a potential supervisor. Applicants must also provide academic transcripts from both undergraduate and master's studies, with a master's GPA of at least 5.0/7.0. Some colleges require applicants to attend an interview to assess the feasibility of the research proposal and the fit with the academic unit.

### Professional practice programs

For example, the Master of Clinical Psychology and the Master of Speech Pathology require, in addition to academic results, at least 100 hours of clinical placement or relevant work experience, plus scores from a professional aptitude test (such as the PCE or GRE Psychology). These programs usually have enrolment caps: in 2024 the Master of Clinical Psychology admitted only 12 international students, with an acceptance rate below 5% 【ANU 2024, College of Health and Medicine Admissions Data】.

## Common Application Mistakes and Things to Watch

Applicants often make mistakes during preparation because of information asymmetry. Below are the three most common errors and how to avoid them.

### GPA conversion errors

Some applicants mistakenly assume ANU accepts Chinese percentage marks directly and skip the **7-point GPA conversion**, so the score calculated by the admissions system comes out lower than expected. ANU's official conversion table shows that a Chinese mark of 85 corresponds to 6.0 on the 7-point GPA scale, while 80 corresponds to only 5.5. Applicants should run a preliminary check with ANU's online GPA calculator before submitting.

### Expired language test results

IELTS and TOEFL scores are valid for 2 years, but ANU requires them to still be valid at the time of application. For example, if you submit an application in October 2024 for the February 2025 intake, your language test must have been taken no earlier than October 2022. Expired scores are treated as invalid, and you would need to sit the test again.

### Template-style application documents

ANU's admissions committee noted in its 2023 admissions report that more than 60% of personal statements suffer from **template-style writing**, relying heavily on clichés such as "I am passionate about" without mentioning any specific course names or professors. Applicants are advised to read the "Course Structure" page on the program's official website carefully, cite one or two specific courses, and explain how they relate to their own academic plans.

## FAQ

### Q1: How long does ANU postgraduate admission typically take?

According to ANU Admissions Office data for 2024, complete applications take an average of 6 weeks to process, with the College of Business and Economics and the College of Computing taking the longest at up to 8 weeks. Priority-round applications are processed about 2 weeks faster than regular-round ones.

### Q2: Is there a minimum GPA requirement for shuangfei (non-985/211) students applying to ANU?

Yes. ANU's minimum weighted average mark for shuangfei students is usually 85 (out of 100), equivalent to 6.0 on the 7-point GPA scale. Some programs, such as the Master of Finance, require 90 (GPA 6.5). This requirement has been strictly enforced since 2023, and "soft" supplementary materials are no longer accepted 【ANU 2023, Admission Policy Update】.

### Q3: Does ANU accept the Duolingo English Test?

No. As of 2025, ANU recognises only four language tests: IELTS (Academic), TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic and Cambridge English (C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency). Duolingo scores are not accepted for any program.
