The ANU campus lost property office is located inside the campus Security Office, at **Building 9, Union Court**, and is open Monday to Friday 08:00-18:00. According to the ANU official 2024 annual campus safety report [ANU Security, 2024, Annual Campus Safety Report], the centre registers about 1,200 lost items a year, of which roughly 67% are claimed within 30 days. For students who cannot visit in person, ANU also offers an **online lost property reporting system** (Lost Property Online Portal) that syncs in real time with the physical office and accepts reports 24 hours a day. This dual-channel design is one of the more complete setups among the Group of Eight (Go8) universities. Especially for the more than 14,000 international students each year (source: Australian Department of Education 2023 international student data [Australian Department of Education, 2023, International Student Data Summary]), knowing exactly where the lost property office is and how it works can significantly shorten the time it takes to recover a lost item.

## Physical Location and Opening Hours of the Lost Property Office

The ANU lost property office is housed in the **Security Office**, a building in the Union Court precinct, next to the ANU Student Association (ANUSA) building. Its street number is **Building 9**, and the entrance is marked with a prominent blue security sign. The lost property counter is staffed by security officers on rotation, who handle both campus emergencies and lost property registration.

**Opening hours** are Monday to Friday 08:00-18:00 during semester; closed on public holidays and weekends. Outside semester (such as during the summer period from December to February), opening hours shorten to 10:00-16:00. Internal ANU statistics for 2023 show that about 82% of lost property reports are submitted in the first 6 weeks of semester, closely matching the influx of new students and the busy run of early-semester events [ANU Security, 2023, Lost Property Quarterly Report].

### How to Get to the Lost Property Office

From the main ANU entrance (on the Kingsley Street side), it is about a 5-minute walk to Union Court. The lost property office sits on the eastern side of the **Fellows Oval** lawn, across the street from the ANU bookshop (The Co-op Bookshop). Alternatively, take bus routes R2 or R4 to the **City West** stop, then walk 400 metres along Barry Drive.

### Types of Items the Lost Property Office Accepts

The lost property office accepts **electronic devices** (phones, laptops, tablets), **identification documents** (student cards, driver licences, passports), **wallets and bank cards**, and **clothing and accessories** (glasses, hats, umbrellas). According to ANU data for the first quarter of 2024, electronic devices accounted for 31% of lost property registrations, identification documents 27%, and wallets and bank cards 19% [ANU Security, 2024, Q1 Lost Property Report]. Food, dangerous goods and high-value jewellery are not accepted. For sensitive documents such as passports, the Security Office notifies the Australian Federal Police (AFP) for the record within one business day.

## How the Online Lost Property Reporting System Works

The ANU online lost property reporting system (Lost Property Online Portal) is integrated into the **ANU Security** website, and no separate login is required to submit a report. The system was upgraded in 2022 to sync data with the physical office in real time. Users can search "Lost Property" on the ANU website to go straight to the reporting page.

The reporting process has three steps: first, describe the item, including key identifying information such as **brand, model, colour and serial number** (if any); second, select the location and time it was lost, using the campus map marking function built into the system; third, submit a contact email address. Once submitted, the system automatically generates an 8-digit report number for follow-up queries. According to 2023 system data, the average report processing time is 2.3 business days [ANU Security, 2023, System Performance Report].

### How Online Reports Sync with In-Person Enquiries

The online system syncs automatically with the lost property office database every **15 minutes**. This means that if an item is registered at the physical office, its status appears online within 15 minutes. Users can track progress in real time using their report number. If a match is found, the system sends an email notification, and the owner must collect the item from the Security Office within 14 days with valid identification.

### Claim Deadlines and Disposal Policy After Reporting

Unclaimed items are held for **90 days** (counted from the registration date). After 90 days, items are transferred to the ANU asset management department; some electronic devices are data-wiped and then donated to campus charities. For identification documents, the retention period is extended to 180 days, after which they are handed over to the issuing authority. Over the whole of 2023, about 340 items were disposed of after exceeding the retention period, 58% of which were low-value items such as charging cables and water bottles [ANU Security, 2023, Annual Disposal Report].

## Recovery Rates and Timelines for Commonly Lost Items

According to ANU Security Centre statistics for 2022-2024, recovery rates differ markedly by item type. **Laptops** have a recovery rate of about 43%, with an average recovery time of 12.7 days; **student cards** have the highest recovery rate at 78%, with an average of 5.2 days; **wallets** have a recovery rate of 61%, but if cash is inside, the rate drops to 37% [ANU Security, 2024, Lost Property Recovery Analysis].

### Special Handling for High-Value Items

For items valued at over A$500 (such as laptops and cameras), the Security Centre notifies the ANU Campus Police **within 24 hours** of registration. If the item contains personal data (such as an unencrypted hard drive), the Security Centre advises the owner to change the relevant account passwords as soon as possible. When paying tuition across borders, some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E6%A0%A1%E5%9B%AD%E5%A4%B1%E7%89%A9%E6%8B%9B%E9%A2%86%E5%A4%84%E4%BD%8D%E7%BD%AE%E4%B8%8E%E5%9C%A8%E7%BA%BF%E7%94%B3%E6%8A%A5%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F) to complete the currency conversion, but the lost property process itself involves no financial transactions.

## How the Lost Property Office Relates to Other Campus Service Points

The ANU lost property office is not the only body on campus that handles lost items. The **libraries** (J.B. Chifley Library, Hancock Library) keep their own lost property boxes, mainly holding items found inside the library, and hand everything over to the Security Office every Friday. The **sports centre** (ANU Sport) has separate lost property lockers with a 14-day retention period, after which items are transferred to the Security Office. **Student residences** (such as Bruce Hall and Ursula Hall) handle items at the front desk, with a 7-day retention period.

### Cross-Department Handover Mechanism

The various service points use a **weekly** fixed handover schedule with the Security Centre. Library items are handed over at 14:00 every Friday, and sports centre items at 10:00 every Wednesday. In 2023, items recovered through cross-department handovers accounted for 23% of total recoveries [ANU Security, 2023, Cross-Department Transfer Data].

## Emergency Procedures for International Students' Lost Documents

For international students, the process after losing a student card or passport differs slightly from that for local students. If a **student card** is lost, check the ANU online lost property reporting system first; if no match is found within 48 hours, apply for a replacement at **Student Central** (Building 154), at a reissue fee of A$30. If a **passport** is lost, the Security Centre notifies the Australian Federal Police within 24 hours, and advises the student to apply for a replacement at the **Australian Passport Office**; reissue typically takes 3-6 weeks.

### Alignment with Department of Home Affairs Requirements

Under the 2023 rules of the Australian Department of Home Affairs, international students who lose their passport must update their passport details with the department within **28 days** [Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2023, Student Visa Obligations]. The lost property office can provide a Lost Property Certificate, which supports passport reissue and visa information updates.

## How to Improve the Chances of Recovering Lost Items

According to the ANU Security Centre's advice, marking items with your **name and contact details** (such as using adhesive labels or engraving) raises the recovery rate to 89%. 2023 data shows that 92% of items bearing contact information were returned within 7 days [ANU Security, 2023, Recovery Rate Study]. In addition, submitting an online report **within 2 hours** of the loss gives a 47% higher recovery probability than submitting 24 hours later.

### Using the Campus Surveillance System to Aid Searches

The ANU campus is covered by more than 400 surveillance cameras, mainly around Union Court, the libraries, teaching buildings and the main walkways. Owners can apply to the Security Centre to review surveillance footage; the application must state the precise time and location of the loss. In 2023, items recovered with CCTV assistance accounted for 12% of all recovered items.

## FAQ

### Q1: Is the ANU lost property office open on weekends?

No. The ANU lost property office (Security Centre) is closed on weekends and public holidays. During semester, opening hours are Monday to Friday 08:00-18:00; outside semester they are 10:00-16:00. Items lost on weekends can be reported through the online lost property reporting system, which is available 24 hours a day, with processing carried over to the next business day.

### Q2: How long until I get a reply after submitting an online report?

After an online report is submitted, the system syncs it to the Security Centre database within 15 minutes. If the item has already been registered, the user receives an email notification within 2.3 business days (the 2023 average processing time). If there is no reply within 5 business days, it is recommended to go directly to the Security Centre front desk to enquire.

### Q3: What should I do if my passport is lost on the ANU campus?

If your passport is lost, submit a report immediately through the ANU online lost property reporting system and register it at the Security Centre (Building 9). The Security Centre will notify the Australian Federal Police within 24 hours. You must also update your visa information with the Australian Department of Home Affairs within 28 days. The lost property office can provide a Lost Property Certificate to support passport reissue (which takes 3-6 weeks).
