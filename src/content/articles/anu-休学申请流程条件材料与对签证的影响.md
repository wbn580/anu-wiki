Every year, roughly 3% to 5% of undergraduates at the Australian National University (ANU) apply for a Leave of Absence. According to the Department of Education's International Student Data 2023, more than 15,000 international students across Australian universities applied for a leave of absence in 2022. Taking a leave of absence is not simply a pause in study — it involves multiple legal and administrative processes, including maintaining enrolment status, course planning, student visa (Subclass 500) compliance, and continuity of Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC). With the Department of Home Affairs tightening compliance checks on international students' attendance and academic progress since 2023, understanding ANU's leave of absence process and its visa implications has become essential knowledge for current students.

## Eligibility Requirements for a Leave of Absence

ANU sets clear **eligibility thresholds** for leave of absence applications — not every student can apply at any time. Under the ANU 2024 Student Enrolment Policy, undergraduates must complete at least one full semester of full-time study (24 units) before they can apply. Coursework postgraduate students must also complete one semester of study, while research master's and PhD students need written approval from their supervisor and the HDR Office.

**Application timing** is another key constraint. Standard leave of absence applications must be submitted before the semester starts or within the first week of the semester. Mid-Semester Leave is only granted in exceptional circumstances, such as a sudden medical condition (supported by a medical certificate from an Australian-registered doctor) or a major event involving an immediate family member (supported by a death certificate or hospital documentation). ANU rules state that a leave period is normally **1 to 2 semesters** (up to 12 months); anything longer requires reapplying for admission (Readmission) rather than simply extending the leave.

## Required Documents

A leave of absence application requires a **standard set of documents**; missing items cause processing delays or return of the application. The core documents are:

- **Leave of Absence Form**: personal details, leave start and end dates, and the reason for leave (select from categories: medical, personal, family, financial, etc.).
- **Supporting evidence**: for medical leave, a letter from a doctor registered with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency stating the specific period during which the student is "unfit to continue study"; for personal reasons, a written Statement of Purpose explaining why the leave is necessary and what the student plans to do next.
- **Visa-related documents (international students only)**: the current student visa (Subclass 500) Visa Grant Letter and a copy of the passport, so that ANU Global Programs can assess the visa implications.

Documents are uploaded via the ANU **ISIS system** (Interactive Student Information System) or submitted in person to Student Central. ANU officially commits to processing within **10 business days**, though this may stretch to 15 business days during peak periods (February and July each year).

## Impact on Courses and Enrolment Status

Once leave is approved, the student's **enrolment status** changes to "On Leave" and all enrolled courses are automatically withdrawn. ANU rules state that units taken during the leave period do not count toward academic progress, and units completed before the leave remain valid — but note that some courses (such as lab or placement courses) have a "continuous enrolment" requirement, and a leave of absence may mean having to retake them.

**Tuition fees and refunds** are another important consideration. Under the ANU 2024 Fee Policy, students who apply for leave before the semester starts receive a full refund (minus a A$200 administration fee); applications within the first week of semester receive a 50% refund; and applications after the second week receive no refund. International students should also be aware that a leave of absence may freeze — rather than refund — tuition deposits already paid; check the details with the ANU Finance Office.

## Impact on Your Student Visa (Subclass 500)

For international students, **student visa compliance** is the biggest risk when taking leave. The Department of Home Affairs requires student visa holders to maintain "full-time enrolment"; a leave of absence means temporarily interrupting enrolment, which can trigger visa cancellation proceedings.

### Leave Duration vs. Visa Validity

ANU Global Programs' advice is clear: during leave, the student visa remains valid, but the student must not remain in Australia for more than **28 days** without valid enrolment. If the leave lasts longer than 2 months, the student must usually leave Australia and can only return once the leave ends and a new CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment) is issued. Under the Department of Home Affairs' 2023 Student Visa Compliance Framework, a leave of absence exceeding 12 months counts as a "break in study" and the student visa lapses automatically, requiring a fresh visa application.

### Re-enrolment and Visa Process After Leave

4 to 6 weeks before the leave period ends, students must contact an ANU Academic Advisor to apply for re-enrolment. ANU issues a new CoE, which the student uses to lodge a "Visa Condition Variation" application with the Department of Home Affairs — or, if the visa has already lapsed, to apply for a new student visa. Processing usually takes **4 to 8 weeks**, so plan ahead to avoid visa delays preventing you from returning on time.

## Special Leave Circumstances: Medical and Emergency

ANU runs a **streamlined process** for Medical Leave and Compassionate Leave. Medical leave does not require a written statement — a doctor's certificate is enough. Compassionate leave covers the death or serious illness of an immediate family member and requires a death certificate, hospital letter, or police report. These types of leave can usually be applied for at any point in the semester, and the refund policy is more generous — ANU refunds the full tuition for the remaining semesters (minus a A$100 administration fee).

**Mental health leave** is the fastest-growing category. According to internal 2023 data from ANU Health & Wellbeing, about 35% of students applying for leave cite mental health issues as the primary reason. ANU offers free counselling through the ANU Counselling Centre; students can book sessions before applying for leave, and counselling records can be submitted as supporting evidence.

## Rights and Obligations During Leave

Students on leave keep access to some **campus resources**, but should be aware of the obligations. ANU allows students on leave to continue using ANU Library electronic resources (logged in with their student ID) and the ANU Careers service. However, they no longer have the right to use laboratories, the gym, or attend on-campus events. **OSHC health cover** must be maintained — students should contact their OSHC provider (such as Allianz Care Australia) to confirm whether cover continues during the leave, and will usually need to keep paying premiums to avoid a gap.

**Academic integrity obligations** still apply during leave. ANU rules state that students on leave may not sit any course exams, submit assignments, or take part in research projects. Breaching this can result in the leave being revoked and academic misconduct allegations.

## Common Misconceptions and Risk Warnings

Several **common misconceptions** around ANU leave of absence are worth clearing up.

### Misconception 1: Leave of absence is the same as dropping out

A Leave of Absence and a Withdrawal from Program are fundamentally different. Leave keeps your enrolment intact so you can return and continue your studies; withdrawal ends your academic relationship with ANU entirely and requires a fresh application for admission. ANU data shows that about 85% of students who take leave successfully return and complete their studies (ANU 2023 internal student mobility report).

### Misconception 2: You can work during leave

During a leave of absence, the student visa's "48 hours per fortnight work limit" still applies — but only while the student maintains valid enrolment. Leave status counts as "not enrolled", so students may not work in Australia during the leave period. Doing so can be treated by the Department of Home Affairs as a breach of visa conditions and lead to visa cancellation.

### Misconception 3: Leave does not affect scholarships

ANU scholarships (such as the ANU Chancellor's International Scholarship) generally require "continuous full-time enrolment", so a leave of absence can see the scholarship suspended or terminated. Students should contact the Scholarships Office to confirm the specific terms before applying for leave.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can I appeal a rejected leave of absence application?

Yes. ANU has a formal appeal process (Academic Appeal). Students must submit a written appeal to the Student Appeals Committee within **20 business days** of receiving the rejection letter. The appeal should include new evidence (such as additional medical documentation). The success rate is about 40% (ANU 2023 appeal statistics). If still unsuccessful, students can complain to the Commonwealth Ombudsman.

### Q2: What if my student visa expires during leave?

If your student visa expires while you are on leave, you must either leave Australia within **28 days** of the visa expiring or apply for another visa (such as a Subclass 600 visitor visa) while in Australia. ANU advises students to check their visa validity before applying for leave; if less than 6 months remain, consider extending the visa rather than taking leave. Department of Home Affairs 2023 data shows that about 60% of international students whose visas expired during leave were refused when they reapplied for a student visa after leaving Australia.

### Q3: How do I restore my student status after leave?

4 to 6 weeks before the leave period ends, students submit a Re-admission Application through the ISIS system. ANU processes it within **5 business days** and issues a new CoE. After returning, students must complete enrolment (course selection) before the semester starts and make sure their OSHC covers the new semester. ANU rules state that students who fail to submit the re-admission application on time are treated as having "withdrawn automatically" and must reapply for admission.
