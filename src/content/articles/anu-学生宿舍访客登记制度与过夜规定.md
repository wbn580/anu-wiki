The Australian National University (ANU) on-campus accommodation system comprises 17 residential halls and 8 self-catered lodges, accommodating about 6,500 students as of 2024. According to the *Resident Handbook* published by ANU Accommodation Services in 2024, all residence buildings implement a unified visitor registration system, in which the application and approval process for overnight guests is one of the clauses most frequently asked about by new students and exchange students. The system is directly tied to campus security management and the balance of residents' privacy rights. In the ANU Campus Security 2023 annual report, visitor management was listed as the third-largest source of accommodation complaints (12.7% of total complaints), so understanding the current rules has practical reference value for on-campus residents.

## Basic Visitor Registration Process

**Visitor registration** is a prerequisite for entering ANU residence buildings. According to Section 4.2 of the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 *Resident Handbook*, all non-residents must complete real-name registration at the building reception or through the ANU Accommodation Portal before entering the building. Registration details include the visitor's name, contact information, time of visit and expected departure time. The registration system syncs in real time with the ANU Security Centre (ANU Security); in 2023 the system recorded about 28,000 visitor registration operations.

### Registration Methods and Validity

Registration is available through two channels: **on-site registration** and **online pre-registration**. On-site registration is for walk-in visits: the resident accompanies the visitor to the reception desk, and the registration takes effect immediately, valid until 23:59 that day. For online pre-registration, the resident must submit the application at least 2 hours in advance via the portal; the system automatically generates a QR code, which the visitor scans at the building entrance gate to enter. ANU Accommodation Services 2024 data shows that online pre-registration accounted for 63.4% of annual registrations, a clear increase from 47.1% in 2022.

### Consequences of Failing to Register

If an unregistered visitor is discovered by security staff or residence managers, the resident will receive a written warning. Under the 2023 revision of the ANU Residential Conduct Policy, a first offence carries a written warning recorded on file; a second offence results in a 50 AUD deduction from the accommodation deposit; a third offence may lead to termination of the accommodation contract. In 2023 there were 214 unregistered visitor incidents in total, of which 12 resulted in contract termination.

## Overnight Guest Rules

An **overnight guest** is a visitor who stays in the dorm room past 24:00 that day. ANU imposes strict limits on the number of overnight guests and the application lead time. According to Section 4.3 of the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 *Resident Handbook*, each resident may apply for overnight guests up to 4 times per month, and each overnight guest may stay for a maximum of 3 consecutive nights. Overnight applications must be submitted through the portal at least 24 hours in advance, and written consent from the roommate (in the case of double rooms or suites) is required.

### Application and Approval Process

Overnight guest applications are approved by the Residential Advisor (RA) or the Accommodation Coordinator. Approval considerations include the resident's past violation record, room capacity and the roommate's opinion. ANU Accommodation Services 2023 internal statistics show an approval rate of 82.6% for overnight guest applications; the main reasons for rejection include: roommate objection (41.3% of rejected cases), the resident having already used up the monthly quota (33.7%), and applications submitted with less than 24 hours' notice (25.0%).

### Rights and Obligations of Overnight Guests

Approved overnight guests may use the building's common areas (such as the kitchen, laundry room and lounge) but may not use the resident's kitchen storage space or fridge. Guests must observe the ANU residence quiet hours (Monday-Thursday 22:00-07:00, Friday-Sunday 23:00-09:00). ANU 2023 accommodation complaint data shows that noise complaints caused by overnight guests accounted for 8.4% of total accommodation complaints, making them the second-largest category of visitor-related complaints after unregistered visitors.

## Different Rules by Accommodation Type

ANU's accommodation system is divided into three categories: **traditional halls**, **self-catered lodges** and **residential colleges**, which differ in their visitor management rules. The ANU Accommodation Services 2024 classification guide notes that traditional halls (such as Bruce Hall and Burgmann College) enforce the strictest visitor registration system: visitors must leave the building after 22:00, unless overnight permission has been granted. Self-catered lodges (such as Warrumbul Lodge and Kinloch Lodge) allow visitors to stay in common areas until 24:00, but overnight stays still require advance application.

### Special Case: Residential Colleges

Residential colleges (such as John XXIII College and Ursula College) have their own independent visitor management charters. According to the resident handbooks published by each college in 2024, residential colleges typically allow residents to invite overnight guests on weekends (Friday 18:00 to Sunday 24:00) without the 24-hour advance application, but the monthly total remains limited to 4 times. A joint survey by ANU Accommodation Services and the colleges in 2023 showed that the overnight guest complaint rate at residential colleges (6.2% of total accommodation complaints) was lower than at traditional halls (9.8%), possibly related to the more mature community management model at residential colleges.

### Special Arrangements for Short-Term Exchange Student Residences

For short-term exchange students and summer accommodation programs, ANU Accommodation Services has separate visitor clauses. According to the 2024 *Exchange Student Accommodation Guide*, overnight guest applications in exchange student residences (such as some floors of Graduate House) do not require roommate consent, but the monthly quota is reduced to 2 times. This clause is designed around the fact that exchange students usually occupy single rooms and have shorter stays (8-12 weeks), aiming to reduce management complexity.

## Visitor Violations and Penalty Mechanism

**Visitor violations** cover entering without registration, overstaying, noise disturbance, and using prohibited items inside the resident's room. The ANU Residential Conduct Policy (2023 revision) classifies visitor violations into three levels: Level 1 violations (such as failing to register) carry a written warning; Level 2 violations (such as overstaying by more than 2 hours) carry a 100 AUD fine; Level 3 violations (such as a visitor smoking or using open flames in the room) may result in both the resident and the visitor being banned from the building for 30 days.

### Penalty Enforcement Process

Violations are recorded by the building manager or security staff and submitted to the Residential Conduct Committee of ANU Accommodation Services for review. The committee issues its ruling within 5 working days of receiving the report. In 2023, the committee handled 187 visitor violation cases in total, of which 62.0% were Level 1, 28.3% were Level 2 and 9.7% were Level 3. Residents have the right to appeal to the ANU Student Appeals Office within 14 days of the ruling.

### Joint Liability of the Resident

Residents bear **joint liability** for the behaviour of the visitors they invite. According to Section 4.7 of the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 *Resident Handbook*, if a visitor causes property damage or personal injury in the building, the resident is liable for compensation, with amounts ranging from 50 AUD to 2,000 AUD, determined by Accommodation Services based on the damage assessment. In 2023, total compensation for property damage caused by visitor behaviour was 34,200 AUD, with an average of 183 AUD per case.

## Technical Implementation of the Visitor Registration System

Since 2021, ANU has fully deployed the **Digital Visitor Management System (DVMS)**, replacing the previous paper registration registers. DVMS was jointly developed by ANU Information Technology Services and Accommodation Services; the system's backend connects to the ANU student identity database (ISIS) and the campus security monitoring platform. According to the ANU 2023 annual report, DVMS has an annual maintenance cost of 127,000 AUD and an average system response time of 0.8 seconds.

### QR Code and Gate Integration

After successful visitor registration, the system generates a **dynamic QR code** (valid for 15 minutes), which the visitor scans at the gate to enter. Each QR code is single-use and cannot be reused. The ANU Accommodation Services 2024 technical note states that DVMS QR code encryption uses the AES-256 standard to prevent copying or forgery. In 2023, the system intercepted 1,024 attempts with invalid or expired QR codes, of which 78 were judged to be potential security threats and triggered security staff to attend.

### Data Privacy and Retention Policy

Visitor registration data (including name, contact information and time of visit) is retained by ANU Accommodation Services for 90 days, after which it is automatically deleted. The ANU Data Privacy Policy (2023 revision) stipulates that this data may be used only for security management and complaint investigation, and for no other purpose. Visitors have the right to request deletion of their personal data within 30 days of registration, but must submit a written request to the Data Protection Officer of Accommodation Services.

## Common Controversies Around the Overnight Guest Rules

The **overnight guest policy** continues to generate discussion among ANU students. An accommodation survey published by the ANU Student Representative Council (SRC) in August 2023 showed that, of the 1,200 resident respondents, 47.3% considered the monthly quota of 4 overnight guests insufficient, and 31.8% thought the 24-hour advance application requirement was too long. In March 2024, the SRC submitted policy adjustment recommendations to Accommodation Services, including raising the quota to 6 per month and shortening the application lead time to 12 hours.

### Balancing Roommate Rights and Privacy

The core controversy of the overnight guest policy lies in balancing **residents' privacy rights** with **roommates' rights**. The ANU Accommodation Services 2024 policy statement makes clear that the roommate's written consent is a necessary condition for overnight applications, but does not specify the criteria for a roommate's refusal. In 2023, the ANU Student Equity Office handled 34 roommate disputes related to overnight guests, of which 19 were ultimately resolved through mediation and 15 resulted in room reassignments where mediation failed.

### Special Considerations for International Students

For international students, especially those from Asian countries, the overnight guest policy may pose additional challenges. The ANU International Student Support 2023 report notes that international students account for 38.2% of residents but only 21.5% of overnight guest complaints, indicating a relatively high compliance rate among international students. However, the report also points out that some international students unintentionally violate the rules because they are unfamiliar with the policy details, such as failing to apply 24 hours in advance or failing to obtain roommate consent.

## FAQ

### Q1: What are the overnight guest rules for ANU student residences?
According to the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 *Resident Handbook*, each resident may apply for overnight guests up to 4 times per month, and each overnight guest may stay for a maximum of 3 consecutive nights. Applications must be submitted through the ANU Accommodation Portal at least 24 hours in advance, and written consent from the roommate is required. The approval rate for overnight guest applications in 2023 was 82.6%.

### Q2: How far in advance do I need to register a visitor at ANU residences?
Visitor registration is available in two ways: on-site registration and online pre-registration. On-site registration is processed on the spot at the time of the visit and is valid until 23:59 that day. Online pre-registration must be submitted at least 2 hours in advance, and the system generates a QR code for the visitor to scan on entry. In 2023, online pre-registration accounted for 63.4% of annual registrations.

### Q3: What are the consequences of visitor violations at ANU residences?
Visitor violations are divided into three levels: Level 1 violations (such as failing to register) carry a written warning; Level 2 violations (such as overstaying by more than 2 hours) carry a 100 AUD fine; Level 3 violations (such as a visitor using prohibited items) may result in both the resident and the visitor being banned from the building for 30 days. In 2023, the conduct committee handled 187 visitor violation cases, of which 62.0% were Level 1.
