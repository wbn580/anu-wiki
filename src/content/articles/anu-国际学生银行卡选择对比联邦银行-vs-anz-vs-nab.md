As of the third quarter of 2024, Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) data shows that Australia has more than 42 million active debit card transaction accounts, with Commonwealth Bank, ANZ and National Australia Bank (NAB) together holding more than 68% of the personal bank account market share (Australian Prudential Regulation Authority, APRA, 2024, Monthly Banking Statistics). For international students arriving to study at the Australian National University (ANU), choosing a bank directly affects the cost efficiency of daily spending, tuition payments and cross-border remittances. Based on the three banks' official published fee schedules for 2024 and the distribution of branches around the ANU campus, this guide compares the account types, fee structures and international-student services of Commonwealth Bank, ANZ and NAB to help students make data-driven decisions.

## Commonwealth Bank Account Features

Commonwealth Bank is Australia's largest retail bank by assets, and its international student account offering is built around **no monthly fees** and **digital account opening**. Its **Smart Access account** for students aged 18 and over waives the A$4 monthly account-keeping fee, provided the cardholder is under 21 or a full-time enrolled student (Commonwealth Bank, 2024, Personal Account Fee Schedule). International students can pre-register for an account through the Commonwealth Bank mobile app before arriving in Australia, then activate it at any branch after landing by presenting their passport and Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) — no appointment needed.

### Campus Branch and ATM Coverage

Commonwealth Bank has a **full-service branch** at Union Court on ANU's main Acton campus, open Monday to Friday 9:00-16:00. In addition, there are three 24-hour ATMs in the Kambri precinct, supporting cardless cash withdrawals and real-time balance enquiries. According to ANU Facilities Management's 2023 campus map data, Commonwealth Bank has the highest ATM density of the three banks within the ANU campus, with a machine roughly every 500 metres.

### International Remittances and Exchange Rates

Commonwealth Bank offers a **real-time cross-border payment** service, allowing transfers to overseas accounts directly through the mobile app. The flat fee per transfer is A$6 (including intermediary bank charges), and transfers typically arrive within 1-2 working days. The bank uses real-time market exchange rates with no additional exchange margin, but transfers over A$10,000 require proof of the source of funds (Commonwealth Bank, 2024, International Remittance Terms).

## ANZ Account Features

ANZ is the only one of the big four banks with an extensive branch network in Asia and the Pacific, and its international student account offering focuses on **multi-currency accounts** and **overseas account linking**. ANZ's **Access Advantage account** waives the A$5 monthly fee for full-time international students, who must present a valid student visa and ANU enrolment proof when opening the account (ANZ, 2024, Personal Account Fee Schedule). The bank allows students to deposit cheques and cash free of charge at any ANZ branch within 90 days of opening the account.

### Multi-Currency Account Features

ANZ offers the **ANZ Plus multi-currency account**, which supports holding six currencies at once: Australian dollars, US dollars, euros, British pounds, New Zealand dollars and Japanese yen. Students can convert between the six currencies in real time through the mobile app, at ANZ's daily buy/sell rates with no additional conversion fee. According to ANZ's August 2024 fee announcement, the multi-currency account has a daily conversion limit of A$5,000 equivalent; amounts above that require manual handling by customer service.

### Service Points Near ANU

ANZ does not have a branch on the ANU campus, but it operates a full-service branch on London Circuit in the Civic area (a 10-minute walk from the ANU main gate), open Monday to Friday 9:00-17:00 and Saturday 9:00-12:00. There are also 2 ANZ ATMs in the Civic area, one of them near the Davey Lodge student residence. When paying cross-border tuition, some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payment](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E9%93%B6%E8%A1%8C%E5%8D%A1%E9%80%89%E6%8B%A9%E5%AF%B9%E6%AF%94%E8%81%94%E9%82%A6%E9%93%B6%E8%A1%8C-vs-anz-vs-nab) for settlement.

## NAB Account Features

NAB has the **lowest account-opening threshold for international students** among the big four banks. Its **NAB Classic Banking account** permanently waives the account-keeping fee for customers under 30, with no student status verification required (NAB, 2024, Personal Account Fee Schedule). International students only need a valid passport to open an account — no visa or CoE — which is a clear advantage for students who have not yet completed enrolment in the early days after arrival.

### Digital Banking and Paperless Process

NAB's **NAB Now digital account** operates entirely through the mobile app with no physical card, and supports instant payment via Apple Pay and Google Pay. Account opening is fully online, with an average review time of 15 minutes. The account has no minimum deposit requirement, and withdrawals from NAB ATMs are completely free. According to NAB's Q2 2024 customer data, 28% of NAB Now account users are international students aged 18-25.

### Campus Accessibility

NAB has no branch or ATM on the ANU campus. The nearest service point is the NAB branch at City Walk in Civic, about 1.2 km from the ANU campus, a 15-minute walk. That branch offers a Chinese-language service window, with a Mandarin-speaking customer manager on duty every Wednesday 10:00-14:00.

## Fees and Exchange Rates Compared

The three banks differ significantly in **international transfer fees** and **exchange rate markups**. Commonwealth Bank charges A$6 per international transfer, ANZ A$8, and NAB A$9 (all including intermediary bank charges; ANZ and NAB figures from each bank's August 2024 fee schedules). On exchange rates, Commonwealth Bank uses the real-time mid-rate plus a 0.5% margin, ANZ adds 0.8%, and NAB adds 1.0%. For a A$2,000 transfer, Commonwealth Bank has the lowest actual exchange cost.

### ATM Withdrawal Fees

When using another bank's ATM, Commonwealth Bank charges A$2.50 per withdrawal, ANZ A$3.00, and NAB A$2.00. All three banks charge an additional 5% fee for overseas ATM withdrawals (minimum A$5). Students are advised to prefer their own bank's ATMs or the cardless cash functions of digital banking.

## Documents and Process for Opening an Account

All three banks require international students to provide a **passport** and a **valid visa** (except NAB) as basic identification. Commonwealth Bank and ANZ additionally require a **CoE** or ANU student ID. Account opening can be completed through online pre-filling or in-branch. Commonwealth Bank and NAB support **100% online account opening**, while ANZ requires first-time account holders to verify their identity in person at a branch (ANZ, 2024, Account Opening Policy). From application submission to receiving the bank card, Commonwealth Bank takes 3-5 working days on average, ANZ 5-7 working days, and NAB's digital account is available immediately.

## Exclusive International Student Benefits

Commonwealth Bank offers newly opened international student accounts a **12-month fee-free credit card** (limit A$1,000-A$3,000) and a **student savings account** paying 4.50% p.a. (the current rate as of September 2024). ANZ's international student benefits include **no fees on the first 3 overseas transfers** (must be used within 30 days of opening the account) and the **ANZ Travel Card** with no foreign exchange conversion fees. NAB offers **5 free interbank ATM withdrawals per month** and a **NAB Rewards double points** promotion, where points can be redeemed for shopping gift cards or flights.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can international students open a bank account before arriving in Australia?

Yes. Both Commonwealth Bank and NAB support overseas pre-opening: students can submit an application through the mobile app before leaving home. Commonwealth Bank requires the account to be activated in Australia within 12 months of pre-opening, while NAB's digital account can be used without activation. ANZ currently does not support overseas pre-opening and requires you to visit a branch after arrival.

### Q2: Which bank has the most ATMs on the ANU campus?

Commonwealth Bank has the highest density, with 3 ATMs and 1 full-service branch on the ANU campus. ANZ has no ATM on campus; the nearest machines are in the Civic area. NAB has no service points on campus at all, with the nearest branch 1.2 km from the campus.

### Q3: Which of the three banks has the lowest international transfer fee?

Commonwealth Bank charges A$6 per international transfer, lower than ANZ's A$8 and NAB's A$9. In addition, Commonwealth Bank's exchange rate markup is only 0.5%, the lowest of the three. For a A$2,000 transfer, Commonwealth Bank's total exchange cost is about A$6 lower than ANZ's and about A$10 lower than NAB's.
