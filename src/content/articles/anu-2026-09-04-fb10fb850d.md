## Core Curriculum Structure

The Bachelor of Computer Science at the Australian National University (ANU) is designed to provide a strong foundation in computing. The curriculum includes compulsory core courses that cover essential topics such as programming, algorithms, data structures, and computer systems. These core courses are mandatory for all students, ensuring a consistent base of knowledge. Students also have the opportunity to select elective courses from various specializations, including artificial intelligence, data science, software engineering, and more. The structure allows for flexibility, enabling students to tailor their studies to their interests and career goals.

## Specializations in Computer Science

ANU's computer science program offers several specializations or majors that students can pursue. These include:

![ANU computer science students working on projects in a lab](https://img.ulec.com.cn/留学-单校wiki/anu-2026-09-04-fb10fb850d-image-02-2026-1679x1300.jpg)


- **Artificial Intelligence**: Focuses on machine learning, robotics, and intelligent systems.
- **Data Science**: Emphasizes statistical analysis, big data, and data-driven decision making.
- **Software Engineering**: Covers software design, development, and project management.
- **Cybersecurity**: Addresses security principles and practices in computing.
- **Human-Computer Interaction**: Studies the design of interactive systems and user experience.

These specializations allow students to deepen their knowledge in a specific area, making them more competitive in the job market.
