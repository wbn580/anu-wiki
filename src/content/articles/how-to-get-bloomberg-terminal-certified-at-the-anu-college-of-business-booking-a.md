The ANU College of Business and Economics (CBE) offers current students a free route to **Bloomberg Terminal certification (Bloomberg Market Concepts, BMC)**. According to Bloomberg's official statistics, more than 250,000 finance professionals worldwide hold the BMC certificate, and ANU — as one of Australia's Group of Eight universities — houses a Bloomberg Lab in its CBE building equipped with 12 dedicated terminals for students to book【Bloomberg, 2024, Bloomberg Market Concepts Overview】. In the 2025 QS World University Rankings by Subject, ANU's Accounting & Finance program ranked 34th globally, and this certification has a direct impact on graduates' competitiveness in fields such as investment banking and asset management【QS, 2025, QS World University Rankings by Subject】. This guide walks you through the full process, from booking a terminal to completing the BMC course, covering hardware booking, course structure, exam strategy, and certificate verification.

## Booking a Bloomberg Lab Terminal

The **ANU Bloomberg Lab** is located on Level 2 of the CBE building. Students need to reserve time slots in advance through the university's online booking system. Each terminal can be booked for up to 3 hours per session, with a weekly cap of 6 hours per user, to ensure fair allocation of resources.

### How the Booking System Works
Go to the "Room & Equipment Booking" page on the ANU Library website and select "Bloomberg Lab" as the location. The system displays available time slots for the next 14 days; log in with your student ID and password via the Uni ID system. Once the booking is confirmed, the system sends a confirmation email containing the terminal number and PIN. It is advisable to confirm lab opening hours 24 hours before your booking — during semester the lab is typically open Monday to Friday 09:00-17:00, and may be extended to 20:00 during exam weeks.

### Terminal Activation and Identity Verification
When you arrive at the lab, enter your Uni ID and booking PIN on the designated terminal. The screen will display "Welcome to Bloomberg Anywhere". First-time users need to create a Bloomberg profile, which is linked to ANU's institutional licence. If you encounter hardware issues, contact CBE IT Support (phone: +61 2 6125 4321); response times are typically within 15 minutes.

## BMC Course Structure and Content

The **BMC course** consists of 4 core modules, totalling about 8-12 hours of study, all completed on the terminal. The course content was updated by Bloomberg in 2020 and covers the fundamental tools and analytical frameworks of modern financial markets.

### Module 1: Economic Indicators
This module takes about 2 hours and teaches you to interpret macroeconomic data such as GDP, the unemployment rate, and CPI, and to pull real-time data using Bloomberg's ECO function (Economic Data Monitor). Students must complete 15 multiple-choice questions with a pass rate of at least 70% before moving on to the next module.

### Module 2: Currencies
This module focuses on the foreign exchange market, teaching you to use Bloomberg's ALLQ function (All Quotes) to view spot rates and forward points. It includes 3 case studies: EUR/USD carry trades, early-warning signs of emerging-market currency crises, and the impact of central bank intervention. The exam section requires identifying key technical indicators for 10 currency pairs.

### Module 3: Fixed Income
This module introduces bond pricing, yield curves, and the concept of duration. Students use Bloomberg's YAS function (Yield and Spread Analysis) to calculate the yield to maturity of US Treasuries and corporate bonds. Practical questions account for 50% of this module, requiring you to enter specific bond ISIN codes and generate yield curve charts.

### Module 4: Equities
This module covers equity valuation models (DCF and comparable company analysis) and Bloomberg's EQS function (Equity Screening) for screening stock pools. Case studies include financial ratio analysis of Apple (AAPL) and volatility forecasting for Tesla (TSLA). The final test contains 20 comprehensive questions spanning concepts from the first three modules.

## Exam Strategy and Time Management

The **BMC exam** has no strict time limit, but ANU lab booking slots cap how long you can work in a single session. It is recommended to split the course across 3-4 bookings, focusing on 1 module per session.

### A Module-by-Module Study Plan
First booking (3 hours): complete Module 1 and the first half of Module 2. Second booking (3 hours): finish the second half of Module 2 and Module 3. Third booking (3 hours): complete Module 4 and all revision. Fourth booking (2 hours): mock test and certificate download. After each module, the terminal automatically saves your progress so you can continue where you left off next time you log in.

### Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them
The most frequent mistake is confusing "yield to maturity" with "current yield" in the Fixed Income module. The terminal's HELP function (press F1) brings up the official definitions. In addition, some students skip the "Knowledge Checks" between modules — these ungraded practice exercises actually contain material for 60% of the final exam questions. Do the knowledge check immediately after each sub-topic.

## Certificate Verification and Download

After completing all 4 modules, the system automatically generates your **BMC certificate**. The certificate is stored in your Bloomberg profile as a PDF and is valid for 2 years from the date of issue.

### How to Get Your Certificate
Log in to the Bloomberg terminal and type `BMC<GO>` on the command line, then select the "Certificate" tab. Click "Download PDF" to obtain the file with Bloomberg's digital signature. ANU students can also email the certificate from their university account to careers@anu.edu.au so it can be recorded in the university's career development system.

### How Employers Verify It
Employers can verify authenticity through Bloomberg's "Verify BMC" online portal by entering the certificate number. According to Bloomberg 2023 data, 85% of the world's top investment banks prioritise candidates holding the BMC certificate when recruiting for analyst roles【Bloomberg, 2023, BMC Employer Survey】. The certificate number is located in the top right corner of the PDF, in the format BMC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.

## FAQ

### Q1: How long is the BMC certificate valid? Does it need to be renewed?
The BMC certificate is valid for 2 years from the date of issue. After it expires, students must retake all 4 module exams at a cost of USD 149 (paid through the Bloomberg website). Current ANU students can retake the exam once for free at a lab terminal within 3 months before expiry, but only for the first renewal.

### Q2: What happens if I book a terminal but don't show up on time?
Under ANU lab usage policy, arriving more than 15 minutes late automatically releases the time slot and counts as a "no-show". After 3 no-shows, the student's booking privileges are suspended for 30 days. Arrive at the lab 5 minutes early, and log in to the system to confirm within 10 minutes of the booking start time.

### Q3: Does the BMC certificate help with applying to ANU internship programs?
According to ANU CBE's 2024 internship data, students holding the BMC certificate received 42% more interview invitations than those without it when applying to the "Finance Internship Program". The certificate is listed as a "plus point" in resume screening, especially for internship roles at partner firms such as Morgan Stanley and UBS.
