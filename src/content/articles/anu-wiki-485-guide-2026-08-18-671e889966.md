## Understanding the 485 Second Post-Study Work Visa

The 485 second post-study work visa is available to eligible graduates who have studied and lived in a regional area. It extends the time you can stay and work in Australia after completing your studies.

## Duration of the Second Visa

The additional period granted depends on where you studied and lived. The exact duration is determined by the regional categorisation of your location.

## Requirements for the Second Visa

To be eligible, you must have held the first 485 visa and have lived and studied in a designated regional area. Specific conditions apply, including the location of your study and residence.

## Using the Second Visa for Work Experience

The second visa allows you to stay and work in regional Australia, giving you more time to gain professional experience in your field. This can strengthen your resume and may help with future visa pathways.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is the 485 second visa?

The duration varies based on the regional area where you studied and lived.

### What are the requirements?

You need to have held the first 485 visa and have studied and lived in a designated regional area.

### Can I use the second visa to gain work experience?

Yes, the visa permits you to work in Australia, so you can accumulate professional experience during the additional period.
