The ANU Sport Management programs (Bachelor of Sport & Exercise Science / Master of Sport Management) are practice-oriented courses offered by the ANU College of Health and Medicine. According to the Australian Sports Commission's (ASC, 2023) National Sport Participation Report, Australia's sports industry is worth about A$8.3 billion a year and is expected to create more than 140,000 jobs by 2025. Thanks to its location in the national capital, Canberra, ANU works directly with the Australian Institute of Sport (AIS) and several national sporting organisations, providing students with **university team internship opportunities**. The programs rank in the global top 150 in the 2024 QS rankings for sports-related subjects, and the university team internship system is a core part of the curriculum, with application requirements built around three hard criteria: academic performance, practical experience, and English language ability.

## How the University Team Internship System Works

University team internships in ANU's sport management programs are not a single role but a tiered practical system. According to the Student Internship Handbook published by ANU Sport (2024), internships fall into three categories: **Game Day Operations**, **Athlete Development Support**, and **Administration & Marketing**. Each category works with different university teams, such as the basketball, rugby, football, and athletics teams.

An internship usually lasts one semester (12 weeks), with a minimum of 10 hours and a maximum of 20 hours per week. Students must complete a reflective journal and a project report during the internship; the report is worth 30% of the course's final grade. ANU Sport has signed memoranda of understanding with more than 20 university representative teams (the Blue & Gold series) and offers roughly 40 to 60 internship places a year.

### Game Day Operations

This role involves hands-on **match-day management** for university home games. Duties include venue set-up, timing and scoring operation, crowd direction, and post-game clean-up. ANU Sport hosted 86 home fixtures in the 2023 season, and interns must attend at least 85% of them.

### Athlete Development Support

This stream focuses on **sports science support**. Under the guidance of accredited strength and conditioning coaches or exercise physiologists, interns design training plans for university athletes and record data. Applicants must have completed ANU's foundational sports science course (SPEX2001) with an average mark of at least 65%.

### Administration & Marketing

Students run university team social media accounts, liaise with sponsors, and promote **events**. In 2023, ANU Sport's intern-run Instagram accounts grew followers by 12.3% — a metric that feeds into internship assessment.

## Eligibility and Academic Requirements

All internship applicants must clear the common threshold set by the ANU College of Health and Medicine. According to the ANU Course Catalogue (2024 edition), **undergraduate applicants** must have completed at least 48 units in sport management-related courses with a weighted average mark (WAM) of 60 or above. **Postgraduate applicants** must have completed the first two semesters (24 units) of their master's program with a WAM of at least 65.

For English proficiency, applicants whose first language is not English must provide an IELTS score of at least 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0. ANU accepts TOEFL iBT as an alternative: a total score of 80 with at least 20 in writing. These language requirements match ANU's general admission standards and carry no extra weighting.

### Prerequisite Courses

Students applying for Game Day Operations must have completed SPEX2003 (Introduction to Event Management). The Athlete Development Support role requires scores of 70 or above in both SPEX2001 and SPEX2002. The Administration & Marketing role has no specific prerequisites, though MGMT2001 (Principles of Marketing) is recommended.

### Background Checks

All internships involving athlete contact (especially Athlete Development Support) require an **AFP National Police Check**, paid for by the student at about A$42 (2024 rate). Results must be submitted 4 weeks before the internship starts.

## Application Process and Key Dates

The application cycle for ANU university team internships follows the academic calendar strictly. Under the 2024 schedule published by ANU Sport, the **Semester 1 internship** application window runs from October 1 to November 15 of the previous year, and the **Semester 2 internship** window from April 1 to May 15 of the current year. Late applications are not accepted.

The process has three steps: submit the online application form, upload your transcript and English test results, and attend an interview. Interviews are conducted jointly by ANU Sport staff and the head coach of the relevant team, last about 20 minutes, and assess communication skills and understanding of the sports industry. 2023 data shows an interview pass rate of about 65%.

### Document Checklist

Applicants need: a copy of their ANU student ID, a recent transcript (official or exported from MyANU), IELTS or TOEFL results (if applicable), a CV of no more than two pages, and a 500-word statement of motivation explaining why they chose this internship stream and what their career goals are.

### Interview Scoring

Interviews are scored out of 100: **industry knowledge** 30 points, **communication** 25 points, **teamwork experience** 25 points, and **time management** 20 points. A total below 60 means rejection.

## Where the Internship Places Actually Are

Not every ANU university team offers the same number of internships. According to ANU Sport's 2023 annual report, **basketball** offers the most places (12), followed by **rugby** (8) and **football** (7). Athletics, tennis, and swimming together offer about 15 places, but competition for them is fiercer.

### Internship Time Allocation

Internship hours must not clash with the class timetable. ANU requires that internship activity does not take up the core teaching window of 9 am to 1 pm, Monday to Friday. As a result, most internships are scheduled after 2 pm or on weekend match days. A 2023 survey found that about 78% of interns commit 12 to 15 hours per week.

### Stipend and Credit

ANU university team internships are **unpaid** but carry 6 units of credit (equivalent to one standard course). Students cover their own transport and meal costs. ANU Sport provides free meals on match days, but not at other times.

## Internship Assessment and Grade Conversion

At the end of the internship, ANU Sport grades students on three metrics. **Supervisor assessment** (40%) comes from the internship supervisor (usually a team coach or ANU Sport staff member) based on work performance. **Report assessment** (40%) is awarded by the course coordinator based on the quality of the internship report. **Peer feedback** (20%) comes from anonymous peer reviews within the intern cohort.

Grades are pass/fail, but the transcript shows "Pass" or "Fail" for the internship. Students who fail must reapply the following semester and get only one retake opportunity. ANU 2023 data shows a 91% pass rate; failures were mainly due to insufficient attendance (below 80%) or plagiarism in the report.

### Report Submission Requirements

The internship report must be submitted to Turnitin within 14 days of the internship ending and may not exceed a 15% similarity rate. Reports must be 3,000 to 4,000 words and include an internship journal, case analysis, and reflective summary. Late submissions lose 5% per day.

### Extra Opportunities for Top Interns

Each semester, ANU Sport selects 2 to 3 **outstanding interns**, who receive a letter of recommendation from ANU Sport and priority eligibility for the following year's joint AIS internship program. Of the 2023 outstanding interns, 2 later received paid internship offers from the Australian Rugby League (ARL).

## How ANU Compares with Other Universities

ANU's university team internship system is mid-sized within the Group of Eight (Go8). According to UniSport Australia (2023) statistics, ANU offers about 50 sports internship placements a year — fewer than the University of Sydney (about 80) and the University of Melbourne (about 70), but up from ANU's own 35 in 2021.

ANU's edge is its proximity to the AIS. The AIS is in Canberra, just 3.5 km from ANU's main campus, and some internship programs let students complete work inside AIS facilities. Sydney and Melbourne may have more placements, but their sites are spread out and commuting costs are higher.

### Internship Quality Indicators

A 2022 internal report from the ANU College of Health and Medicine shows that students who completed a university team internship had an employment rate of 87% within 6 months of graduating, versus 72% for those who did not. Of those, 43% went into the sports industry; the rest entered education, government, and health.

### Notes for International Students

International students applying for internships must hold a valid student visa (Subclass 500), and internship hours must stay within the visa's 48-hours-per-fortnight work limit. ANU Sport provides international students with a visa compliance confirmation letter, but students bear the risk of any breach themselves.

## FAQ

### Q1: Are there extra restrictions on international students for ANU sport management internships?

International students must meet the same academic requirements as domestic students, but must also provide IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 6.0) or an equivalent TOEFL score. In addition, internship hours must be kept strictly within the 48-hours-per-fortnight limit on the student visa. In 2023, ANU Sport took on 18 international interns, or 36% of all interns.

### Q2: Do I need to contact the team coach before applying?

No. All internship applications go through ANU Sport's unified online system, and coaches are not involved in the initial screening. Coaches do take part in the interview stage, but applicants should not contact coaches proactively outside the application window — doing so can get the application flagged as irregular. The 2024 policy explicitly prohibits this.

### Q3: If I fail the internship, does it affect graduation?

Failing the internship does not directly prevent graduation, but you must reapply and complete it the following semester. Each internship course allows only one retake; if you fail again, you must negotiate an alternative with the course coordinator, such as completing an independent research project. 2023 data shows that 9% of interns failed their first attempt, and 80% of them passed on retake.
