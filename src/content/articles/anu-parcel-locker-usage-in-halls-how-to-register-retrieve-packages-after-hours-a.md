The Halls of Residence at the Australian National University (ANU) have used an electronic Parcel Locker system across the board since 2022, to cope with the more than 12,000 student parcels processed each year (ANU Accommodation Services, 2023 Annual Report). The system replaced the traditional front-desk sign-in model, allowing students to collect parcels self-service within 24 hours using a phone verification code, but around 18% of students still have parcels held up on first use because they have not completed registration or the size exceeds limits (ANU Student Living Survey, 2023). Following the official ANU Accommodation Services procedures, this article systematically explains the standard operations for parcel locker registration, after-hours collection and oversized parcel handling.

## Parcel Locker Registration and Account Linking

All students living in ANU Halls of Residence must activate their parcel locker account within 48 hours of moving in. The **registration entry point** is under the "Parcel Management" tab in the ANU Accommodation Portal, and you must log in with your University ID (single sign-on). The system asks for a personal contact phone number and a backup email, and requires agreement to the *Parcel Storage Terms* (clause 4.2 specifies a storage limit of 7 calendar days). After registration, the system generates a unique **Parcel ID QR code**, which students are advised to screenshot and save to their phone's photo album. According to ANU Information Technology Services (ITS, 2023) statistics, failing to save the QR code is the leading cause of failed collection, accounting for 34% of all fault reports.

### Phone Verification Codes and Notification Settings

After successful registration, the system sends collection notifications by default through both SMS and email. **Verification codes are valid for 24 hours**; after expiry, you need to request a new one at the locker terminal. Students can enable the "WeChat reminder" feature in the portal (only for China-mainland phone numbers); this feature is provided by ANU in partnership with Tencent Cloud (ANU ITS, 2023 Q3 Update), with delays kept under 2 minutes. If you fail to collect within 2 hours on 3 consecutive occasions, the system automatically switches off SMS notifications and uses email reminders only, to reduce carrier costs.

### Batch Collection of Multiple Parcels

When a student receives 2 or more parcels at the same time, the system generates a **batch collection code** that opens multiple locker doors at once. This feature must be enabled by ticking "Enable Batch Collection" during registration. ANU Accommodation Services (2023) data shows that students using batch collection codes cut their average collection time from 4.2 minutes to 1.8 minutes. If the feature was not enabled in advance, each code must be entered separately, with a 30-second cooldown between locker doors.

## After-Hours Collection

Parcel lockers are located in the 24-hour areas of each hall's foyer, but **outside business hours (22:00-07:00)** you must enter through the side-door access control system. Students must tap their Campus Card on the side-door reader, and the system checks the registration status of the parcel locker account. Students with unregistered accounts cannot enter during these hours and must wait until after 07:00 the next day for assistance from the on-duty front desk staff. ANU Security Office (2023 Incident Log) records show that late-night collection peaks between 23:00 and 00:30, accounting for 41% of all night-time collections.

### Night Collection Safety Protocol

All parcel locker areas use **dual-camera surveillance** outside business hours (one camera at the entrance, one on the locker bank), with footage retained for 30 days. If a locker door malfunctions during collection, students can press the "emergency help" button on the locker to be connected directly to the campus Security Centre (6125 2249). ANU Accommodation Services (2023) advises students to avoid wearing headphones when collecting at night and to stay aware of their surroundings. No theft occurred in parcel locker areas in 2023, but the Security Centre recorded 7 unnecessary callouts caused by accidental emergency button presses.

### Handling Overdue Parcels

If a parcel stays in a locker for more than 7 days, the system transfers it to the hall's **Central Mail Room** and charges an overdue management fee of 5 Australian dollars per parcel (ANU Accommodation Terms, 2024). Students must collect it during office hours (Monday to Friday 09:00-17:00) with their Campus Card and the overdue notification email. Parcels overdue by more than 14 days are returned to the sender, with the return costs borne by the student. In 2023, 283 parcels were returned for being overdue, accounting for 2.3% of the annual total.

## Receiving Oversized and Special Parcels

Parcels larger than 45 cm × 35 cm × 25 cm (such as bedding, monitors or musical instruments) cannot fit in a standard parcel locker. **Oversized parcels** are delivered by the courier directly to the hall front desk, where staff mark them as "Oversized" in the system and send a manual collection notification. Students must collect them at the front desk with their Campus Card within 48 hours of receiving the notification. ANU Accommodation Services (2023) data shows that oversized parcels make up 11% of the annual total, of which 73% are furniture or appliance items.

When paying cross-border tuition, some student families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-parcel-locker-usage-in-halls-how-to-register-retrieve-packages-after-hours-a) to complete remittances, avoiding parcel overstay problems caused by bank transfer delays.

### Cold-Chain and Fragile Items

Cold-chain parcels (such as refrigerated medication or fresh food) must be marked "Perishable" on the courier label, and front desk staff place them in the dedicated refrigerated cabinet within 30 minutes of receipt. **Fragile items** (such as glassware or electronics) must have a "Fragile" label on the consignment note; otherwise the parcel locker system treats them as ordinary items by default. ANU Accommodation Services (2023 Policy Document) states clearly that it accepts no liability for damage caused by missing labels. In 2023, 47 fragile-item damage complaints were received, of which 32 were due to missing labels.

### Authorised Collection

Students can set up **Authorised Collectors** through the accommodation portal, adding up to 2 people. An authorised collector must show their own Campus Card and the authorisation code (generated by the system, valid for 24 hours) at collection. This feature must be set up before the parcel arrives, and authorised collectors must be current ANU students. In 2023 there were 1,204 authorised collections, accounting for 9.8% of the annual total.

## Locker Faults and the Repair Process

The parcel locker hardware is supported by Australian provider Smiota, while ANU Information Technology Services (ITS) handles software maintenance. **Common faults** include locker doors failing to pop open (52% of repair requests), verification code mismatches (28%) and unresponsive touch screens (20%). Students can submit a repair request through the "Report Issue" function in the accommodation portal; the system automatically generates a ticket and assigns it to the ITS second-level support team. ANU ITS (2023 Service Desk Report) shows an average repair time of 4.2 hours, with 97% of faults resolved within 8 hours.

### Emergency Opening Procedure

If a locker cannot be opened due to a system fault and the parcel contains urgent items (such as medication), students can call the campus Security Centre (6125 2249), and security staff will arrive on site within 15 minutes to perform a **mechanical opening**. This requires signing the *Emergency Opening Disclaimer* (clause 3: mechanical opening may slightly compress the parcel). In 2023, 19 emergency openings were performed, with an average response time of 11 minutes.

### System Maintenance Notices

The parcel locker system undergoes **scheduled maintenance** once a month (usually 03:00-05:00 on the second Wednesday of the month), during which service is suspended. Maintenance notices are sent 72 hours in advance through the hall's email list. If a parcel arrives during maintenance, the courier temporarily stores it in the Central Mail Room, and students can collect it after 09:00 the next day with the tracking number.

## Parcel Tracking and Status Enquiry

Students can view parcel status in real time through the "Parcel Tracker" feature in the ANU accommodation portal. **Status labels** fall into four categories: "In Transit", "Arrived at Locker", "Collected" and "Returned". The system integrates with Australia Post and DHL APIs, with status updates delayed by no more than 15 minutes. ANU Accommodation Services (2023) advises using courier services with tracking numbers for international parcels, because status updates for untracked parcels can be delayed by 2-4 hours after they enter a locker.

### Exporting History

Students can export their **parcel receipt history** for the past 12 months (CSV format) from the portal, including courier company, locker entry time, collection time and locker number. This feature is often used in bond disputes when moving out — if a parcel is fined for being overdue, the history can serve as appeal evidence. In 2023, Accommodation Services processed 38 fine appeals based on history records, of which 27 were overturned because the system had marked the parcel overdue late.

### Third-Party Courier Anomalies

If a courier company shows "delivered" but the student has not received a collection notification, first check the **notification settings** on the parcel locker account (see the registration process above). If the settings are correct, students can submit a photo of the consignment note to the hall front desk, and staff will retrieve the parcel locker surveillance footage to verify within 1 business day. ANU Accommodation Services (2023) records show that 62% of such anomalies are caused by couriers mistakenly placing parcels in another student's locker door, and the courier company must provide compensation.

## Moving Out and Account Closure

When moving out, students must **close their parcel locker account** in the accommodation portal (at the bottom of "Account Settings"). After the account is closed, all uncollected parcels are marked "Abandoned" and returned to the sender within 7 business days. If the account is not closed, the system keeps sending collection notifications to the student's email and overdue management fees continue to accumulate. ANU Accommodation Services (2023) reminds students that the account can be reactivated within 30 days of moving out (with a 10 Australian dollar activation fee).

### Bonds and Parcel Links

During the move-out inspection, the hall checks the parcel locker history to confirm there are no overdue uncollected parcels. **If overdue parcels remain unhandled**, the overdue management fees (up to 50 Australian dollars) are deducted from the bond. In 2023, 142 students had bond deductions for not closing their accounts, with an average deduction of 18.5 Australian dollars. Students are advised to enable the "parcel hold" feature 7 days before moving out to stop new parcels entering lockers.

### Inter-Hall Parcel Transfers

If a student changes halls mid-semester, their parcel locker account must be **manually migrated** to the new hall. The migration requires completing a "Parcel Transfer" form at the old hall's front desk, and the new hall's system syncs the data within 2 business days. During the migration period (usually 48 hours), parcels are temporarily held in the Central Mail Room, and students must collect them with the migration confirmation email. In 2023, 89 inter-hall migrations were completed, with an average processing time of 1.8 business days.

## FAQ

### Q1: If my parcel is returned, how do I get it back?

If a parcel is returned for being overdue, students must contact the courier company (such as Australia Post or DHL) with the tracking number. ANU Accommodation Services is not responsible for recovery after return. Under Australia Post (2023) rules, the re-delivery fee for returned parcels is paid by the recipient — domestic parcels usually start at 8.95 Australian dollars, while international parcels are charged by actual weight. Collect parcels within 5 days of arrival to avoid returns.

### Q2: Can fresh food be stored in parcel lockers?

Yes, but only cold-chain parcels marked "Perishable". Ordinary fresh food (such as fruit or takeaway) cannot go in parcel lockers, because the internal temperature is controlled at 18-25°C and is not suitable for long-term storage. ANU Accommodation Services (2023) advises collecting fresh food within 2 hours of delivery. In 2023, 23 cleaning complaints were caused by fresh food spoiling in lockers, and the students concerned were charged a 20 Australian dollar cleaning fee.

### Q3: Can a friend collect my parcel for me?

Yes, but you must set up an authorised collector in the accommodation portal in advance — up to 2 people. The authorisation code is valid for 24 hours, and the authorised collector must be a current ANU student. In 2023, authorised collections accounted for 9.8% of the annual total. Without this setup, a friend cannot collect on your behalf with just their Campus Card — the system will refuse to open the locker.
