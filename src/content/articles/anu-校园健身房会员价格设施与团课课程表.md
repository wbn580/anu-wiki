The on-campus gym system at the Australian National University (ANU) is jointly operated by ANU Sport and ANU Wellbeing and Safety, covering two main venues — the **Acton campus** and the **Daley Road Sports Centre** — and offering students and staff membership prices 30-40% below the market average, plus more than 50 group classes per week. According to the Australian Sports Commission (ASC) 2023 *University Sports Facilities Report*, the ANU gym is the largest on-campus sports complex in the Australian Capital Territory (ACT), with more than 120,000 visits per year. Based on ANU Sport's official 2024 fee schedule and class timetable, this article systematically sets out the membership pricing structure, the facilities list, the group class timetable and usage notes, providing a structured reference for students planning to use on-campus fitness resources.

## Membership Pricing Structure and Payment Methods

ANU gym memberships come in **semester** and **annual** plans, with prices varying by membership category (student/staff/alumni) and usage period (peak/off-peak). The standard student annual fee for 2024 is **AUD $399** (about RMB 1,870), roughly AUD $1.09 per day, which is lower than the Canberra city average of AUD $65-85/month at commercial gyms (about AUD $780-1,020/year)【ANU Sport 2024, Membership Fee Schedule】.

### Student Standard Membership

Full-time undergraduate and postgraduate students can apply for the **Student Standard** membership at $399/year (or $199/semester). It includes unlimited access to the Acton campus gym, the Daley Road Sports Centre, indoor basketball courts and squash courts, but does not cover the pool (a separate Aquatic Access add-on card is required at $50/year). Off-peak membership (Monday to Friday 06:00-16:00 and all day on weekends) costs $299/year.

### Staff and Alumni Memberships

ANU staff (including contract researchers) pay $549/year, and alumni (within 5 years of graduation) pay $449/year. Both types enjoy the same facility access as standard members, but alumni must verify with their ANU Alumni Card number. According to ANU Sport's internal 2023 data, about 62% of members choose the annual plan, as it saves about 18% in total cost compared with buying by semester.

### Payment Channels and Add-On Services

Membership fees can be paid by direct debit from the ANU student account (ISIS), credit card or bank transfer. For international students needing cross-border payments, some families use professional channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E6%A0%A1%E5%9B%AD%E5%81%A5%E8%BA%AB%E6%88%BF%E4%BC%9A%E5%91%98%E4%BB%B7%E6%A0%BC%E8%AE%BE%E6%96%BD%E4%B8%8E%E5%9B%A2%E8%AF%BE%E8%AF%BE%E7%A8%8B%E8%A1%A8) to complete the remittance, avoiding exchange-rate fluctuations and high handling fees. In addition, members can purchase **Personal Training** packages ($60-120/hour) or a **Group Fitness Pass** ($15 per session, $25 for non-members).

## Core Facilities and Layout

The ANU gym system comprises three main sports venues with a total usable floor area of about 8,500 square metres, equipped with more than 200 pieces of cardio and strength equipment. According to Fitness Australia's 2022 *University Facility Standards*, ANU's equipment density (units per square metre) reaches 0.024, higher than the national university average of 0.017.

### Acton Campus Gym

Located in **Building 12** (University Avenue entrance), it is ANU's busiest venue, with an average daily footfall of about 450. Facilities include:
- **Strength training zone**: 4 Life Fitness free-weight racks, 12 Hammer Strength machines, 2 squat racks (with safety hooks)
- **Cardio zone**: 20 treadmills (including 5 NordicTrack models with incline adjustment), 15 elliptical trainers, 10 recumbent bikes
- **Free-weights zone**: dumbbell sets (2.5-50 kg), 6 Olympic bars, kettlebell sets (8-32 kg)

### Daley Road Sports Centre

Located **next to the Daley Road car park**, it focuses on group classes and ball sports. Facilities include:
- **Multi-purpose sports hall**: 3 standard basketball courts (convertible to 2 volleyball courts or 6 badminton courts)
- **Squash courts**: 4 international-standard squash courts (booking required 24 hours in advance on the ANU Sport website)
- **Yoga/Pilates studio**: equipped with 40 Manduka yoga mats and 20 foam rollers

### Swimming Pool and Sauna

The ANU swimming pool is located at the **Acton campus pool** (outdoor, 25 m x 6 lanes), with water temperature maintained at 26-28°C all year round. Access is limited to Aquatic Access add-on card holders. The sauna is a dry-heat type set at 70-80°C, limited to 15 minutes per session; you must collect a timing token from the front desk first.

## Group Class Timetable and Schedule

ANU offers **55-60 group fitness classes** per week, falling into four categories: high-intensity interval (HIIT), yoga/Pilates, dance and combat. The timetable is updated once per semester and published on the ANU Sport website and the ANU App. According to the 2024 Semester 1 (S1) schedule, average occupancy during peak hours (Monday to Thursday 17:00-19:00) reaches 78%, so arriving 15 minutes early is recommended.

### HIIT and Strength Classes

- **HIIT Circuit**: 8 classes per week, 45 minutes each, featuring a 10-station circuit (battle ropes, box jumps, medicine balls, etc.). Suitable for intermediate-level and above.
- **BodyPump**: a Les Mills licensed class, 6 per week, using barbells and steps, focused on muscular endurance. Bring your own towel and collars.
- **Boxing Fit**: 4 classes per week, including 30 minutes of boxing combination drills and 15 minutes of core work. 12-ounce boxing gloves are provided (sanitise before use).

### Yoga and Flexibility Classes

- **Vinyasa Flow**: 10 classes per week, 60 minutes each, suitable for all levels. The studio provides yoga blocks and straps.
- **Yin Yoga**: 4 classes per week, 75 minutes each, focused on deep connective-tissue stretching; rehydrate after class.
- **Pilates Mat**: 6 classes per week, using resistance bands and Swiss balls, with core activation making up about 40% of the session.

### Dance and Specialty Classes

- **Zumba**: 5 classes per week, Latin-dance style, burning on average 350-500 kcal per class (based on a 70 kg body weight).
- **Dance Cardio**: 3 classes per week, mixing hip-hop and jazz elements, suitable for absolute beginners.
- **Aqua Aerobics**: 2 classes per week (summer semester only), held in the shallow end of the pool, with low joint impact.

## Opening Hours and Holiday Arrangements

ANU gyms are open **357 days** a year, closing only on Christmas Day (25 December), New Year's Day (1 January) and Good Friday (the Friday before Easter). Regular opening hours are as follows:
- **Monday to Friday**: 06:00 - 22:00
- **Saturday to Sunday**: 08:00 - 20:00
- **Semester breaks (June-July and December-February)**: Monday to Friday 07:00 - 20:00, weekends 09:00 - 18:00

According to the ANU Wellbeing 2023 annual report, membership usage drops about 40% during holidays, but facility maintenance (such as equipment calibration and floor cleaning) is concentrated in this period; members are advised to check the "Maintenance Notice" section of the ANU Sport website for temporary closures.

## Booking System and Usage Rules

All members and visitors must book time slots or classes through the **ANU Sport online booking system** (requires ANU single sign-on login). Non-member visitors can buy a single-entry pass at the front desk for $18/session ($12 for students), limited to 50 passes per day.

### Booking Process and Cancellation Policy

- Class booking: select a class on the "Group Fitness" page of the ANU Sport website and click "Book Now"; the system automatically sends a confirmation email.
- Cancellation rule: cancellations must be made at least **2 hours** before the class starts, otherwise it counts as a "No-Show" record. Three No-Shows accumulated in a semester will freeze booking privileges for 7 days.
- Court booking: squash courts and basketball courts can be booked via the "Court Booking" module, up to 2 hours per booking, open 7 days in advance.

### Safety and Code of Conduct

- Closed-toe sports shoes are required in the strength area; bare feet and flip-flops are prohibited.
- Wipe down equipment after use (disinfectant spray and paper towels are provided); a first offence draws a warning, and a second offence suspends membership for 72 hours.
- Bring your own padlock for lockers; lockers are cleared at closing time each day, and items left overnight are moved to Lost & Found (kept for 14 days).

## Special Programmes and Health Services

ANU gyms partner with **ANU Wellbeing and Safety** to provide integrated services for students' mental and physical health. New in 2024 is the **Fitness Referral Program**, under which on-campus GPs can write exercise prescriptions, and members receive 6 free Personal Training sessions (45 minutes each).

### Sports Injury Prevention Clinic

Every Tuesday and Thursday 14:00-17:00, physiotherapists registered with the ACT Physiotherapy Association are on site providing free basic assessments (15 minutes each). Book in advance on the ANU Health Centre website; there are 20 spots per week.

### Student Sports Team Support

ANU fields 32 varsity teams (such as ANU Blue basketball and ANU Owls rugby). The gym gives team members priority time slots (exclusive use of the strength area Monday to Friday 06:00-08:00). In 2023, varsity athletes' average fitness test results (e.g. 1RM squat, Beep Test scores) were 22% higher than those of non-varsity members【ANU Sport 2023, Annual Performance Report】.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can ANU gym members bring friends in?

Yes, but they need to buy a visitor pass. Members can bring **1 visitor** per visit; the visitor must register at the front desk and pay $12 (students) or $18 (non-students). The same visitor is limited to 4 entries per month. The member must accompany the visitor at all times, otherwise the visitor will be asked to leave.

### Q2: Can I get a refund if I move away or take leave of absence mid-semester?

You can apply for a pro-rata refund, but a written application must be submitted before week 4 of the semester. Under ANU Sport's 2024 policy, a $50 administration fee is deducted and the remaining amount is refunded to the original payment account. Students on leave of absence must provide proof of leave issued by ANU Student Central; processing takes 10-15 working days.

### Q3: Do I need to bring my own yoga mat for group classes?

No. All yoga/Pilates studios at ANU gyms provide Manduka yoga mats (sanitised after every class), with 40 mats available on a first-come, first-served basis. However, bringing your own towel and drinking water is recommended, as no free towel rental is offered during classes (disposable towels can be purchased at $2 each).
