Since 1 July 2023, the Australian Department of Home Affairs has extended the maximum stay of the **Temporary Graduate visa (Subclass 485)** to 4 years for eligible international graduates, and Australian National University (ANU) graduates who hold designated degrees can benefit from this change [Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2023, Migration Amendment Regulations 2023]. According to Australian government education data, the total number of international students in Australia exceeded 700,000 in 2023, and around 60% of graduates applied for a Temporary Graduate visa after completing their studies [Australian Department of Education, 2024, International Student Data]. For ANU graduates, applying for this visa is a key step between study and working in Australia. The core requirements include completing a registered course of at least 92 weeks, meeting the English language standard (IELTS overall 6.0 with no band below 5.0) and obtaining a police clearance certificate from the Australian Federal Police. Based on the official guidelines updated by the Department of Home Affairs in July 2024, this article outlines the eligibility conditions, required documents and current processing times for ANU graduates applying for the 485 visa.

## Visa categories and who they apply to

The **Temporary Graduate visa (Subclass 485)** has two main streams: the **Graduate Work Stream** and the **Post-Study Work Stream**. For ANU graduates, the vast majority fall under the Post-Study Work Stream, which requires applicants to hold a qualifying degree (such as a bachelor's, master's or doctoral degree) completed through at least 2 years (92 weeks) of full-time study in Australia. The policy changes after 1 July 2023 further refined the stay periods: 2 years for a bachelor's degree (including honours), 3 years for a master's degree (coursework), 4 years for a master's degree (research) and 4 years for a doctoral degree [Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)].

### Post-Study Work Stream

This stream does not require a skills assessment and applies to graduates of any ANU CRICOS-registered course (such as the Bachelor of Commerce or Master of Engineering). Applicants must lodge the application while in Australia, within 6 months of completing their studies (dated from the school-issued Completion Letter). In April 2024, the Department of Home Affairs further clarified that **online study time** (due to COVID-19 travel restrictions) can count towards the Australian study requirement, but by no more than 40% of the total study duration.

### Graduate Work Stream

This stream applies to graduates of ANU vocational education and training (VET) courses or specific undergraduate/postgraduate diplomas. It requires the field of study to be on the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL) and a skills assessment from the relevant assessing authority. In 2024, the visa duration for this stream was 18 months, and applicants must be under 35 (with some PhD graduates eligible up to 50).

## Core eligibility requirements

The **study requirement** is the foundation of the application. Applicants must complete a CRICOS-registered course at ANU with a total duration of at least 92 weeks (about 2 years), and must have spent at least 16 months actually studying in Australia. For English language, IELTS must be at least 6.0 overall with no band below 5.0 (or the equivalent PTE Academic score of 50 overall with 36 in each skill); some courses such as medicine may require higher scores. The **health and character requirements** stipulate that applicants must hold adequate health insurance (OVHC) and pass a medical examination. The police clearance must be issued by the Australian Federal Police (AFP) and is valid for 12 months.

### Age and visa history

Applicants must be under 50 at the time of application (Post-Study Work Stream) or under 35 (Graduate Work Stream, with exemptions for PhD graduates). Having never held a 485 visa (as a primary applicant) is a strict requirement. If you held a student visa (Subclass 500) while studying at ANU, its conditions (such as work hour limits) automatically cease to apply once you hold a 485.

### English test validity

English test results (such as IELTS or PTE Academic) must have been obtained within 3 years before the application is lodged. ANU graduates who completed at least 5 years of full-time study in an English-speaking country (such as Australia, the UK, the US or Canada) are exempt from the English test requirement. In January 2024, the Department of Home Affairs confirmed it accepts TOEFL iBT scores (79 overall with 21 in writing), though note that some test centres suspended this exam due to technical issues.

## Required documents

**Core documents** include: your passport (all pages showing visa labels and entry/exit stamps), the ANU Completion Letter and official Academic Transcript. The Completion Letter must clearly state the course CRICOS code, study start and end dates and the degree conferral date. **English proficiency evidence** requires original test reports from IELTS, PTE Academic or TOEFL iBT. Health insurance evidence (OVHC policy) must cover 12 months from visa grant (Post-Study Work Stream) or 18 months (Graduate Work Stream).

### Supporting documents

- **Police clearance**: a National Police Check from the Australian Federal Police (AFP), applied for online via the AFP website, costing AUD 42 (2024 rate).
- **Birth certificate**: must be translated into English and certified by a notary. Chinese applicants can obtain a bilingual (Chinese-English) notarised certificate through the notary office at their place of household registration.
- **Passport photos**: recent (within 6 months) passport-size photos (45mm x 35mm) on a white background; digital copies must meet Department specifications.

### Special document requirements

If a spouse or children are included in the application, relationship evidence (such as a marriage certificate or birth certificates) and English proficiency evidence are required (a spouse of the primary applicant needs IELTS 4.5 overall or equivalent). ## Application process and timeline

**Online applications** are lodged through the Department of Home Affairs' ImmiAccount system. Applicants create an account, complete Form 485 and upload all documents. The application fee is AUD 4,600 (July 2024 rate), plus a biometrics collection fee (about AUD 85). **Biometrics collection** must be completed within 14 days of lodging the application; applicants in Australia can book an appointment at an Australian Visa Application Centre (AVAC) or selected Australia Post outlets.

### Processing times

According to Department of Home Affairs data from July 2024, 50% of Post-Study Work Stream applications are processed within 44 days and 90% within 6 months. The Graduate Work Stream takes a little longer: 50% within 48 days and 90% within 7 months. **Priority processing** is not available for the 485 visa, so applicants need to be patient. Check ImmiAccount regularly for status updates and keep your contact details (email, phone) current.

### When to apply

The best time to apply is 1–2 months after completing your studies, to avoid delays from incomplete documents or peak-season backlogs. January to March 2024 was a peak application period (matching Australia's summer graduation season), with processing times potentially stretching beyond 90 days. The ANUCareers careers service recommends that graduates start their application as soon as they receive the Completion Letter to maximise the visa validity period.

## Rights and obligations after the visa is granted

**Work rights**: 485 visa holders can work full-time in Australia (no weekly hour limit) with no restriction on any particular employer. During the visa's validity, holders can freely change employers, occupations or industries. **Study rights**: holders can take short courses (such as vocational training or language courses) but must not enrol as full-time students (for example, to pursue a second degree). To continue studying, you need to apply for a student visa (Subclass 500) before the 485 expires.

### Visa conditions

The 485 visa carries condition 8107: holders must not engage in activities inconsistent with the visa category (such as unlawful work). If the visa is cancelled, it will affect future visa applications. **Health insurance**: you must maintain OVHC continuously until the visa expires. In July 2024, the Department of Home Affairs announced stronger compliance checks on health insurance — being uninsured can lead to visa cancellation.

### Pathways to permanent residence

The 485 visa can serve as a bridge to skilled migration (such as Subclass 189/190) or employer-sponsored migration (Subclass 482/186). ANU graduates living and working in the ACT can gain extra points through ACT nomination (Subclass 190). In June 2024, the ACT government increased the weighting for ANU graduates employed locally from 10 to 15 points [ACT Government, 2024, ACT Critical Skills List].

## Common questions and things to watch out for

**Visa renewal**: the 485 can only be applied for once as a primary applicant. If you complete a higher degree within the visa's validity (for example, upgrading from a master's to a PhD), you need to apply for a new student visa before the 485 expires. **COVID-19 replacement policy**: ANU graduates affected by travel restrictions (studying overseas between 1 February 2020 and 30 June 2023) can apply for a replacement visa (Replacement Stream), with an application deadline of 1 January 2027 [Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2024, COVID-19 Replacement Stream].

### Common refusal reasons

- **Failure to meet the Australian study requirement**: total study duration under 92 weeks or fewer than 16 months of actual study in Australia.
- **Expired language test**: the English test result was older than 3 years at lodgement.
- **Incomplete documents**: missing police clearance or health insurance evidence.
- **Late application**: lodged more than 6 months after completing studies.

### 2024 policy developments

In February 2024, the Australian Government announced it would raise the 485 English language requirement from IELTS 6.0 to 6.5 overall (no band below 5.5), with the change expected to take effect in early 2025. ANU graduates are advised to lodge their applications before the change takes effect. In addition, the Department of Home Affairs is reviewing the "designated degree" list and may add more ANU courses (such as the Master of Data Science) to the extended stay categories.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can I work in Australia while my 485 application is being processed?

Yes. After lodging a 485 application, applicants receive a Bridging Visa A, which permits full-time work. Data from July 2024 shows that bridging visas are usually granted 1–2 weeks after the application is lodged. Bridging Visa A remains valid until the 485 is granted or refused.

### Q2: If my ANU master's degree took 1.5 years, do I meet the 2-year study requirement?

No. The Australian study requirement stipulates a minimum course duration of 92 weeks (about 2 years). If your course lasted 1.5 years (such as ANU's Master of Financial Management), you can top up to 2 years by completing an additional relevant Graduate Diploma. In 2024, ANU offers several 0.5-year Graduate Diploma programs (such as the Graduate Diploma of Economics) that can follow on from your master's.

### Q3: How long can I stay in Australia after my 485 expires?

After the visa expires, holders usually have a 28-day grace period to apply for another visa (such as a Visitor visa Subclass 600 or Student visa Subclass 500) or to leave the country. In 2024, the Department of Home Affairs penalty for overstaying is a 3-year re-entry ban. It is recommended to lodge a new visa application at least 1 month before your current visa expires.
