The Australian National University (ANU) enforces strict formatting rules for academic writing in undergraduate and postgraduate courses, covering citation styles, margins, fonts, line spacing, and heading hierarchy. According to the Student Writing Guide published by the ANU Academic Skills Centre (2024), assignments that fail to meet formatting requirements may be returned for revision or penalised directly, affecting up to 10% of the final grade. At the same time, Times Higher Education (THE, 2024) data places ANU 67th in global university rankings, and its rigorous academic standards are a key factor in maintaining that reputation. This article systematically sets out ANU's current essay formatting rules, focusing on the three main citation styles — Harvard, APA 7th, and AGLC4 — and introduces the officially recommended reference management tools, to help students meet academic standards on their first submission.

## General Formatting Requirements

ANU sets uniform minimum standards for **margins**, fonts, and line spacing. Under the ANU Student Assignment Submission Guidelines (2024 edition), all text-based assignments must use 2.54 cm (1 inch) margins on all sides. For fonts, Times New Roman or Arial at 12 points is recommended. Line spacing must be set to **double-spacing**, with no extra blank lines between paragraphs; paragraphs are distinguished by a first-line indent of 1.27 cm (0.5 inches). Heading levels must be marked using Word's built-in "heading styles": level 1 headings bold and centred, level 2 headings bold and left-aligned, level 3 headings indented, bold, and italic. These requirements apply to all course essays unless the lecturer specifies otherwise in the assignment brief.

### Running Heads and Page Numbers

Each page must carry a **running head** in the top right corner in the format "student initials + space + last four digits of student ID", for example "JD_4567". Page numbers start from the first page of the body and are placed centred at the bottom of the page. The title page shows no page number but counts toward the total page count. According to ANU Academic Registrar's Office (2023) statistics, about 12% of mark appeals relate directly to page number formatting errors.

### Title Page Rules

The title page must include the essay title, course code and name, lecturer's name, submission date, and the student's name and ID. The title should use **title case**, meaning the first letter of every content word is capitalised while prepositions and conjunctions (such as of, and, the) are lowercase unless at the start. The title page carries no running head but must state "Submitted in partial fulfilment of the requirements for [course code]" centred at the bottom.

## Choosing a Citation Style

ANU requires students to clearly acknowledge all cited sources and prescribes **preferred citation styles** for different disciplines. Social sciences, business, and education generally use APA 7th (American Psychological Association 7th edition); history, philosophy, and some humanities disciplines use Harvard style; and law programs must use AGLC4 (Australian Guide to Legal Citation 4th edition). ANU Library (2024) provides detailed comparison tables for each style on its citation guide pages and stresses that "mixing styles" is an academic irregularity that can trigger an academic integrity investigation.

### APA 7th Key Points

The core principle of APA 7th is the author-date system. In-text citations take the form (Author surname, year), for example (Smith, 2020). Direct quotations require page numbers, such as (Smith, 2020, p. 45). The reference list is ordered alphabetically by author surname with a 1.27 cm hanging indent. **DOIs (Digital Object Identifiers)** are mandatory in APA 7th: all references with a DOI must include it in the format "https://doi.org/xxxx". According to a sample survey by the ANU Academic Skills Centre (2024), about 34% of students omitted the DOI on first submission, losing citation marks.

### How Harvard Style Differs

Harvard style is similar to APA 7th but with key differences: Harvard uses "and" rather than "&" in in-text citations, e.g. "Smith and Jones (2020) argued..."; author names in the reference list are given in full rather than abbreviated; and the publication year is placed after the author names rather than in parentheses. In addition, Harvard's reference list is titled "Reference List" rather than "References". ANU's official template (2024 edition) provides examples of both styles for students to download and use directly.

These tools automatically generate in-text citations and reference lists and support one-click switching between multiple citation styles. EndNote 21 integrates directly with Word, allowing citations to be inserted in real time while writing. ## Special Formatting Scenarios

Some courses impose additional formatting requirements, such as **lab reports**, **legal memoranda**, and **theses**. Lab reports need separate "Methods" and "Results" sections, with figures and tables numbered sequentially and captioned. Legal memoranda must use the AGLC4 footnote citation system rather than in-text citations. Theses (Honours/Masters by coursework) are usually submitted through ANU's electronic thesis submission system (eThesis), with stricter formatting requirements including PDF/A format, embedded fonts, and metadata tags.

### Figures, Tables, and Appendices

Figure captions go below the figure in bold, for example "Figure 1: GDP Growth Rates (2010–2020)". Table titles go above the table. Appendices must be first mentioned in the body and numbered alphabetically (Appendix A, Appendix B). Each appendix must contain at least one self-contained content unit and must not be a blank page. Under ANU Graduate Research School (2024) rules, appendices do not count toward the word limit, but more than 5 appendices require prior approval from the supervisor.

### Electronic Submission Formats

All essays must be submitted through ANU's Turnitin system, which automatically checks **text similarity**. Turnitin accepts .docx, .pdf, and .rtf files; Apple Pages and Google Docs cannot be uploaded directly. Before submitting, ensure the file is no larger than 20 MB and that fonts are embedded. The ANU Academic Skills Centre (2024) advises students to use the "print preview" function to check margins and page breaks before submission.

## Common Errors and a Self-Check Checklist

Based on the ANU Academic Skills Centre's (2024) analysis of 1,500 assignments over the past three years, the most common formatting errors are: margins not set to 2.54 cm (28%), mixed citation styles (22%), and reference lists not ordered alphabetically (15%). Students are advised to use the following **self-check checklist**: 1) Are margins uniformly 1 inch? 2) Is the font 12-point Times New Roman or Arial? 3) Is line spacing double? 4) Do in-text citations fully correspond to the reference list? 5) Are DOIs included? 6) Does the title page contain all required information? 7) Do page numbers start from the body?

### Penalty Standards

Penalty policies for formatting errors vary by college. The College of Business and Economics (CBE) Assignment Submission Policy (2024) states formatting issues can cost up to 5% of the assignment mark; the College of Law takes a "zero tolerance" approach to citation errors, deducting 0.5 points per error up to a maximum of 10 points. The School of Art (SoA) allows students to request one formatting review before submission, with a review turnaround of 3 working days.

### Tool Assistance

Beyond reference managers, ANU provides free access to **Grammarly Premium** (renewed 2024). The tool detects grammar errors, spelling issues, and passive voice frequency, but its citation checking is limited to APA, MLA, and Chicago styles — it does not support AGLC4. Students can activate their account through the "online tools" page on the ANU Library website; activation is valid for 12 months.

## FAQ

### Q1: Must ANU essay margins be 2.54 cm? What tolerance is allowed?

Yes, margins for all text-based assignments must be set to 2.54 cm (1 inch). ANU Academic Registrar's Office (2023) allows a tolerance of ±0.1 cm. Anything outside this range (for example 2.0 cm or 3.0 cm) is treated as non-compliant and may attract penalties. Word's default "Normal" margins are 2.54 cm, so using that preset is recommended.

### Q2: Do I still need to manually check a reference list auto-generated by EndNote?

Yes. EndNote's generated lists are based on metadata imported by the user, and that metadata can contain errors. ANU Library (2024) testing shows that about 7% of references imported directly from Google Scholar contain author name spelling errors or wrong publication years. It is therefore advisable to check every entry against the original source before submission.

### Q3: Must law essays use AGLC4? Can APA 7th be used instead?

Law courses (such as the LAWS series) mandate AGLC4; APA 7th cannot be substituted. AGLC4 uses a footnote citation system that is completely different from APA's in-text citations. The ANU College of Law (2024) states clearly that using a non-designated style results in the assignment being returned for rewriting, with no extra time granted. Students can download the full AGLC4 PDF (120 pages) from the ANU Library's AGLC4 guide page, or use Zotero's AGLC4 plugin to generate footnotes automatically.
