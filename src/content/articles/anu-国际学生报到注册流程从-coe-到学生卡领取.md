ANU International Student Enrolment: From CoE to Collecting Your Student Card

In the 2024-2025 academic year, about 78% of the international students admitted to the Australian National University (ANU) arrived under the Simplified Student Visa Framework (SSVF) of the Department of Home Affairs, and the average enrolment cycle each year runs 8 to 12 weeks [Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Student Visa and Simplification Program Report]. The process — from obtaining a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) to collecting your physical student card — passes through several official milestones, including the visa application, online enrolment, health insurance purchase and on-campus identity verification. With the Australian immigration authorities tightening the scrutiny of visa document authenticity in 2025, completing enrolment on time is critical to staying compliant with your student visa [Department of Education, 2024, International Student Data and Compliance Update]. Based on ANU's official policies and Australian government public data, this article walks through the full path international students take from CoE to student card.

## Getting Your CoE and Applying for the Visa

The **CoE** is the core document you need before applying for an Australian student visa. After ANU confirms your acceptance of the offer and receipt of the first tuition deposit (usually 50% of the first semester's tuition or AUD 15,000), it generates the CoE electronically within 5 business days. The CoE shows your name, course code (CRICOS code), course start and end dates and total tuition, and is a mandatory field in the visa application system (ImmiAccount). In 2024, about 92% of ANU international students lodged their visa application within 30 days of receiving the CoE [Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Student Visa Lodgement Statistics].

### Visa Documents and Health Checks

A visa application requires the CoE, proof of Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC), evidence of English ability (such as an IELTS score of 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0) and proof of funds (covering the first year's tuition, living costs of AUD 21,041 and return airfare) [Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Student Visa Document Checklist Tool]. The medical examination must be completed at an eMedical centre designated by the Australian immigration authorities; the average wait for an appointment is 7 to 14 days. In 2024, the average processing time for ANU international student visas was 42 days, with about 65% of applications granted within 8 weeks.

### CoE Deferrals and Changes

If you need to adjust your CoE because of a deferred start or a course change, you must submit a written application to ANU's international admissions office at least 4 weeks before the course starts. A CoE deferral is only allowed for the same course; changing courses requires a new CoE and a visa update. In 2024, ANU handled about 1,200 CoE change applications, with an average processing time of 10 business days.

## Online Enrolment and Course Selection

**Online enrolment** is a key step in the ANU international student onboarding process and must be completed after the visa is granted and before the course begins. ANU's ISIS (Integrated Student Information System) opens the enrolment window 4 weeks before each semester; you log in to confirm your personal details, select your courses (usually 4 courses per semester, each worth 6 units) and activate your student email. In the first semester of 2024, about 87% of ANU international students completed enrolment within two weeks of the window opening [ANU, 2024, Student Enrolment Statistics Dashboard].

### Course Selection Strategy and Credit Limits

ANU requires international students to take at least 3 courses (18 units) per semester to maintain full-time status. Watch out for prerequisites and timetable clashes when choosing. ISIS shows live course capacity, and popular courses such as COMP1100 (Introduction to Computer Science) usually fill up within 48 hours of enrolment opening. In 2024, about 23% of international students had to adjust their course selection within a week of enrolling because of clashes.

### Tuition Payment and Buying OSHC

During online enrolment, you confirm your tuition payment status. ANU accepts bank transfer, credit cards (Visa/Mastercard) and third-party payment platforms. For cross-border tuition payments, some study-abroad families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E6%8A%A5%E5%88%B0%E6%B3%A8%E5%86%8C%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B%E4%BB%8E-coe-%E5%88%B0%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E5%8D%A1%E9%A2%86%E5%8F%96), which offer real-time rate locking and multi-currency payment options. You must also upload your OSHC certificate during enrolment; ANU's partner insurers include Medibank, Allianz and BUPA, with premiums based on the cover period — for example, 12 months of OSHC costs roughly AUD 600 to 800 [Department of Health, 2024, Overseas Student Health Cover Guidelines].

## On-Campus Check-In and Identity Verification

**On-campus check-in** is the in-person step after online enrolment, usually scheduled during O-Week, two weeks before the semester starts. Bring your passport, Visa Grant Letter and a printed CoE to ANU Student Central (in the Hancock Building) to verify your identity. In 2024, ANU welcomed about 4,500 international students during O-Week, with check-in taking 15 to 20 minutes per student on average [ANU, 2024, Orientation Week Activity Report].

### Biometrics and Visa Verification

Under Department of Home Affairs requirements, ANU must check at check-in that your biometrics (fingerprints and photo) match your visa record. If you haven't provided biometrics in Australia before, you must visit a designated Australian Visa Application Centre (AVAC) within 14 days of check-in. In 2024, about 8% of international students had to resubmit visa materials because of biometric mismatches.

### Student Email and Campus Network Activation

After identity verification, ANU assigns each student a unique UNI ID and password used to activate your student email (format uXXXXXXX@anu.edu.au) and the campus wireless network (Eduroam). The student email is the official channel for course notices, transcripts and visa updates, and must be activated within 24 hours of check-in. In 2024, about 96% of students set up their email on the day they checked in.

## Collecting Your Student Card and Activating It

The **student card** (ANU ID Card) is your key to the library, laboratories, printing services and campus facilities. Cards are made on the spot after check-in — ANU Student Central has two self-service card printers, with an average production time of 3 minutes. In 2024, ANU issued 11,200 new student cards, about 30% of them to international students [ANU, 2024, Student ID Card Issuance Report].

### Card Features and Access

The card has a Mifare chip that supports door access (such as 24-hour library entrances and laboratories), print top-ups (via the PaperCut system) and public transport concessions (usable as an ACT MyWay card). International students must upload a personal photo through ISIS within 7 days of collection to activate the card face, or the card is locked. In 2024, about 5% of students needed a card reissued because they hadn't uploaded a photo.

### Lost or Stolen Cards

If your card is lost, report it within 24 hours to ANU Security (phone: +61 2 6125 2249) and pay the AUD 30 replacement fee at Student Central. Replacement cards are usually ready within 3 business days. In 2024, ANU processed about 800 card replacement requests, 62% of them from international students.

## Health Insurance and Medical Registration

**OSHC** is a compulsory requirement for the student visa, covering basic medical care, hospital treatment and some medicines. You upload your insurance certificate at enrolment, but you must also contact your insurer (such as Medibank or Allianz) within 14 days of check-in to activate your insurance card and download the electronic certificate. In 2024, the Department of Health reported that about 15% of international students couldn't be seen at the campus medical centre (ANU Health Centre) because they hadn't activated their OSHC in time [Department of Health, 2024, OSHC Compliance Data].

### Registering with the Campus Medical Centre

ANU Health Centre is in Building 15 and offers GP services and mental health counselling. International students register at the front desk with their student card and OSHC certificate; a first appointment usually takes 24 to 48 hours to arrange. In 2024, the ANU Health Centre saw about 2,800 international students, about 40% of them for the first time.

### Vaccinations and Insurance Claims

Australia recommends international students get the flu vaccine (once a year, about AUD 25) and a COVID-19 booster (free). OSHC claims are submitted through your insurer's online system — Medibank's average claim processing time in 2024 was 10 business days.

## Academic Support and Orientation

**O-Week** is ANU's structured arrival program for international students, usually held in the week before the semester starts. The 2024 O-Week ran about 120 events, covering academic integrity lectures, library tours, campus safety training and international student socials. According to ANU Global Programs, international students who attended at least 3 O-Week events had a 12% higher first-semester course pass rate than those who didn't [ANU, 2024, Orientation Week Impact Study].

### Academic Integrity and Learning Resources

ANU requires all international students to complete the online Academic Integrity Module during O-Week — about 45 minutes long, with a pass mark of 80% or above. Learning resources include the 24-hour areas of ANU Library, free tutoring at the Academic Skills Centre (limited to 2 sessions per person per week, 45 minutes each) and drop-in help at the Maths Centre.

### Clubs and Community

ANU has more than 200 student clubs, about 30 of them aimed at international students, such as the ANU Chinese Students and Scholars Association (CSSA) and the ANU International Student Society. In 2024, ANU international student clubs registered 1,800 members, and students who joined club activities rated their social satisfaction at 4.2/5.0 on average.

## FAQ

### Q1: How soon after getting my CoE must I apply for the visa?

Apply within 30 days of receiving your CoE. In 2024, the average processing time for ANU international student visas was 42 days; if you lodge within 8 weeks of the course start date, you risk delays. The Department of Home Affairs recommends allowing at least 8 to 12 weeks for visa processing [Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Student Visa Processing Times].

### Q2: Can I apply for my student card online in advance?

No. ANU student cards must be collected in person through the on-campus check-in process; they can't be mailed. You need to bring your passport to Student Central during O-Week to have the card made on the spot. In 2024, about 92% of students received their card on the day they checked in; the remaining 8% came back the next day due to missing documents.

### Q3: Can I buy OSHC after arriving in Australia?

Yes, but it must be purchased before your visa is granted — OSHC is a mandatory visa application document, and you upload the certificate in ImmiAccount. If you switch insurers after arriving, notify ANU and update the system within 14 days. In 2024, about 5% of international students changed OSHC providers after arrival.
