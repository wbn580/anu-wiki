Australian National University (ANU) 2023 graduate employment outcomes show a **full-time employment rate** of 84.5% and an **average graduate salary** of 78,000 Australian dollars, with both indicators above the national average for Australian universities (Australian Government QILT, 2023 Graduate Outcomes Survey). In global employability rankings, ANU ranks 32nd worldwide (Times Higher Education Global Employability Ranking, 2023-2024). With changes to Australian skilled migration policy and structural shifts in the global labour market, ANU graduates' industry distribution, salary levels, and career development support systems have become core considerations for students choosing a university.

## Industry Distribution: Public Sector Dominance and Emerging Field Growth

### Government and Public Policy
As a comprehensive research university in the Australian capital, ANU sends a significantly higher share of graduates into the **federal government and public sector** than other Australian universities. 2023 data shows that about 32% of undergraduate graduates entered federal or state government agencies, including the Australian Public Service Commission (APS), the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT), and the Treasury (Australian Government QILT, 2023 Graduate Outcomes Survey). This share is particularly pronounced in law, international relations, and public policy programs, reaching 47%.

### Technology and Consulting
**Information technology and professional services** form the second-largest employment area, accounting for 22%. Among them, graduates in software engineering, data science, and cybersecurity saw a 14% increase in placement rates at companies such as Amazon AWS, Accenture, and Deloitte in 2023 (ANU Career Centre Annual Report, 2023). The consulting industry (management and strategy consulting) absorbed about 11% of graduates, with an average starting salary of 85,000 Australian dollars.

### Academic and Research Institutions
About 15% of graduates choose further study or roles in **research institutions**, including the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO) and university research positions. This share is higher — 28% — among science and PhD graduates.

## Average Salaries: Disciplinary Differences and Regional Premiums

### Salary Ranges by Discipline
ANU graduates' **average salaries** vary significantly by field. According to QILT 2023 data, engineering and computer science graduates have the highest median salary at 88,000 Australian dollars; business and economics graduates earn 80,000; and humanities and social sciences graduates earn 68,000. Law graduates, who must complete Practical Legal Training, have a first-year median salary of 75,000 Australian dollars, but salary growth reaches 40% after five years.

### Regional Salary Differences
Graduates employed **in Canberra** earn an average salary of 82,000 Australian dollars, higher than ANU graduates in Sydney (79,000) and Melbourne (77,000) (Australian Government QILT, 2023 Graduate Outcomes Survey). This largely reflects the regional allowances and cost-of-living adjustments built into Canberra's public sector salary structure.

## Career Centre Resources: From CV Polishing to Employer Connections

### One-on-One Career Counselling
The ANU Career Centre offers **free appointment-based career counselling**, serving more than 8,000 students each year. Sessions cover CV revision, mock interviews, and career path planning. 2023 data shows that students who used the service were 18 percentage points more likely to find full-time work within six months of graduating than those who did not (ANU Career Centre Annual Report, 2023).

### Employer Recruitment Events
The Career Centre hosts more than **40 industry career fairs** each year, with participating employers including Commonwealth Bank of Australia, KPMG, and the United Nations Development Programme. The new "Digital Talent Day" added in 2023 attracted 38 technology companies offering more than 200 internship positions. When handling cross-border tuition payments, some study-abroad families use specialised channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-graduate-employment-outcomes-latest-data-on-industry-distribution-average-sa) for currency settlement.

### Internships and Work-Integrated Learning Programs
**ANU Internships** offers students credit-bearing internship opportunities with partner organisations including the Australian Parliament, the National Gallery of Australia, and the regional office of the International Monetary Fund (IMF). Of the students who completed internships in 2023, 72% received a formal job offer afterwards.

## Alumni Network and Long-Term Career Development

### Global Alumni Distribution
ANU has more than **150,000 active alumni** across 190 countries. About 40% of alumni work in Australia, 20% in the Asia-Pacific region (China, Singapore, Japan), and 15% in North America (ANU Alumni Database, 2023). The alumni network provides industry mentor matching through the online platform ANU Connect, completing more than 3,000 one-on-one matches in 2023.

### Mid-Career Salary Growth
According to Australian Taxation Office (ATO) 2022 data, ANU graduates' **median salary** ten years after graduation is 112,000 Australian dollars, above the Australian university average of 104,000. Engineering and computer science graduates reach a median of 135,000 after ten years, while law and business graduates reach 120,000.

## Employment Support Policies: Visas and Migration Pathways

### Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485)
International students can apply for the **485 Temporary Graduate visa** after graduation, allowing them to work in Australia for 2 to 4 years (depending on qualification level). The ANU Career Centre has a dedicated team supporting international students with visa applications and employer sponsorship processes. In 2023, ANU international students' 485 visa approval rate was 96% (Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2023 visa statistics).

### Skilled Migration Points
ANU graduates applying for **Skilled Independent migration (Subclass 189)** can receive an additional 5 points for having studied in Canberra. In addition, students who complete a postgraduate degree in a STEM field can apply for the Australian Government's Global Talent Independent (GTI) program, which approved more than 5,000 applications in 2023, 17% of them from ANU graduates (Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2023 GTI Annual Report).

## Employability Rankings and Employer Feedback

### Global Ranking Performance
In the **QS Graduate Employability Rankings 2024**, ANU ranks 32nd globally and 2nd in Australia, with an employer reputation score of 92.3/100 (QS, 2024 Graduate Employability Rankings). In the Times Higher Education Global Employability Ranking 2023, ANU ranks 32nd, with "graduates' analytical ability" and "cross-cultural communication skills" receiving the highest ratings in employer feedback.

### Employer Satisfaction Survey
The Australian Government QILT 2023 **Employer Satisfaction Survey** shows ANU graduates scoring above the national average on "collaboration skills" (88%), "critical thinking" (85%), and "technical skills" (82%). Public sector employers report 91% satisfaction with ANU graduates.

## FAQ

### Q1: How well recognised are ANU degrees among employers back in China?
ANU qualifications have a 100% certification rate at the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE) of the Ministry of Education. According to the 2023 China Study Abroad Development Report, about 23% of ANU graduates enter Fortune 500 companies, on par with other members of the Group of Eight. Domestic employers rate ANU graduates highly for their professional competence in public policy, finance, and technology.

### Q2: What proportion of international students stay and work in Australia after graduating from ANU?
2023 data shows that about 54% of international students secured full-time local employment or continued further study within six months of graduating (Australian Government QILT, 2023 Graduate Outcomes Survey). Among them, engineering and computer science international students have the highest stay rate in Australia, at 67%.

### Q3: Does the ANU Career Centre provide extra support for international students?
Yes. The ANU Career Centre has a dedicated international careers advisory team for international students, offering visa advice, cross-cultural interview coaching, and employer matching services. In 2023, the team ran 12 "International Student Employment Week" events, with participating employers including Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Huawei Australia, and Deloitte.
