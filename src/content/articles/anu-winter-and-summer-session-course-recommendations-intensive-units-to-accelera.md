The Australian National University (ANU) runs two intensive teaching sessions each year — **Winter Session** (June–July) and **Summer Session** (November–February) — and students can accelerate their degree completion by taking 6–12 units of credit in **intensive units**. According to the official ANU 2024 academic calendar, Winter Session typically runs for 6 weeks (about 42 days) and Summer Session for 8 weeks (about 56 days), with each course delivering 20–24 contact hours per week. Australian Government Department of Education 2023 *Higher Education Student Data* shows that about 34% of undergraduate students nationwide have enrolled in at least one summer or winter session course during their studies, with ANU students accounting for 41% of that figure. For students who want to finish a standard 3-year bachelor's degree (240 units) one to two semesters early, or free up time for an Honours year, intensive session courses offer a clear pathway.

## The Structure of Intensive Session Courses and How Credit Works

**Intensive session courses** carry the same credit value as regular semester courses: each course is usually 6 units, with some worth 12 units (such as research projects or internships). ANU rules allow students to take up to **12 units** during Winter/Summer Session (that is, two 6-unit courses), and the credit counts towards overall degree progress. Courses are delivered in a concentrated format: Winter Session classes meet 4–5 times per week for 3–4 hours each; Summer Session classes meet 3–4 times per week for 3–5 hours each. Assessment typically includes a mid-session exam, a final essay or project report, and some courses include online quizzes.

### H3: Credit Transfer and Degree Progress Calculation
Make sure the courses you choose are required or elective options in your Study Plan. ANU's **Program and Course Approval** system (updated 2024) lets students submit course requests up to 4 weeks before each session begins. For example, a Bachelor of Commerce student who takes a 6-unit required course (such as BUSN7008) in Winter Session can use it to replace the same course in Semester 1 or 2, freeing up regular-semester time for an internship or thesis. The ANU 2023 *Course Handbook* notes that about 72% of intensive session courses are recognised as degree core or program electives.

### H3: Fees and Scholarship Eligibility
Winter/Summer Session tuition is charged per unit and matches regular semester rates: in 2024, international students paid AUD 1,080–1,320 per unit (depending on course level). The Australian Government 2023 *Student Assistance Scheme* report shows that ANU offers a limited number of **Summer Session Scholarships** for intensive sessions (worth AUD 1,500–3,000), covering 20%–40% of tuition. Application deadlines usually fall 6–8 weeks before the session begins (Winter: end of April; Summer: end of September).

## Popular Disciplines: Business and Economics

**Business and economics** is the most popular area in ANU intensive sessions, accounting for 38% of all Winter/Summer enrolments (ANU 2024 course enrolment data). Recommended courses include **ECON1101** (Principles of Microeconomics, 6 units) and **FINM1001** (Foundations of Finance, 6 units), both offered in Summer Session. Content emphasises quantitative analysis, with 3 hours of lectures and 2 hours of tutorials per week. The ANU College of Business and Economics 2023 *Teaching Evaluation Report* shows that intensive session cohorts achieve an average GPA of 5.2/7.0, slightly above the regular semester average of 4.9/7.0, partly thanks to smaller class sizes (25–35 students per class).

### H3: Course Difficulty and Prerequisites
**ECON1101** requires high school mathematics (Australian ATAR Mathematical Methods or equivalent), while **FINM1001** requires ECON1101 or equivalent as a prerequisite. The ANU 2024 *Course Prerequisites* document states that intensive session courses do not lower academic standards — students must cover in 6 weeks what a regular semester covers in 12. When it comes to paying tuition across borders, some international students and their families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-winter-and-summer-session-course-recommendations-intensive-units-to-accelera) to complete the currency exchange and make sure funds arrive on time.

## Popular Disciplines: Computer Science and Data Science

**Computer science** courses are the fastest-growing area of intensive session enrolments, up 27% year-on-year in 2023–2024 (ANU 2024 *Session Enrolment Statistics*). Recommended courses include **COMP1100** (Programming Fundamentals, 6 units) and **DATA1001** (Introduction to Data Science, 6 units), both offered in Winter Session. Courses use project-based assessment: COMP1100 comprises 4 programming assignments (60%) and 1 final exam (40%). ANU School of Computing 2023 *Course Feedback* data shows that in the Winter Session COMP1100 cohort, 89% of students completed all assignments within the 6 weeks, with an average mark of 65 out of 100.

### H3: Labs and Online Resources
ANU provides **24/7 lab access** for intensive sessions (24-hour computer labs in the Hanna Neumann Building). Students can use ANU's **Wattle** learning management system for recorded lectures and code repositories. According to the ANU 2024 *Technology Facilities Report*, lab utilisation during Winter Session was 73%, below the regular semester rate of 91% — meaning shorter queues.

## Popular Disciplines: Humanities and Social Sciences

**Humanities and social sciences** courses make up about 22% of intensive session offerings (ANU 2024 course data), making them a good fit for students who need to satisfy General Education requirements. Recommended courses include **PHIL1004** (Critical Thinking, 6 units) and **HIST1209** (Australian History, 6 units), both offered in Summer Session. These courses are essay- and discussion-based, with weekly reading loads of about 150–200 pages. The ANU College of Arts and Social Sciences 2023 *Course Evaluation* notes that in the Summer Session PHIL1004 cohort, students' critical writing skills (measured by standardised tests) improved by 18%, compared with 14% in regular semesters.

### H3: Language Requirements and Support
All intensive session courses are taught in English. ANU requires international students to have an overall IELTS score of at least 6.5 (no band below 6.0). The University offers free writing tutoring through the **Academic Skills Centre** (2 sessions per week, 1 hour each), and in the 2023 Winter Session, 67% of participants improved their grades by at least one level (ANU 2023 *Student Support Report*).

## How to Plan Your Intensive Session Timetable

**Timetable clashes** are the most common challenge in intensive sessions. ANU's **Timetable Planner** tool (updated 2024) lets students simulate their schedule before enrolling. It's a good idea to stagger intensive courses against regular semester courses: for example, if a Winter Session course ends in late June, you get 4 weeks of rest before Semester 2 starts at the end of July. ANU 2023 *Academic Progress Research* shows that 83% of students who took Winter Session courses maintained or improved their GPA in the following Semester 2.

### H3: Credit Caps and the Approval Process
Students can take up to **48 units** per academic year (including intensive sessions: 24 units in regular semesters + 12 units in intensive sessions + others). Course requests go through the **ISIS** system and require college approval. ANU 2024 *Academic Policy* states that intensive session courses cannot be used as substitutes for supplementary exams. Application deadlines: 15 May for Winter Session and 15 October for Summer Session.

## Challenges of Intensive Session Courses and How to Manage Them

**Time management** is the main challenge: intensive session courses involve 2–3 times the weekly contact hours of regular semester courses. The ANU Student Wellbeing Centre 2023 *Session Stress Survey* shows that Winter Session students report an average stress level of 6.8/10, above the regular semester figure of 5.2/10. Coping strategies include: using the **ANU Study Planner** template (free to download from Wattle) to break weekly tasks into daily goals; and joining **Peer-Assisted Study Sessions** (PASS, 1 session per week for 1.5 hours) — in the 2023 Winter Session, PASS participants scored on average 8 points higher (ANU 2023 *Academic Support Report*).

### H3: Mental Health and Support Resources
ANU offers the **Counselling Service** (free, 50 minutes per session) and the **ANU Wellbeing** app. In the 2023 Summer Session, about 12% of students used these services (ANU 2024 *Health Services Annual Report*). It's recommended that students book an initial appointment 2 weeks before their course starts.

## Evidence That Intensive Sessions Accelerate Graduation

According to the ANU 2023 *Degree Completion Rate Report*, students who complete at least 2 intensive session courses **graduate on average 0.7 years earlier** (down from 3.2 to 2.5 years). Among students who enrolled between 2020 and 2023, about 14% completed their 240-unit bachelor's degree within 2.5 years thanks to intensive session courses. Australian Government Department of Education 2023 *Higher Education Completion Data* shows ANU's 4-year completion rate is 73%, above the national average of 68%, partly attributed to the popularity of intensive session options. In addition, intensive session tuition (AUD 1,080–1,320 per unit) matches regular semester rates, yet students can save 1–2 semesters of living costs (the ANU 2024 *Tuition and Living Costs Guide* estimates Canberra's monthly cost of living at about AUD 1,800–2,500).

## FAQ

### Q1: Do Winter/Summer Session courses affect my visa status?
The Australian Department of Home Affairs 2024 *Student Visa Conditions* require student visa (Subclass 500) holders to enrol in at least 12 units per semester (full-time study load). Intensive sessions count as a separate session: if you take only 6 units in a Winter/Summer Session, make sure you complete 24 units in regular semesters to meet the annual 48-unit requirement. ANU International Student Services 2023 data shows that about 3% of intensive session enrolments prompted reminders about insufficient credit load, but no student had their visa cancelled for this reason.

### Q2: Do intensive session grades count towards my GPA?
Yes. The ANU 2024 *Academic Assessment Policy* is explicit that intensive session grades count towards your weighted average mark (GPA) on the same basis as regular semester grades. In the 2023 Winter Session, the average course GPA was 5.0/7.0, on par with regular semesters (ANU 2023 *Grade Distribution Report*). Transcripts note 'Winter Session' or 'Summer Session', but there is no special weighting.

### Q3: Can international students work during intensive sessions?
Under 2024 Australian immigration rules, international students may work up to 48 hours per fortnight during their studies. Winter/Summer Session courses involve 20–24 contact hours per week, so students need to budget their working hours carefully. The ANU 2023 *International Student Employment Survey* shows that about 31% of international students chose not to work during intensive sessions in order to focus on their courses.
