In the 2024–2025 academic year, the Australian National University (ANU) enrolled about 10,400 new international students, making up 44% of the total student body (ANU 2024 Annual Report). Enrolment Day is a mandatory administrative process for every international student, covering CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment) verification, Student ID Card collection, course enrolment, and tax document submission. According to Department of Education 2024 data, international students take an average of 2.5 working days to complete enrolment in Australia, but incomplete documents or system errors can stretch that to 5–7 days. Using ANU's official procedures as the benchmark, this article lists a chronological checklist from arriving on campus to completing every registration step, including document preparation, online system operations, on-site service points, and common causes of delay.

## 2–4 Weeks Before Enrolment Day: Online Pre-Enrolment and Document Preparation

Before arriving in Australia, international students must complete online pre-enrolment through ANU's **ISIS (International Student Information System)**. ANU usually emails the enrolment link 3–4 weeks before the semester starts; students must complete the following by the deadline (generally 2 weeks before the semester begins): upload a scan of the personal details page of your passport, fill in emergency contact information, and confirm that the course code on your CoE matches the semester. According to ANU's 2024 international student guide, students who miss the pre-enrolment deadline are automatically flagged by the system and must wait an extra 1–2 hours for manual review during on-site enrolment.

### Document Checklist

Students need the following originals or certified copies: **passport** (valid for the entire course duration), **printed CoE** (the PDF version ANU emails you), **Visa Grant Letter**, and **Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) certificate**. ANU requires all international students to provide their OSHC policy number on Enrolment Day; if you have not bought cover yet, you can purchase it on the spot at the ANU Student Centre on campus for 530 AUD per person per year (ANU Student Centre 2024 fee schedule).

### Tax Documents: TFN and Bank Account

The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) requires all international students working in Australia to apply for a **Tax File Number (TFN)**. Before Enrolment Day, students can apply online through the ATO website; the number usually arrives by post within 10–15 working days. If it has not arrived in time, you can fill in a TFN Application Declaration form on Enrolment Day and hand it in at the Student Centre. It is also worth opening an Australian bank account in advance (such as Commonwealth Bank, ANZ, or NAB) so you can link your tuition payment account on Enrolment Day. For cross-border tuition payments, some student families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-international-student-enrolment-day-checklist-from-presenting-your-coe-to-co) to settle their foreign exchange.

## On Enrolment Day: On-Site Process and Schedule

ANU's Enrolment Day usually takes place 1–2 days before the semester starts at **ANU Student Central (Building 154)**, open from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm (2024 academic year schedule). Arrive 30 minutes early — during peak hours (9:00–11:00 am) queues can reach 200–300 people. The on-site process has four main steps: **identity verification**, **course enrolment confirmation**, **student ID card production**, and **campus service activation**.

### Identity Verification and CoE Check

Students must present their passport, printed CoE, and Visa Grant Letter to staff. Staff verify the course code, semester start dates, and tuition payment status on the CoE through ANU's **Student Information System (SIS)**. If tuition has not been paid, the system shows a "Financial Hold" flag, and students must pay on the spot by credit card, BPAY, or bank transfer before moving to the next step. According to ANU Finance 2024 data, about 12% of international students have their enrolment delayed because tuition has not arrived.

### Course Enrolment: Confirm Subjects and Timetable

After identity verification, students confirm their chosen courses in ANU's **Programs and Courses** system. If you selected courses during pre-enrolment, staff print an Enrolment Confirmation Form for you to sign; if not, you can do it on a designated computer on site. ANU requires all international students to enrol in at least 2 courses (24 units) and at most 4 courses (48 units) per semester. Timetable clashes are a common problem — in Semester 1 2024 there were more than 1,800 clash reports — so students need to adjust to clash-free slots on Enrolment Day.

## Collecting Your Student ID Card

The student ID card is the most important identity credential on the ANU campus, used for library borrowing, exam identity checks, building access, and printing services. Collect it at the **ID Card Counter inside Student Central**: staff take your photo on the spot (uploaded photos are not accepted), print the card, and activate it. Production takes about 5–10 minutes and the card is issued immediately. The **student ID number** is linked to ANU's student system (ISIS) and can be used to log in to campus Wi-Fi (eduroam) and your student email.

### Student Card Features and Replacement

The card has a built-in **MIFARE chip** supporting contactless payments (such as campus coffee shops and printing stations). If you lose it, the replacement fee is 30 AUD under the 2024 standard, and you must submit a written application at Student Central. ANU recommends linking your student card to your bank account in ISIS right away so you can top up for on-campus spending. According to ANU Facilities Management 2024 statistics, about 4.2% of student cards are lost each semester, and 60% of those are returned to lost property within 24 hours.

## Campus Service Activation: Email, Wi-Fi, and OSHC

On Enrolment Day, students must activate three key services: the **ANU student email** (ending in @anu.edu.au), **eduroam wireless network**, and **OSHC insurance activation**. Email accounts are usually generated within 24 hours of pre-enrolment but need an initial password entered on site to activate. Wi-Fi login uses your student ID number and email password. OSHC activation requires entering your policy number on the insurer's website (such as Allianz Care or Medibank) and usually takes effect within 1 hour.

### Student Email and Official Communications

All official ANU notices — including course changes, exam schedules, and grade releases — are sent to your student email. ANU IT Services reported in 2024 that about 15% of international students fail to check their email promptly in the first week after enrolment, causing them to miss course selection deadlines. Set up email forwarding to your personal inbox on Enrolment Day, and install the official ANU Mobile app to receive push notifications.

## 1–3 Days After Enrolment: Confirmation and Next Steps

After Enrolment Day, students must log in to the **ISIS system** within 48 hours to confirm all courses appear on the "My Enrolment" page. If anything is missing or wrong, submit a correction request through Student Central within 5 working days. In addition, ANU requires all international students to check in for **Orientation Week (O-Week)** within 7 days of enrolment; those who do not are treated as having failed to complete the enrolment process.

### Tuition Payment Confirmation

Tuition payment status can be checked under "Financials" in ISIS. Bank transfers usually take 3–5 working days to arrive; credit card and BPAY payments show up almost immediately. ANU Finance 2024 data shows about 8% of international students receive payment reminder notices after enrolment because they never confirmed their payment status. Print your Fee Receipt on Enrolment Day and keep it until the end of semester.

## Common Causes of Delay and How to Handle Them

According to ANU Student Centre's 2024 internal data, the most common causes of delay on Enrolment Day are: **name mismatch between the CoE and passport** (22% of delayed cases), **tuition not received** (18%), and **visa validity under 6 months** (15%). Solutions: check 2 weeks in advance that the name spelling on your CoE exactly matches your passport; if it does not, contact the ANU Admissions Office for a correction letter. Visa validity issues must be resolved before Enrolment Day by contacting the Department of Home Affairs to apply for a new visa — ANU will not enrol students whose visa has less than 6 months of validity.

### System Outages and Manual Processing

If the ANU SIS system goes down on Enrolment Day (there was one 3-hour outage in Semester 1 2024), students are given a Manual Registration Form to fill in by hand, which staff then enter manually. This process usually takes 2–3 working days; during that time students can attend O-Week activities normally, but cannot use campus printing services.

## FAQ

### Q1: Do I have to be there in person on Enrolment Day? Can someone go for me?

ANU requires all international students to attend in person for identity verification and the student card photo. Under ANU's 2024 enrolment policy, only fully online students can apply for remote enrolment, and they must submit a written application 2 weeks in advance and receive approval. On-site enrolment cannot be done by proxy — there are no exceptions.

### Q2: Can I still enrol if I forget my printed CoE?

Yes, but you will wait longer. At the "Document Retrieval" counter in Student Central, present your passport and staff will retrieve the electronic CoE from ANU's system. This takes 20–30 minutes of manual processing, and you must sign an Acknowledgment of Document Retrieval form. In 2024, 6% of international students used this channel because they did not bring their CoE.

### Q3: Can I provide my own student card photo? What are the on-site photo requirements?

No. ANU requires the student card photo to be taken by staff on Enrolment Day; uploaded or personal photos are not allowed. Requirements include: face unobstructed (no hats or sunglasses), plain white background, and a neutral expression. If the photo fails the requirements (such as closed eyes or shadows), staff will ask you to retake it, and each retake takes about 5 minutes.
