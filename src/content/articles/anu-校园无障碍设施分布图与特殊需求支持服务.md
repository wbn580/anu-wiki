The Australian National University (ANU) main Acton campus's **accessible facilities** coverage and **special needs support services** make it one of the first campuses among the Group of Eight (Go8) universities to pass a compliance audit under the *Disability Discrimination Act* (DDA). According to the *Campus Accessibility Report* released by ANU's Equity and Diversity Office in 2023, the main campus has **47 accessible toilets**, **23 lifts** and **more than 12 kilometres of ramps and gentle pathways meeting AS 1428.1** (the Australian standard for accessible circulation). Meanwhile, Australian Department of Education, Skills and Employment (DESE) 2022 data shows that about **7.8%** of students enrolled in Australian higher education institutions report a disability or chronic health condition, while ANU's figure for the same period was **9.2%** — slightly above the national average. As the mid-term review of the *Disability Strategy 2021-2031* progresses in 2024, ANU's accessibility work on both the physical environment and academic services is becoming a key indicator for international students assessing campus inclusiveness.

## Campus Physical Accessibility Facilities

ANU's Acton campus covers about 145 hectares of undulating terrain, but the core teaching and living areas already have **accessible connections between major building clusters**. The campus Accessible Route Map, updated every semester by ANU Facilities and Services, covers 12 main routes, from Union Court to Chifley Library and from the John Curtin School of Medical Research to Bruce Hall.

### Accessible Entrances and Lift Provision

As of Semester 1, 2024, **all teaching buildings on the ANU main campus have accessible entrances**, 26 of them fitted with automatic or sliding doors. As for lifts, **23 lifts** are distributed across the library precinct, the science college buildings and the residential halls — including 2 at Hancock Library, 3 at the Marie Reay Teaching Centre and 4 at Kambri Precinct (ANU's new student centre). All lifts have braille buttons, voice annunciation and low-level control panels, meeting the AS 1735.12 standard.

### Accessible Toilets and Parenting Rooms

Of the **47 accessible toilets** on campus, 34 are standalone accessible/unisex toilets and 13 are convertible family facilities (Changing Places). The 3 family toilets at Kambri Precinct have powered changing tables and ceiling-track hoists to support users with significant mobility impairments. In addition, the campus has **5 parenting rooms**, located at the Marie Reay Teaching Centre, beside Kambri Café, at University House, Chifley Library and Hancock Library, each equipped with power outlets, a refrigerator and privacy curtains. According to ANU's 2023 *Campus Facilities Satisfaction Survey*, about 78% of users considered the number of accessible toilets "adequate or sufficient".

### Parking and Transport Connections

There are **32 accessible parking spaces** across campus, along the Acton entrance, Sullivans Creek Road and Daley Road, all within 150 metres' walk of the major teaching buildings. ANU runs a **free campus shuttle bus (ANU Express Shuttle)** — every vehicle has a low-floor design with wheelchair bays and audio announcements. The shuttle covers routes from the Acton campus to Canberra City and Belconnen, running daily from 6:30 am to 6:30 pm every 20 minutes.

## Structure of the Special Needs Support Services

ANU's special needs support service (Access & Inclusion) sits within the Division of Student Life. In 2023 it served **more than 1,200 registered students**, up 34% from 2019. The service covers four pillars: academic adjustments, assistive technology, mental health support and housing coordination.

### Academic Adjustments

Registered students can apply for a **personalised academic adjustment plan (Access Plan)**, which may include: extra time in exams (typically 25 additional minutes for every hour of exam), alternative exam formats (oral/electronic), in-class note-taking assistance, priority seating and assignment extension arrangements. ANU 2023 internal data shows that **about 83%** of Access Plan applications are approved within 15 business days. Applications require diagnostic documentation from a medical or psychological professional; supporting documents in languages including Chinese, Japanese and Korean are handled free of charge by ANU's official translation service.

### Assistive Technology and Learning Tools

ANU provides a **free assistive technology software** list, covering screen readers (JAWS/NVDA), speech recognition (Dragon NaturallySpeaking), mind-mapping tools (MindManager) and word prediction software (WordQ). **6 accessible computer stations** across campus are located at Chifley Library, Hancock Library, the Marie Reay Teaching Centre and Kambri Precinct, fitted with height-adjustable desks and chairs, large monitors and specialised keyboards. Software installation can be completed remotely through the ANU IT Service Desk, usually within 2 business days.

### Mental Health and Disability Support

The ANU Health Centre provides **free counselling services**, with 3,200 consultations in 2023. For students with ongoing mental health needs, the Access & Inclusion team can coordinate weekly one-on-one wellbeing coaching sessions. ANU also partners with the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) to help international students apply for temporary NDIS access support, assisting 27 international students with registration in 2023.

## Accessible Accommodation

Within ANU on-campus accommodation, **about 15% of beds** are classified as accessible rooms. The breakdown is as follows: Bruce Hall (8 rooms), Ursula Hall (6), Wright Hall (10), Daley Road Apartments (12) and Lena Karmel Lodge (4). All accessible rooms come with **adjustable care beds, low-level wardrobes, accessible showers** and emergency call buttons. Common areas (kitchens, laundries, lounges) meet the AS 1428.1 standard, with corridor widths of no less than 1,200 mm.

### Application Process and Priority

Students must indicate their accessibility needs on the accommodation application form and upload relevant medical evidence. ANU's Accommodation Office allocates rooms according to **level of need**, prioritising students with mobility impairments, visual impairments and chronic conditions. 2023 data shows the success rate for accessible room applications was **91%**, with an average waiting time of 2 weeks. Students who do not receive an accessible room are offered temporary accommodation coordination and transport subsidies.

## Campus Navigation and Information Accessibility

ANU has developed the **ANU Access Map** (a custom map built on the Google Maps API) marking every accessible entrance, lift, toilet and parking space. The map updates **in real time**: when a facility is temporarily closed (such as a lift under maintenance), the listing is updated within 48 hours. Tactile ground surface indicators are installed at major campus intersections, covering the 1.2-kilometre route from the Acton bus interchange to Kambri Precinct. Students with visual impairments can request a **personal guided tour**, provided by the ANU Students' Association (ANUSA) volunteer team — 83 tours were completed in 2023.

## Special Support for International Students

To address international students' specific needs, ANU holds a dedicated Access & Inclusion information session during **international student O-Week**, which 142 international students attended in 2023. The ANU International Student Support office has **2 Chinese-speaking advisers** and **1 Korean-speaking adviser**, who can help translate medical documents, explain the Australian healthcare system and coordinate NDIS applications. When paying cross-border tuition fees, some international families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E6%A0%A1%E5%9B%AD%E6%97%A0%E9%9A%9C%E7%A2%8D%E8%AE%BE%E6%96%BD%E5%88%86%E5%B8%83%E5%9B%BE%E4%B8%8E%E7%89%B9%E6%AE%8A%E9%9C%80%E6%B1%82%E6%94%AF%E6%8C%81%E6%9C%8D%E5%8A%A1) to complete the currency transfer.

### Medical Document Translation and Certification

ANU accepts translations certified by the **National Accreditation Authority for Translators and Interpreters (NAATI)** at the professional level. International students can submit NAATI-certified translations with their Access Plan application, with no further notarisation required. ANU's official partner translation agencies take 5-7 business days, and the cost is covered by the ANU Student Support Fund (capped at A$200 per year). In 2023, the fund spent A$18,000 on translation services.

## Complaints and Feedback Mechanisms

ANU operates an **accessibility complaints hotline** (phone: +61 2 6125 3346) and an online feedback form, with complaints processed within 10 business days. In 2023, **47 accessibility-related complaints** were received: 65% concerned lift faults or temporary closures, 22% concerned accessible toilet cleanliness and 13% concerned occupied accessible parking spaces. ANU Facilities and Services publishes a quarterly *Accessibility Complaints Handling Report* setting out corrective measures. If a complaint is not resolved within the timeframe, students may lodge a formal complaint with the Australian Human Rights Commission.

## FAQ

### Q1: Where are the main accessible toilets on the ANU campus?
The ANU main campus has 47 accessible toilets in total, mainly at Kambri Precinct (4), the Marie Reay Teaching Centre (3), Chifley Library (2), Hancock Library (2) and the college buildings (the remaining 36). Every toilet has an emergency call button and automatic doors. The complete list of locations can be checked in real time on the ANU Access Map.

### Q2: How do international students apply for ANU academic adjustment services?
International students should submit their Access Plan application through the ANU Access & Inclusion website at least **6 weeks** before the start of semester, uploading NAATI-certified translations of their medical evidence. Approval takes an average of **15 business days**. 2023 data shows the approval rate for international students was **89%**, broadly on par with domestic students. It is advisable to attend the Access & Inclusion information session during O-Week for guidance.

### Q3: When is the application deadline for accessible accommodation at ANU?
ANU accommodation applications usually open on **1 August** and close on **31 October** (for students entering the following year's Semester 1). There is no separate deadline for accessible room applications, but it is recommended that you note your needs on the accommodation form and submit your medical evidence **before 31 October**. In 2023, the success rate for accessible room applications was **91%**, with an average waiting time of 2 weeks.
