Around 450 students from the Australian National University (ANU) study at overseas partner universities through the semester exchange program each academic year, while the university receives the same number of incoming international exchange students. According to the Australian Government Department of Education's 2023 *International Student Mobility Report*, ANU is one of the Group of Eight (Go8) members with the largest number of participants in **student exchange programs**, and its partner network covers more than 120 universities in 38 countries and regions around the world. Based on official 2025 data from ANU's Global Programs office, this article systematically sets out the partner university list, the **credit transfer** mechanism and the application process, providing a practical reference for students planning to go on exchange in the 2025-2026 academic year.

## Partner University Distribution and Regional Characteristics

ANU's exchange partners are divided into six regional blocs, with Asia-Pacific accounting for about 42%, Europe 35%, North America 15%, and the remainder spread across the Middle East, Africa and South America. **Partner universities** are selected on criteria including academic ranking, course-matching quality and student support services.

### Key Partner Universities in Asia-Pacific

Asia-Pacific is the strategic focus of ANU's exchange program. In Japan, the partner universities are the University of Tokyo, Kyoto University, Osaka University and Waseda University, each accepting 2-4 ANU students per semester. Korean partners include Seoul National University, Korea University and Yonsei University, of which Seoul National University opens 10 exchange places each year. Mainland Chinese partners include Tsinghua University, Peking University, Fudan University and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, with 3-5 exchange places per university per semester. According to ANU's 2024 *Annual Report on Global Exchange Programs*, the most popular exchange destinations for ANU students in the Asia-Pacific region are, in order, the National University of Singapore, the University of Hong Kong and the University of Tokyo.

### European and North American Partners

European partners are concentrated in the UK and Germany. UK partners include University College London, the University of Edinburgh and the University of Manchester, with 5-8 exchange places per semester. German partners include LMU Munich, Heidelberg University and Freie Universität Berlin, some of which offer English-taught courses. The main North American partners are the University of California system (including six campuses such as Berkeley and Los Angeles), the University of Toronto and the University of British Columbia, with 3-6 exchange places per semester. US universities typically require applicants to have an academic GPA of 5.0/7.0 or above.

## Credit Transfer Mechanism and Course Matching

**Credit transfer** is the core of an exchange application. ANU uses the Australian Higher Education Credit System, under which 1 ANU credit point corresponds to roughly 12-15 hours of study load. Courses taken during the exchange must complete a credit conversion assessment in advance.

### Credit Conversion Standards and Process

ANU Global Programs requires exchange students to take courses equivalent to 18-24 ANU credit points per semester (usually 3-4 courses). Credit conversion is assessed on **course content matching** and falls into three tiers: full match (100% credit conversion), partial match (50%-75% conversion) and non-transferable (0%). Students must submit course outlines through ANU's "Course Pre-Approval System" at least 8 weeks before departure, for review by their college's academic committee. 2024 data shows that about 78% of pre-approval applications received a result within 15 business days.

### Transcript Conversion and GPA Calculation

Grades earned during the exchange are recorded on the ANU transcript as **raw scores** and are not directly converted into an ANU GPA. ANU uses a "Credit Exemption" system, under which exchange course grades do not count towards ANU's weighted average, but the credit points do count towards the total required for the degree. According to the ANU Academic Policy Manual (2024 edition), students must achieve at least the equivalent of a "Pass" at the host university to receive credit exemption. Some disciplines, such as law, medicine and engineering, have stricter matching requirements for exchange courses, so students should confirm details with their college academic adviser in advance.

## Eligibility and Key Dates

**Eligibility** requires applicants to be current ANU undergraduate or coursework postgraduate students who have completed at least 48 ANU credit points. The minimum GPA requirement is 4.0/7.0, and some competitive host universities require 5.0/7.0 or above.

### Application Rounds and Deadlines

ANU's semester exchange program opens two application rounds each year. Round one (exchange semester July of the same year to February of the following year) closes on 31 August of the preceding year; round two (exchange semester February to June of the following year) closes on 31 March of the same year. According to ANU Global Programs 2025 data, each round receives an average of 320 applications, with an acceptance rate of about 65%. Application materials include a personal statement, academic transcript, proof of language proficiency (some non-English-speaking host universities require IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90 or above) and two academic reference letters.

### Language Requirements and Visa Preparation

Host universities that teach in languages other than English usually require **proof of language proficiency**. For example, applying to Japanese universities requires a JLPT N2 certificate or above, while French universities require DELF B2 or DALF C1 results. ANU's International Student Services office advises students to start their visa application immediately after receiving their exchange offer. Australian passport holders can travel to most partner universities visa-free or with a visa on arrival, but Chinese students need to apply for the destination country's visa at least 3 months in advance.

## Funding and Scholarships

**Financial support**: ANU offers several scholarships for exchange students. The ANU Global Exchange Scholarship provides a one-off grant of A$2,000-5,000 to each successful student; in 2024, 120 scholarships totalling about A$480,000 were awarded. In addition, the Australian Government's "New Colombo Plan" provides grants of up to A$7,000 for students going on exchange in the Asia-Pacific region, and 85 ANU students received this funding in 2024.

### Living Costs and Tuition Arrangements

During the exchange, students pay normal tuition to ANU (i.e. ANU tuition, not the host university's tuition); accommodation and living costs are borne by the students themselves. According to ANU's 2024 *Exchange Program Budget Guide*, one semester of living costs in Tokyo (including accommodation, food and transport) is about A$8,000-12,000, while London is about A$10,000-15,000. For cross-border tuition payments, some international student families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%AD%A6%E6%9C%9F%E4%BA%A4%E6%8D%A2%E9%A1%B9%E7%9B%AE%E7%94%B3%E8%AF%B7%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97%E5%90%88%E4%BD%9C%E9%99%A2%E6%A0%A1%E5%90%8D%E5%8D%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%AD%A6%E5%88%86%E4%BA%92%E8%AE%A4) to complete their currency transfers. Students can also apply to the ANU Student Loan Scheme, borrowing up to A$2,000 for pre-exchange expenses.

## Academic Preparation and Course Selection Strategy

**Course selection** directly affects credit conversion efficiency. ANU Global Programs advises students to prioritise courses with more than 60% content overlap with the core courses of their major.

### Cross-Disciplinary Enrolment Restrictions

Some partner universities impose **enrolment restrictions** on exchange students. For example, the National University of Singapore restricts exchange students from taking medicine, dentistry and law courses, and the University of Tokyo only opens arts and basic science courses to exchange students. ANU students must submit a "Preferred Course List" with their application and prepare 3-5 backup courses. 2024 data shows that about 22% of exchange students had to adjust their original study plans because of course conflicts.

### Academic Integrity and Assessment Differences

Host universities' **assessment methods** may differ from ANU's. For example, European universities generally use a "Continuous Assessment" model, where assignments and mid-term exams account for 60%-70% of the final grade, while most ANU courses are weighted towards the final exam. Students should familiarise themselves with the host university's academic integrity policies before departure, to avoid academic misconduct allegations arising from differences in citation formats or collaborative assignment rules. ANU's Academic Skills Centre provides pre-exchange online training modules covering academic norms across different countries.

## Accommodation and Settling In

**Accommodation** is usually arranged with assistance from the host university's international student office. ANU Global Programs advises students to submit their accommodation application within 4 weeks of receiving their exchange offer.

### Accommodation Types and Costs

Host universities offer on-campus dormitories, off-campus apartments and homestays. At the University of Hong Kong, for example, on-campus dormitories for exchange students cost HK$4,000-6,000 per month (about A$750-1,130), while off-campus apartments rent for HK$8,000-12,000 per month. Waseda University's exchange-student dormitories cost JPY 50,000-80,000 per month (about A$500-800). Some European universities, such as LMU Munich, do not provide on-campus housing, so students must rent privately through third-party platforms.

### Health Insurance and Medical Support

All exchange students must purchase **Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)** covering all medical expenses during the exchange. ANU partners with Allianz Global Assistance to offer a dedicated insurance plan at A$478 per year (2024 rate). Students going to non-English-speaking countries should prepare common medications in advance and note the emergency medical service contact details at their destination.

## Return and Credit Conversion Confirmation

**Credit conversion confirmation** is a key step after the exchange ends. Students must submit the host university's official transcript to the Global Programs office within 4 weeks of returning to ANU.

### Transcript Review and Conversion Timeline

The ANU Academic Records Office completes the credit conversion review within 20 business days of receiving the transcript. 2024 data shows that about 93% of exchange courses were successfully granted credit exemption, with the remaining 7% rejected for mismatched content or grades below the required standard. Students can check the status of their credit conversion through the ANU ISIS system. If you disagree with a conversion outcome, you can appeal to the college academic committee within 10 business days.

### Impact on Degree Progress

Courses taken during the exchange can affect **degree progress**. ANU requires undergraduates to take at least 18 credit points per semester, and this rule applies to the exchange semester as well. If exchange courses fail to convert successfully, students must make up the courses in a later semester, which may delay graduation. Students are advised to work out a "degree progress contingency plan" with their college academic adviser before departure, specifying alternatives in case some courses fail to convert.

## FAQ

### Q1: What is the minimum GPA requirement for the ANU exchange program? Does it apply to all disciplines?

The minimum GPA requirement for the ANU exchange program is 4.0/7.0, but about 35% of partner universities require 5.0/7.0 or above. The requirement applies to all undergraduate and coursework postgraduate students, but disciplines such as law, medicine and engineering have additional course-matching requirements. 2024 data shows that the acceptance rate for applicants with a GPA below 4.5 was about 42%.

### Q2: During the exchange, do I pay tuition to ANU or only to the host university?

During the exchange, students pay normal tuition to ANU only; no tuition is paid to the host university. ANU's 2024 international student tuition rates are A$20,000-25,000 per semester (depending on the program), and this fee already covers the exchange semester. Accommodation, living costs, flights and insurance are borne by the student.

### Q3: Can the exchange semester be extended to a full year? How do I apply?

Yes. ANU allows students to apply for a two-semester (one academic year) exchange, but this must be indicated in the initial application. 2024 data shows that about 15% of exchange students chose the full-year option. The application process is the same as for a single semester, except that an additional "Full-Year Exchange Plan" must be submitted, explaining the course arrangements and credit conversion plan for both semesters. Some partner universities offer accommodation and visa benefits for full-year exchange students.
