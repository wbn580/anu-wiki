Australian National University (ANU) awarded more than AU$24 million in scholarships to international students in 2023, covering undergraduate and postgraduate study, with about 65% of these being renewable scholarships that require recipients to meet specific academic standards at the end of each academic year (ANU Scholarships Office, 2023, *Annual Scholarship Report*). According to the *International Student Data Summary* released by the Australian Government Department of Education in 2024, the average scholarship renewal rate across Australian universities in 2023 was about 72%, while ANU's rate came in slightly above the national average at 76% — yet nearly one in four students still lost their funding for failing to meet the conditions (Australian Government Department of Education, 2024, *International Student Data Summary 2023*). Based on ANU's official policy documents, this article systematically sets out the core conditions for renewing an international student scholarship, the grade conversion rules, common risk points, and the remedy mechanisms available, so current students can plan their academic path in advance.

## The Core Framework of Renewal Conditions

ANU's scholarship renewal review is carried out at the end of each year, based on your weighted average mark (WAM) across the full year rather than individual subject results. **Renewal conditions** fall into three main tiers: most undergraduate scholarships require a WAM of at least 60% (equivalent to a Credit grade); postgraduate scholarships usually require a WAM of at least 65% (Distinction); and a few highly competitive scholarships (such as the ANU Chancellor's International Scholarship) require a WAM of at least 70% (High Distinction). ANU's website states clearly that renewal review looks at academic results only — extracurricular activities and community service are not taken into account (ANU Scholarships Office, 2024, *Scholarships Terms and Conditions*).

### Grade Conversion Rules

ANU uses a 7-point GPA system, but scholarship renewal is assessed directly against the percentage WAM. WAM is calculated by multiplying each course mark (0-100) by that course's unit value, summing the results, then dividing by the total units. **Key conversion benchmarks**: 50-59 is a Pass, 60-69 is Credit, 70-79 is Distinction, and 80 and above is High Distinction. For example, if you take 4 courses in a semester (each worth 6 units) and score 65 in three of them and 58 in the fourth, your WAM is (65×6×3 + 58×6) ÷ (6×4) = (1,170 + 348) ÷ 24 = 63.25 — enough to satisfy undergraduate renewal conditions but below the postgraduate threshold.

### Credit Load Requirements

Grades aside, you must complete at least 75% of your enrolled credit load within the **renewal review period**. Under ANU's 2024 *Student Progress Policy*, full-time students enrol in 48 units per year (equivalent to 8 standard courses). If the units you actually complete fall below 36 because of leave of absence, course withdrawal or failed subjects, the scholarship is automatically suspended. Special circumstances such as medical reasons can qualify for an exemption, but written evidence must be submitted before the review deadline (ANU Academic Progress Committee, 2024, *Student Progress Policy*).

## Common Scholarship Types and Their Renewal Standards

ANU offers around 20 renewable scholarships to international students, of which **three have the widest coverage**: the ANU Chancellor's International Scholarship, the ANU College of Business and Economics International Scholarship, and the ANU College of Engineering and Computer Science International Merit Scholarship. Their specific renewal requirements are set out below.

### ANU Chancellor's International Scholarship

This scholarship is open to undergraduate applicants and provides an annual tuition fee reduction of AU$10,000 to AU$25,000, covering up to four years. The renewal condition is a **WAM of at least 70% at the end of each academic year**, with no fewer than 48 units enrolled for the year. If your first-year WAM is 68%, the scholarship is downgraded to the lowest tier (AU$10,000) the following year; two consecutive years of falling short means permanent cancellation (ANU Scholarships Office, 2024, *Chancellor's International Scholarship Terms*). 2023 data shows the renewal success rate for this scholarship was 81%, with about 12% of students who came close but missed the threshold receiving a one-off downgrade as a buffer (ANU Scholarships Office, 2023, *Annual Scholarship Report*).

### College-Level Scholarships

The ANU College of Business and Economics International Scholarship requires postgraduate students to hold a WAM of at least 65% for renewal, and to pass at least 3 courses (18 units) per semester. If a failed course drops your WAM to 62%, you can retake the course the following semester and have your average recalculated — but you must lodge a grade review application within four weeks of the supplementary exam. The College of Engineering and Computer Science International Merit Scholarship, by contrast, lets students apply once for an **"Academic Recovery Plan"** after a first failure to meet the standard; the plan requires a WAM above 70% in the following semester, or the scholarship is permanently cancelled (ANU College of Engineering and Computer Science, 2024, *Scholarship Policy*).

## Renewal Review Process and Key Dates

ANU's renewal review runs in three steps: **results calculation, eligibility verification, and outcome notification**. At the end of November each year, the student information system (ISIS) automatically extracts each student's full-year WAM and unit data, and in early December the Scholarships Committee manually double-checks it. Outcomes are usually emailed to your ANU student account by 15 December, and you must confirm acceptance of the renewal within 14 days (ANU Scholarships Office, 2024, *Renewal Process Timeline*).

### Review Dates and the Appeals Window

The key dates are: 30 November (results data cut-off), 15 December (outcome notification), and 15 January the following year (appeals deadline). If you believe your results were calculated incorrectly, you can lodge a **written appeal** between 15 December and 15 January, attaching your transcript and course outline. Of the appeals lodged, about 23% were overturned because of grade entry errors or course weight adjustments (ANU Academic Progress Committee, 2024, *Appeals Data Summary*). For cross-border tuition payments, some families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E5%A5%96%E5%AD%A6%E9%87%91%E7%BB%AD%E9%A2%86%E6%9D%A1%E4%BB%B6%E4%B8%8E%E6%88%90%E7%BB%A9%E8%A6%81%E6%B1%82) to settle the currency conversion.

### Special Circumstances

If illness, family emergencies or natural disasters cause your results to fall short, you can apply for a **temporary exemption**. A medical certificate or official document must be submitted within 30 days of results being released, and the exemption lasts up to one academic year. In 2023, ANU received 127 exemption applications and approved 61% of them, with medical reasons accounting for 78% (ANU Student Wellbeing Office, 2024, *Special Consideration Report*).

## Consequences of Falling Short and How to Recover

Students who fail to meet the renewal conditions face one of three outcomes: **downgrade, suspension, or permanent cancellation**. A downgrade usually applies when your result is close to the threshold (for example, a WAM within 2 points of the requirement) and you have no prior record; the scholarship amount is cut by 30%-50%. Suspension applies when you miss the mark for the first time by a wider margin (a WAM more than 5 points below the requirement); it lasts one semester, during which you receive no funding but keep your enrolment. Permanent cancellation applies after two consecutive years of falling short, or in cases of serious academic misconduct.

### Academic Recovery Plan

ANU offers an **Academic Recovery Plan** to students in suspended status, including free tutoring, academic planning advice, and weekly progress tracking. Students on the plan must reach the renewal WAM requirement in the following semester and must not fail any subjects. 2023 data shows that about 58% of students who took part in the recovery plan successfully had their scholarship reinstated (ANU Academic Skills Centre, 2024, *Recovery Program Outcome Report*).

### Alternative Funding Sources

If your scholarship is permanently cancelled, you can apply for other non-renewable ANU scholarships, such as the ANU International Merit Scholarship (a one-off award of AU$5,000-15,000) or college-level emergency funding. In addition, the Australian Government provides up to AU$15,000 per year through the **Destination Australia Program** for students studying in regional areas; ANU students at the Canberra campus are eligible to apply (Australian Government Department of Education, 2024, *Destination Australia Program Guidelines*).

## Renewal Conditions and Your Student Visa

Scholarship renewal status can affect **student visa (Subclass 500)** compliance. Under the Australian Department of Home Affairs' 2024 rules, a failed renewal does not by itself lead to visa cancellation, but if poor results push your GPA below the visa's minimum requirement (usually a 50% pass rate), the university must report you to the Department of Home Affairs. In 2023, ANU submitted 43 reports to the department about unsatisfactory academic progress, with about 30% of those cases linked to failed scholarship renewals (Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2024, *Student Visa Compliance Report*).

### Visa Conditions: The 80-Hour Work Limit

International students on a student visa may work no more than 48 hours per fortnight (the new rule in place since July 2023). If your scholarship is suspended, you may need to rely on part-time income, but the **work hour limit** is not adjusted based on scholarship status. Students whose scholarship has been suspended are advised to apply for an emergency loan through the university's financial aid office before increasing their working hours, so they do not breach their visa conditions (Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2024, *Student Visa Work Conditions*).

### Academic Progress and Visa Renewal

If failed renewal leaves you short of units and you need to extend your study period, you must apply to the Department of Home Affairs for a **new student visa**. ANU's International Student Services offers free visa renewal guidance and processed 214 related applications in 2023, with an average processing time of 6 weeks (ANU International Student Services, 2024, *Visa Support Report*).

## Common Misconceptions Among International Students

Several surveys show that international students hold **systematically skewed ideas** about the renewal conditions. An ANU survey of 1,200 scholarship holders in 2023 found that about 34% mistakenly believed "as long as you don't fail any subjects, the scholarship renews" — the actual standard is WAM, not pass rate; a further 22% were unaware of the minimum credit load requirement and withdrew from too many courses mid-semester, which suspended their scholarship (ANU Student Experience Survey, 2023, *Scholarship Knowledge Assessment*).

### Grade Calculation Misconceptions

Some students confuse individual subject marks with WAM. For example, scoring 85 (High Distinction) in one course but only 45 (Fail) in another can leave your WAM at just 65 — still enough for undergraduate renewal but below the postgraduate requirement. ANU recommends checking your live WAM through the ISIS system at the end of each semester rather than relying on rough estimates.

### Changing Majors and Credit Transfer

Changing majors or going on cross-institutional exchange can affect your renewal conditions. If you move from the ANU College of Business and Economics to the College of Arts and Social Sciences, your scholarship type may change and the renewal standard has to be reassessed. In 2023, ANU handled 89 renewal cases involving a change of major, and in about 18% of them the WAM recalculation fell short because course credits did not match (ANU Academic Registrar, 2024, *Transfer Credit Policy Report*).

## Useful Tools and Data Lookup

You can check your **scholarship status** and WAM data in real time through the ANU ISIS system. The ANU Scholarships Office also runs a monthly online Q&A session covering renewal questions. 2024 data shows that students who attended these sessions had a renewal success rate 17% higher than those who did not (ANU Scholarships Office, 2024, *Workshop Impact Analysis*).

## FAQ

### Q1: What is the minimum WAM required for ANU scholarship renewal?

Most undergraduate scholarships require a WAM of at least 60% (Credit level), postgraduate scholarships usually 65% (Distinction), and highly competitive scholarships such as the Chancellor's International Scholarship 70% (High Distinction). 2023 data shows ANU's overall renewal success rate was 76%, and about 40% of students who fell short lost their funding because their WAM sat between 58% and 62% (ANU Scholarships Office, 2023, *Annual Scholarship Report*).

### Q2: If I fail to renew in the first year, can I get the scholarship back in the second year?

It depends on the scholarship type. Some scholarships (such as the Chancellor's International Scholarship) allow a single downgrade, from AU$25,000 down to AU$10,000; two consecutive years of falling short means permanent cancellation. College-level scholarships such as the CBE International Scholarship let you apply for the Academic Recovery Plan after a first miss, with a 58% success rate (ANU Academic Skills Centre, 2024, *Recovery Program Outcome Report*).

### Q3: Will a failed scholarship renewal affect my student visa?

It does not directly lead to visa cancellation, but if poor results push your GPA below the visa's minimum requirement (a pass rate below 50%), the university must report it to the Department of Home Affairs. In 2023, ANU submitted 43 such reports, about 30% of which related to failed scholarship renewals. Contact International Student Services within 30 days of results being released to avoid visa risk (Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2024, *Student Visa Compliance Report*).
