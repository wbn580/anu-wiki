The Australian National University (ANU) adjusts the opening hours of its core campus services during public holidays and semester breaks (such as Christmas, Easter and the winter break), which directly affects how international students plan their daily lives and errands. According to the International Student Data Summary published by the Australian Department of Education in 2023, ANU enrolled 10,421 international students in 2023, accounting for 40.4% of the total student body (Australian Department of Education, 2023, International Student Data Summary). Meanwhile, the ACT Government sets 11 public holidays a year, of which Christmas Day (25 December) and New Year's Day (1 January) are full statutory shutdown days, when campus services shrink dramatically or close entirely (ACT Government, 2024, Public Holidays in the ACT). This entry systematically compiles how ANU's main service facilities change their opening hours over the holidays for students' reference.

## Student Central and Academic Services

**Student Central** is ANU's main counter for administrative matters such as enrolment, transcripts and visa documents. During a normal semester it is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. But over the **Christmas to New Year period** (usually 24 December to 2 January) Student Central closes completely, with no counter or phone service. It also closes for the 4 days of the Easter weekend (Good Friday to Easter Monday). During the winter break (late June to early July) hours shrink to Monday to Thursday, 10:00 am to 4:00 pm, closed on Fridays.

### Online Services and Emergency Contact

While services are closed for the holidays, students can still lodge non-urgent matters as online tickets through the **ANU ISD (Information Services Desk)**. According to ANU's official 2024 service notice, online ticket response times during the holidays stretch from the usual 2 working days to 5-7 working days (ANU, 2024, Student Central Holiday Hours). Urgent visa issues must be taken directly to the Australian Department of Home Affairs — ANU does not provide emergency visa processing during holidays.

### Academic Advising and Supervisor Appointments

Academic Advisors in the colleges do not offer face-to-face consultations during the holidays. In the first week after the semester ends (usually the "Stuvac" study vacation week), advisors are still bookable, but their availability shrinks to 3 hours a day. **Enrolment** change requests are not processed on public holidays themselves; the system still accepts online submissions, but review is deferred to the next working day.

## The Library System

ANU's library system comprises the **Chifley Library, Hancock Library, Law Library** and **Art & Music Library**, and holiday hours differ markedly between them. According to the ANU Library 2024 timetable, Chifley Library, the main library, offers 24-hour, 7-day service during semester, but on public holidays it switches to **10:00 am to 6:00 pm**. On Christmas Day (25 December) and New Year's Day (1 January) all libraries are completely closed.

### Special Holiday Sessions

During the winter break (the third week of June to the third week of July), all libraries keep uniform hours of Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 7:00 pm, closed on weekends. At Easter, Good Friday and Easter Sunday are closed all day, while Easter Saturday and Easter Monday open from **12:00 noon to 6:00 pm**. On Labour Day (first Monday in October) and the King's Birthday (second Monday in June), libraries open for the afternoon only.

### Online Resources and Study Spaces

**Electronic resources** (databases, e-books and journals) at all libraries remain accessible 24 hours a day during the holidays. The 24/7 Study Zone, on the lower level of Chifley Library, never closes and requires your student card to enter. According to ANU's 2024 facilities report, the 24/7 Study Zone averaged 62% occupancy during the holidays, above the 45% semester average (ANU Facilities, 2024, Space Utilization Report).

## Dining and Retail Services

Food and retail outlets on the ANU campus are run by the **ANU Union** and **commercial tenants**, and holiday hours are far from uniform. The main dining hub (Kambri Precinct) has more than 15 food vendors during semester, but on public holidays only **2 to 3** stay open, usually chain brands (such as Subway or Boost Juice). Over the Christmas to New Year period, Kambri Precinct closes entirely for about 10 days.

### Supermarkets and Convenience Stores

The on-campus **IGA X-press** supermarket (in Kambri) shortens its public holiday hours to 10:00 am to 6:00 pm (normally 7:00 am to 10:00 pm). It closes on New Year's Day and Christmas Day. The **7-Eleven** convenience store at University House never closes, but during the holidays it opens only 8:00 am to 8:00 pm (normally 24 hours). For cross-border tuition payments, some families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E8%8A%82%E5%81%87%E6%97%A5%E6%A0%A1%E5%9B%AD%E6%9C%8D%E5%8A%A1%E5%BC%80%E6%94%BE%E6%97%B6%E9%97%B4%E6%B1%87%E6%80%BB) to convert and remit funds, reducing the impact of exchange rate fluctuations.

### Delivery and Takeaway Services

During the holidays, **food delivery** around campus (such as Uber Eats and Menulog) shrinks its coverage and some restaurants close. According to ACT Government 2023 data, the number of active restaurants within 1 km of campus drops by about 60% during the holidays (ACT Government, 2023, Business Activity Report). Stock up on food in advance, or use the campus vending machines (available 24 hours).

## Health and Medical Services

The **ANU Health Centre** (in Building 12) closes completely on public holidays, with no appointments or emergency services. Its regular hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm. During the holidays students must go to the emergency departments of **Canberra Hospital** (in Garran) or **Calvary Hospital** (in Bruce). According to ACT Health 2024 data, the average wait at the Canberra Hospital emergency department on public holidays is about 3.5 hours, longer than the 2.8 hours on working days (ACT Health, 2024, Emergency Department Performance Report).

### Mental Health Support

The **ANU Counselling Centre** does not offer face-to-face sessions during the holidays, but the **24-hour crisis line** (1300 572 112) keeps running. **Lifeline Australia** (13 11 14) and **Beyond Blue** (1300 224 636) also provide year-round telephone support. According to ANU's 2023 student wellbeing survey, about 18% of international students reported feeling lonely or anxious during the holidays, up from 12% during semester (ANU, 2023, Student Wellbeing Survey).

### Pharmacy Services

The on-campus **Chemist Warehouse** (in Kambri) is open 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on public holidays, closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day. The **Priceline Pharmacy** in the city stays open over the holidays, but with reduced hours of 10:00 am to 4:00 pm. Note that prescription medicines cannot be renewed through the ANU Health Centre during the holidays — plan ahead.

## Transport and Parking Services

The **ANU Express Shuttle**, the on-campus bus, does not run on public holidays. During semester it operates Monday to Friday, linking the main areas of campus. During the holidays students must rely on **ACT public transport** (Transport Canberra). According to the Transport Canberra 2024 timetable, bus services on public holidays drop to the normal Sunday schedule — roughly one bus every 30 to 60 minutes, with some routes suspended (Transport Canberra, 2024, Holiday Timetable).

### Parking and Bicycles

**Paid parking** on campus (such as the Kambri and Burns car parks) is free on public holidays. However, **time limits** (such as 2-hour parking zones) still apply during the holidays, and violations are fined. **Bicycle parking** facilities are available year-round, but campus lighting is reduced during the holidays, so use a U-lock and park in monitored areas. According to the ANU Security 2023 report, bicycle thefts during the holidays run about 25% higher than during semester (ANU Security, 2023, Annual Crime Report).

### Taxis and Ride-Sharing

During the holidays, **Uber** and **Didi** operate normally in Canberra, but wait times may stretch to 15-20 minutes (normally 5-10 minutes). **Taxis** (such as Canberra Cabs) need to be booked by phone, with a 10% surcharge during the holidays.

## Sports and Leisure Facilities

The **ANU Sport and Recreation Centre** (in Building 40) closes on public holidays, including the gym, pool and sports courts. Its regular hours are Monday to Friday, 6:00 am to 9:00 pm, and weekends 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. During the holidays students can use external facilities such as the **Canberra Olympic Pool** (in Civic) or **Club Lime** (in Belconnen), which usually stay open but with shorter hours.

### Outdoor Spaces

Outdoor areas such as the **Sullivans Creek** walking track and the **Black Mountain** nature reserve are open year-round, but maintenance (rubbish collection and lighting) is reduced during the holidays. According to ANU Estate Management 2024 data, campus lighting is cut by about 40% during the holidays, so avoid walking alone to remote areas at night (ANU Estate Management, 2024, Campus Lighting Audit).

### Student Club Activities

The **ANU Student Association** (ANUSA) and **PARSA** (the postgraduate association) do not organise events during the holidays, though some clubs may arrange their own gatherings. Check the **ANU Student Life** website for the holiday activity calendar. 2023 data shows only about 5 clubs stayed active during the holidays, far below the 120 during semester (ANUSA, 2023, Club Activity Report).

## Accommodation and Daily Living Support

**ANU Accommodation** (including Bruce Hall, Burgmann College and others) stays open during the holidays, but front desk hours are shortened. Over the Christmas to New Year period, most residential colleges' front desks are open only 9:00 am to 12:00 noon, with after-hours and evening needs handled by duty staff on the emergency phone. **Mail and parcels** are not distributed during the holidays; collect them with your student card once services resume.

### Laundry and Kitchen Facilities

**Laundries** in the residential buildings (coin- or card-operated) are available year-round, but maintenance response times during the holidays stretch to 48 hours (normally 24 hours). **Shared kitchens** may be partially closed during the holidays for cleaning, so it is wise to have a personal rice cooker or microwave. Under ANU Accommodation's 2024 policy, open-flame cooking equipment is prohibited in rooms during the holidays.

### Emergency Accommodation Support

**ANU Safer Communities** provides 24-hour emergency accommodation advice (phone: 6125 3346) for situations involving homelessness or safety threats. During the holidays response times are unchanged, but referrals to external shelters (such as ACT Shelter) may be delayed by the holidays. 2023 data shows the service received about 40 enquiries during the holiday period, 65% of them from international students (ANU Safer Communities, 2023, Annual Report).

## FAQ

### Q1: Does the ANU campus close completely over Christmas?

On Christmas Day (25 December) and New Year's Day (1 January) the main campus services close completely, including Student Central, the libraries and the Health Centre. But the 24/7 Study Zone, the 7-Eleven convenience store and outdoor spaces stay open. About 80% of food vendors shut between 24 December and 2 January (ANU, 2024, Holiday Service Schedule).

### Q2: How do I get emergency medical help during the holidays?

When the ANU Health Centre is closed, go to the Canberra Hospital emergency department, where the average holiday wait is 3.5 hours. For a mental health crisis call 1300 572 112 (ANU 24-hour line) or Lifeline on 13 11 14. Pharmacy services shorten their holiday hours to 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.

### Q3: Is parking free on campus during the holidays?

Yes. All paid car parks on campus are free on public holidays, but time limits (such as 2-hour parking zones) still apply, and violations are fined $120. The campus shuttle does not run during the holidays, and bus services drop to one every 30-60 minutes (Transport Canberra, 2024, Holiday Timetable).
