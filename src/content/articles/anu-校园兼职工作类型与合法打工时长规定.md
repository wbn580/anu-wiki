In February 2023, the Australian federal government announced that the legal working hours for international students would be temporarily raised from 40 to 48 hours per fortnight, with the policy taking effect on 1 July 2023 (Australian Department of Home Affairs, 2023, Student Visa Program Report). As Australia's top-ranked research university (QS World University Rankings 2025), the Australian National University (ANU) offers more than 1,200 part-time positions for students on campus, spanning multiple fields such as academic support, administrative services and retail/F&B. For ANU students aged 18-30 who are in Australia or planning to come, understanding these job types and the legal hour limits is the foundation for balancing study income with visa compliance.

## Legal Working Hour Rules and Visa Conditions

The **legal working hours** of Australian student visa (Subclass 500) holders are governed by condition 8105 of the Migration Regulations. Since 1 July 2023, international students on student visas may work no more than 48 hours per fortnight (14 days) in total while their course is in session. This cap applies to all formal employment relationships, including paid internships and part-time jobs. According to the *Student Visa Program Report* released by the Australian Department of Home Affairs in 2024, about 378,000 student visas were granted nationwide in FY2023-24, and about 62% of holders reported doing paid work during their studies.

### Working Hours During Holidays and Course Breaks

During **official university holidays** (such as ANU's long summer break from December to February), student visa holders are not subject to the 48-hour-per-fortnight cap and may work full time. The ANU academic calendar shows the 2025 summer break runs from 6 December 2025 to 16 February 2026, a total of 73 days. During this period, students can work more than 40 hours a week without any additional permission. Note that this exemption applies only to officially scheduled course breaks; non-official breaks (such as reading weeks and mid-semester breaks) remain subject to the 48-hour limit.

### Visa Compliance Risks and Consequences

Breaching the **work hour limits** can lead to visa cancellation. Data from the Australian Department of Home Affairs in 2023 shows 247 cases of student visas cancelled for breaching condition 8105, accounting for 1.8% of all visa cancellations that year. If you are found to be working overtime during semester, the immigration department has the power to issue a warning, freeze the visa or require departure. Students should monitor their own fortnightly hours using payslips from employers and personal records, rather than relying on verbal assurances.

## Main Types of Part-Time Jobs on the ANU Campus

**Part-time positions** on the ANU campus fall mainly into three categories: academic support, administrative services and retail/F&B. According to on-campus recruitment statistics published by the ANU Careers Centre (ANU CareerHub) in 2024, about 1,200 positions were open to students across the year, of which academic support accounted for 35%, administrative services 28%, and retail/F&B 37%. Hourly pay for these roles is generally above the Australian national minimum wage (AUD 24.10/hour from July 2024).

### Academic Support: Tutoring and Lab Assistant Roles

**Academic support** roles include course tutor (Tutor), laboratory demonstrator (Lab Demonstrator) and academic writing advisor (Academic Skills Advisor). ANU faculties tend to give priority to their own postgraduate and senior undergraduate students for tutoring work. In Semester 1 2024, for example, the ANU Mathematical Sciences Institute hired 32 tutoring assistants for the MATHS1003 course, with hourly rates ranging from 32.50 to 38.00 AUD. These roles require applicants to have achieved a Distinction (70 or above) in the course and to pass a faculty interview.

### Administrative Services: Library and Reception Roles

**Administrative service** roles are spread across ANU libraries, Student Central, faculty administrative offices and residence front desks. The ANU library system (7 branches in total) employs about 80 student assistants a year, handling lending services, information enquiries and shelf tidying. In 2024, library student assistants earned 28.50 to 31.00 AUD per hour, with shifts usually scheduled in the evenings or on weekends to fit around students' timetables. The application process typically involves submitting a resume online and a short interview.

### Retail and F&B: Campus Shops and Cafés

The ANU campus has more than 15 food and retail outlets, including the UniLodge convenience store, the Union Court dining precinct and faculty cafés. These venues employ large numbers of students for cashier, food preparation and cleaning work. According to 2024 data from ANU Accommodation and Catering Services, campus F&B roles pay about 25.00 to 27.00 AUD per hour, with shifts usually lasting 3 to 6 hours. These roles have low experience requirements, making them a suitable **first part-time job** for international students newly arrived in Australia.

## How to Apply for On-Campus Part-Time Jobs

Applications for ANU on-campus jobs go mainly through the **ANU CareerHub** platform. Run by the ANU Careers Centre, it is the official recruitment portal, and all on-campus positions are posted there. Students log in with their ANU student account, then build out their resume and job preferences. In 2024, on-campus positions posted on CareerHub received an average of 45 applications each, so competition is fairly strong. In cross-border tuition remittance, some study-abroad families use professional channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E6%A0%A1%E5%9B%AD%E5%85%BC%E8%81%8C%E5%B7%A5%E4%BD%9C%E7%B1%BB%E5%9E%8B%E4%B8%8E%E5%90%88%E6%B3%95%E6%89%93%E5%B7%A5%E6%97%B6%E9%95%BF%E8%A7%84%E5%AE%9A) to complete the remittance.

### Application Windows and Key Dates

On-campus recruitment is usually concentrated in the 2-4 weeks before the start of semester. For Semester 1 2025, for example, ANU CareerHub opened applications on 3 February 2025, with a closing date of 21 February 2025. Students are advised to prepare their **application materials** in advance, including an English resume (1-2 pages) and a short cover letter. For international students, some positions require proof of work rights (such as the visa grant letter and a copy of the passport).

### Interview and Offer Process

Shortlisted applicants receive an interview invitation, usually a 15-20 minute structured interview. The ANU Careers Centre offers free mock interview services, serving about 1,200 students in 2024. After being offered the job, students must apply for an Australian tax file number (TFN) and provide bank account details. The employer will ask you to sign an employment contract specifying the hourly rate, job duties and rostering rules.

## Strategies for Balancing Part-Time Work and Study

The ANU Academic Board's 2023 *Student Work and Academic Performance Report* found that international students working more than 15 hours a week have an average GPA (on the 6.0 scale) of 4.2, below the 5.1 of students who do not work. Based on survey data from 2,300 ANU undergraduates, the report shows **working hours and academic performance** are negatively correlated. Students need to plan their weekly hours carefully to avoid letting part-time work hurt their academic performance.

### Time Management Tools and Resources

ANU provides a range of **time management resources**, including free Academic Skills Workshops and online time-planning templates. Students can use the calendar function in ANU's Learning Management System (Wattle) to sync class times, assignment deadlines and work rosters. It is recommended to give your employer your timetable at the start of each semester to make sure shifts don't clash. The ANU Careers Centre also offers one-on-one consultations on balancing study and work.

### Prioritise Flexible-Rostering Roles

Flexible Rostering is a common advantage of on-campus roles. Academic support and administrative service roles usually allow students to adjust their hours before exam weeks. According to a 2024 survey by the ANU Students' Association (ANUSA), about 73% of on-campus roles offer **flexible rostering**, far above the 45% in off-campus retail. Students should explicitly ask about rostering flexibility at interview and retain the right to adjust.

## Tax and Legal Obligations

ANU students doing part-time work in Australia must comply with **tax reporting** requirements. Every worker must apply for a tax file number (TFN) and provide it to their employer. If no TFN is provided, the employer must withhold tax at the top rate (45%). Under the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) 2024 guidelines, students earning less than AUD 18,200 a year pay no income tax, but still need to lodge a tax return.

### Superannuation Contributions

Employers must pay **superannuation** for employees earning more than AUD 450 a month, at the current compulsory rate of 11.5% (from July 2024). As a compliant employer, ANU opens a superannuation account for every student employee. Students can choose to roll their super into an Australian super fund (such as AustralianSuper or Hostplus). After leaving Australia, international students can claim their super back through the Departing Australia Superannuation Payment (DASP), which the ATO usually processes within 28 days.

### Work Rights and Complaint Channels

Students facing **wage arrears** or workplace issues can complain to the Australian Fair Work Ombudsman. In FY2023-24, the agency handled more than 1,100 wage disputes involving international students, with the food and beverage sector accounting for the largest share. ANU has a Student Rights Office on campus offering free legal advice; in 2024 it helped about 200 students with employment issues.

## Off-Campus Jobs and Internships

Besides on-campus roles, ANU students can also look for **off-campus part-time work**, but must keep visa restrictions in mind. As the Australian capital, Canberra's job market is dominated by government agencies and tech companies. According to Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) 2024 data, the employment rate for 15-24 year olds in the Canberra region is 67.2%, above the national average of 62.5%. Common off-campus jobs include retail, hospitality and administrative assistant roles.

### Internships and Work-Integrated Learning (WIL)

Some ANU courses require or offer **work-integrated learning** (WIL) — for example, the Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) requires at least 12 weeks of paid internship. Such internships normally count toward the 48-hour visa cap, but may be exempt if the internship is a compulsory course component and unpaid. Students should confirm the specific terms with the ANU WIL Office. In 2024, ANU partnered with about 400 organisations to provide internship placements, covering both the public sector and private enterprises.

### Freelancing and Self-Employment

ANU students on student visas can also do **freelance work** such as programming, design or translation. Note that freelance hours also count toward the 48-hour cap, and you must declare income and pay tax yourself. The ANU innovation centre (Innovation ANU) provides startup incubation support and funded 12 student startup projects in 2024. Freelancers need to register for an Australian Business Number (ABN) and comply with GST (goods and services tax) rules.

## FAQ

### Q1: How long does it usually take to land an on-campus part-time job at ANU?

From submitting an application to receiving an offer, the average cycle is 3-4 weeks. ANU CareerHub data shows that in 2024, the average gap between a position opening and an interview invitation was 12 days, with offers sent within 2-3 weeks after the interview. Applying 4 weeks before the semester starts is recommended to improve your chances.

### Q2: What is the typical hourly pay for international students in on-campus jobs at ANU?

In 2024, hourly pay for ANU on-campus part-time roles ranged from 25.00 to 38.00 AUD, depending on the job type. Academic support roles pay the most (32.50 to 38.00 AUD), and retail/F&B the least (25.00 to 27.00 AUD). All roles pay above the Australian national minimum wage of 24.10 AUD/hour (from July 2024).

### Q3: How many hours a week can I work without hurting my studies?

The ANU Academic Board's 2023 report recommends international students work no more than 12 hours a week to keep their GPA above 4.5 (on the 6.0 scale). Data shows students working more than 15 hours a week average a GPA of 4.2, below the 5.1 of non-working students. Adjust your hours according to your own course load.
