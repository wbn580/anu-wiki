Mental Health First Aid (MHFA) training at the Australian National University (ANU) is an officially accredited standardised course designed to teach participants the basic skills to recognise, understand and respond to mental health crises in others. According to Mental Health First Aid Australia (2023), about 4.5% of Australian adults experience a major depressive episode each year, while the rate among young people (aged 16-24) is higher, reaching 15.4%. On the ANU campus, the 2022 student health survey found that about 34% of respondents reported moderate to severe psychological distress. As demand for mental health support among Australian university students continues to rise, the MHFA certificate is becoming one of the non-technical qualifications employers recognise when hiring, particularly in education, healthcare, human resources and community services. The training usually runs over two days, and participants who pass the assessment receive an internationally recognised certificate valid for three years.

## Course structure and content

The ANU standard MHFA course follows the national curriculum framework set by Mental Health First Aid Australia (MHFA Australia). The course totals about 12 hours, usually delivered over two consecutive training days of roughly 6 hours each. Content covers four core modules: **depression**, **anxiety disorders**, **psychotic disorders** and **substance use disorders**. Each module includes three teaching components: symptom recognition, crisis intervention strategies and navigation of support resources.

### Core teaching modules

The course uses the "ALGEE" action plan as its central teaching framework: A (assess risk of suicide or harm), L (listen non-judgmentally), G (give reassurance and information), E (encourage professional help), E (encourage self-help strategies). Participants master the practical application of the framework through role plays, case discussions and small-group exercises. The MHFA Australia (2022) course evaluation report shows that trained participants improved their accuracy in recognising mental health problems by an average of 28% and their confidence in intervening by 41%.

### Crisis scenario simulations

The training includes a dedicated crisis scenario module covering four high-priority situations: **suicidal ideation**, **panic attacks**, **acute psychotic episodes** and **substance overdose**. Each scenario comes with a standardised intervention checklist, and participants must complete at least one full ALGEE intervention drill in a simulated environment. ANU Health & Wellbeing (2023) notes that this kind of simulation training shortens participants' response time in real situations by an average of 3.2 minutes.

## 2024-2025 course dates and registration

ANU Health & Wellbeing runs MHFA courses regularly throughout the academic year. Confirmed Semester 2, 2024 dates include: 24-25 August, 28-29 September, 26-27 October and 23-24 November. Preliminary Semester 1, 2025 dates are expected to be published on the ANU student portal in November 2024. Each class is capped at 20 participants to ensure the quality of teaching interaction. For cross-border tuition payments, some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payment](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-mental-health-first-aid-training-course-dates-registration-cost-and-why-its) to complete currency conversion.

### Registration process

Registration is completed through the "Workshops & Training" page on the ANU Health & Wellbeing website. Students must log in with their ANU unified identity (uni ID). The course is open to all current undergraduate and postgraduate students and staff. Some sessions are prioritised for Resident Advisors at student residential halls, with remaining places allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. ANUSA (2023) recommends registering at least two weeks before the course start date, because places typically fill within 48 hours during peak periods (such as the start of semester).

### Waitlist mechanism

If a course is full, the system automatically offers a waitlist option. The ANU Health Centre says the average conversion rate for waitlists is about 35%, with places typically released 5-7 days before the course starts. Waitlisted students receive an email notification and must confirm their place within 24 hours.

## Registration fees and financial support

ANU MHFA course fees vary by participant status. **Full-time ANU students** receive a subsidised rate of AUD 45 per course in 2024. **Staff** pay AUD 120. External participants (not affiliated with ANU) pay AUD 250. All fees include course materials, the MHFA manual and the certification fee.

### Fee waivers and scholarships

ANU Health & Wellbeing offers **full fee waivers** for students experiencing financial hardship. Applications require a short letter (no more than 300 words) and proof of current financial situation (such as a Centrelink payment notice or student loan records). In 2023, about 18% of enrolled students obtained free training through this channel. In addition, some colleges (such as the College of Health & Medicine and the School of Psychology) include MHFA training in student professional development budgets, and students can apply to their college's student services office for reimbursement.

### Refund policy

Cancellations more than 7 working days before the course start date receive a full refund; cancellations 3-7 working days before receive a 50% refund; cancellations fewer than 3 working days before, or no-shows, receive no refund. Refund requests must be submitted through the ANU Health Centre online form and are processed within 10-15 working days.

## Certification and validity

On completing the course and passing the assessment, participants receive a digital certificate issued by **Mental Health First Aid Australia** (MHFA Australia). The certificate is valid for **three years** from the course completion date. It carries a unique number that can be verified on the MHFA Australia website.

### Renewal and advanced courses

When the certificate expires, holders must complete a 4-hour **Refresher Course** to maintain their certification. The refresher usually costs 50% of the standard course fee, i.e. AUD 22.50 for ANU students. MHFA Australia also offers advanced courses including **Youth MHFA**, **Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander MHFA** and **Workplace MHFA**. ANU currently offers only the standard course and the youth version; Youth MHFA increased to 3 intakes per semester in 2024.

### International recognition

The MHFA certificate is recognised in Australia, New Zealand, Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom and parts of Europe. According to MHFA Australia (2023) global network data, more than 25 countries have adopted the training system. For ANU students planning to work overseas after graduation, the certificate can waive workplace health training modules in some countries (such as Canada).

## Value for job applications

In the Australian labour market the MHFA certificate is regarded as an important **soft-skill credential**. According to a Seek Australia (2023) analysis of job descriptions, about 12% of healthcare roles and 8% of education roles explicitly list "Mental Health First Aid certificate" as a preferred condition or bonus. In human resources, community services and customer management, the figure is about 5-6%.

### Industry-specific value

In **education**, the New South Wales Department of Education (2022) requires all newly hired school counsellors and residential staff to complete MHFA training within their first year of employment. In the **corporate sector**, Westpac (2023) links its internal "Mental Health Supporter" program to MHFA certification — employees holding the certificate receive an additional 2 days of paid mental health support leave per year. For ANU graduates, the MHFA certificate can be listed as "relevant practical experience" in personal statements when applying for postgraduate programs such as a Master of Clinical Psychology or Master of Public Health.

### CV presentation tips

ANU Careers (2023) advises students to list the MHFA certificate under "Professional Development" or "Certifications" on their CV, with the year of completion and certificate number. In interviews, the ALGEE framework can be cited as an example of teamwork and crisis management ability. The centre's data shows that among ANU graduates in 2022-2023, job seekers holding the MHFA certificate received interview invitations on average 9 days sooner than those without it.

## Student feedback and participation data

ANU Health Centre (2023) surveyed 312 students who completed training in 2022. Results show that 94% of participants found the course content "very practical" and 89% said they "applied at least one learned skill within a month of training". The most common applications were "recognising emotional changes in roommates or classmates" (67%) and "providing emotional support in group discussions" (52%).

### Participant demographics

Among ANU students who took MHFA training in 2023, **first-year undergraduates** made up the largest share at 38%; postgraduate students (coursework and research combined) accounted for 29%; residential hall Resident Advisors 18%; and the remainder were staff and external participants. Health and medical students made up 44% of participants, followed by social sciences (21%) and engineering and computer science (12%).

### Long-term impact

A follow-up study by the ANU School of Psychology (2023) (n=89) found that 6 months after completing MHFA training, participants' stigma scores toward mental health dropped by 0.7 standard deviations (measured with the Community Attitudes to Mental Illness scale, CAMI), and willingness to proactively seek psychological help increased by 32%. The study also found the effect was more pronounced among male participants, whose stigma scores fell 0.4 standard deviations more than women's.

## FAQ

### Q1: Does the ANU MHFA course require any prior knowledge or qualifications?

No. The course is open to all current ANU students and staff, with no psychology or medicine background required. Participants must be at least 18 years old. Course materials are in English; an IELTS 6.5 or equivalent level is recommended because the role-play components require spoken communication. 2024 data shows that about 92% of non-native English speakers complete the course successfully.

### Q2: Can I still use the certificate after graduation? Do I need to renew it?

The certificate is valid for three years from the course completion date and remains valid after graduation, with no fee required to maintain certification. If renewal is needed after three years, you can take the Refresher Course (4 hours, 50% of the standard course fee). ANU alumni can use their alumni card for the alumni rate on refresher courses, set at AUD 30 in 2024.

### Q3: Can I still attend if I missed the registration deadline?

You can join the waitlist. According to ANU Health Centre 2023 data, the waitlist conversion rate is about 35%, with places typically released 5-7 days before the course starts. In addition, 1-2 extra sessions may be added at the end of each semester; details are posted on the ANU student portal and the Health Centre's social media accounts. A confirmed extra session for Semester 2, 2024 is scheduled for 7-8 December.
