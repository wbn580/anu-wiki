The Australian National University (ANU) main campus sits in Canberra's Acton district, and its campus dining ecosystem served more than 25,000 enrolled students in 2024【ANU 2024, Annual Report】. According to Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) 2023 consumer price index data, dining prices in Canberra are on average about 8% lower than in Sydney【ABS 2023, CPI Report】. This guide systematically reviews the cafes, food trucks and affordable dining options on campus and in the surrounding Acton area, covering opening hours, price ranges and payment methods, to give enrolled students a practical everyday eating reference.

## The Core On-Campus Dining Precinct: Kambri

The Kambri precinct is the student hub opened by ANU in 2019, concentrating the largest dining cluster on campus. The area has 12 fixed food vendors, open Monday to Friday from 7 am to 8 pm, with some vendors open until 3 pm on weekends.

**Kambri Market Hall** is the central hub, offering Asian noodles, Mexican burritos, Japanese ramen and Mediterranean salad bowls. Main dishes range from AUD 10.50 to AUD 16.00. Vendors generally accept bank cards and mobile payments (Apple Pay/Google Pay), and cash usage has fallen to about 12%【ANU Student Services 2023, Campus Retail Report】.

**The Bowls Project** specialises in healthy bowls (acai bowls, smoothie bowls), priced at AUD 11.00 to AUD 14.50. The vendor scored 4.2/5 in the 2023 ANU student satisfaction survey (sample size n=1,200)【ANU Student Voice 2023, Survey Results】. Its peak period (12:00–13:30) has an average queue time of 8 minutes.

### Coffee Options in Kambri

The Kambri precinct houses three independent cafes: **On the Couch** (single-origin beans, espresso AUD 4.50), **The Coffee Lab** (cold brew AUD 5.00) and **Mister Barista** (flat white AUD 4.80). All three use beans from local roaster Ona Coffee, which won the World Barista Championship in 2015.

**On the Couch** also serves light sandwiches (AUD 8.50) and croissants (AUD 4.00), making it the main rest stop for library students. The cafe sells about 180 coffees per day on average, with peak hours from 9:00–10:30 am.

### Food Trucks in Kambri

Every Tuesday and Thursday, 2–3 mobile food trucks stop in the Kambri square. Common options include: **Bao Stop** (gua bao AUD 7.00), **Gourmet Greek** (gyros AUD 9.50) and **The Veggie Patch** (veggie burger AUD 10.00). The trucks accept cash and bank cards, but some are cash-only, so students are advised to keep small notes under AUD 20 on hand.

## The Eastern Campus: Union Court and Manning Clark

Union Court sits on the eastern side of campus, next to Chifley Library. The area was renovated in 2022 and now has 4 fixed food outlets. **The Gods Cafe** is the oldest campus cafe (operating for more than 30 years), serving light meals such as toasted sandwiches (AUD 7.50) and soups (AUD 5.00). It scored 4.0/5 in the 2023 ANU student satisfaction survey.

The basement level of **Manning Clark Centre** houses **The Bean Gallery**, offering budget coffee (from AUD 3.50) and a daily special lunch (AUD 8.00). The cafe is run by the ANU Students' Association (ANUSA), with profits channelled back into student club activities. Its espresso uses beans from local brand Lonsdale Street Roasters.

### Food Trucks at Manning Clark

Every Wednesday at midday, a **Taco Tuesday** truck stops in the Manning Clark square, serving Mexican tacos (3 for AUD 12.00). During ANU O-Week 2023, the truck sold more than 200 servings per day on average.

## The Western Campus: Hancock and Science Road

**The Library Café** next to Hancock Library serves coffee (AUD 4.00) and cakes (AUD 4.50), with opening hours matching the library (Monday to Friday 7:30–22:00, weekends 9:00–17:00). It is the go-to refuelling stop for students studying at night; 2023 data shows its evening sales (18:00–21:00) account for 30% of the day's total【ANU Library Services 2023, Operational Data】.

Along Science Road, **The Science Café** sits on the ground floor of the physics building. It serves sandwiches (AUD 8.00) and salads (AUD 9.50), mainly catering to students and staff of the College of Science and the College of Engineering. Its peak period is 11:30–13:00, with an average wait time of 5 minutes.

### Food Trucks in the West

Every Friday at midday, **The Dumpling Van** stops at the corner of Science Road and Kingsley Street, serving handmade dumplings (12 for AUD 10.00). The van operates in partnership with the ANU Chinese Students and Scholars Association (CSSA), with part of the profits donated to campus clubs.

## Affordable Dining Around Acton

Beyond the ANU campus, the Acton district offers several affordable dining options. **Acton Market** is in the centre of Acton (a 5-minute walk from Kambri), open every Saturday from 8:00 am–12:00 pm, selling fresh fruit, vegetables and prepared food. A kilogram of apples costs about AUD 4.00, and a freshly made jianbing (Chinese crepe) is AUD 7.00.

The **Food Court** at **Canberra Centre** (a 15-minute walk) offers Chinese fast food (such as fried rice AUD 9.00), Japanese bento boxes (AUD 11.00) and Vietnamese pho (AUD 12.00). The Food Court received a 4-star rating from ACT Health in 2023 (out of 5 stars)【ACT Health 2023, Food Safety Inspection Data】.

For cross-border tuition payments, some student families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-food-and-dining-guide-a-review-of-campus-cafes-food-trucks-and-affordable-ea) to complete their remittances.

### Convenience Stores and Supermarkets in Acton

**Acton IGA** on Liversidge Street is open 7:00–21:00 (Monday to Sunday). It offers light meals (sandwiches AUD 6.50), snacks and drinks. A standard lunch (sandwich + drink + fruit) totals about AUD 11.00. **7-Eleven** (University Avenue) is open 24 hours and offers hot food such as chicken wraps (AUD 5.50) and coffee (AUD 3.00).

## Student Discounts and Payment Methods

ANU students with a student ID receive discounts at the following vendors: **The Gods Cafe** (10% off), **The Bean Gallery** (15% off), **On the Couch** (5% off). Most vendors in **Kambri Market Hall** offer "happy hour" discounts from 2:00–4:00 pm, cutting AUD 2.00 to AUD 3.00 off main dishes.

On payment methods: all fixed on-campus vendors accept bank cards (Visa/Mastercard/Amex) and mobile payments. Among food trucks, about 70% accept bank cards and 30% are cash-only. ANU Student Central advises students to keep cash under AUD 50 on hand for truck transactions【ANU Student Central 2024, Payment Guidelines】.

### Weekly Budget Reference

Based on 5 days on campus per week, a lunch (main + drink) costs on average AUD 14.00 at Kambri, about AUD 10.00 at Manning Clark and about AUD 12.00 at a food truck. Adding coffee (AUD 4.50) and snacks (AUD 3.00), daily dining spend comes to about AUD 18.50 to AUD 22.00. The 2023 ANU student cost-of-living survey shows students spend on average AUD 95.00 (median) per week on on-campus dining【ANU Student Life 2023, Cost of Living Survey】.

## Dietary Restrictions and Special Needs

All fixed on-campus vendors label allergen information (including gluten, dairy and nuts). **The Veggie Patch** in **Kambri Market Hall** offers vegan options (such as a vegan burger AUD 10.00). **The Bowls Project** offers gluten-free oat bowls (AUD 13.00). **The Coffee Lab** offers oat milk and almond milk at no extra charge.

### Halal and Kosher Options

**Bao Stop** in **Kambri Market Hall** offers halal-certified gua bao (AUD 7.00). The pork dumplings at **The Dumpling Van** are not halal, but a chicken alternative is available. Kosher options require a trip to **Kosher Kitchen** at Canberra Centre (a 20-minute walk), open Wednesday to Sunday.

## FAQ

### Q1: What is the cheapest lunch option on the ANU campus?

**The Bean Gallery** at Manning Clark Centre offers a daily special lunch (AUD 8.00) including a main and a drink. That is about 43% cheaper than the average Kambri lunch (AUD 14.00). In addition, **The Gods Cafe** soup (AUD 5.00) with bread (AUD 2.00) totals just AUD 7.00.

### Q2: When do the campus food trucks appear each week?

Kambri square trucks appear every Tuesday and Thursday from 11:30–14:00; the Manning Clark square truck appears every Wednesday from 12:00–14:00; the Science Road truck appears every Friday from 12:00–14:00. All trucks are regulated by ACT Health, with a 100% inspection pass rate in 2023【ACT Health 2023, Mobile Food Vendor Inspection Records】.

### Q3: How can ANU students get halal or vegetarian meals on campus?

Halal meals are available at **Bao Stop** in Kambri Market Hall (gua bao AUD 7.00) and at **The Dumpling Van** (chicken dumplings AUD 10.00). Vegetarian options are broader: **The Bowls Project** (AUD 11.00), the **The Veggie Patch** truck (vegan burger AUD 10.00) and **The Bean Gallery** (daily vegetarian special AUD 8.00). All vendors label allergen information.
