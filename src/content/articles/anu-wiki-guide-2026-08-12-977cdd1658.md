## UK Student Visa 2026: What You Need to Prepare

Planning to study in the UK in 2026? The visa process involves several updated requirements, including proof of funds and a tuberculosis (TB) test. This guide walks through the key changes and provides a practical pre-submission checklist.

## Updated Funding Requirements for 2026

One of the most critical parts of your application is demonstrating you have enough money to support yourself and pay for your course. For 2026, the UK Home Office has set specific financial thresholds. You must show you have held the required funds for a set period, typically 28 consecutive days, before applying. The exact amount depends on your course location and whether you have already paid tuition fees or hold an official scholarship.

## Tuberculosis (TB) Test Requirement

If you are coming from a country where TB is prevalent, you must take a tuberculosis test at an approved clinic. The test must be completed before you submit your visa application, and you must include the certificate with your documents. Ensure the clinic is on the UK government's approved list for your country.

## Other Key Material Changes

Beyond funding and TB test, there are other documents you must prepare. These include:

- A valid passport or travel document.
- Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from your UK university.
- Proof of English language ability (if required).
- Academic qualifications as stated in your CAS.
- Any previous passports that show your travel history.

Always check the official UK government guidance before applying, as requirements can change.

## Pre-Submission Checklist

Use this checklist to make sure you have everything in order:

- [ ] Valid passport with at least one blank page.
- [ ] CAS number from your university.
- [ ] Financial documents showing the required funds for the specified period.
- [ ] Tuberculosis test certificate (if applicable).
- [ ] English language test results (if required).
- [ ] Academic certificates and transcripts.
- [ ] Passport photos that meet UK visa specifications.
- [ ] Completed online visa application form.
- [ ] Visa application fee payment confirmation.
- [ ] Biometric appointment confirmation.

Double-check each item against the latest official instructions to avoid delays.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much money do I need to show for the UK student visa in 2026?

The exact amount varies based on your course location (London vs. outside London) and whether tuition is already paid. Check the current Home Office financial requirements for the precise figure.

### When should I take the TB test?

You must take the TB test at an approved clinic before you submit your visa application. The certificate is usually valid for six months, so plan accordingly.

### Where can I find the official UK visa guidance?

Always refer to the official UK government website (gov.uk) for the most current and authoritative information. This guide is for reference only.
