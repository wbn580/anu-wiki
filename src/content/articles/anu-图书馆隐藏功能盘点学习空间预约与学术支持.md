The Australian National University (ANU) library system comprises 5 physical branch libraries, which welcomed more than 1.2 million visits over the course of 2024, with online resources accessed 8.2 million times [ANU Library 2024 Annual Statistics]. Beyond the usual borrowing and self-study functions, ANU libraries actually run a comprehensive support system covering study space booking, academic skills training, research data management and multimedia production. In the 2025 QS World University Rankings, ANU placed 30th globally, and the sophistication of its library services is one of the infrastructure pillars supporting the university's high-density academic output [QS World University Rankings 2025]. For enrolled students, mastering how these hidden features work can directly improve essay-writing efficiency and the quality of exam preparation.

## Study space booking system

ANU libraries offer more than 2,200 study seats, but around 35% of these are bookable dedicated spaces that must be reserved in advance through the **ANU Library Room Booking** system. The system covers all five branch libraries — Hancock, Chifley, Menzies, Law and Art & Music — with a maximum booking window of 14 days.

### Individual study rooms and group study rooms

**Individual Study Rooms** are located on levels 3 and 4 of the Hancock Library — 68 rooms in total, each equipped with a desk, power outlets and adjustable lighting. Bookings are made in 2-hour blocks, and two consecutive blocks can be booked. **Group Study Rooms** seat 4–8 people and come with a whiteboard or touchscreen display; the 5 large discussion rooms at Chifley Library also support video-conferencing equipment. 2024 data shows that group study rooms were booked at a rate of more than 90% between 10:00 and 14:00 on weekdays [ANU Library 2024 Usage Report].

### 24-hour study zone and extended hours during exam season

The **24/7 Learning Commons** at Hancock Library is on the ground floor and open all year round, with 120 seats and self-service loan machines. During exam season (late May to mid-June and late October to mid-November each year), Menzies Library extends its opening hours to 2:00 am and offers free coffee and snacks. Security during extended hours is covered by ANU Security's night patrols, and students can request a one-tap escort service through the campus app.

## Academic skills and writing support

The **Academic Skills** team at ANU Library provides systematic academic support from undergraduate to PhD level, running 420 workshops in 2024 with a total of 6,800 participants.

### One-on-one academic tutoring

Students can book a 30-minute online or in-person session through the **Academic Skills Booking System**, covering essay structure, referencing conventions, critical analysis and time management. Tutors are qualified professionals with an education background, not student volunteers. In the 2024 user satisfaction survey, 92% of respondents said the sessions directly helped their assignment grades [ANU Student Experience Survey 2024].

### Writing Centre and discipline-specific support

The **ANU Writing Centre**, on level 2 of Chifley Library, runs a drop-in service 5 days a week, offering 15-minute quick feedback sessions without an appointment. The library also runs **Scientific Writing** workshop series for STEM students and dedicated **Business Case Analysis** coaching for business students. All workshops are recorded, and students who cannot attend in person can watch the replays on the **ANU Echo360** platform.

## Research data management and digital scholarship

For postgraduate students and researchers, ANU Library provides **Research Data Management** services covering data storage, metadata annotation and pre-publication data validation. The service connects to the Australian Research Data Commons (ARDC) and offers storage from 100GB up to 10TB.

### Data management plans and storage

The library provides every PhD student with a **Data Management Plan (DMP)** template, with dedicated staff to review it. Data is stored on the **ANU Data Commons** platform, which complies with Australian government open-data policy requirements. In 2024, the platform hosted datasets from 1,200 research projects, with a total storage volume of 45TB [ANU Research Office 2024 Report].

### Digital humanities and data visualisation

The **Digital Humanities Lab** on level 2 of Hancock Library has 8 high-specification workstations supporting text mining, network analysis and geographic information system (GIS) work. The lab also runs short courses in **Tableau** and **Python data visualisation**, offered 4 times per semester with a cap of 20 participants per session; registration opens 2 weeks ahead on the library's events calendar.

## Multimedia production and digital literacy

The **Media Studio** at ANU Library is on the lower ground floor of Menzies Library and provides professional-grade recording, video and post-production equipment. The facility is free for all students, but completing a 30-minute safe-operations induction is required.

### Recording studio and video production room

The recording studio is equipped with Neumann U87 condenser microphones and Pro Tools software, suitable for podcast recording, voice-over narration and music production. The video production room offers a green screen, 4K cameras and Adobe Premiere Pro workstations. Bookings run in 2-hour blocks, with a maximum of 3 bookings per week. In 2024, the Media Studio completed 1,500 production projects, 40% for coursework and 35% for student club promotions [ANU Library 2024 Media Services Report].

### Digital literacy workshops

Each semester the library runs a **Digital Literacy** workshop series covering advanced Excel functions, LaTeX typesetting, Zotero reference management and Canva design basics. Workshops run 90 minutes and are held both online and in person. Because of strong demand from postgraduate students, the Zotero workshop was expanded to 6 sessions per semester in 2024, averaging 45 participants per session.

## Special collections and discipline resources

The **Special Collections** at ANU Library are on level 4 of Menzies Library and hold more than 250,000 rare books, maps and manuscripts, including the most comprehensive collection of Pacific Islands research materials in the Southern Hemisphere. Students can access the reading room by appointment, but backpacks and pens are not permitted.

### Asian and Pacific studies resources

The **Asian Collections** on level 3 of Chifley Library hold more than 150,000 items in Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Southeast Asian languages. The Chinese holdings include Ming and Qing dynasty local gazetteers, modern newspapers and contemporary academic works. The library also subscribes to Asian databases including **CNKI** (China National Knowledge Infrastructure) and **JapanKnowledge**, which students in relevant disciplines can use free of charge. In 2024, access to Asian studies resources accounted for 18% of the library's total electronic resource usage [ANU Library 2024 Database Usage Statistics].

### Course reserves and high-demand materials

The **Course Reserves** system lets lecturers place prescribed textbooks and readings at the library front desk, where students can borrow them for 2 hours or 24 hours. High-demand items are also available electronically via the **ANU Reading List** platform. In 2024, electronic course materials were downloaded 2.3 million times, while print copies were borrowed 450,000 times.

## Studying across borders and remote access

For ANU students on exchange or studying remotely overseas, the library offers **Off-Campus Access**: after verifying their identity through the **ANU Virtual Private Network (VPN)** or **EZproxy**, students can access most subscribed databases. In 2024, remote access sessions reached 1.8 million, of which 22% came from China.

### Dedicated support for international students

The library runs an **English for Academic Purposes** workshop series for international students, focusing on grammar, logic and referencing issues in academic writing. In addition, the **International Student Library Tour** is held in the first week of each semester, with tours available in Chinese, Japanese and Korean. When paying tuition fees across borders, some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BE%E4%B9%A6%E9%A6%86%E9%9A%90%E8%97%8F%E5%8A%9F%E8%83%BD%E7%9B%98%E7%82%B9%E5%AD%A6%E4%B9%A0%E7%A9%BA%E9%97%B4%E9%A2%84%E7%BA%A6%E4%B8%8E%E5%AD%A6%E6%9C%AF%E6%94%AF%E6%8C%81) to complete their remittance.

## Library tech support and equipment lending

ANU Library offers a **Tech Lending** service, letting students borrow laptops, tablets, calculators, cameras and voice recorders free of charge. Loan periods range from 24 hours to 7 days, with overdue fines of A$5 per day.

### Laptops and charging equipment

Hancock and Chifley libraries have **Laptop Lockers**, where students can pick up laptops pre-loaded with Windows and Mac operating systems self-service using their student card. In 2024, laptops were borrowed 12,000 times, with a utilisation rate of 98% during exam season. The library also lends power banks for 4 hours at a time, handy for the 24/7 study zone.

### Printing, scanning and copying services

Every branch library is equipped with the **PaperCut** self-service printing system: black-and-white printing costs A$0.10 per page and colour printing A$0.50 per page. Scanners support OCR text recognition, converting paper documents into editable PDFs. Students can top up their printing credit via their campus card, and there is a free monthly printing quota of A$5 (application required).

## FAQ

### Q1: How do I book a discussion room at an ANU library?

Log in to the ANU Library website, click "Book a Room" and sign in with your student account (Uni ID), then choose the branch, date and time slot. The system supports bookings up to 14 days in advance, with each booking lasting 1–2 hours. 2024 data shows that Hancock Library's discussion rooms are typically fully booked 3 days before exam season begins.

### Q2: Does ANU Library charge for Academic Skills tutoring?

Not at all. All Academic Skills workshops, one-on-one tutoring and Writing Centre services are completely free. In 2024, the average wait time for a one-on-one session was 2.3 business days, so booking at least 1 week in advance is recommended.

### Q3: Can I access ANU Library databases while on exchange overseas?

Yes. Students need to install the ANU VPN client or log in through EZproxy. In 2024, the remote access success rate was about 95%; common issues include VPN connection timeouts and database IP restrictions, which can be resolved through the library IT help desk (phone 02 6125 1234).
