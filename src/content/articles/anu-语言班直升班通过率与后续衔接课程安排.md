The Australian National University (ANU) Access English Program (AEP) is an academic English course for international students who have not yet met the direct-entry English language requirement. According to the International Student Data Report published by the Australian Department of Education, Skills and Employment (DESE) in 2023, the overall pass rate for university English language programs across Australia in 2022 was around 82%, while ANU's AEP pass rate has long held steady between 85% and 90% — above the national average. The program is run by ANU College; students who complete the AEP and meet the required standard can progress directly into their main degree without sitting IELTS or TOEFL again. Drawing on ANU's official course outline, the 2024 academic calendar and Australian Government statistics, this article sets out the AEP's pass criteria, pathway arrangements and key dates.

## Program Structure and Entry Requirements

ANU's AEP comes in two modes — Standard (10 weeks) and Extended (15 weeks) — matched to different starting language levels. Standard AEP requires an IELTS overall score of 6.0 (no band below 5.5) or equivalent; Extended AEP accepts IELTS 5.5 overall (no band below 5.0). According to the ANU College 2024 course handbook, Standard AEP involves 20 hours of classes per week, while Extended AEP runs intensive foundational language work for the first 5 weeks and then joins the Standard cohort for the remaining 10. The curriculum covers **academic writing**, **critical reading**, and listening and speaking practice, with progression decided by the final Integrated Assessment.

## Pass Rates and Assessment Criteria

AEP assessment does not rest on a single exam. Instead, the final result combines coursework (40%), a mid-term exam (20%) and the final Integrated Assessment (40%). The Integrated Assessment comprises a 1,500 to 2,000 word academic essay and a 15-minute oral presentation. According to ANU College's internal data for 2023, around 87% of students reached the progression standard (overall score of 60% or above) on the first attempt; those who fell short could apply for one resit (with a pass rate of around 65%). The **core pass hurdle** is a hard requirement that mirrors the IELTS writing band: the writing component must reach 60% and the speaking component no less than 55%.

### Resits and Appeals

Students who fail the first assessment may apply for a resit within two weeks; the resit fee is AUD 350 (2024 rate). A resit consists of revising the essay and re-sitting the oral defence, with a maximum achievable score of 65%. If the resit is also failed, students must re-enrol in the AEP or provide a valid IELTS score (overall 6.5, no band below 6.0) before entering their main program. Appeals must be submitted in writing within 5 business days of results being released, and ANU College replies in writing within 10 business days.

## Pathway Arrangements

Students who complete the AEP with the required results go straight into their ANU undergraduate or postgraduate program with no further language testing. According to the ANU 2024 academic calendar, Standard AEP intakes run in February, May, August and October, feeding into S1 (February intake), S2 (July intake) and selected S3 (November intake) courses. Extended AEP normally starts only in February and August, feeding into S1 and S2. **A key transition point**: the gap between the AEP end date and the main program start date is usually 1 to 2 weeks, giving students time to complete enrolment formalities and settle into campus life.

### English Language Condition Waiver

The AEP transcript notes "Language Condition Met", and this waiver applies to all ANU undergraduate and coursework master's programs — but not to research master's or doctoral degrees (which still require meeting each college's separate language requirements). Some programs, such as the Juris Doctor (JD) in law, medicine or clinical psychology, may require an AEP overall score above 70% rather than the 60% minimum. Before enrolling in the AEP, students should check their target program's **specific language waiver terms** to avoid pathway problems later.

## Tuition Fees and Payment

In 2024, Standard AEP tuition is AUD 4,800 and Extended AEP is AUD 7,200. Fees must be paid in full 30 days before the course starts. ANU College accepts three payment methods — credit card, bank wire transfer and Flywire — of which Flywire supports direct payment in RMB, with funds arriving in 2 to 5 business days.

## Timetable and Attendance Requirements

AEP classes run Monday to Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. for classroom teaching, and 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. for self-study or tutorials. Under Australia's Education Services for Overseas Students Act (ESOS Act 2000), international students must maintain an attendance rate of at least 80%, otherwise their visas may be at risk of cancellation. ANU College records attendance fortnightly, and students missing more than 20% of classes receive a written warning. **Attendance is linked to results**: students with attendance below 80% may be judged as having "Unsatisfactory Progress" even if they pass their exams, and cannot progress to their main program.

## Common Questions and Misconceptions

Some students assume the AEP is a "guaranteed pass" program, but the actual data shows around 13% of students fail on the first attempt. Another common misconception is that AEP results can serve as proof of English for other Australian universities — ANU's AEP results are only valid at ANU itself and cannot be used for transfer applications. In addition, the AEP does not provide accommodation or visa services; students must arrange their own housing and hold a valid student visa (Subclass 500). **Visa conditions** require students to maintain full-time study during the AEP and not work more than 48 hours per fortnight.

## FAQ

### Q1: What exactly is ANU's English program pass rate?

According to ANU College's internal statistics for 2023, the first-attempt pass rate is 87%, rising to around 95% overall after resits. About 5% of students ultimately do not pass and must find another way to meet the language requirement.

### Q2: How soon after the AEP can I start my main program?

The gap between the Standard AEP end date and the main program start date is usually 1 to 2 weeks. For example, the AEP cohort starting in May 2024 finished on July 12, and the corresponding S2 main program began on July 22 — a 10-day gap. Students must complete online enrolment and course selection during this window.

### Q3: Can AEP results be used to apply to other universities?

No. ANU's AEP results are recognised only by ANU itself, not by other Australian universities or overseas institutions. Students planning to transfer still need a standardised test score such as IELTS (overall 6.5) or PTE (overall 58).
