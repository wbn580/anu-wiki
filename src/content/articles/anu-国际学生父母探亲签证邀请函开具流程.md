ANU international students applying for an Australian visitor visa (Subclass 600) for their parents or relatives must request a formal Letter of Invitation from the University. Per the Department of Home Affairs' 2024 Visitor Visa Application Guidelines, the letter, while not mandatory, effectively proves the applicant's family relationship and visit purpose, improving processing efficiency. ANU International Student Services 2023 data shows over 400 students apply for this letter each academic year, mainly to support parents' visa applications. This article covers the full application process and key considerations.

## Eligibility and Prerequisites

**Applying for an invitation letter** requires meeting three conditions. First, the applicant must be a currently enrolled ANU international student holding a valid student visa (Subclass 500) with an "Active" CoE (Confirmation of Enrollment). Second, the invitee is limited to the student's **parents, legal guardians or spouse**; siblings, grandparents and other collateral relatives are excluded, per the Department of Home Affairs' 2024 "family sponsor" definition. Third, the student must be enrolled for the current semester in ISIS with no outstanding tuition fees or academic penalty records.

### Student Visa Validity Requirements

The **student visa validity** must cover the invitee's planned travel period; if fewer than 6 months remain, the University may require proof of renewal or an updated CoE. Clause 8202 of the 2023 Student Visa Conditions requires students to maintain enrolment, otherwise sponsorship eligibility may lapse.

### Invitee Identity Restrictions

The invitee must hold a valid Chinese passport with **validity of at least 6 months** beyond the planned departure date. If the invitee has previously been refused an Australian visa or had a visa cancelled for overstaying or breaching conditions, ANU International Student Services may refuse to issue the letter. Such issues must first be resolved through a migration agent or legal adviser.

## Document Checklist

Students need the following documents before applying: a scan of the student's **passport bio-data page** (PDF, no larger than 2MB); a scan of the invitee's passport bio-data page; family relationship documents, such as a **birth certificate** or household register (hukou) pages showing the student-parent relationship (English translation by a NAATI-accredited translator required); and the current semester's **Enrolment Confirmation** from ISIS.

### Family Relationship Documentation Requirements

Chinese birth certificates must be notarised and undergo consular authentication by the **Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs** or an authorised agency. The 2024 Document Authentication Guidelines note that unauthenticated Chinese documents may not be accepted. Allow 2-3 months for this process to avoid delaying the visa application.

### Translation Requirements

All non-English documents must be translated by a **NAATI-accredited translator**, with signature, NAATI number and translation date attached. Self-translations and uncertified translations are not accepted; the translation must be merged with the original into one PDF for upload.

## Online Submission Process

Applications are submitted via the "Document Request" function in the **ISIS system**. After logging in, select "Student Services" > "Letter of Invitation", enter the invitee's name, passport number, date of birth, relationship to the student, planned entry and departure dates (to the exact day) and the purpose of the visit (e.g. "tourism" or "family visit"). The system generates an application number; all documents must be uploaded within 24 hours.

### Review Timeframe and Outcome Notification

ANU International Student Services completes the review within **5 working days**; the approved letter is sent in PDF to the student's ANU email (ending in @anu.edu.au). If documents are incomplete, a request for additional documents arrives within 3 working days and the review period restarts. Under the 2024 Student Service Standards, urgent applications (e.g. flights booked within 14 days) can be expedited by phone or in person at the Student Centre, with review in 2 working days.

### What the Invitation Letter Contains

The letter includes the student's full name, student visa number (Visa Grant Number), CoE number, the invitee's full name and passport number, a **statement of relationship** (e.g. "the student is the son/daughter of the invitee"), the visit start and end dates, and the inviter's contact details (phone and ANU address). It bears the ANU crest and the seal of International Student Services, and takes effect once signed by the student.

## Common Refusal Reasons and Countermeasures

The 2023 Visitor Visa Refusal Analysis Report lists the top three refusal reasons for Chinese applicants: **insufficient financial evidence** (42%), **weak ties to the home country** (31%) and **unclear purpose of visit** (18%). An ANU letter corroborates the visit purpose but cannot replace financial evidence or home-ties materials.

### Supplementary Financial Evidence

The invitee should provide a bank deposit certificate of at least **50,000 RMB** (frozen until 1 month after planned departure), or the student's Australian bank statements for the last 3 months (balance of at least AUD 3,000). ANU International Student Services also recommends the student's own **tuition payment receipts** as supplementary evidence of financial capacity.

### Evidence of Ties to the Home Country

The invitee should provide a **certificate of employment** (Chinese plus English translation, stating position, salary and approved leave) and copies of property or vehicle registration certificates; retirees should provide their retirement certificate and pension statements. Submitting these with the ANU letter reduces refusal risk. For cross-border tuition payments, some families use professional channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E7%88%B6%E6%AF%8D%E6%8E%A2%E4%BA%B2%E7%AD%BE%E8%AF%81%E9%82%80%E8%AF%B7%E5%87%BD%E5%BC%80%E5%85%B7%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B) to complete the currency conversion, and transaction records can serve as supplementary evidence of fund flows.

## Validity Period and Re-application Limits

ANU-issued **invitation letters are valid for 3 months** from the date of issue; if the invitee does not lodge the visa application within that period, a new letter is required. Each letter covers a **single visit** only (Subclass 600 is often single- or multiple-entry, but the letter supports a single-visit purpose).

### Re-applying After a Visa Refusal

If the invitee's visa is refused, the student must reapply with the Refusal Letter and supplementary materials. Under the 2023 policy update, the same invitee may apply at most 2 times within 12 months; beyond that, a written explanation letter describing changes in visit purpose or materials is required.

## Fees and Service Channels

ANU International Student Services **issues invitation letters free of charge**, with no administrative fees; students bear the costs of notarisation, translation and postage (for paper copies). Under the 2024 Student Services Fee Schedule, postage is AUD 15 (within Australia) or AUD 30 (international express).

### Contact Details and Office Hours

Students can enquire Monday to Friday (9:00-17:00 Australian Eastern Time) via: ANU International Student Services (Building 12, Room 1210); phone +61 2 6125 7258; email international@anu.edu.au. For emergencies, call the ANU 24-hour security hotline: +61 2 6125 2249.

## FAQ

### Q1: Must the invitation letter be applied for by the student personally? Can someone else apply on their behalf?

The student must apply personally through ISIS; ANU does not accept third-party applications (including education agents). If a student cannot log in (e.g. a locked account), they must visit International Student Services in person with student ID and passport to reset the password. Under the 2023 Student Account Management Policy, unlocking takes 1 working day.

### Q2: How long does the invitee's visa application take after the letter is submitted?

For Chinese passport holders lodging Subclass 600 online, the median processing time is **14 days** (January-June 2024 data); about 75% of applications are decided within 21 days and 90% within 30 days. Allow at least **2 months** for the full cycle: invitation letter (5 working days), document preparation (1-2 weeks) and visa processing (2-4 weeks).

### Q3: If my parents plan to stay in Australia for more than 3 months, must the invitation letter state this?

Yes. The "planned departure date" must reflect the actual departure date. A visitor visa (Subclass 600) allows a maximum single stay of **12 months**, but stays over 3 months usually require additional health cover. Clause 8101 of the 2024 Visitor Visa Conditions requires medical insurance (OVHC) covering the full stay beyond 3 months. ANU recommends noting "parents plan to stay XX months" on the letter so the visa officer can assess the application.
