The Australian National University (ANU) Statistical Data Analysis Certification (SAS Certification) is a professional qualification run by the ANU statistics school in partnership with SAS Institute. According to figures published by the ANU statistics school in 2024, more than 420 students have enrolled in the program since it launched in 2020, and about 78% of candidates pass the foundational certification (SAS Certified Specialist: Statistics & Machine Learning) on their first attempt. The Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) lists it as a preferred qualification in its 2023 recruitment standards for data analyst roles [ABS, 2023, *Data Analyst Job Classification Standards*]. With demand for data science talent in Australia growing by about 15% year-on-year in 2024 (according to Jobs and Skills Australia), this certification has direct value for students aiming at careers in government statistics, financial analysis or medical research.

## Statistical Methods Course Pathway

ANU offers a suite of **statistical methods courses** closely aligned with the SAS certification exam syllabus, covering the full chain of knowledge from basic programming to advanced modelling. Designed by the statistics school (RSFAS), the courses sit at three levels: introductory (STAT3011/STAT3012), intermediate (STAT4038/STAT4039) and advanced (STAT7040/STAT7041). Every course includes a SAS programming practice component, and students must complete at least 6 project assignments based on real data.

### STAT3011: SAS Programming and Data Analysis Fundamentals

This course is a compulsory prerequisite for the SAS certification, with 3 hours of lectures and 2 hours of lab work per week. Content covers the **DATA step**, the **PROC step** and basic statistical analysis (descriptive statistics, t-tests, analysis of variance). According to the ANU 2024 course outline, students complete a comprehensive project cleaning and performing initial analysis on the 2021 Australian census data in SAS. The course pass rate is about 85%, while the SAS certification exam pass rate is slightly lower, at about 72%.

### STAT4038: Statistical Machine Learning

An intermediate course, STAT4038 focuses on **supervised and unsupervised learning** in SAS, covering linear regression, logistic regression, decision trees, random forests and cluster analysis. It runs on the SAS Viya platform, and students submit a modelling report based on a dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository at the end of semester. An internal ANU statistics school review from 2023 found that students who took the course scored about 14 percentage points higher on average in the advanced module of the SAS certification exam than students who did not.

## Practice Dataset Resources

ANU provides SAS certification candidates with practice datasets at several levels, covering both structured and unstructured data. The datasets come mainly from three sources: the ANU research data repository (RDS), the Australian government's open data platform (data.gov.au), and the official teaching datasets supplied by SAS Institute.

### On-Campus Database Access

Through the library's **ANU Data Commons** platform, ANU students can access more than 200 public datasets spanning economics, public health, ecology and engineering. For example, the **Household, Income and Labour Dynamics in Australia (HILDA)** dataset (2023 updated release) contains longitudinal data on more than 17,000 households, making it well suited to practising mixed-effects models and time-series analysis. Students log in with their student ID to download files in SAS format (.sas7bdat).

### Third-Party Dataset Integration

Some courses require higher-level practice on **Kaggle competition datasets**. In 2024, the ANU statistics school recommended three specific datasets: Titanic survival prediction (for logistic regression), Boston housing prices (for multiple linear regression — note this dataset has been removed from UCI but remains available on the ANU mirror), and credit card fraud detection (for imbalanced classification). ANU staff have pre-processed these datasets into SAS-compatible formats and provide standard scoring scripts alongside them.

## Exam Fee Subsidy Scheme

ANU offers **SAS certification exam fee subsidies** to current students to lower the financial barrier. The subsidy is funded jointly by the statistics school and the ANU Students' Association (ANUSA), with a total annual budget of about 25,000 AUD.

### Subsidy Levels and Eligibility

Subsidies come in two tiers: **full subsidy** (covering 100% of the exam fee) and **partial subsidy** (covering 50%). Full subsidies go to students whose family annual income is below 60,000 AUD, with proof via a Centrelink income statement or a Notice of Assessment. Partial subsidies are open to all full-time students who have a GPA of at least 5.0 (out of 7.0) and have completed at least one SAS-related course. In 2024, the SAS certification base exam fee is 250 AUD (tax included) and the advanced exam fee is 350 AUD.

### Application Process and Deadlines

Students apply through the **scholarships and subsidies application system** on the ANU statistics school website, attaching their academic transcript and SAS exam registration confirmation. Application windows open twice a year: 1 March to 15 April (for the June exam) and 1 August to 15 September (for the November exam). 2023 data shows that about 65% of applicants received a subsidy, with an average approval time of 14 working days for full subsidies.

For cross-border tuition payments, some international students' families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payment](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-sas-certification-exam-preparation-statistical-methods-courses-practice-data) to settle their foreign exchange.

## Exam Registration and Preparation Timeline

SAS certification exams are administered worldwide by Pearson VUE, and ANU students can sit the computer-based test at the Pearson professional test centre on the Acton campus in Canberra. Exams can be booked year-round, but the ANU statistics school recommends following a **within-semester preparation timeline**: register in March, sit the base exam in May, and take the advanced exam in August.

### Exam Format and Scoring

The base exam (A00-240) consists of 60 multiple-choice questions, lasts 90 minutes, and requires a passing score of 70%. The advanced exam (A00-250) has 45 multiple-choice questions and 3 programming questions, lasts 105 minutes, and requires a passing score of 65%. Internal ANU statistics school statistics for 2024 show that students spend on average 80 hours preparing for the base exam and 120 hours for the advanced exam.

### Free Mock Test Resources

ANU offers **two free mock tests**, held in weeks 5 and 10 of semester respectively. Authorised by SAS Institute, the mocks use adapted versions of past exam questions and generate a detailed error analysis report after scoring. Students can book a place through the ANU **Wattle learning management system**.

## Alumni Employment and the Value of Certification

The SAS certification carries a clear **salary premium** in the Australian job market. According to the Australian Data Science Association (ADSA) 2024 salary survey, the median annual salary of SAS-certified data analysts is 95,000 AUD, about 12% higher than the 85,000 AUD median for those without certification [ADSA, 2024, *Data Science Salary Survey*].

### Employer Recognition

The Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA) and Queensland Health all explicitly listed the SAS certification as "preferred" or a "plus" in their 2023-2024 job advertisements. The ANU Careers service's 2023 graduate destination survey found that 91% of graduates who completed the SAS certification found full-time work within 6 months of graduating, compared with 78% of those without it.

## FAQs and Preparation Resources

Students can visit the ANU statistics school's **SAS certification page** for the latest exam syllabus, historical pass rates and tutor consultation schedules. ANU libraries also subscribe to the official SAS textbook in electronic form (*SAS Certification Prep Guide*), which students can borrow free of charge.

### Study Groups and Tutoring

The ANU statistics school runs a **weekly exam preparation study group** led by PhD student tutors. In semester 1, 2024, 47 students took part, and 82% of them passed the base exam. Sessions are held in the statistics school labs in the H.C. Coombs Building.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can I sit the SAS certification exam on campus at ANU?
Yes. The ANU Acton campus has a Pearson VUE authorised test centre, located at Building 42A (H.C. Coombs Building). Bookings must be made on the Pearson VUE website at least 14 days in advance. The 2024 seat fee is 0 AUD (included in the exam fee), but cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the exam, otherwise 50 AUD is deducted.

### Q2: If I fail the exam on my first attempt, can I retake it for free?
No. SAS Institute does not offer free retakes. However, the ANU statistics school offers one **discounted retake voucher** at 50% of the regular price (125 AUD). You must apply within 30 days of your first exam attempt and provide the failing score report. 2023 data shows a retake pass rate of about 68%.

### Q3: Can I appeal if my subsidy application is rejected?
Yes. You can submit a written appeal to the statistics school's academic committee within 14 working days of receiving the rejection notice, attaching supporting materials such as proof of family income or a supervisor's reference letter. In 2023, the appeal success rate was 31%, with an average processing time of 21 working days.
