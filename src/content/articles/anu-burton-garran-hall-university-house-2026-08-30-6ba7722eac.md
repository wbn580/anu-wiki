## Overview of ANU Housing Options

ANU offers three main types of accommodation: university dormitories (such as Burton & Garran Hall), college residences (such as University House), and off-campus rentals. Each option varies in price, location, facilities, social atmosphere, and application difficulty. This guide compares these aspects to help applicants and new students choose based on their budget and needs.

## University Dormitories (e.g., Burton & Garran Hall)

University dormitories are typically located on campus, providing easy access to classes and campus facilities. They offer furnished rooms, shared common areas, and meal plans. The social atmosphere is vibrant, with many opportunities to meet other students through organized events. Application is usually straightforward, with a single application form and a deposit required to secure a place. Contracts are typically for the academic year, with options to extend.

## College Residences (e.g., University House)

College residences like University House offer a more independent living experience while still being on campus. They provide self-contained apartments or studios with kitchenettes, and some include meal options. The social atmosphere is more relaxed, with common lounges and occasional events. Application may require a separate process, and deposits are higher. Contracts are often for 12 months, with flexible start dates.

## Off-Campus Rentals

Off-campus rentals include private apartments, houses, or shared accommodations in the surrounding suburbs. Prices vary widely depending on location and property type. They offer greater independence and flexibility, but require managing utilities, internet, and other bills. The social atmosphere depends on the housemates and neighborhood. Application involves searching listings, attending inspections, and submitting rental applications with references. Deposits are typically four weeks' rent, and lease terms are usually 6 or 12 months.

## Application Timeline and Policies

For on-campus options, applications typically open several months before the academic year. A deposit is required to confirm a place, and contracts are signed electronically. For off-campus rentals, start searching at least 2-3 months before moving, as good properties are snapped up quickly. Always inspect the property before signing, and be aware of common rental traps such as hidden fees, unclear lease terms, and unverified landlords.

## Making Your Choice

Consider your budget, desired level of independence, and social preferences. If you want a built-in community and easy campus access, dorms are a good fit. If you prefer more privacy but still want to be on campus, college residences offer a balance. If you value independence and are willing to manage your own affairs, off-campus rentals provide flexibility. Weigh the pros and cons based on your personal needs and priorities.
