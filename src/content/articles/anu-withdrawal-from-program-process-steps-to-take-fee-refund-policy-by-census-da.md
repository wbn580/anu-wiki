Since January 2025, the Australian Department of Home Affairs has amended international student visa condition 8202, explicitly stating that students who terminate their studies before completing their enrolled course (i.e., withdraw) may face automatic visa cancellation. According to the Department of Education's 2024 International Student Data report, 1,247 international students at the Australian National University (ANU) submitted withdrawal applications in the 2023 academic year, and about 32% of those applications were lodged after the Census Date, leaving students unable to obtain a full tuition refund. ANU's withdrawal process spans three core dimensions: academic enrolment changes, tuition fee settlement, and immigration compliance obligations — a delay in any one of these can produce irreversible consequences. Drawing on ANU's official policies and Australian migration regulations, this article walks through the withdrawal steps, the refund differences before and after the Census Date, and the potential impact on your student visa.

## The Formal Steps and Key Dates for a Withdrawal Application

ANU's withdrawal process begins when a student submits a **Withdrawal from Program** application through ISIS (Interactive Student Information System). According to ANU's 2025 Academic Policies and Procedures handbook, students must confirm whether they have completed course enrolment for the current semester before applying; outstanding course assessments may delay the processing of a withdrawal application.

### Official Channels for Submitting an Application

Students must log in to ISIS and select "Withdraw from Program" under the "My Program" section. The system asks for a withdrawal reason (such as personal health, financial hardship, or transferring to another institution) and requires supporting documents such as medical certificates or offer letters. ANU Student Central carries out an initial review within 5 business days of receiving the application; if the materials are incomplete, the student is notified to supply more. **The submission timestamp in the ISIS system** is the key evidence for determining refund eligibility, and the deadline on the Census Date itself is 23:59 Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST).

### Key Time Windows

The effect of a withdrawal depends on how the submission date relates to the Census Date. ANU's Census Date for Semester 1 2025 was 31 March 2025. If you submit before this date, you not only owe no tuition for that semester but also receive a 100% refund of any tuition already paid. If you submit after the Census Date, the **tuition debt** takes effect immediately: you are liable for the full semester fee, and no later withdrawal action can reverse that debt. Under ANU's financial regulations, a withdrawal application lodged after the Census Date generates no refund at all, regardless of whether you actually attended classes.

## The Tiered Structure of the Tuition Refund Policy

ANU's tuition refund policy falls into three clear tiers based on how the withdrawal timing relates to the Census Date. The policy applies to all international and domestic students, although government-sponsored students may be subject to different repayment mechanisms.

### Full Refund Before the Census Date

Students who withdraw before the Census Date receive a refund of all tuition paid for that semester, minus a non-refundable application fee (about AUD 100). Refunds are usually returned via the original payment method, with a processing period of 15 to 20 business days. According to ANU's 2024 Tuition Fee Policy, if you paid by credit card, a 1.5% fee may be deducted from the refund. **Full-refund** eligibility does not require you to state a reason — you simply need to complete the ISIS submission before the deadline.

### No Refund After the Census Date

Once the Census Date has passed, ANU offers no tuition refund or credit of any kind. This policy is clearly set out in ANU's 2025 Academic Calendar, and no exceptions are made for special circumstances such as sudden illness or family emergencies. If you need to withdraw for serious medical reasons, you can submit a **Special Consideration** application to ANU Student Central, but a refund is granted only as an exceptional approval, and you must provide documentation from a tertiary-level medical institution. According to ANU's 2023-2024 internal data, the approval rate for such exemption applications is about 18%, with an average processing time of 28 days.

### Fee Calculation for Withdrawing Across Semesters

For students enrolled across multiple semesters, a withdrawal affects only the current and future semesters' enrolments. If you withdraw after the Census Date, tuition for completed past semesters is not refunded, but future semester enrolments are cancelled at no additional cost. For example, if a student withdraws after the Semester 2 Census Date, Semester 1 tuition is non-refundable, but any pre-enrolment fees for Semester 3 are automatically revoked. The **automatic waiver of future-semester fees** is the one provision in ANU's withdrawal policy that works in favour of late withdrawers.

## The Legal Impact on Your Visa Status

After an international student withdraws, their compliance under the student visa (Subclass 500) immediately comes under scrutiny under Australian migration law. Under condition 8202 of the Migration Regulations 2018, students must remain enrolled in a CRICOS-registered course, and withdrawing constitutes a breach of that condition.

### How Visa Cancellation Is Triggered

Within 14 business days of the student completing the withdrawal, ANU must report the change of status to the Department of Home Affairs through **PRISMS (Provider Registration and International Student Management System)**. The report shows the student as "ceased enrollment". Once the PRISMS record is updated, the Department may automatically trigger visa cancellation proceedings. According to the Department of Home Affairs' 2024 Student Visa Compliance Guidelines, the student receives a Notice of Intention to Consider Cancellation (NOICC) within 28 days of the report being submitted, and has 14 days to respond. The **consequences of visa cancellation** include forced departure and a three-year ban on applying for most Australian visas.

### Visa Alternatives After Withdrawal

If you plan to stay in Australia after withdrawing, you must obtain a new valid visa before the PRISMS report is submitted. Common paths include applying for a CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment) from another education provider while keeping your current visa valid, or switching to a Visitor visa (Subclass 600) for a short stay. Under the Department of Home Affairs' 2025 policy, if you lodge a new visa application within 28 days of withdrawing and provide a reasonable explanation for the transition, the Department may defer cancelling your original visa. **The CoE transfer** must be completed before you withdraw, to avoid a visa gap.

### The Consequences of an Unreported Withdrawal

If a student simply stops attending classes without going through ANU's official withdrawal channels, ANU will still report the student as not enrolled to PRISMS after the **Census Date**. This kind of "silent withdrawal" also triggers visa cancellation proceedings, and the student receives no tuition refund at all. According to Department of Home Affairs 2023 data, visa cancellations caused by silent withdrawal account for 41% of all international student visa cancellations, with the average processing time shortened to 21 days. Students should always submit a formal application through ISIS rather than merely informing a supervisor or dropping classes.

## The Impact of Withdrawal on Your Academic Record

Withdrawing leaves a permanent record on your ANU academic transcript. According to ANU's 2025 Academic Records Policy, how the withdrawal is recorded depends on when it was submitted.

### Transcript Notation Before the Census Date

Courses withdrawn before the Census Date appear on the transcript as "WD" (Withdrawn). This notation is not counted in your GPA and does not affect your academic average. **A WD record** generally does not hurt your chances when applying for postgraduate study or transferring later, although some competitive programs (such as medicine or law) may ask you to explain the withdrawal. According to ANU Admissions Office 2024 guidance, WD records do not appear in the GPA calculation section of the official transcript.

### Transcript Notation After the Census Date

If you withdraw after the Census Date, the course appears on your transcript as "WN" (Withdrawn with Fail), which is treated like a fail and calculated into your GPA as 0 points. **A WN record** stays on your transcript permanently and cannot be overwritten by later course grades. Under a 2023 ruling by the ANU Academic Board, a WN record is not removed even if you later re-enrol at the university. If you plan to transfer to another Australian university, a WN record may be taken into account in the target institution's admission review, especially for programs with limited places.

## Financial and Accommodation Arrangements After Withdrawal

Withdrawing affects more than tuition — it also touches accommodation fees you have already paid, your health cover (OSHC), and scholarship eligibility. ANU's accommodation services and finance office each handle post-withdrawal matters independently.

### Ending Your Accommodation Contract

Contracts for ANU on-campus accommodation (such as Bruce Hall, Lodge, etc.) are usually tied to your semester enrolment. When you withdraw, the accommodation contract automatically lapses, but you must give the Accommodation Office 28 days' written notice. Under ANU Accommodation Services' 2025 terms, if you withdraw after the Census Date, accommodation fees are subject to a penalty equal to 50% of the contract's remaining term. **The accommodation penalty** is calculated as: remaining weeks × weekly rent × 0.5. For example, if 10 weeks remain and the weekly rent is AUD 350, the penalty is AUD 1,750.

### OSHC Refunds and Scholarship Termination

International students' OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover) is usually prepaid to the end of the course. After withdrawing, you can apply to your insurer for a refund of the remaining period, but you must provide a withdrawal confirmation letter issued by ANU. According to the Australian Department of Health's 2024 OSHC Refund Guidelines, refunds take 30 to 45 business days to process. In addition, any scholarship ANU has awarded you (such as the ANU Chancellor's International Scholarship) terminates immediately upon withdrawal; scholarships already paid out do not need to be returned, but future instalments are cancelled. **Termination notices** are sent by the ANU Scholarships Office within 10 business days of the withdrawal being confirmed.

## Transfer Pathways After Withdrawal

Some students withdraw not to give up on their studies entirely but to move to another institution. ANU's policy supports students in completing transfer formalities before withdrawing, to avoid a visa interruption.

### Release Letter

Under clause 7 of Australia's National Code 2022, international students who have completed the first semester of their course must obtain a release letter from their current provider before transferring to another school. ANU decides on transfer requests within 10 business days of receiving them. If you have studied in your current course for more than 6 months, ANU will normally issue the release letter unconditionally; if you have studied for less than 6 months, you must provide a reasonable reason (such as course mismatch or personal health). **Release letters** are issued free of charge, but you must submit the transfer application through ISIS and attach the offer letter from the target institution.

### The CoE Transfer Window

Once you have the release letter, you must apply for a CoE from the new school within 28 days. If you miss this deadline, ANU will report you as not enrolled to PRISMS, which leads to visa cancellation. According to Department of Home Affairs 2025 data, the average processing time for a CoE transfer is 7 business days, but during peak periods (such as February and July) it can stretch to 14 business days. Plan ahead and avoid submitting transfer applications around the Census Date, when the PRISMS system is under heavy load.

## Mental Health and Support Resources

The decision to withdraw can weigh heavily on students. ANU offers several free support services to help students weigh the consequences before withdrawing.

### Academic Advice and Counselling

ANU Student Central offers one-on-one academic advice before withdrawal, helping students assess the impact on their course progress and visa. In addition, the ANU Counselling Service provides free, confidential psychological support to students facing withdrawal-related stress, with appointments usually available within 3 to 5 business days. **Counselling** appointments can be booked online through the ANU Health & Wellbeing portal — no doctor's referral is needed.

### Financial Assistance Programs

For students considering withdrawal because of financial hardship, ANU runs the **Student Hardship Fund**, which can provide emergency grants of up to AUD 2,000. Applications must include proof of income, bank statements, and a statement of withdrawal risk. According to ANU's 2024 financial report, the fund distributed AUD 487,000 in 2023, with an average grant of AUD 1,250 and a 10-business-day approval cycle. Try applying for this fund before submitting a withdrawal application, to see whether withdrawal can be avoided.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can I still get a partial tuition refund if I withdraw after the Census Date?

No. Under ANU's 2025 Tuition Fee Policy, a withdrawal application lodged after the Census Date generates no tuition refund, whether or not you attended classes or submitted a special circumstances application. The only exception is serious medical reasons approved by ANU Student Central, but the approval rate for such exemptions is just 18%, and tertiary-level medical evidence is required. In the 2023 academic year, ANU received 412 such exemption applications and approved only 74.

### Q2: How soon after withdrawal is a student visa cancelled?

ANU reports the status change to PRISMS within 14 business days of the withdrawal being completed. After receiving the report, the Department of Home Affairs usually issues a Notice of Intention to Consider Cancellation (NOICC) within 28 days. You then have 14 days from receiving the notice to submit an explanation. If you do not respond, the visa is formally cancelled within 35 days. According to Department of Home Affairs 2024 data, the average total time from the withdrawal report to visa cancellation is 63 days.

### Q3: Can I re-apply for a different ANU program after withdrawing?

Yes, but be mindful of the timing. After withdrawing, you must wait at least one semester before re-applying to another ANU program. When you re-apply, any previous WN records (if you withdrew after the Census Date) are included in the admission review. Under ANU Admissions Office 2025 policy, when applicants with WN records apply for competitive programs, the 0-point grade is included in the GPA calculation. In 2024, ANU rejected 31 re-applicants whose WN records pulled their GPA below 4.0.
