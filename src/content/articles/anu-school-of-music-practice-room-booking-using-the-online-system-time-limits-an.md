The School of Music at the Australian National University (ANU), located on the Acton campus in Canberra, has more than 40 practice rooms across Llewellyn Hall and the Peter Karmel Building for students' daily practice and rehearsals. According to ANU's official 2024 *Student Facilities Usage Guidelines*, average daily occupancy of the School's practice rooms exceeds 78% during the semester, and during peak hours (Monday to Friday 16:00–20:00) the average wait for a booking is 23 minutes【ANU, 2024, Student Facilities Usage Guidelines】. Meanwhile, a 2023 cultural facilities audit by the ACT Government found that the ANU School of Music is the largest provider of public music practice space in the Canberra region, holding 34% of all pianos owned by the city's educational institutions【ACT Government, 2023, Cultural Infrastructure Audit Report】. With Semester 1 2025 course enrolment now open, practice room booking rules and piano maintenance terms are among the most frequently asked administrative questions from new and visiting students.

## How the Online Booking System Works

The ANU School of Music uses a web-based booking platform called the "Music Room Booking Portal", which is linked to students' UNIX accounts and university email addresses. All enrolled students can log in with their ANU ID and view real-time availability in the **booking system**.

### Logging In and Identity Verification

Go to the "Facilities" section of the ANU School of Music website and click "Book a Practice Room" to reach the login page. The system uses **ANU single sign-on (SSO)** and, after verifying your identity, shows the booking permissions associated with your degree program. Undergraduate students, postgraduate students and visiting scholars are assigned different priority queues, and PhD candidates and honours students get an additional 15-minute priority booking window each day.

### Choosing a Room and Allocating Time Slots

After logging in, the system shows floor plans for both the Peter Karmel Building (PK) and Llewellyn Hall, marking each practice room's number, floor area (in square metres) and current status (free/occupied/under maintenance). The **minimum booking unit** is 30 minutes, and the maximum continuous booking is 2 hours (120 minutes). Before you submit, the system displays the room's sound insulation rating (Class A/B/C): Class A rooms suit orchestral rehearsals, while Class C rooms are for solo vocal practice only.

### Confirmation and Cancellation Rules

Once a booking is submitted, the system emails a confirmation to your ANU address containing a unique booking reference number. To cancel, you must do so through the Portal **at least 90 minutes** before the booking start time; otherwise it is recorded as a no-show. Under ANU's 2024 *Facilities Usage Regulations*, three consecutive no-shows automatically freeze your booking privileges for 7 days【ANU, 2024, Facilities Usage Regulations】.

## Time Limits and Daily Quotas

The School of Music caps how many hours each student can book per day and per week, to balance demand across the roughly 1,200 music students (including minors) at the university.

### Daily and Weekly Caps

Undergraduates can book a maximum of **3 hours** per day; postgraduates 4 hours. The weekly cumulative caps are 12 hours (undergraduate) and 16 hours (postgraduate). The system resets the quota counter at midnight every Monday. Once you hit your quota, the Portal blocks new bookings until the next cycle begins.

### Priority Rules During Peak Periods

During examination weeks (the last two weeks of June and November each year), the School activates its **peak period management mechanism**. Each student's daily quota is automatically reduced to 2 hours, and all bookings must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance. This rule operates under ANU's 2023 *Academic Calendar and Facility Scheduling Agreement* and is designed to protect venue access for graduation performances and end-of-semester technical rehearsals【ANU, 2023, Academic Calendar and Facility Scheduling Agreement】.

### Off-Peak Flexibility

During semester breaks (December to February, and July) and non-teaching weeks, the daily cap is lifted and only the weekly totals of 12 hours (undergraduate) / 16 hours (postgraduate) remain. Off-peak, students may also book up to **3 hours** continuously, provided the booking notes state the purpose of the practice (for example, "repertoire preparation" or "technical practice") so administrators can audit it.

## Piano Maintenance and Usage Rules

The ANU School of Music owns **27 upright pianos** and **9 grand pianos**, distributed across the practice rooms and the main stage of Llewellyn Hall. A registered piano technician (RPT) employed by the School tunes and inspects each instrument every two weeks.

### Tuning Schedule and Emergency Repairs

Under the service contract ANU signed with the Australian Piano Technicians Association (APTA) in 2024, each piano is tuned at intervals of no more than 14 days, to a tuning standard of A4=440Hz±0.5Hz【APTA, 2024, Service Contract ANU-2024-017】. If students notice sticky keys, tuning drift or pedal faults, they can raise a ticket through the "Report Fault" function in the Portal. The School promises an initial diagnosis within **48 hours**, with non-structural faults repaired within 72 hours.

### Prohibited Conduct and Penalties

The School explicitly prohibits placing any objects on pianos (including music stands, water cups and phones), using non-original piano stools, and writing on or sticking labels to the keys. First offences draw a written warning; a second offence **freezes all practice room booking privileges for 30 days**. Across 2023, the School issued 12 infringement notices, 8 of which involved drink containers left on piano lids【ANU, 2024, Discipline Records Summary】.

### Applying to Use the Grand Pianos

The grand pianos (mainly in Llewellyn Hall Rooms 1 and 3 and on the main stage) are reserved for **higher degree students** (masters and PhD) and supervisor-approved graduation performances. Applicants must complete the *Grand Piano Use Application Form*, which is jointly approved by the course coordinator and the School's facilities manager. Approval generally takes 5 working days; once granted, students can book rehearsal sessions of up to 4 hours each.

## Soundproofing and Environmental Controls

A practice room's sound insulation rating directly affects booking availability. The School divides rooms into three classes: Class A (sound insulation ≥50dB, suitable for brass and percussion), Class B (40–49dB, suitable for strings and woodwind) and Class C (30–39dB, suitable for voice and electronic keyboards). The booking interface always shows a room's insulation class, and students must confirm that the room matches their instrument type.

### Temperature and Humidity Monitoring

All practice rooms are fitted with **temperature and humidity sensors** that upload readings to the School's central management system every 15 minutes. The target environment for piano storage is 20–22°C with a relative humidity of 45%–55%. If sensor readings stay outside this range for more than 30 minutes, the system automatically alerts the facilities manager. In July 2024, winter heating in Canberra pushed humidity in 3 Class C rooms down to 38%, and the School temporarily closed those rooms for 48 hours to install humidifiers【ANU, 2024, Environmental Monitoring Log】.

### Noise Complaints and Time Restrictions

Practice sessions after 21:00 may only use Class B and Class C rooms, and doors must be kept closed. **Class A rooms cannot be used after 21:00**, to avoid disturbing performances in the adjacent Llewellyn Hall and nearby residential areas. The School handles about 15–20 noise complaints per semester, roughly 70% of which involve brass instruments used outside designated hours.

## Use by Other Colleges and Visitors

Students outside the School of Music (for example, individual practitioners from other ANU colleges) can apply for temporary access through the "Guest Access" function, but must provide written approval from their supervisor or course coordinator. **The visitor booking cap is 2 hours per week, in Class C rooms only.**

### External Users

External music groups (such as ensembles affiliated with the Canberra Symphony Orchestra) can rent practice rooms at an hourly rate: AUD 15/hour for Class C, AUD 25/hour for Class B and AUD 40/hour for Class A. Rental applications must be submitted through ANU Commercial Services at least 14 working days in advance. In 2023, external rentals accounted for 9% of total booked hours【ANU, 2024, Commercial Facility Usage Report】.

### Special Arrangements for International Students

For international students, the School opens a **priority booking channel** in the two weeks before the semester starts, letting them plan their practice time in advance to adjust to the time-zone shift. This channel serves about 200 new students a year, and its booking success rate is about 22 percentage points higher than the regular channel.

## Infringement Records and the Appeals Process

The School's facilities management team keeps an electronic infringement database covering four categories: no-shows, overstaying booked time, equipment damage and behavioural breaches. Each record stays on file for **12 months** before being automatically cleared.

### How to Appeal

Students who dispute an infringement finding can submit a written appeal to the School of Music's Student Affairs Office within 10 working days of receiving notice. Appeals must attach supporting evidence (such as system screenshots, email correspondence or witness statements). The office issues a decision within 15 working days of receiving the appeal. In 2024, 9 appeals were lodged, 5 of which were partially or fully upheld.

### Escalating Penalties

Once a student accumulates 3 infringement records, the system automatically triggers the penalty ladder: 3rd infringement — booking privileges frozen for 14 days; 4th — 30 days; 5th or more — frozen until the end of semester. During a freeze, students can still use the open practice stations in the School's common areas (no booking required, first come, first served). When paying cross-border tuition, some study-abroad families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-school-of-music-practice-room-booking-using-the-online-system-time-limits-an) to complete their remittances.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can the practice room booking system be used on mobile?

Yes. The ANU Music Room Booking Portal uses responsive web design and can be accessed directly from a mobile browser — no separate app needed. However, the system is not compatible with the WeChat in-app browser; Chrome or Safari is recommended. 2024 statistics show that about 43% of bookings are made on mobile devices【ANU, 2024, Portal Usage Analytics】.

### Q2: Can I get compensation if a piano breaks down during my booked session?

Yes. You must raise a "Report Fault" ticket through the Portal within 30 minutes of the fault occurring and keep your booking reference number. If the technician confirms the fault, the session does not count toward your daily/weekly quota, and the system automatically credits you an equal amount of booking time (capped at 2 hours). In 2024, such compensation was granted 37 times, with an average processing time of 1.8 days.

### Q3: Can students who are not music majors book practice rooms?

Yes, but with limited permissions. Students outside the School of Music must apply through "Guest Access", with a cap of 2 hours per week in Class C rooms only (sound insulation 30–39dB). Applications must include approval from a supervisor or course coordinator and take 2 working days to process. In Semester 1 2024, bookings by non-music students made up 11% of all bookings.
