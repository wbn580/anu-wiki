The ANU Masters Thesis submission process involves three core hard requirements: Formatting Review, Turnitin Similarity Report, and final Binding and Submission. According to the *Thesis Submission Guidelines* published by the ANU HDR Office (2025), theses that fail the formatting review face delayed conferral of the degree, while theses with a Turnitin similarity score above 15% may trigger an academic integrity investigation. In addition, data from the Department of Education (2024) shows that the average ANU postgraduate thesis submission cycle in 2023 was 14.6 weeks, with around 22% of delays directly caused by non-compliant formatting or similarity reports. Based on ANU's official policy documents, this article systematically sets out a complete operational checklist from formatting review to final binding, helping postgraduate students pass every review stage in one go before the deadline.

## Formatting Review Core Standards

ANU's **formatting requirements** for master's theses are set uniformly by the College Academic Committee, though individual colleges may have minor variations. The common standards include: body text in 12pt Times New Roman or Arial, line spacing of 1.5 or double, and margins of no less than 25 mm (1 inch) on all sides. Heading levels must use Arabic numeral numbering (e.g., 1.1, 1.1.1), and the **reference list** must follow APA 7th edition or the citation style specified by the college. According to the *Formatting Review Checklist* from the ANU Research Office (2025), the header should contain the thesis's full title (no more than 50 characters) and the footer should show page numbers (Arabic numerals from the first page of the body, Roman numerals for the front matter). Before submission, students upload a PDF through ANU's HDR Thesis Submission Portal, and the system automatically checks whether the margins and fonts match the template.

### Title Page and Declaration Page Requirements

The title page must include the full thesis title, the author's name, student number, degree name (such as Master of Science), submission date, and college name. The declaration page must include an **Originality Statement** and a Copyright Statement, with format templates downloadable from the ANU Library website. Under ANU postgraduate research policy (2024, Policy 4.2.1), the declaration page must bear the student's handwritten signature, scanned and embedded in the PDF; electronic signatures are not accepted. Theses missing the signature, or with a signature date later than the submission date, are returned for revision.

### Abstract and Keywords Format

The Abstract is limited to 300 to 500 English words, must appear on its own page, and is placed before the table of contents. Below the abstract, **3 to 5 keywords** should be listed, separated by semicolons. The ANU Academic Skills Centre (2024) recommends that keywords cover the thesis's core methodology, research subject, and geographic scope to improve discoverability in the ANU digital thesis repository (ANU Open Research). The abstract page is not counted toward the body word count but must use the same font and line spacing as the body.

## Turnitin Similarity Report Requirements and Submission Process

All ANU master's theses must pass a **similarity check** via Turnitin before formal submission, generating a Similarity Report. Under the rules of the ANU Academic Integrity Office (2025), the overall thesis similarity must not exceed 15%, and the match rate for any single source must not exceed 3%. Theses above these thresholds require the student to submit a written explanation of the reasonableness of the cited sources for review by the college's Academic Integrity Committee. The Turnitin check should be run after the thesis is finalised but before the formatting review, because formatting adjustments (such as headers, footers, and reference lists) can affect the similarity percentage. Students can submit drafts through ANU's Wattle course platform or by logging directly into a standalone Turnitin account, with reports generated within 24 hours of each submission.

### Interpreting the Similarity Report and Revision Strategies

The Turnitin report colour-codes matching text: blue (0-24%), green (25-49%), yellow (50-74%), and red (75-100%). ANU recommends focusing on the **yellow and red zones**, which usually indicate direct copying of whole passages. Common problems include: incorrect citation formatting (such as missing quotation marks), repeated matches in the reference list, and overlap between glossaries and public databases. Revision strategies include rewriting long quotations (quotations over 40 words must be indented), merging multiple short citations, and using the citation management tools Zotero or EndNote offered by the ANU Library to standardise formats. According to case statistics from the ANU Academic Skills Centre (2024), around 68% of similarity exceedances can be resolved simply by fixing the citation format.

### Timing the Turnitin Report Submission

A Turnitin report is valid for 30 days; beyond that period it must be regenerated. Students should complete the Turnitin check **at least 14 days before the final submission date** to leave time for revisions. The ANU HDR Office (2025) recommends uploading the Turnitin report (PDF format) as an attachment to the HDR portal at the same time as submitting the formatting review. If the report shows similarity above 15%, the system automatically blocks the formatting review process until the student submits a revised version. ## Binding Requirements and Final Submission

After passing the formatting review and Turnitin check, ANU requires master's theses to be submitted as **one hardbound copy** to the college office, plus one electronic copy (PDF) to the ANU Library digital collection. The hardbound copy must have a black or dark blue hard cover, with the full thesis title, author's name, and degree name hot-foil stamped on the front. The binding specification is A4 size (210mm x 297mm), with the spine thickness determined by the number of pages, typically 3 to 15 mm. According to the ANU Library (2025) *Thesis Binding Guidelines*, students may choose the on-campus print shop (such as ANU Print Services) or an external accredited binder (such as Canberra Bindery); the cost is borne by the student, averaging AUD 45 to 80 (including GST).

### Electronic Submission Format Requirements

The electronic PDF must be completely identical to the printed copy, with a file size no larger than 100 MB. The PDF must include **bookmarks** for navigation to each chapter. Images and tables must be embedded in an editable format (such as vector graphics) with a resolution of no less than 300 DPI. The ANU Digital Library (2025) requires the electronic thesis to carry metadata, including a DOI, a subject classification number (such as the Dewey Decimal Classification), and an ORCID identifier. Students can fill in the metadata form in the submission portal, and the system automatically generates an XML file embedded in the PDF.

### Submission Deadlines and Extension Applications

The final submission deadline for master's theses is usually the last day of the degree-awarding semester (e.g., June 30 for Semester 1 and November 30 for Semester 2). Students who cannot submit on time must lodge an **Extension Request** at least 4 weeks before the deadline, accompanied by a written letter of consent from their supervisor. Under ANU postgraduate research policy (2024, Policy 5.1.3), an extension may not exceed 6 months, and an extension fee applies (AUD 250/month under the 2025 rate). Theses submitted late without an approved extension are automatically deferred to the next semester for degree conferral and may affect visa status (for international students).

## FAQ

### Q1: What should I do if my Turnitin similarity report is above 15%?

If similarity exceeds 15%, the student must submit a written explanation (no more than 500 words) to the college's Academic Integrity Committee within 7 business days, justifying the reasonableness of every high-match source. According to ANU Academic Integrity Office (2025) data, about 72% of such explanations are accepted, provided the matching text comes from standard terminology, reference lists, or direct quotations. At the same time, the thesis must be revised and resubmitted to Turnitin until the similarity drops below 15%.

### Q2: Does the hardbound copy have to be produced by an ANU-designated binder?

No. ANU accepts the services of any accredited binder, but the binding must meet the specifications before submission (hard cover, foil-stamped title, A4 size). The ANU Library (2025) recommends the on-campus print shop (ANU Print Services), whose average binding fee is AUD 55 and which offers a 48-hour rush service. External binders such as Canberra Bindery start at AUD 45, but shipping costs are extra.

### Q3: How long does the formatting review take?

The formatting review typically takes 5 to 10 business days, depending on the college's workload. The ANU HDR Office (2025) recommends submitting the formatting review application at least 3 weeks before the final submission deadline. If the review is not passed, resubmission after revision takes an additional 3 to 5 business days. 2024 data shows that around 84% of theses passed the formatting review on the first submission, with the main issues concentrated in margins and reference list formatting.
