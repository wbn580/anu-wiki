The Australian National University (ANU) runs more than 200 cultural exchange events for international students every academic year, spanning four categories — academic transition, language practice, cross-cultural adjustment and career development — with participants from over 110 countries [ANU Global Programs 2023 Annual Report]. According to Australian Department of Education 2024 data, ANU had 10,421 international students, accounting for 35.8% of all enrolled students, of whom about 52% were from China [Australian Department of Education, 2024, International Student Data]. Faced with such a diverse student body, ANU uses a structured events calendar to shorten the cultural adjustment period — its official data shows that international students who attend 3 or more cultural events have a first-year retention rate 14.7 percentage points higher than those who do not participate [ANU Student Experience Survey 2023]. Using ANU's official events calendar as its framework, this article outlines the types of major cultural exchange programs, their registration windows and the steps to sign up, for the benefit of current and incoming international students.

## Release schedule and channels for the events calendar

ANU schedules international student cultural exchange events around both semesters and holidays, publishing four official calendars a year. The **semester events calendar** is released on the ANU Global Programs website and in the Events section of the ISIS student portal 4 weeks before the start of each semester (mid-January and mid-June); the **holiday events calendar** is announced earlier, mid-semester (weeks 6-8), covering the mid-semester break, the winter break (June-July) and the summer break (November-February). Since 2022, ANU has also synced the events calendar into its mobile app, ANU Mobile (iOS/Android), where students can filter events by date, type or language and receive push reminders 48 hours in advance. According to the ANU digital services team's 2023 statistics, about 73% of international students register for events through ANU Mobile, with the rest using website forms or in-person counters.

### O-Week events

The first week of each semester (O-Week) is the busiest period for events. In Semester 1 2024, for example, O-Week featured 47 events, of which the **International Student Welcome** is a mandatory check-in activity: all international students arriving in Australia for the first time must complete check-in within 7 days before the start of semester. The event runs for 3 hours and covers visa condition briefings, how to use your OSHC insurance, distribution of the campus safety map and help opening a local bank account. Registration is done through the "International Student Check-in" module in ISIS at no extra cost.

### Cultural workshops during the semester

Between weeks 3 and 12, ANU runs 4-6 cultural workshops each week on topics such as **"Decoding Australian Slang"**, **"Cultural Differences in Academic Writing"** and **"Religious Diversity and Dining Etiquette"**. Each workshop lasts 90 minutes, caps at 25 participants and is offered on a first-come, first-served basis. Registration is centralised under the "Workshops" tab in ANU Mobile and opens 72 hours before the workshop starts. ANU Language Centre tracking data from 2023 shows that students who completed all 6 cultural workshops made on average 31% fewer citation-formatting errors in their subsequent academic writing.

## Main event types and content

ANU divides its international student cultural exchange events into five core categories, each targeting a different stage of adjustment and set of skills.

### Language Exchange Program

Run by the ANU School of Literature, Languages and Linguistics, this program matches about 400 language partner pairs each semester. Participating students fill in an online application form stating their native language and target language (the available languages include English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Indonesian and others — 12 in total), and the system pairs partners algorithmically based on language level and schedule. Once matched, both partners must complete at least 8 conversations of 60 minutes each, either in person or online, during the semester. Students who complete all 8 conversations earn the **ANU Co-Curricular Record**, which can be attached to their transcript as evidence of soft skills. The registration window opens in weeks 1-2 of each semester, and applications are submitted through the ANU Language Exchange website.

### Cross-Cultural Buddy Program

Co-hosted by the ANU Students' Association and Global Programs, this program recruits about 200 local student volunteers and 200 international students each semester to form Buddy groups. Each group has 4-6 members, including at least 2 local students. Activities include weekend city tours, family dinner experiences and national park hikes, with all costs (transport and entry tickets) covered by ANU. Registration for the **Buddy Program** runs in two stages: international students first submit a personal interests questionnaire in week 1, then confirm their participation after receiving their group assignment in week 2. Semester 2 2023 data shows that 89% of program participants said it "significantly increased how often they socialised with local students".

### International Food Festival and Cultural Night

In weeks 5 and 10 of each semester, ANU hosts two large events at the Kambri cultural centre: the **International Food Festival** and the **Cultural Night**. Stalls at the food festival are run by student clubs — in 2023 there were 32 stalls covering 28 national cuisines — while the Cultural Night features music performances, traditional dress displays and interactive games. Both events are free to enter, but food festival tickets must be claimed in advance on ANU Mobile (2 tickets per person, first come, first served). In Semester 2 2023, 1,800 food festival tickets were distributed, and all of them were claimed within 47 minutes of registration opening.

## How to register, step by step

Registration for ANU international student cultural exchange events is spread across three main platforms; choose the channel that matches the event type.

### Registering through ISIS

For mandatory activities (such as O-Week check-in and visa compliance seminars) and events that require identity verification. Steps: log in to ISIS → click the "Events" tab → filter by the "International Student" category → select the event and click "Register". The system sends a confirmation email to your ANU email address 24 hours before the event. Note: ISIS registrations cannot be cancelled online once submitted; contact Student Central (phone +61 2 6125 4321) to have it processed manually.

### Registering through the ANU Mobile app

For non-mandatory activities such as workshops, the Buddy Program and language exchange. Steps: open ANU Mobile → tap the "Events" icon → search using the keywords "Culture" or "Language" → tap the event card → fill in a short questionnaire (usually 3-5 questions) → submit. Once registered, the app generates a QR code that is scanned for check-in at the event. If an event is full, the system automatically adds you to the waitlist and moves you up in order as people cancel. 2023 data shows a 34% waitlist-to-confirmed conversion rate for workshop-type events.

### Registering in person

Some activities (such as claiming food festival tickets or signing up to perform at the Cultural Night) can only be done in person at the counter. The counter is located in the **Student Central** lobby at Kambri and is open Monday to Friday 9:00-17:00 (AEST). Bring your student ID card (ANU ID Card) with you. If you can't come in person, someone else can do it on your behalf, but you must provide the original authorisation letter and photocopies of both students' ID cards. When paying tuition across borders, some families use professional channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E6%96%87%E5%8C%96%E4%BA%A4%E6%B5%81%E6%B4%BB%E5%8A%A8%E6%97%A5%E5%8E%86%E4%B8%8E%E6%8A%A5%E5%90%8D%E6%96%B9%E5%BC%8F) to complete the currency conversion.

## Benefits and incentives for participating

ANU has set up a multi-tier incentive system to encourage international students to keep participating in cultural exchange events.

### Co-Curricular Record certification

Every 10 hours of cultural activities completed (including workshops, the Buddy Program and language exchange) qualifies for an entry on your **Co-Curricular Record**. The record is officially certified by ANU and includes the activity name, duration, organising body and key skill tags (such as cross-cultural communication and teamwork). Before graduation, students can apply through ISIS to generate their final record, which can be attached to job applications or postgraduate study applications as supporting material. In 2023, ANU issued 2,347 Co-Curricular Records, 61% of them to international students.

### Activity points and rewards

Each event you attend earns you between 10 and 50 **ANU Experience Points**, which accumulate and can be redeemed for rewards: 100 points for an ANU commemorative hoodie, 300 points for a 50 AUD Kambri dining voucher, and 500 points for one free on-campus accommodation upgrade (from a standard single room to an en-suite room, for one semester). Points can be tracked in real time in the "Rewards" section of ANU Mobile, and redemptions are processed at designated counters during the last two weeks of each semester.

## Special program: Global Leadership Program

The **Global Leadership Program (GLP)** is ANU's advanced cultural leadership program for international students, with only 60 places available each academic year. The program runs for 8 months (March-October) and comprises 4 modules: a cross-cultural team project, community service (30 hours), industry visits (3 visits) and a final personal cultural impact report. Students who complete all modules receive a certificate and letter of recommendation from ANU. Eligibility: a GPA of at least 5.0/7.0 and participation in at least 3 ANU cultural events. Application materials include a personal statement (500 words), one letter of recommendation and screenshots of your event participation records. Applications close on 28 February each year and are submitted through the ANU Global Programs website.

## Common problems and how to handle them

Some international students run into registration failures, scheduling clashes or event cancellations. Below are the standard processes provided by the university.

### If registration fails

If you see "Registration Failed" when registering on ISIS or ANU Mobile, common causes include: an inactive student account, OSHC insurance not yet registered, or a clash between the event and your class timetable. First check whether "Account Status" in ISIS shows "Active", then make sure your OSHC was activated before the semester started. If the problem persists, screenshot the error page and send it through the Student Central ticketing system — you can usually expect a reply within 2 business days.

### Clashing events and swapping sessions

If you've registered for two events that overlap, cancel one of them through ANU Mobile at least 48 hours before it starts, otherwise it will be marked as a "No Show". Students who accumulate 3 No Shows are barred from registering for any free events for the next 30 days. To swap to a different session of the same event, click "Change Session" on the event detail page; the system shows remaining spots and automatically releases your original one. 2023 data shows that about 12% of registrants ended up swapping sessions.

## FAQ

### Q1: How do I view the full calendar of ANU international student cultural exchange events?

The full calendar is available on the "Events Calendar" page of the ANU Global Programs website, which is organised by semester and month and can be exported to Google Calendar or Outlook. The ANU Mobile app also includes a real-time events list. The calendar is usually published 4 weeks before the start of each semester; the Semester 1 2024 calendar went live on 15 January and includes 213 events.

### Q2: Do I have to pay to register for events?

More than 95% of international student cultural exchange events are free for current ANU students. The only exception is the Global Leadership Program, which charges a 150 AUD materials fee (covering certificate production and transport for industry visits); it can be paid through ISIS after you're accepted. Food festival tickets are free, but some stalls may charge a small surcharge (e.g. dishes made with special ingredients, 2-5 AUD per serving).

### Q3: If I miss the registration window, can I still attend?

Some events keep a waitlist, and you'll be moved up in order as registered participants cancel. 2023 data shows the waitlist-to-confirmed conversion rate was 34% for workshop-type events and 18% for the Buddy Program. For the mandatory O-Week check-in, if you miss the window you must go to the Student Central counter within 7 days after the semester starts to complete a late check-in; otherwise your visa compliance record may be affected.
