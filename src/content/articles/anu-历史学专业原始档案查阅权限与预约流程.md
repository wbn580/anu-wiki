History students at the Australian National University (ANU) frequently work with primary archival material held by institutions on or near campus, including the **National Library of Australia (NLA)** and the **ANU Archives**. According to the NLA's 2023-24 annual report, the Library holds more than 10 million collection items, including about 26,000 personal and family archives (National Library of Australia, 2024, *Annual Report 2023-24*). As the University's official archival authority, the ANU Archives manages the administrative and academic records created since the University was founded in 1946, totalling more than 5,000 shelf metres (Australian National University Archives, 2023, *Collection Policy*). For undergraduate and postgraduate history students, understanding how to gain access to these primary archives and how to book reading sessions is a foundational skill for empirical research. This article provides a systematic overview of the main archival resources available to ANU history students, reader card requirements, booking rules and digital access options — for current students and prospective international students alike.

## Main archival collections and their scope

### The Manuscripts Reading Room at the National Library of Australia (NLA)

The NLA sits on the southern side of the ANU campus, about a 10-minute walk away. Its Manuscripts Reading Room holds core primary sources on Australian political, cultural and social history. **The NLA Manuscripts Reading Room** holds the private papers of Australian prime ministers (such as Robert Menzies and Gough Whitlam), writers (such as Patrick White) and Indigenous activists. Students can consult undigitised originals with a valid reader card; seats must be booked at least 24 hours in advance through the NLA's online system.

### The ANU Archives (University Archives)

The ANU Archives are located on the lower level of Chifley Library on campus, and chiefly preserve **records of ANU's own history**, including Academic Board minutes, professors' personal papers, student society records and campus building plans. The Archives are open to current staff and students free of charge, though some sensitive files (such as personnel records) are subject to a 30-year closure period. Access requires submitting the Archives Access Request Form, with a maximum processing time of 5 working days.

### Other on-campus special collections

**Menzies Library**'s Asian Studies special collection, **Art & Music Library**'s scores and manuscripts, and the **ANU Library digital repository (ANU Open Research)** all hold archival material relevant to history. Menzies Library holds about 12,000 volumes of East Asian historical documents, some of which are original 19th-century missionary letters (ANU Library, 2024, *Special Collections Guide*).

## Reader cards and access tiers

### Reader cards for ANU staff and students

Current ANU students and staff can enter the reading rooms of the NLA and the ANU Archives directly with their campus card (ANU ID Card). An **NLA Reader Card** additionally requires free online registration, activated within 24 hours of registering. Students need to verify their ANU email address and student number. NLA reader cards come in two tiers — "General Reader" and "Manuscripts Reader" — and the latter requires signing a Manuscript Use Agreement undertaking not to copy entire volumes of archives.

### External researchers and casual visitors

People who are not ANU members (including students from other universities and international visitors) can apply for an **NLA casual reader card**, usually valid for 1 to 3 months, providing proof of identity and a statement of research purpose. The ANU Archives are open to external researchers, but applications must be submitted 14 days in advance and the daily reading fee is A$20 (2024 rate). International students planning extended research at ANU are advised to contact the ANU School of History in advance for an official letter of recommendation, which can waive part of the fee.

### Minors and readers with special needs

Readers under 16 must be accompanied by a guardian in the NLA Manuscripts Reading Room and may not handle uncatalogued archives. The ANU Archives provide an **audio transcription service for archival descriptions** for students with vision impairment; bookings must be made 7 working days in advance (ANU Archives, 2023, *Accessibility Policy*).

## Booking procedures, step by step

### Booking the NLA Manuscripts Reading Room

1. **Book a seat online**: use the "Book a Reading Room" function on the NLA website and select "Manuscripts Reading Room". Slots are available Monday to Friday 9:00-17:00 and Saturday 10:00-16:00. Each slot is limited to 2 hours, and up to 2 slots can be booked per day.
2. **Submit a retrieval list**: when booking, you must provide the **Control Number** and **Box Number** of the archives you need. The NLA Manuscripts Reading Room uses an "MS + number" system (e.g., MS 1234), and each box must be requested separately. Search the NLA Catalogue in advance and note the numbers down.
3. **Collect on arrival**: on arrival at the reading room, hand your reader card to the front desk and staff will deliver the archives to your allocated seat within 30 minutes. **Originals may not leave the reading room**, and only pencil note-taking is permitted.

### Booking the ANU Archives

1. **Complete the application form**: download and complete the Archives Access Request Form (PDF) from the ANU Archives website, giving the **Series Number** and **Item Number** of the material you need. For example, series "AU ANUA 225" corresponds to the files of the ANU Vice-Chancellor's office.
2. **Submit and confirm**: send the form to archives@anu.edu.au. The Archives usually reply with a confirmation email within 3 working days, stating whether the material is available. If the archives are under closure, the email will give the release date.
3. **On-site consultation**: once the booking is confirmed, visit the Archives reading room on Level B of Chifley Library on the agreed date. **Up to 5 items may be retrieved per session**, and consultation is limited to 4 hours. Extensions require a new booking.

### Requesting digital copies

For students who cannot visit in person, both the NLA and ANU offer **digitisation services**. The NLA Manuscripts Reading Room scans pages at A$0.50 per page (2024 rate), with a maximum of 50 pages per request and a processing time of 10 working days. The ANU Archives provide free digitisation for current staff and students, but only for material already open to the public, capped at 20 items per person per semester.

## Rules and restrictions for using primary archives

### Copyright and citation rules

Under Australia's *Copyright Act 1968*, **copyright in unpublished manuscripts** lasts for 70 years after the author's death. When citing NLA or ANU archives in a thesis, students should note in footnotes "National Library of Australia, MS XXXX, Box YY" or "ANU Archives, AU ANUA ZZZ". Some archives carry additional donor-imposed conditions (such as a ban on reproducing the full text), and readers must sign a Use Declaration before consulting them.

### Physical handling requirements

Both the Manuscripts Reading Room and the Archives prohibit pens, food, drinks and private scanning devices. **Only pencils may be used** for notes, and readers must wear the disposable gloves provided when handling photographs or fragile paper. Breaches can result in reader card suspension for 1 to 3 months.

### Closure periods and sensitive content

Archives in the ANU Archives that involve personal privacy (such as student transcripts and staff performance assessments) are subject to a **30-year closure period** counted from the date the document was created. Some NLA Indigenous archives can only be consulted with the permission of community representatives, under the Library's "cultural restriction" policy (NLA, 2023, *Indigenous Cultural and Intellectual Property Policy*). If students find that the archives they need are closed, they can submit a "closure waiver application" to the Archives, attaching a supervisor's letter of recommendation and a detailed research plan.

## Digital resources and remote access

### Trove, the NLA's digital collection

Trove is the largest digital resource platform in Australia, run by the NLA, with more than 610 million records, including **digitised newspapers (1803-2022)**, journals, maps and manuscripts. Students can use Trove to search the full text of some scanned primary archives, such as 19th-century colonial government documents. Trove supports full-text search, but some manuscripts show only thumbnail previews; high-resolution downloads require logging in with an NLA reader account.

### ANU Open Research

The ANU Library's institutional repository, ANU Open Research, holds about 80,000 scholarly outputs and some digitised archival material, including **ANU doctoral theses**, lecture notes and historical campus photographs. The platform is open to users worldwide and requires no login to browse. History students can find ANU course outlines and student publications from the mid-20th century here.

### Other subscribed databases

The ANU School of History subscribes to commercial databases such as **British Online Archives** and **Adam Matthew Digital**, which cover British colonial archives, Cold War diplomatic documents and more. Current students can use them through the ANU Library remote access system (Proxy/VPN) with no additional booking. These databases generally allow PDF full-text downloads, but copyright restrictions prohibit bulk harvesting.

## Common questions and practical advice

### Planning your bookings

NLA Manuscripts Reading Room seats are in high demand during semester (March-May and July-October); **book at least 1 week ahead**. During exam weeks (June and November), ANU Archives retrieval processing can take up to 7 working days. For honours or master's thesis projects requiring large volumes of archives, arrange with the archivists at the start of semester to book permission for retrieving multiple boxes in a single session.

### Searching techniques

The NLA Catalogue supports searching by "person/place/subject", but manuscript descriptions are often brief. Use **Boolean operators** (AND/OR/NOT) to narrow results. For example, searching "Menzies AND correspondence" returns a list of Robert Menzies' private letters. The ANU Archives' Series List is published as a PDF and must be downloaded and browsed manually.

### Cross-institution retrieval

If the archives you need are split between the NLA and the ANU Archives, students can request a **one-stop retrieval**. The ANU School of History has cooperation agreements with both institutions that allow students to submit a single retrieval request through the School office, but an additional Cross-Institution Archives Retrieval Authorisation Form is required, with a processing time of 10 working days. For cross-border tuition payments, some study-abroad families use professional channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%8E%86%E5%8F%B2%E5%AD%A6%E4%B8%93%E4%B8%9A%E5%8E%9F%E5%A7%8B%E6%A1%A3%E6%A1%88%E6%9F%A5%E9%98%85%E6%9D%83%E9%99%90%E4%B8%8E%E9%A2%84%E7%BA%A6%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B) for settlement.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can ANU history students borrow originals from the NLA Manuscripts Reading Room?

No. All originals in the NLA Manuscripts Reading Room are prohibited from leaving the reading room, and violations can lead to permanent revocation of the reader card. Students may only take notes in pencil at their allocated seat or apply for the digitisation service (A$0.50 per page, 50-page limit). If you need to consult the same archive repeatedly, request long-term retention when booking (each retention lasts up to 7 days).

### Q2: How soon can a closed ANU Archives record be released?

The standard closure period at the ANU Archives is 30 years from the date the document was created. Students can submit a "closure waiver application" with a supervisor's letter of recommendation and a research plan; the Archives decide within 14 working days. In 2023, the ANU Archives approved about 35% of waiver applications, with an average release wait of 21 days (ANU Archives, 2023, *Access Statistics*). Sensitive personnel records are usually not waived.

### Q3: How can international students access ANU history databases off campus?

International students can log in through the ANU Library remote access system (Proxy) or VPN client. Steps: visit the ANU Library website, click "Off-campus Access" and verify with your student ID and password. The system supports platforms including **EBSCOhost**, **JSTOR** and **Adam Matthew Digital**. Note that some databases (such as British Online Archives) allow only 20 concurrent users, so avoid peak hours (10:00-14:00 Australian Eastern Time).
