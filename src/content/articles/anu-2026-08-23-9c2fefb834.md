## Overview

The Australian National University (ANU) publishes graduate employment survey data that sheds light on outcomes for its master's graduates. This article compares key metrics—employment rate, median starting salary, industry distribution, and employer satisfaction—for popular programs in computing, engineering, business, and law, to help prospective students evaluate their options.

## Employment Rate and Salary

According to the survey, master's graduates from computing programs have a high employment rate, with a median starting salary that reflects strong demand in the tech sector. Engineering graduates similarly show robust employment outcomes, with salaries varying by specialisation. Business master's graduates also demonstrate solid employment rates, though starting salaries can differ based on industry. Law graduates, particularly those in professional practice, tend to have competitive employment outcomes, with salary bands influenced by firm size and location.

## Industry Distribution and Employer Satisfaction

Computing graduates are commonly employed in software development, IT consulting, and data analytics. Engineering graduates often find roles in infrastructure, energy, and manufacturing. Business graduates frequently work in finance, consulting, and marketing. Law graduates typically enter private practice, government, or corporate legal roles. Employer satisfaction across these fields remains generally positive, with employers noting the strong analytical skills and academic rigour of ANU graduates.

## Considerations for Prospective Students

While employment and salary data are valuable indicators, they should be considered alongside personal interests, career goals, and the broader economic context. ANU's location in Canberra offers unique internship and networking opportunities, particularly with government agencies and research institutions. For international students, the survey also provides information on outcomes within Australia and internationally.

## Questions and Answers

### What is the median starting salary for ANU computing master's graduates?
The survey indicates a median starting salary that is competitive within the tech industry, though exact figures are not specified here.

Finance, consulting, and marketing are listed as the most common industries for business graduates.

Employer satisfaction is reported as generally positive across computing, engineering, business, and law, with no major differences noted.
