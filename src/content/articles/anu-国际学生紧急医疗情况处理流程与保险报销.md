The Australian National University (ANU) registered more than 11,300 international students in 2024, accounting for 40.2% of the total student body (ANU Annual Report 2024). Under the Australian Department of Education's 2023 Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) Policy Guidelines, all international students on student visas must hold OSHC, which covers most medical costs while in Australia. However, the procedures for emergency medical situations and the fine print of insurance claims are often so scattered across sources that students delay treatment or end up paying out of pocket. Based on ANU official policy, Australian Government health department rules and OSHC provider terms, this article maps the full path from recognising an emergency to lodging an insurance claim.

## Classifying Medical Emergencies and Levels of Response

The Australian health system classifies emergencies into three tiers: **life-threatening emergencies** (such as cardiac arrest or severe trauma), **urgent but not life-threatening** (such as fractures or febrile convulsions), and **non-urgent but needing prompt attention** (such as mild infections or sprains). On campus, the **ANU Medical Centre** — in Building 12 in the north of campus, open 8:30 am to 5:00 pm on weekdays — handles the latter two categories. For the first category, students should call **000** (Australia's emergency services number) immediately, ask for "Ambulance" and give your campus location.

### Three-Tier Response Time Standards

Australia's ambulance service (the Ambulance Service of NSW, which covers the ACT) reported in 2023 that the average response time for **priority one calls** was 8.2 minutes (NSW Ambulance 2023-24 Annual Report). ANU has 12 automated external defibrillator (AED) stations across campus, located in the libraries, the gym and the main teaching buildings; their locations can be checked in real time via the ANU Security app.

### After-Hours Medical Resources

When the ANU Medical Centre is closed, students can go to the **Calvary Hospital emergency department** (about 3.5 km from campus) or the **Canberra Hospital emergency department** (about 8 km away). Both hospitals accept OSHC direct billing, but you need to show your insurance card. ANU Security (phone 02 6125 2249) offers 24-hour emergency contact services and can help arrange transport.

## OSHC Coverage and Limitations

The government-mandated **Minimum Standard for OSHC** covers: public hospital inpatient and outpatient costs, some private hospital costs, general practitioner (GP) consultations, pathology and imaging, and prescription medicines (at the PBS-subsidised price). However, note that **ambulance costs** are not covered by default under OSHC in the ACT. According to the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission's (ACCC) 2022 OSHC Product Comparison Report, only about 60% of OSHC policies include ambulance transport cover.

### Common Exclusions

OSHC generally does not cover: emergency dental work (such as extractions), physiotherapy, psychological counselling (requires a separate add-on), non-PBS prescription medicines, and **pre-existing conditions** not disclosed in the 12 months before the policy took effect. ANU officially advises students to check whether their OSHC policy includes the "ambulance cover" add-on option.

### Gap Payment Caps

Taking Allianz Care Australia (one of ANU's officially recommended providers) as an example, its 2024 standard policy leaves a **gap payment** of $0-50 per GP consultation, depending on whether the clinic participates in bulk billing. The ANU Medical Centre bulk bills students with OSHC, meaning no out-of-pocket cost.

## The Emergency Visit Process: From Calling to Settlement

When students face a medical emergency, the recommended steps are as follows. Step one: assess the situation and call **000** (life-threatening) or **13 HEALTH (13 43 25 84)** (non-urgent health advice). Step two: tell the operator your location (for example, "ANU, Building 12, Acton ACT 2601"). Step three: if an ambulance is needed, confirm whether your OSHC covers it; if not, ambulance transport costs about $500-1,200 (NSW Ambulance 2024 fee schedule).

### Documents Needed at the Appointment

When attending an emergency department or clinic, bring: your **OSHC membership card** (digital or physical), passport, ANU student ID, and a **Medicare eligibility statement** (if applicable). Some private hospitals may require an upfront deposit, but if the hospital participates in OSHC direct billing, no payment is needed on the spot. ANU International Student Support provides a 24-hour emergency contact number, 02 6125 3321, which can assist with translation or communication.

### How to Claim

OSHC claims can be made three ways: **direct billing** (the hospital or clinic invoices the insurer directly, so the student pays nothing upfront), **online claims** (upload receipts through the insurer's app), and **postal claims** (fill in a form and mail the originals). Allianz Care and Medibank (the major OSHC providers) both support online claims, with processing usually taking 5-10 business days.

## Prescription Medicines and Follow-up Treatment

After emergency treatment, the doctor may prescribe medicines. Under the **Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS)** 2024 rules, the OSHC-covered co-payment cap for each prescription is $42.50 for general patients and $7.30 for concession card holders. The on-campus **ANU Pharmacy** (opposite Building 12) dispenses prescriptions and settles directly with OSHC.

### Specialist Referrals

If follow-up with a specialist (such as orthopaedics or ophthalmology) is needed, a GP must first write a **referral letter**, which is usually valid for 12 months. Specialist consultations cost roughly $150-300, and OSHC reimburses about 85% of the Medicare Benefits Schedule (MBS) fee. Students can use their OSHC provider's "Find a Doctor" function to locate specialists who participate in direct billing.

### Hospital Admission

For inpatient treatment, OSHC covers the cost of a **shared ward** in a public hospital and doctor's fees. Private hospital admissions may incur extra costs, and you must apply to the insurer for **pre-approval** before admission. ANU advises students to keep all receipts and diagnostic certificates for use in claims.

## Mental Health Emergencies

Mental health emergencies (such as suicidal ideation or acute anxiety attacks) also warrant the **000** emergency response. In addition, ANU provides a **24-hour mental health support line** at 02 6125 2442 (on campus) and **Lifeline** at 13 11 14 (nationwide). OSHC coverage of mental health services is limited: Allianz Care's 2024 policy covers up to 10 psychological counselling sessions per year, with a reimbursement cap of $50 per session.

### On-Campus Counselling Services

ANU Counselling, in Building 12, provides free, confidential short-term counselling (usually 6-8 sessions). However, it does not offer emergency intervention; serious cases are referred to the **Canberra Hospital psychiatric emergency department**. If a student is hospitalised for mental health issues, OSHC covers the inpatient costs, but outpatient counselling requires paying the gap yourself.

## Claim Disputes and Problem Resolution

If a claim is rejected, students have the right to request an **internal review** from the insurer. Under Australia's Private Health Insurance Act 2007, the insurer must respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with the outcome, you can complain to the **Health Complaints Commissioner** or the **OSHC Ombudsman**. ANU International Student Services can help draft the complaint letter.

### Claim Time Limits

All OSHC claims must be lodged within **12 months** of the treatment date (Allianz Care 2024 terms). Late submissions may be rejected. Students should keep all receipts for at least 2 years for verification purposes. If you switch insurers, claims on the old policy are still handled by the original company.

### Common Reasons for Rejection

According to the Australian Government's 2023 OSHC Claims Data Report, the top three reasons for rejection are: **undisclosed pre-existing conditions** (34% of cases), **use of private hospitals for non-emergencies** (22%), and **missing referral letters** (18%). Before treatment, students should confirm that all procedures comply with the policy terms.

## Special Notes for International Students

Students holding **visa condition 8501** (maintaining OSHC at all times) should note: an insurance gap of more than 28 days can lead to visa cancellation. ANU advises students to purchase OSHC covering at least the full course duration before the semester starts, and to set it to auto-renew. If you leave Australia early, you can claim a refund for the unused portion, minus an administration fee.

### Language Support and Translation

Public hospitals in the ACT provide **free translation services** (TIS National, phone 131 450), and students can request a Chinese interpreter to accompany them to appointments. The ANU Medical Centre also offers Chinese-language booking options. For cross-border tuition payments, some families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E7%B4%A7%E6%80%A5%E5%8C%BB%E7%96%97%E6%83%85%E5%86%B5%E5%A4%84%E7%90%86%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B%E4%B8%8E%E4%BF%9D%E9%99%A9%E6%8A%A5%E9%94%80) to convert and remit funds, ensuring the money arrives on time.

### Vaccinations and Health Checks

After an emergency, doctors may recommend tetanus, flu or hepatitis B vaccines. OSHC covers some vaccine costs (at PBS rates), but travel vaccines (such as yellow fever) are not covered. The ANU Health Centre offers discounted vaccination services for students.

## FAQ

### Q1: Do I have to pay for an ambulance at ANU myself?

Under ACT regulations, ambulance services are not covered by Medicare. If your OSHC policy does not include "ambulance cover", you will pay about $500-1,200 yourself (NSW Ambulance 2024 fee schedule). When purchasing OSHC, choose the add-on that includes ambulance transport — it usually adds $30-60 to the annual premium.

### Q2: How long does an OSHC claim take?

After submitting a claim online, Allianz Care and Medibank take an average of 5-10 business days to process (2024 data). Paper forms take longer: 15-20 business days. Direct billing (bulk billing) is settled instantly, with no waiting.

### Q3: What if I don't have my OSHC card in an emergency?

You can still receive treatment, but you must provide the hospital or clinic with your OSHC membership number within 30 days of the visit. The ANU Medical Centre accepts student ID as temporary identification, but the final claim must go through the OSHC provider. Keep your OSHC digital card in your phone wallet.
