The Australian National University (ANU) allows international students, once specific conditions are met, to audit certain courses in a non-award capacity. Under the Course Audit Policy published by ANU in 2024, international students may apply to audit up to 2 courses per semester, and audited courses earn no credit and do not count towards the enrolment load required for full-time student visa status. The Department of Home Affairs' 2023 Student Visa Conditions Guide states explicitly that international students on a student visa (Subclass 500) must maintain full-time enrolment, and that audited courses cannot replace the credit courses required for their degree. This policy framework changed after ANU's academic calendar was adjusted for 2025: the audit application deadline for Semester 1 was moved forward to the Friday of Week 2, five working days earlier than in 2024. For students planning to use auditing to broaden their academic horizons or explore interdisciplinary directions, understanding the application process and the boundaries of permission is essential.

## Eligibility and Prerequisites

**International students** wishing to audit a course must meet three hard conditions at the same time: hold a valid ANU student visa, be enrolled in at least 3 full-time credit courses (no fewer than 24 units per semester), and have written consent from the Course Convenor of the course they want to audit. According to the Operational Guide to Audit Applications published by ANU Student Central in 2024, applicants must submit a "Course Audit Request" form in the Wattle system, together with a scanned copy of the consent letter signed by the teacher. If the audited course involves laboratory, studio or clinical practice components, additional approval from the Associate Dean (Education) of the college is also required.

### Credit Load Limits

Audited courses do not count towards the **full-time study load**. ANU policy stipulates that no more than 2 courses may be audited per semester, and the total contact hours of audited courses cannot exceed 25% of the total contact hours of the credit courses in which the student is enrolled. Taking a standard 6-unit course (3 lecture hours per week) as an example, auditing that course takes up roughly 39 hours of class time per semester. The Department of Home Affairs' 2023 rules on Student Visa Work Limits and Study Requirements note that if auditing causes a student to fail the full-time enrolment requirement (at least 24 units per semester), their visa status may be affected.

### Academic Background and Prerequisites

Some advanced courses (3000-level and above) require auditors to have results in the relevant **prerequisite courses**. The 2025 edition of the ANU Programs and Courses catalogue shows that about 12% of 4000-level courses explicitly state that "auditing requires the Course Convenor to review the applicant's academic background". Applicants must upload a copy of their transcript in Wattle so that the teacher can judge whether they have the foundation to understand the course content. If the teacher considers that the auditor lacks the necessary prior knowledge, they may refuse the application, and that decision is not appealable.

## Application Timeline

**The application window** is fixed from Monday of Week 1 to Friday of Week 2 of each semester (17 February to 28 February for 2025 Semester 1). The Academic Calendar published by ANU Student Central in 2024 states that late applications lapse automatically, with no catch-up window. Applicants must go to the "Enrolment" module in the ANU student portal (ISIS) and select the "Course Audit Request" function.

### Document Submission and Review Timeline

Applications must upload three documents: the **consent letter** signed by the teacher (PDF format, including the course code and semester), a personal statement explaining the purpose of the audit (no more than 200 words), and proof of enrolment in the current semester's courses. ANU policy requires Student Central to complete its review within 5 working days of receiving the complete documents. 2024 data shows that about 78% of applications received an outcome within 3 working days. If the application is approved, the audit record is marked as "AU" (Audit) status in the ISIS system.

### Activation of Course Access

After approval, the Wattle system activates the auditor's access to the course site within 24 hours. Auditors can view the course outline, reading materials and lecture recordings (where provided), but **cannot submit assignments or sit exams**. The Wattle User Permissions guide published by ANU Information Technology Services (ITS) in 2024 notes that audit accounts have "submit assignments" and "view grades" disabled by default. If a teacher wants an auditor to take part in discussions or group activities, a separate permission-change request must be submitted to ITS.

## Permitted Scope and Activity Boundaries

**Classroom participation** is the core point of contention in audit permission. ANU policy is clear: auditors may attend lectures and tutorials, but may not speak or ask questions in class discussions unless explicitly authorised by the course teacher. An internal survey of the ANU College of Arts and Social Sciences in 2024 found that about 34% of teachers email auditors in Week 1 to say "questions are welcome", but that authorisation must be reconfirmed each semester.

### Assessment and Credit Exclusion

Audited courses **earn no credit whatsoever**, and auditors take part in no form of assessment (including mid-semester exams, essays and group projects). Clause 7.3 of the ANU Assessment Policy states that auditors may not ask teachers to mark their work or provide grade feedback. If an auditor needs proof of course completion, they can apply to Student Central for an "Audit Completion Letter", which states the course name, semester and audit status but contains no grade.

### Resource Use Restrictions

Auditors can access the **electronic library resources** assigned to the course (through the ANU Library portal), but cannot use laboratory equipment, licensed professional software or college-specific workshop spaces. The Facilities Use Policy published by ANU Research Services in 2024 stipulates that auditors who need specific equipment (such as chemistry laboratory fume hoods or engineering 3D printers) must submit a separate "Facility Access Request" and obtain approval from the college safety manager. This approval process usually takes 10-15 working days.

## Visa Compliance and Work Limits

**The student visa conditions** (Visa Condition 8202) require international students to maintain full-time enrolment. Audited courses are not counted in the enrolment load, so auditors must ensure their enrolled credit courses meet the requirement of at least 24 units per semester. The Department of Home Affairs' 2023 Student Visa Compliance Handbook gives an example: if a student enrols in three 6-unit courses (18 units in total) and audits one course, that student is automatically in breach of their visa because they have not reached the 24-unit full-time standard. Consequences of a breach include visa cancellation or refusal of a later renewal.

### Work Hours Calculation

Audited courses do not count towards the basis for calculating **work entitlements**. Under the Department of Home Affairs rules for 2023, international students may work 48 hours per fortnight (in effect since 1 July 2023), and this limit is based only on the study load of enrolled credit courses. Auditing does not change the work-hour cap. Consultation records from the ANU International Student Support office for 2024 show that about 15% of audit applicants mistakenly believed auditing would increase their work allowance; in fact there is no such link.

## Auditing Across Semesters and Multiple Courses

**Continuing to audit** the same course requires a fresh application each semester. ANU policy states that audit permission is valid only for the current semester and cannot be extended automatically. If a student wants to audit the same course again the following semester, they must submit a new application within the next semester's window. 2024 data shows that about 22% of audit applications came from repeat auditors, most of them postgraduate students auditing undergraduate foundation courses to consolidate their subject knowledge.

### Multi-Course Audit Strategy

When auditing 2 courses at the same time, applicants must note any **timetable clashes** in each application. ANU Student Central requires auditors to check course timetables themselves and ensure the audited courses do not overlap with enrolled credit courses. If a clash occurs, Student Central will reject the audit application for the conflicting course. In Semester 1 2024, about 8% of audit applications were rejected due to timetable clashes. Applicants are advised to check in advance using the ANU Timetable Planner tool.

## Special Courses and Exceptions

**Laboratory courses** are generally not open to auditors. The Laboratory Safety Policy published by the ANU College of Science in 2024 states that laboratory courses involve chemicals, biological samples or precision instruments and are restricted to formally enrolled students. Auditors who want to observe an experiment can apply for a "Lab Demonstration Visit", which must be carried out under the supervision of a laboratory assistant and lasts no more than 2 hours per visit. The College approved 12 such observation visits in 2024.

### Auditing Postgraduate Courses

Auditing **postgraduate courses** (6000-level and above) requires additional approval from the ANU Graduate Research School. ANU policy stipulates that auditors of postgraduate courses must be enrolled research master's or PhD students. 2024 data shows that about 5% of audit applications targeted postgraduate courses, 91% of them from ANU's own postgraduate students. Coursework master's students who want to audit a postgraduate course must submit a recommendation letter from their supervisor.

When paying cross-border tuition, some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payment](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E8%AF%BE%E7%A8%8B%E6%97%81%E5%90%AC%E7%94%B3%E8%AF%B7%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B%E4%B8%8E%E8%AE%B8%E5%8F%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4) for settlement.

## Withdrawal and Record Management

**Withdrawing from an audit mid-semester** requires submitting an "Audit Withdrawal" application to Student Central before the Friday of Week 8. ANU policy states that late withdrawal will result in the audit record being marked "Withdrawn Audit"; this record is kept in the academic file but does not affect the GPA. 2024 data shows that about 6% of auditors withdrew mid-semester, mainly due to time-management difficulties or course content exceeding expectations.

### Use of Audit Records

Audit records **do not appear on the Academic Transcript**. If students need to prove their audit experience, they can apply for a "Co-curricular Record", managed by ANU Student Life, which lists the audited course names and semesters but notes "not assessed". When applying to some postgraduate programs, recommendation letters may mention audit performance, but ANU does not provide an official assessment of audit results.

## FAQ

### Q1: Will auditing a course affect my student visa renewal?

Auditing itself does not affect the visa, provided the auditor is enrolled in at least 24 units of full-time courses each semester. According to the Department of Home Affairs' 2023 Student Visa Conditions Guide, if auditing causes a student's actual enrolled credits to fall below 24 units, their visa status will be in breach. Records from the ANU International Student Support centre for 2024 show that about 3% of audit applicants were rejected by Student Central for not meeting the full-time requirement.

### Q2: Do I need to pay extra tuition fees to audit a course?

No. ANU's 2024 Fee Policy states that auditing does not incur additional tuition fees, but the student must already have paid the tuition for their full-time courses that semester. If a student is enrolled in fewer than 24 units, they must still pay the tuition shortfall per unit even if they apply to audit. In Semester 1 2024, 11 students were required to pay the shortfall or have their audit permission cancelled for insufficient credits.

### Q3: Can an audited course be converted into a formal credit course?

No. ANU policy is clear that an audited course cannot be converted into a credit course during or after the semester. Under clause 5.2 of the ANU Course Enrolment Policy, students who want credit must formally enrol in the course the following semester and complete all assessment requirements. 2024 data shows that about 7% of auditors subsequently formally enrolled in the same course, and 83% of them earned credit.
