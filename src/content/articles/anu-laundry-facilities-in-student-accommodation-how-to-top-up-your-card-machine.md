Laundry facilities in ANU on-campus accommodation are operated by third-party provider **Washstation** across all 12 student residence buildings. As of 2025, every residence building is equipped with at least 4 industrial washing machines and 4 dryers, with a single wash costing AUD 4.50 and drying AUD 3.50. According to **ANU Accommodation Services (2024)**, more than 92% of laundry fault reports are resolved within 24 hours. With Semester 1 2025 enrolments reaching 26,000 students (**Australian Government Department of Education, 2025**), demand from new residents for guidance on topping up laundry cards, machine usage costs, and fault reporting has risen sharply. This article provides a systematic operating guide based on the official operator manual and user feedback.

## Laundry Card Top-Up Process

### Getting and Activating Your Physical Card
Every student moving into ANU accommodation receives a pre-loaded **Washstation laundry card** from the residence front desk during check-in. The card uses RFID contactless technology and has an initial balance of AUD 10.00 (included in the accommodation deposit). To activate it, hold the card against the machine's card reader for 3 seconds until the screen shows "Card Activated". If the card is lost, a replacement costs AUD 5.00 and can be requested from the residence front desk.

### Online Top-Up Methods
The laundry card can be topped up online via the **Washstation official portal**. Users visit washstation.com.au/top-up, enter the 16-digit number printed on the back of the card, and choose a top-up amount (minimum AUD 10.00, maximum AUD 100.00). Payment methods include Visa, Mastercard, and **Alipay** (processed through the SecurePay gateway). The new balance usually syncs to the card within 2 minutes. When settling cross-border tuition fees, some study-abroad families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-laundry-facilities-in-student-accommodation-how-to-top-up-your-card-machine), but laundry card top-ups only accept the three payment methods listed above.

### Offline Top-Up Locations
If online payment fails, students can top up with cash at the following locations:
- **Bruce Hall front desk**: Monday to Friday 08:00-20:00
- **Fenner Hall front desk**: Monday to Sunday 09:00-18:00
- **Wright Hall self-service top-up machine**: available 24 hours, accepts AUD 5.00, 10.00, and 20.00 banknotes only

## Machine Costs and Cycles

### Washing Machine and Dryer Pricing
Every washing machine and dryer has an **LCD display** showing the current price and remaining time. The fees are as follows:
- **Standard wash**: AUD 4.50 per load, 45-minute cycle (cold wash + high-speed spin)
- **Large item wash**: AUD 6.00 per load, 60-minute cycle (for doonas and down jackets)
- **Drying**: AUD 3.50 per load, 35-minute cycle (warm air drying)
- **Extra spin**: AUD 1.00 per load, 5-minute cycle

According to the **Washstation Operator Manual (2025)**, all machines use **HE high-efficiency water-saving technology**, with each standard wash using around 35 litres of water — below the Australian average household washing machine usage of 50 litres per load.

### Payment Methods and Cycles
Payment is accepted only via the **laundry card** or the **Washstation mobile app** (available for iOS and Android). The app supports Apple Pay and Google Pay, with a transaction fee of AUD 0.30 per use. Machines lock automatically after payment until the cycle ends. If a cycle is cancelled midway, the fee already deducted is not refunded. Students are advised to use machines during off-peak hours (such as 10:00-14:00) to reduce waiting times — according to the **ANU Residential Life Survey (2024)**, the average wait during peak hours (18:00-21:00) is 12 minutes.

## Fault Reporting and Repair Process

### Fault Types and Identification
Common faults include:
- **Card jam**: the machine does not start after payment and the screen shows "Payment Error"
- **Door lock fault**: the door will not open after the wash cycle ends
- **Leaking**: water pooling at the base of the machine
- **Incomplete drying**: clothes still damp after the drying cycle ends

Each machine has a **unique device number** on its side (format e.g. W-101-05), which must be provided when reporting a fault.

### Fault Reporting Channels
Faults can be reported through any of the following three channels:
1. **Washstation website**: visit washstation.com.au/report, fill in the device number, fault description, and contact details; the system automatically generates a work order (reference number format: R-2025-XXXX).
2. **Residence front desk**: tell the duty staff directly, and the front desk will submit a work order through the internal system within 30 minutes.
3. **Washstation app**: use the "Report Issue" menu to upload photos and track repair progress in real time.

According to the performance indicators of **ANU Facilities Management Division (2025)**, routine faults (such as card jams and door locks) receive a response within 4 hours, while complex faults (such as circuit board replacement) are completed within 48 hours. If a machine remains unrepaired for more than 72 hours, students can apply for a **temporary laundry subsidy** (AUD 10.00 per claim, credited to the accommodation account).

## Laundry Room Rules and Safety

### Operating Hours and Capacity Limits
All laundry rooms are **open 24 hours**, but some residences (such as **Burgmann College**) restrict noise between 22:00-07:00 and prohibit dryer use during those hours (as dryers exceed 65 decibels). Each washing machine has a maximum load of 8 kg of dry laundry (roughly 2 pairs of jeans + 5 t-shirts); overloading can cause machine faults or poor wash results.

### Safety and Hygiene Rules
According to **ANU Accommodation Policy (2024)**, the following are prohibited:
- Washing **sneakers** or **rugs** in the machines (can damage the drainage system)
- Using **bleach** or **strongly acidic cleaners** (breaches chemical management regulations)
- Leaving wet clothes in a machine for extended periods (more than 2 hours) (can cause mould)

Violators are fined AUD 50.00 and the incident is recorded on their accommodation conduct record. Students are advised to use **laundry bags** to protect delicate items and to remove clothes promptly after the cycle finishes.

## Common Questions and FAQ

### Q1: Will the machine warn me when my laundry card balance is low?
Yes. When the balance falls below AUD 5.00, the machine screen displays "Low Balance" and a red light flashes. You can still finish the current wash, but you cannot start another cycle. It is recommended to keep the balance above AUD 20.00 to avoid interruptions. According to **Washstation user data (2025)**, around 67% of failed top-ups occur when the balance is below AUD 3.00.

### Q2: What happens to my clothes and money if the power goes out mid-cycle?
If the power outage lasts no more than 15 minutes, the machine automatically resumes when power is restored; if it exceeds 15 minutes, the machine locks and shows "Power Failure". In that case, contact the front desk or report the fault via the app; maintenance staff will remove your clothes within 2 hours, and AUD 4.50 will be automatically refunded to your laundry card through the work order system. This policy covers 100% of power outage scenarios (**Washstation Terms of Service, 2025**).

### Q3: Can I use my own detergent, or must I use the built-in supply?
All ANU laundry rooms require the use of the **machine's built-in automatic dispensing system**. Each washing machine has three liquid compartments (main wash, pre-wash, fabric softener), supplied by Washstation with **phosphate-free eco-friendly detergent**. Bringing your own detergent, fabric softener, or bleach is prohibited, with a fine of AUD 20.00. This measure reduces chemical residue and protects the residence drainage system, cutting around 1.2 tonnes of chemical pollutant discharge per year (**ANU Sustainability Report, 2024**).
