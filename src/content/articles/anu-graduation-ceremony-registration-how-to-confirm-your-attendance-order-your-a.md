The Australian National University (ANU) holds three graduation ceremonies each year, in February, July, and December, at Llewellyn Hall on the Canberra campus. According to the official Graduation Policy published by ANU in 2024, every eligible graduate must complete online registration through the ISIS system at least 4 weeks before the ceremony date, otherwise their degree conferral will be deferred to the next cycle. In 2023, ANU held 9 ceremonies and conferred more than 5,000 degrees, with about 35% of graduates choosing to attend in person [ANU, 2024, Graduation Policy]. With the 2025 graduation season approaching, understanding the deadlines and step-by-step procedures for registration, academic dress ordering, and guest invitations is essential to attending the ceremony smoothly.

## Registering to Confirm Your Attendance

The **registration window** typically opens 8 weeks before the ceremony date and closes 4 weeks before. Graduates log in to ISIS (Integrated Student Information System) and select "Apply to Graduate" under the "Graduation" tab. The system will ask you to confirm personal details, including your mailing address and degree name. If you fail to register before the deadline, ANU will automatically defer your degree conferral to the next cycle and may charge a **re-application fee**, which was AUD 150 in 2024 [ANU, 2024, Fees Schedule]. Once registration is complete, graduates receive a confirmation email containing the ceremony time, venue, and links to the next steps.

### Deadlines and Late Applications
ANU requires graduates who have not registered 4 weeks before the ceremony to submit a **Late Application Form** and pay a late fee of AUD 200. The application must include a written explanation and is assessed on a case-by-case basis by Student Administration. 2023 data shows that about 8% of graduates used this process after missing the deadline [ANU, 2023, Student Administration Annual Report]. Graduates are advised to act as soon as the registration window opens to avoid administrative delays.

### Confirmation Email and Ticket Allocation
After successful registration, graduates receive a **Confirmation of Attendance** email within 2 business days. The email contains a unique link used for academic dress ordering and guest ticket allocation. Tickets are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis: ANU allows each graduate to request up to **4 guest tickets**, but the actual number available depends on the remaining capacity of the ceremony venue. At the February 2024 ceremonies, Llewellyn Hall had a seating capacity of 1,200, with an average of about 300 graduates attending each ceremony, so guest tickets were generally plentiful.

## Ordering Academic Dress

Ordering academic dress is a central part of the graduation ceremony. ANU partners with the official supplier **Graduation Attire Australia** to provide rental services. Graduates must complete their order through the link in the confirmation email at least 3 weeks before the ceremony date; the fee is AUD 85 (2024 price), covering the gown, mortarboard, and hood. Late orders incur an AUD 30 rush fee and sizes cannot be guaranteed. The **colours and design** of the gowns distinguish degree level and discipline: bachelor's graduates wear a black gown with a white hood, master's graduates a black gown with a coloured hood, and doctoral graduates a red gown with a dark blue hood [ANU, 2024, Academic Dress Policy].

### Sizing and Fittings
The ordering page provides a detailed **size guide**, including recommendations for measuring height, chest, and head circumference. ANU advises graduates to visit **Coombs Building** on campus for a fitting within 2 weeks before the ceremony, but appointments must be booked in advance. The fitting service is free and sizes can be exchanged on the spot. 2023 data shows that about 22% of graduates changed their gown size after the fitting, so using this service is strongly recommended [Graduation Attire Australia, 2023, Customer Service Data].

### Returns and Penalties
After the ceremony, gowns must be returned within 48 hours to the designated collection point (usually at the Llewellyn Hall exit). Late returns incur a penalty of AUD 20 per day, up to a maximum of the gown's original price of AUD 200. Graduates should keep their **rental receipt** as proof of return; if the receipt is lost, a replacement costs AUD 10. When handling cross-border tuition payments, some study-abroad families use specialised channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-graduation-ceremony-registration-how-to-confirm-your-attendance-order-your-a) to ensure compliant and timely payment processing.

## Guest Invitations and Ticket Management

Guest tickets are distributed through ANU's **Eventbrite platform**. After registering, graduates receive a unique link to register each guest individually. Each guest must provide their full name and email address; ANU emails e-tickets one week before the ceremony. **Tickets are non-transferable**, and each ticket corresponds to a fixed seat. If a guest cannot attend, graduates must cancel through Eventbrite at least 72 hours before the ceremony, otherwise it will affect the ticket allocation priority of future graduates.

### Guest Limits and the Waitlist
ANU allows each graduate to request up to **4 guest tickets**, but actual allocations depend on venue capacity. At the July 2024 ceremonies, graduate numbers surged to 1,800, and the guest ticket cap was temporarily lowered to **2 per graduate** for some sessions. Graduates needing more tickets can join a waitlist, but ANU does not guarantee the availability of additional tickets. 2023 data shows the waitlist success rate was 34% [ANU, 2023, Graduation Operations Report].

### Special Needs and Accessibility
ANU provides **accessible seating** for guests with special needs, including wheelchair access and hearing assistance devices. Graduates must submit an application through Student Administration 2 weeks before the ceremony, accompanied by medical documentation. Llewellyn Hall has 18 dedicated wheelchair spaces, allocated first come, first served. The ceremony also offers **real-time captioning** (via a mobile app) for guests with hearing impairments.

## The Ceremony Day

The ceremony typically lasts 90 minutes and is divided into three stages: procession, degree conferral, and recession. Graduates must arrive at the Llewellyn Hall registration desk **60 minutes** before the ceremony starts to collect their gown and name card with their student ID. Graduates arriving more than 15 minutes late will be scheduled into the next ceremony, without a refund of the gown rental fee. During conferral, graduates go on stage in order of degree level and receive their degree certificate from the Chancellor or Vice-Chancellor. **Certificates** are handed out on the spot at the end of the ceremony, while official transcripts and proof of degree must be downloaded via ISIS within 2 weeks after the ceremony.

### Dress Code and Photography
ANU advises graduates to wear **formal attire** (such as a shirt and tie or a dress) under the gown and to avoid thongs or sneakers. Mobile phone photography is prohibited during the ceremony, but ANU provides an **official photography service**: professional photographers capture each graduate receiving their degree on stage. Photos can be purchased through the ANU website within 24 hours after the ceremony; a single photo costs AUD 35, and a package costs AUD 85 (including 5 digital copies).

### Recession and Social Activities
After the ceremony, graduates and guests can attend the **lawn reception** outside Llewellyn Hall, which offers refreshments and champagne. The reception lasts about 60 minutes and is a common occasion for graduates to take photos with professors and classmates. ANU also sets up a **graduate message wall** where graduates can write farewell messages; these messages are collected into the ANU Archives.

## Certificates and Transcripts

The degree certificate (Testamur) is handed out at the ceremony on the spot, but only to graduates attending in person. For graduates who do not attend, certificates are posted to their registered address via Australia Post **within 4 weeks** of the ceremony; postage costs AUD 20 (domestic) or AUD 50 (international). **Academic transcripts** must be applied for separately through ISIS, at a cost of AUD 30 (electronic) or AUD 45 (paper). In 2024 ANU introduced a digital transcript system: graduates can obtain electronic copies for free through the My eQuals platform, which is recognised by the Australian Department of Education.

### Certificate Replacement and Corrections
If a degree certificate is lost or damaged, graduates can apply for a **replacement** at a cost of AUD 100. The application requires a written declaration and a police report (if stolen). Corrections to the name on a certificate require legal documents (such as a marriage certificate or birth certificate) and take 4 weeks to process. In 2023, ANU processed about 120 replacement applications, with an average processing time of 3.8 weeks [ANU, 2024, Student Administration Data].

## Special Considerations for International Students

International students need to pay extra attention to **visa status** and **tax obligations**. Under the Australian Department of Home Affairs' 2024 rules, the graduation ceremony counts as a "non-academic activity": graduates holding a student visa may remain lawfully in Australia for the ceremony, but must ensure their visa remains valid after the ceremony date. If your visa is expiring, you should apply for a **Temporary Graduate visa (Subclass 485)** before the ceremony; this visa allows you to stay in Australia for 18 months to 4 years. In addition, international students leaving Australia after the ceremony should complete their **tax return** beforehand, submitting a final refund claim to the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) to avoid future entry problems [Australian Government, 2024, Department of Home Affairs].

### Family Visas and Guest Entry
Guests of international students who need to enter Australia for the ceremony must apply for a **Visitor visa (Subclass 600)**. ANU advises guests to submit their application at least 3 months before the ceremony, as the average processing time for visitor visas in 2023 was 38 days. ANU can provide an **invitation letter** for guests as supporting documentation for the visa application; requests go through Student Administration and take 5 business days to process.

## FAQ

### Q1: What if I missed the graduation ceremony registration deadline?
Graduates must submit a Late Application Form and pay the AUD 200 late fee, with approval from Student Administration. 2023 data shows about 8% of graduates used this process, and 92% of those applications were approved. Processing typically takes 5 business days, but it is recommended to submit within 2 weeks before the ceremony to avoid being deferred to the next cycle.

### Q2: Can I purchase extra tickets for guests?
ANU does not offer an option to purchase extra tickets. If more tickets are needed, graduates can join the waitlist, but the success rate is only 34% (2023 data). If the waitlist fails, guests are advised to watch the ceremony livestream on the ANU website, which is free and requires no registration.

### Q3: How do I collect my degree certificate after the ceremony?
Graduates attending in person collect their certificate at the ceremony. For graduates who do not attend, certificates are posted to the registered address within 4 weeks of the ceremony: AUD 20 domestic postage and AUD 50 international. If you need it urgently, you can pay AUD 30 for express delivery, which shortens processing time to 1 week.
