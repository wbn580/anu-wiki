The Australian National University (ANU) launched its Short-term Study Abroad Program for international students in 2024, offering university students around the world the chance to spend 2 to 6 weeks in Canberra during the winter or summer break (usually February to March or July to August) for an academic and cultural experience. According to the Annual Summary of International Student Data published by the Australian Department of Education in 2023, nationwide participation in short-term study abroad programs recovered to 78% of 2019 levels in 2022, with ANU hosting short-term exchange students equal to about 6.2% of its international student body (roughly 420 students). The program is managed centrally by the ANU Global Programs office and covers academic seminars, laboratory work and exploration of Indigenous Australian culture, with a transparent fee structure that includes tuition, accommodation and selected activities. As global higher-education exchange continues to recover, the program's **academic rigour** and **cultural immersion** have made it one of the most popular short-term study options for students in the Asia-Pacific region.

## Program Types and Academic Cycles

ANU's short-term study abroad program comes in two formats: the **Winter Program** (usually July to August) and the **Summer Program** (usually February to March). Each session runs for between 14 and 42 days, depending on the course modules chosen. According to the program handbook published by the ANU Global Programs office in 2024, the Winter Program offers 6 academic modules and the Summer Program offers 8, with each module worth 6 ANU units (equivalent to 1.5 US credits or 3 European ECTS credits).

The **academic modules** span four fields: humanities and social sciences, environmental science, international relations, and business management. For example, the "Australian Environmental Policy and Sustainable Development" module in the 2024 Summer Program includes 24 hours of classroom lectures and 18 hours of fieldwork, the latter involving hands-on data collection in Namadgi National Park near Canberra. Students complete the module by submitting a 3,000-word analytical report.

### Eligibility and Language Requirements

Applicants must be **non-Australian citizens** and full-time undergraduate or postgraduate students formally enrolled at an overseas university. According to the application guide updated on the ANU website in May 2024, undergraduate applicants must provide proof of a GPA of at least 3.0 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent). For language proficiency, applicants from non-English-speaking countries must submit an IELTS score of at least 6.5 overall (with no band below 6.0) or a TOEFL iBT score of at least 80. Some academic modules, such as "International Law and International Organisations", require an IELTS score of 7.0 or above.

### Certification and Credit Transfer

ANU issues an **official transcript** within 30 working days of the program's conclusion, listing the courses taken, credits earned and grades awarded (HD/D/C/P/N). Under the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF), ANU credits are recognised across all universities in Australia. For overseas students, credit transfer is assessed independently by the academic affairs office at their home institution. The ANU Global Programs office can provide the course syllabus and assessment criteria as reference documents for the transfer.

## Fee Structure and Budget Breakdown

The **total cost** of ANU's short-term study abroad program varies with program length and included services. Based on official figures published in 2024, the standard fee is A$3,200 for a 2-week program, A$5,400 for a 4-week program, and A$7,800 for a 6-week program. The fee covers tuition, on-campus accommodation (single room with three meals a day, 7 days a week), airport transfers, transport for some cultural activities, and basic health insurance (OSHC). It does not cover international return flights, personal shopping, visa application fees, or medical expenses beyond the basic insurance cover.

Looking at the breakdown, tuition accounts for 55% to 60% of the total fee. For a 4-week program, tuition comes to about A$3,000, accommodation and meals about A$1,800, and the remainder covers administration and activity organisation. When paying tuition across borders, some families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E5%81%87%E6%9C%9F%E7%9F%AD%E6%9C%9F%E6%B8%B8%E5%AD%A6%E9%A1%B9%E7%9B%AE%E4%BB%8B%E7%BB%8D%E4%B8%8E%E8%B4%B9%E7%94%A8) to complete the currency conversion, for more transparent exchange rates and trackable payment status.

### Optional Add-Ons

Students can pay extra to upgrade their experience. For example, upgrading from a single room to a suite (with a private ensuite bathroom) costs an additional A$120 per week, and optional weekend trips (such as a one-day Sydney excursion or a two-day Great Ocean Road tour) cost between A$150 and A$400 each. The 2024 program handbook also notes that all fees are quoted in Australian dollars and may be adjusted for inflation, so applicants should confirm the latest rates before paying.

### Scholarships and Funding

ANU offers **limited scholarships** for the short-term study abroad program, mainly aimed at applicants from developing countries in the Asia-Pacific region. In 2024 the scholarship pool totalled A$50,000, with individual awards of up to A$2,000 to offset program fees. Applicants must submit a 500-word personal statement and a letter of recommendation from their home university along with their program application. According to the ANU Global Programs office's 2023 annual report, the competition ratio for this scholarship is about 8:1.

## Accommodation and Daily Life

During the program, accommodation is arranged by ANU in **on-campus student residences**, mainly Bruce Hall and Ursula Hall at the Acton campus in Canberra. Rooms are standard singles with a bed, desk, wardrobe and internet connection. Shared facilities include kitchens, laundries and common rooms. For meals, the residence dining hall serves three meals a day, with menus that cater to international tastes, including vegetarian and halal options. According to ANU Accommodation Services data from 2024, walking time from the residences to teaching areas ranges from 5 to 12 minutes.

**Support services** include 24-hour security, weekly room cleaning, free Wi-Fi (20GB of data per student per week) and academic tutoring. On arrival day, program coordinators run a campus tour and a Canberra orientation session to help students find nearby supermarkets, banks and medical facilities. Canberra's public bus network (Action Buses) offers half-price fares to students with a valid student card, putting monthly transport costs at about A$80 to A$120.

### Visa and Insurance Requirements

All international students must apply for the short-term category of the **Australian Student Visa (Subclass 500)**. Under the Department of Home Affairs' 2024 visa policy, short-term study abroad programs (up to 12 weeks) may use the streamlined visa process, with processing usually taking 15 to 25 working days. Applicants must provide the Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) issued by ANU, a passport scan, proof of funds (covering at least the program fee and return flights) and proof of health insurance. ANU arranges OSHC for all program students, covering basic outpatient care, hospitalisation and emergency medical transport.

## Course Content and Teaching Methods

Teaching uses a **blended model** combining lectures, seminars, laboratory work and field trips. In the "Australian Biodiversity Conservation" module of the 2024 Winter Program, for example, students complete 12 hours of lectures, 8 hours of hands-on laboratory work extracting DNA, and two full days of field species surveys. Lecturers are all current ANU professors or senior researchers, and some courses are taught under the guidance of Fellows of the Australian Academy of Science.

**Assessment** varies by module. A typical combination is: class participation (10%), group presentation (30%), and written assignments (60%). All work must be submitted through Turnitin, with a similarity score below 15% required to pass. ANU teaching centre statistics for 2023 show that the average final grade for short-term program students was Credit (65-74), with about 22% of students achieving Distinction (75-84) or above.

### Cultural Activities

The program schedules at least one **cultural experience activity** per week, covering Indigenous Australian culture workshops (such as dot painting and bush food cooking), visits to Parliament House in Canberra, guided tours of the Australian War Memorial, and special exhibitions at the National Museum of Australia. New for 2024 is a one-day "eco-tour of Canberra's surrounding vineyards", where students can observe the winemaking process and take part in tastings (for those aged 18 and over). The cost of these activities is included in the total program fee.

## Application Process and Deadlines

The application process has four steps: **online application**, **document review**, **offer of admission** and **payment confirmation**. Applicants create an account on the Study Abroad Application Portal on the ANU Global Programs website, fill in their personal details, select program modules, and upload their transcript, language test results and passport copy. The application deadline is usually 8 weeks before the program starts — for example, the 2025 Winter Program (starting 14 July 2025) has a deadline of 19 May 2025.

The **review period** is 15 working days from receipt of complete documents. Admission results are sent by email and include the formal Offer Letter and payment instructions. Students must pay 50% of the total program fee as a deposit within 14 days of receiving the offer, with the balance due 30 days before the program starts. ANU accepts credit cards, bank wire transfers and third-party payment platforms such as Flywire.

### Cancellation and Refund Policy

Under ANU's 2024 refund terms, students who cancel more than 30 days before the program starts receive a full refund (minus a A$150 administration fee); cancellations 15 to 30 days out receive 50%; and cancellations within 14 days receive no refund. Cancellations due to visa refusal are fully refunded on presentation of the refusal letter. Refunds are processed within 30 working days of receiving the cancellation request.

## Program Strengths and Academic Value

ANU's short-term study abroad program stands out for its **academic rigour** and **accessibility of resources**. ANU ranked 34th in the 2024 QS World University Rankings, with its environmental science and politics disciplines ranked 17th and 10th globally respectively (QS 2024 subject rankings). Program students can use the ANU Library (with a collection of more than 2.5 million volumes), laboratories and sports facilities with the same access rights as formally enrolled students.

For **career development**, students receive an official ANU transcript and a program certificate on completion, and some modules can count towards graduation credit at their home institution. A 2023 survey by the ANU Global Programs office found that about 35% of short-term program participants applied for ANU master's or PhD programs within a year of graduating. In addition, the academic connections built during the program (such as professor recommendation letters) are seen by some students as a significant boost for further study.

### Short-Term vs Long-Term Study Abroad

Compared with a semester or full year of study abroad, short-term programs are more manageable in terms of **time commitment** and **financial pressure**. Using 2024 figures, the average tuition for one ANU semester (about 4 months) for international students is A$15,000 to A$20,000, whereas a 4-week short-term program costs only A$5,400 in total — about 27% to 36% of that amount. Short-term programs also skip the GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) assessment, making the visa process simpler. However, short-term programs cannot match long-term study abroad in total credits (a maximum of 6) or in the depth of cultural adaptation.

## FAQ

### Q1: What is the minimum GPA requirement for ANU's short-term study abroad program?

ANU requires undergraduate applicants to have a GPA of at least 3.0 on a 4.0 scale, or equivalent (such as a UK 2:1 classification, or 80 and above on the Chinese 100-point scale). Some highly competitive modules (such as international law) may require a GPA of 3.3 or higher. Applicants must submit an official transcript from their home university, and ANU completes the review within 15 working days.

### Q2: Do the program fees include international flights and visa fees?

No. The 2024 program fee covers tuition, on-campus accommodation (including three meals a day), airport transfers, transport for some cultural activities and basic health insurance. International return flights, visa application fees (about A$160), personal expenses and medical costs beyond OSHC cover are the student's responsibility. Students are advised to book flights at least 8 weeks in advance for better prices.

### Q3: Do I receive an ANU degree or certificate after a short-term program?

Short-term programs do not award a degree, but students receive an official ANU transcript and a program completion certificate. The transcript lists the courses taken, credits and grades, and can be used as evidence for credit transfer. The completion certificate confirms that the student finished the required contact hours and assessments, and can be included in a CV or further study applications.
