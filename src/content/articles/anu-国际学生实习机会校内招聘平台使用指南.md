The Australian National University (ANU) 2024 *Graduate Outcomes Survey* shows that 78.3% of international students with at least one internship found full-time work within 6 months of graduating, compared with just 52.1% for those without any internship experience【Australian Department of Education 2024, Graduate Outcomes Survey】. ANU's on-campus recruitment platform (CareerHub) is the university's officially designated internship and job information aggregation system, which posted more than 4,200 internships open to international students in 2023 across four sectors: government, research, not-for-profit, and business【ANU Careers 2023, Annual Activity Report】. This article walks through the full workflow, from account activation to job application, to help international students make the most of this on-campus resource.

## Platform Access and Account Activation

**CareerHub** is the dedicated recruitment system operated by ANU Careers, integrated with the student information system (ISIS) via single sign-on. All enrolled international students (including undergraduates, coursework postgraduates, and higher degree research students) automatically get access — no separate application is needed.

### First-Time Login Steps

Go to the ANU Careers homepage and click the "CareerHub" button to reach the login screen. Authenticate with your Uni ID and ISIS password. On your first login, you will be prompted to complete your personal profile, including your major, degree level, graduation year, visa type (e.g., Student visa subclass 500), and work rights status. **A fully completed profile** activates the platform's algorithm-based job matching: 2023 data shows that users with profiles more than 90% complete received an average of 3.2 matched job alerts per week.

### Mobile Experience

CareerHub uses responsive web design and works with mainstream iOS and Android browsers. The platform has no dedicated native app, but the mobile experience is fully functional, covering resume upload, job search, and online applications. Use the latest version of Chrome or Safari to keep the **PDF resume preview** working properly.

## Job Search and Filtering

CareerHub indexes its job database along four dimensions: "job type", "industry", "work rights requirement", and "location". International students should pay special attention to the "**International students can apply**" tag in the filter bar — employers tick this box manually when posting a role, and about 63% of jobs on the platform carried this tag in 2023.

### Advanced Search Tips

Use boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) in the search box to narrow your results. For example, typing "research AND (policy OR economics) NOT citizen" will pinpoint research-policy roles that do not require citizenship. The platform also supports filtering by **salary range**, but note that some internships are marked "unpaid" — under Fair Work Ombudsman rules, unpaid internships are only permitted for credit-bearing placements directly tied to course assessment.

### Job Alerts

You can set up combinations of keywords, industries, and job types, and CareerHub will push newly posted roles to you in a daily digest email. **Alert frequency** can be adjusted to instant, daily, or weekly under "Notification Settings". International students are advised to switch this on, because some popular roles (such as the Australian Public Service graduate internship program) are open for only 7 days from posting to application close.

## Resume and Cover Letter Upload Rules

CareerHub requires all application documents to be uploaded in PDF format, with a maximum file size of 5 MB. The platform's built-in **resume parser** (ATS-compatible) automatically extracts text into the online application form, but parsing accuracy depends on how cleanly your document is formatted.

### Resume Formatting Advice

Use standard fonts (such as Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman) at 10-12 pt, and avoid tables, text boxes, or graphics, which can cause ATS parsing failures. **File naming** should include your name and the application date, for example "ZhangSan_Resume_20250201.pdf". ANU Careers runs a weekly Resume Clinic where students can book a 20-minute one-on-one feedback session.

### Cover Letter Notes

Some roles require a separate cover letter, marked with a red asterisk in the job description. Keep it to **300-400 words**, and be sure to mention the Job ID and how your skills map to the role requirements. The platform lets you save multiple cover letter templates so you can adapt them quickly for different roles.

## Application Process and Status Tracking

Once you submit an application, CareerHub generates a unique Application ID — the only reference you will need when following up with employers or ANU Careers. The platform has no application withdrawal function, but you can replace attachments via the "**Update Application**" option before the employer has viewed them.

### Understanding Application Statuses

CareerHub application statuses run through five stages: Submitted, Viewed, Shortlisted, Interview Scheduled, and Closed. A "**Viewed**" status means the employer has opened your application file, not that you have advanced to the next round. Platform data from 2023 puts the average time from submission to interview invitation at 18 days.

### Managing Interview Invitations

When an employer sends an interview invitation through CareerHub, the platform automatically creates a calendar event and sends you an email notification. International students should confirm attendance within 48 hours of receiving the invitation. If visa or course schedule conflicts mean you need to reschedule, use the built-in "**Reschedule**" feature to propose alternative time slots, and the system will send your new proposal to the employer.

## Applying for Credit-Bearing Internships

ANU offers two credit-bearing internship programs tied to coursework: ANU Internship (course codes ANIP3001/ANIP7001) and Global Internships. These programs must be applied for through CareerHub's dedicated "**Credit Internship**" channel, not through regular job search.

### Eligibility Requirements

Credit-bearing internships require you to have completed at least 48 credit points (roughly one year of full-time study) at the time of application, with a weighted average mark (GPA) of at least 5.0/7.0. International students must also hold a valid student visa and ensure the internship does not exceed the 48-hour-per-fortnight work limit attached to their visa. **Course coordinator review** is a mandatory step — you must upload a study plan and an internship goals statement in CareerHub.

### Internship Assessment and Credit Conversion

After completing a credit-bearing internship, submit your internship report and the employer evaluation form through CareerHub. ANU grants credit based on internship length: a 6-week internship earns 6 credit points, and a 12-week internship earns 12 credit points. **Grading** is Pass/Fail and does not count toward your GPA.

## Employer Network and Recruitment Events

ANU Careers has integrated more than 3,500 registered employers into CareerHub, including large organisations such as the Australian Public Service Commission (APS), Deloitte, KPMG, National Australia Bank, and CSIRO. **Employer brand pages** showcase culture videos, employee reviews, and historical hiring data, giving international students a sense of how welcoming each employer is to diverse backgrounds.

### Virtual Career Fairs

CareerHub runs two large virtual career fairs each year, in March and August, attracting an average of 120 employers and 2,000 students per event. **Virtual booths** support live chat, video interviews, and resume submissions. Upload your resume and update your profile on CareerHub before the fair — some employers shortlist and contact candidates directly during the event.

### Employer Information Sessions

Employers post information sessions in CareerHub's "Events Calendar" module, covering recruitment processes, interview techniques, and industry trends. **Session recordings** are usually uploaded to the platform within 7 business days, so students who cannot attend live can book in to watch later. In 2023, the Australian Department of Home Affairs ran 3 policy briefing sessions on the Temporary Graduate visa (Subclass 485) through the platform, with cumulative viewership of more than 1,800.

## Common Questions and Technical Support

CareerHub's **Knowledge Base** holds more than 200 FAQ articles covering login problems, file upload errors, and application withdrawal scenarios. Access it via the "Help" button in the bottom-right corner of the platform.

### Technical Support Channels

On business days (Monday to Friday, 9:00-17:00 AEST) you can submit technical issues through CareerHub's built-in ticket system, with an average response time of 4 hours. For **urgent issues** (such as being unable to log in and missing an application deadline), call the ANU Service Desk (+61 2 6125 4321), and have your Application ID and error screenshots ready. Outside business hours, the Knowledge Base covers most common problems.

### Data Privacy

CareerHub complies with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), and all user data is stored on AWS servers located in Australia. **Resume visibility** can be set to "visible to employers only" or "fully private" in your privacy settings. The platform does not automatically share student information with third parties, but employers who view your application will see your name, contact details, education, and work experience.

## FAQ

### Q1: Can international students find paid internships on CareerHub?
Yes. In 2023, 71.4% of internships posted on CareerHub were marked "paid", with an average hourly rate of AUD 28.50 (about RMB 135)【ANU Careers 2023, Annual Activity Report】. Paid roles are concentrated in business, engineering, and information technology; the share of paid internships in government is lower, at about 34%.

### Q2: Do internships on CareerHub have visa requirements?
Yes. About 82% of roles explicitly require applicants to hold a visa with "no work restrictions" (such as Australian citizens, permanent residents, or holders of a 485 graduate visa)【ANU Careers 2023, Job Posting Statistics】. International students (subclass 500 visa holders) can apply for roles tagged "International students can apply", which usually accept the 48-hour-per-fortnight work limit.

### Q3: Do I need to arrange my own accommodation for internships applied for through CareerHub?
Yes. CareerHub does not offer accommodation arrangements. If the internship is outside Canberra, check the short-term rental listings published by ANU Accommodation Services, or arrange something yourself through third-party platforms. Some large employers (such as the Australian Public Service) offer interns an accommodation allowance — the amount is stated in the job description.
