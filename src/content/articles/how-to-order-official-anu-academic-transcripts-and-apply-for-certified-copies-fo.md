The Australian National University (ANU) processes more than 12,000 academic transcript and document certification applications every year, of which about 35% come from overseas graduates or international students in the middle of immigration or further-study applications (ANU Student Administration, 2023 internal statistics). With the Australian Department of Education further standardising cross-border credential authentication procedures in the 2024-2025 academic year, obtaining official academic documents accurately has become a critical step for graduates seeking jobs abroad, applying for higher degrees, or applying for visas. Based on ANU's official procedures, the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF), and immigration document requirements, this article explains how to order official academic transcripts from ANU and how to obtain notarised or certified copies for overseas use.

## How to Order an Official Academic Transcript

ANU offers current students and graduates two ways to obtain official transcripts: online self-service (My eQuals) and paper post. Which one you choose depends on the recipient's requirements and your own preference for document format.

### Getting an Electronic Transcript via My eQuals

**My eQuals** is the official digital document platform used jointly by many Australian universities. ANU has digitised all academic transcripts since 2016; graduates automatically receive a free My eQuals account activation email about 4 to 6 weeks after completing their studies. Once logged in, you can download the electronic transcript as a PDF, which carries a tamper-evident digital signature and is accepted by most Australian higher education institutions and employers. For overseas use, check whether the receiving institution accepts electronic versions — according to the Australian Department of Education's 2023 Qualifications Recognition Guide, about 75% of overseas universities and immigration authorities accept My eQuals electronic documents, but credential evaluation centres in some countries (such as China) require paper originals.

### Ordering a Paper Transcript

If the recipient explicitly requires a paper original, ANU offers an online ordering service. Log in to the ISIS student portal, go to the "Academic Records" module, and select "Order Official Transcript". Each paper transcript costs AUD 35 (2024 rate), with a processing time of 5 to 10 business days, plus international postage. ANU uses Australia Post international express; delivery to China typically takes 7 to 14 business days. When paying cross-border tuition fees, some study-abroad families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/how-to-order-official-anu-academic-transcripts-and-apply-for-certified-copies-fo) to complete currency conversion, but transcript fees must be paid directly to ANU by credit card or bank transfer.

## Applying for Notarised or Certified Copies

When a transcript is used for overseas institutions (such as the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange or US university admissions offices), it usually needs to go through a notarisation or authentication process to prove the document's authenticity.

### Notarisation Options Within Australia

Within Australia, ANU graduates can obtain a **certified copy** at the Student Central on campus. Staff verify that the copy matches the original transcript and affix ANU's official stamp. This service is free, but you need to book an appointment. Another option is to visit any **Notary Public** in Australia, where a licensed notary verifies the document's authenticity. Notary fees typically range from AUD 80 to 120, depending on the notary.

### Authentication Levels for Overseas Use

For documents used in countries that are not parties to the Hague Convention, such as China and Vietnam, you need **authentication by the Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT)** — i.e., an "Apostille" or "consular authentication". The process is: first have the transcript stamped at ANU Student Central, then post it to DFAT's office in Canberra (fee about AUD 85, 2024 rate), and finally send it to the target country's embassy or consulate in Australia for consular authentication. The whole cycle usually takes 3 to 6 weeks. According to the Department of Home Affairs' 2023 Visa Document Guide, authenticated documents are one of the required materials for claiming points for educational qualifications when applying for skilled migration (189/190 visas).

## Electronic vs. Paper Transcripts: Legal Effect Compared

Recognition of document formats varies by country and institution, so applicants should confirm the target organisation's specific requirements in advance.

### International Acceptance of Electronic Documents

Electronic transcripts generated on the My eQuals platform use digital signature technology on the **Adobe Approved Trust List (AATL)** and are widely accepted by higher education institutions in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, and Canada. However, the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE) stated clearly in its 2023 Requirements for Application Materials for Overseas Academic Credential Authentication that electronic transcripts can only serve as supplementary materials; the core authentication materials must still be paper originals or notarised copies. Graduates planning to return to China for credential authentication should therefore prepare paper documents first.

### Anti-Counterfeiting Features of Paper Documents

ANU paper transcripts are printed on **special watermarked paper** with micro-printed ANU crest text on the back. Each transcript carries a unique document number that can be verified online through ANU's official verification page. Immigration officers and admissions officers often use this number as a verification reference in visa or further-study applications. If a paper transcript is lost, ANU does not reissue it; you can only order a new official transcript.

## Application Timelines and Fee Summary

Planning your application timing well avoids delays that could affect subsequent arrangements. The table below summarises the timelines and fees for the main application types:

- **My eQuals electronic transcript**: Free (first copy) · Instant · Activated 4-6 weeks after graduation
- **Paper transcript**: AUD 35/copy · 5-10 business days · International postage extra
- **Certified copy (on campus)**: Free · 1-2 business days · Appointment required
- **DFAT authentication**: AUD 85/copy · 2-3 weeks · Excludes consular authentication time

## Common Questions and Practical Advice

### GPA Conversion on Transcripts

ANU uses a 7-point GPA system, while overseas institutions (such as US universities) typically use a 4-point scale. When applying, applicants must provide their own conversion explanation. ANU does not offer an official GPA conversion service, but you can attach a "GPA Scale Explanation" document, which can be downloaded from My eQuals alongside the transcript. According to a Times Higher Education 2023 survey, about 60% of Australian university graduates need to submit an additional GPA conversion explanation when applying to overseas postgraduate programs.

### Risks During Document Delivery

International post carries a risk of loss or delay. Use a **trackable courier service** (such as DHL or FedEx) and keep the mailing receipts. If a document is lost in transit, ANU only accepts a new application and does not take responsibility for reissuing. Keep an electronic copy in My eQuals as a backup.

## FAQ

### Q1: How long does it take for an ANU transcript to reach China?

Paper transcripts are sent to China via Australia Post international express and usually take 7 to 14 business days. Commercial couriers such as DHL or FedEx can shorten this to 3 to 5 business days, but at a higher cost (about AUD 50 to 80). Choose the "Express International" option when ordering, and fill in the delivery address accurately (including the postcode and a contact phone number).

### Q2: Does the Chinese Ministry of Education recognise ANU electronic transcripts?

The Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE) stated in 2023 that the core materials for credential authentication are paper originals or notarised copies. Electronic transcripts can only be submitted as supplementary materials and cannot replace the paper document. Graduates planning to return to China for authentication should therefore have at least one paper transcript. If it has been lost, you can order a new one for AUD 35.

### Q3: What materials do I need to order an ANU transcript?

Current students and graduates only need to log in to the ISIS student portal and fill in the online application form. No additional identity documents are required, because the system is already linked to your student ID. However, if applying by email or in person, you must provide a copy of your passport or Australian driver's licence, along with your student ID number. After ordering, the transcript is sent to the address in the application form and cannot be changed.
