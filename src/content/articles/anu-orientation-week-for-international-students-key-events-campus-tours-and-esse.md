Orientation Week (O-Week) at the Australian National University (ANU) is the first official step for international students arriving in Canberra to settle into campus life. According to ANU website data, more than 4,500 new international students registered for O-Week in semester 1 2024, accounting for 37% of total enrolments that intake [ANU, 2024, Orientation Statistics]. A 2023 report from the Australian Department of Education shows that whether international students complete administrative registration and social integration within the first four weeks after arrival directly affects their first-semester academic retention rate by about 12 percentage points [Australian Department of Education, 2023, International Student Retention Report]. Drawing on the ANU official schedule and established operating procedures, this article systematically covers the five core modules international students must pay attention to: mandatory registration steps, academic transition activities, campus navigation, community integration opportunities and visa compliance essentials.

## Mandatory Administrative Registration Steps

Before attending any social activities, international students must complete ANU's online registration process. The process has two stages: **pre-enrolment** and **in-person verification**.

### Online Pre-Enrolment Deadlines

Pre-enrolment usually opens 6 weeks before the start of classes, with the deadline on the Friday before O-Week begins. Students must upload the following documents through the ANU ISIS system (Integrated Student Information System): the passport information page, the Visa Grant Letter for the student visa (Subclass 500), and proof of English language proficiency (such as an IELTS results slip). ANU updated the system in 2024 with a new **biometric information matching** step: the uploaded photo must match the passport photo, otherwise it triggers manual review, extending processing time by 3 to 5 business days [ANU, 2024, Enrolment Policy Update].

### In-Person Verification

All international students must complete in-person verification at ANU Student Central (located in the Hancock Building) during O-Week. The verification window is Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 4:30 pm. You must bring your original passport and a printed copy of your Visa Grant Letter. Students who do not complete verification before the end of O-Week will have their course enrolment automatically cancelled and must pay a 250 Australian dollar reactivation fee [ANU, 2024, Student Administration Procedures].

## Academic Transition Activities

ANU runs dedicated academic transition activities for international students to help them adapt to Australia's **academic integrity system** and **grading standards**.

### Academic Skills Program Workshops

These workshops are run by the ANU Academic Skills Centre, with 3 sessions per day during O-Week, each lasting 90 minutes. Content includes: how to avoid unintentional plagiarism, the score ranges corresponding to common Australian university grades (HD, DI, CR, PS, FL), and the correct use of the Turnitin system. According to 2023 data, international students who attended these workshops had an average first-semester GPA 0.43 points higher (out of 7.0) than those who did not [ANU, 2023, Academic Skills Impact Report].

### College Orientation Sessions

Each college runs its own orientation sessions, usually scheduled from Tuesday to Thursday of O-Week. For example, the ANU College of Engineering, Computing and Cybernetics arranged laboratory safety training and an introduction to project-based learning (PBL) in 2024, while the ANU College of Arts and Social Sciences focused on participation requirements for tutorial-style teaching. Students should check the college on their letter of offer and reserve a seat in advance through the Orientation App.

## Campus Navigation and Transport

The Canberra campus covers around 145 hectares, so international students need to know the locations of the main teaching buildings, libraries and transport hubs.

### Campus Tours

During O-Week, ANU runs free guided tours daily at 10:00 am and 2:00 pm, departing from Union Court and lasting about 75 minutes. The route covers Chifley Library (open 24 hours), the Marie Reay Teaching Centre (the main lecture theatre building) and the Kambri Precinct (student services and dining area). Tours must be booked in advance on the ANU Orientation website, with a limit of 30 people per group. In 2024, an **accessible route** version was added for students with mobility needs.

### Canberra Public Transport Card (MyWay Card)

International students can collect a MyWay student card from Student Central during O-Week. ANU has partnered with Transport Canberra to offer **half-price student fares** (about 2.30 Australian dollars per single trip, versus 4.60 Australian dollars for an adult ticket). Note that the MyWay card must be activated online within 14 days of first use, otherwise adult fares will be charged [Transport Canberra, 2024, MyWay Card Policy].

## Community Integration and Social Activities

Social activities are an important part of O-Week, and ANU has designed various formats to help international students build their **initial social networks**.

### International Student Welcome Dinner

This event is held on the Wednesday of O-Week at 5:30 pm in the Kambri Precinct, hosted by ANU International Student Services. Free meals were provided in 2024, including halal and vegetarian options. Registration is via the Eventbrite page in advance, with capacity limited to 800 people, usually filling up within 48 hours of opening. The event features **cultural display stalls** run by student societies from countries including China, India and Malaysia.

### Market Day

Market Day is held on the Thursday of O-Week at Union Court. ANU has more than 200 registered student societies, of which around 60 are directly relevant to international students, such as the ANU Chinese Students and Scholars Association (CSSA) and the ANU Indian Cultural Society. Students can pay society membership fees on the day (usually 5 to 15 Australian dollars) and pick up activity calendars. When paying cross-border tuition, some student families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-orientation-week-for-international-students-key-events-campus-tours-and-esse) to complete remittances.

## Visa Compliance and Work Rights

During O-Week, international students should confirm their **student visa conditions** to avoid unintentional breaches.

### Work Rights Session

ANU runs a visa work rights information session on Friday of O-Week at 10:00 am, co-presented by ANU Legal Services and representatives of the Australian Department of Home Affairs. Content covers: the 40-hour-per-fortnight work limit (the standard restored in July 2023), the Tax File Number (TFN) application process, and superannuation payment obligations. In 2024, a new session was added on the transition arrangements for the **Temporary Graduate visa (Subclass 485)**, for students planning to stay and work in Australia after completing their course [Department of Home Affairs, 2024, Student Visa Work Conditions].

### OSHC Activation

All international students must hold Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC). ANU's partner insurer is Allianz Global Assistance, and the insurance card is sent by email within 5 to 7 business days of enrolment. During O-Week, Allianz has an advisory counter at Student Central to help with card activation and claims. Students who do not activate their OSHC will have their course access suspended, including library and online learning platforms [ANU, 2024, OSHC Compliance Policy].

## FAQ

### Q1: Do I have to attend all of O-Week?
Yes. International students must attend at least 3 activities: online pre-enrolment, in-person verification, and the college orientation session. Students who miss these three are considered to have incomplete enrolment procedures, which may affect first-semester course registration. According to ANU 2023 data, about 8% of new international students had their enrolment delayed because they did not attend in-person verification [ANU, 2023, Enrollment Compliance Data].

### Q2: Can I change courses during O-Week?
Yes, but there are time limits. The course change (Add/Drop) window is open Monday to Friday of O-Week, closing at 5:00 pm on Friday. Changing courses after that incurs an administrative fee of 100 Australian dollars per course. It's best to consult an academic advisor at your college orientation before deciding, because some courses have prerequisite restrictions.

### Q3: How do international students apply for a Tax File Number (TFN)?
TFN applications are completed through the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) online system. During O-Week, ANU Legal Services runs form-filling assistance workshops on Thursday from 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm. After applying, it usually takes 28 business days to receive your TFN letter. Holding a TFN is a prerequisite for legal work; in 2024 the work limit for student visa holders was 40 hours per fortnight [Australian Taxation Office, 2024, TFN Application Guidelines].
