## What happened to ISIS?

As of Monday 26 May 2025, the Interactive Student Information System — previously known as ISIS — at The Australian National University has been renamed ANUHub. That is the current name for the system students use when dealing with course enrolment.

Enrolment itself depends on completing certain tasks. Some tasks are mandatory in order to enable course enrolment, so the enrolment step only opens up once those required tasks are done.

## Internal transfer within ANU: what the entry requirements cover

For students thinking about changing programs rather than just changing courses, ANU publishes minimum entry requirements for internal undergraduate transfer within ANU, and it also publishes a separate ANU GPA requirement. For law, the published entry requirements are framed under BLaw(Hons) / FDD Law(Hons) minimum entry requirements for internal postgraduate transfer within ANU, alongside the general minimum entry requirements for internal postgraduate transfer within ANU.

![A student working through online course enrolment on a laptop](https://img.anu.wiki/留学-单校wiki/anu-isis-internal-transfer-gpa-prerequisite-2026-09-10-511993b5f6-image-02-2026-1880x1253.jpg)


ANU lists the ANU Medical School, ANU Extension, Bachelor of Studies, and Diploma of Studies programs as exclusions from the transfer application route, and directs students to contact their College for further information about those programs.

Once an application is submitted and assessed, the outcome is communicated in a particular way: you will be advised of the outcome of your program transfer application by email to your ANU student email account. Monitoring that student email account is therefore the practical way the result reaches you. A Program Transfer Guide is listed as a reference document, published as a PDF.

## Admissions context: GPA scales and institution background

Some admission-related figures published by ANU are tied to the institution a student has previously studied at. Admission requirements can vary if you have studied at multiple institutions or had credits transferred.

For China, ANU publishes percentage-based schedules alongside an ANU GPA. ANU also points students to the most up-to-date admission requirements for their preferred program, since admission to some ANU programs is handled differently.

## Completing or discontinuing your program: account closure timing

The end of a program has its own administrative timeline. If you complete your program in semester one and graduate in July, your ANU student account will be closed from 31 August. If you complete your program in semester two and graduate in December, your ANU student account will be closed from 31 March the following year.

It is important to keep records of the important documents and information that you have stored in ANU student systems before you complete or discontinue your program. The relevant transition and leaving information is grouped under Transition & leaving ANU.
