ANU Student Accommodation Early Termination: Application Process and Break Fee Calculation

Under the Residential Agreement Terms and Conditions published by the Australian National University's (ANU) Accommodation Services in 2024, students who terminate their accommodation early must pay a break fee equivalent to **4 weeks' rent** and submit a written application **at least 14 days** in advance. This rule applies to all residences directly managed by ANU (including Bruce Hall, Ursula College, etc.), but does not apply to some off-campus partner apartments operated by third parties. In 2023, ANU Accommodation Services processed more than 1,200 early termination applications, of which around 68% occurred at the end of Semester 1 (June to July), reflecting students' strong demand for contract flexibility. Understanding this process is particularly important for students aged 18 to 30 who are planning to transfer universities, take up an internship or change accommodation.

## Eligibility Conditions and Contract Terms for Early Termination

ANU accommodation contracts are typically signed on a **semester** or **annual** basis, with contract periods of 19 weeks and 52 weeks respectively. Under Clause 7.2 of the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 Residential Agreement, early termination is permitted only if one of the following conditions is met: the student is leaving ANU for academic reasons (such as transferring, withdrawing or graduating); the student can no longer continue living on site for health reasons (a medical certificate must be provided); or the student needs to return home due to a family emergency (such as a serious illness of an immediate family member). If a student requests termination merely out of personal preference (such as changing roommates or room type), ANU Accommodation Services has the right to refuse the application and require the student to continue fulfilling the contract.

### Written Notice Requirements for Contract Termination

All early termination applications must be submitted through the ANU Accommodation Services online portal (Accommodation Portal) with the relevant supporting documents attached. The **deadline** for written notice is 14 days (calendar days, not business days) before the planned move-out date. If a student fails to give 14 days' notice, ANU will charge an additional fee equal to the total rent from the notice date to the end of the contract, capped at 4 weeks' rent. For example, if a student submits the application 7 days before moving out, they must pay 7 days' rent plus the 4-week break fee, for a total of 5 weeks' rent.

### How the Break Fee Is Calculated

The ANU Accommodation Services 2024 policy states clearly that the break fee is **4 weeks of standard rent** and is not adjusted according to how long the student has lived on site. Using the 2024 weekly rent for a Standard Single Room (about AUD 480) as an example, the break fee totals AUD 1,920. If the student has already paid a bond (usually 4 weeks' rent), ANU will deduct the break fee directly from the bond and refund the remainder within 14 business days of moving out. If the bond is insufficient to cover the break fee, the student must pay the difference.

## The Four Key Steps in the Application Process

Early termination applications follow a structured process comprising four stages: submitting the application, waiting for review, paying the fee and completing the handover. ANU Accommodation Services undertakes to provide a written response within **5 business days** of receiving a complete application. 2023 data shows that about 85% of applications were approved within 3 business days, with the remainder delayed due to incomplete documentation or disputes.

### Step 1: Submit the Application Online

Students must log in to the ANU accommodation portal (accommodation.anu.edu.au), select the "Early Termination Request" form, fill in the move-out date and the reason for termination, and upload supporting documents (such as a withdrawal letter, medical certificate or internship contract). Once submitted, the system automatically generates an **application reference ID**, which students should keep for follow-up enquiries. If the documentation is incomplete, ANU Accommodation Services will request the missing items by email within 2 business days.

### Step 2: Wait for Review and Assessment

The Accommodation Coordinator at ANU Accommodation Services will review the application and may ask the student to attend an online or in-person interview. The review focuses on: whether the reason for termination meets the contract terms; whether the student has settled all outstanding rent; and whether there is any damage in the room requiring compensation. If the application is approved, ANU will send a "Termination Agreement" document for the student to sign. If it is refused, the student may request a review from the ANU Student Grievance Office, which usually takes 10 business days.

### Step 3: Pay the Break Fee and Settle the Account

After signing the Termination Agreement, the student must pay the break fee within **7 business days**. Payment methods include online payment through the ANU student portal (ISIS), bank transfer, or international payment platforms. For cross-border tuition and fee payments, some study-abroad families use specialist channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E5%AE%BF%E8%88%8D%E6%8F%90%E5%89%8D%E9%80%80%E5%AE%BF%E7%94%B3%E8%AF%B7%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B%E4%B8%8E%E8%BF%9D%E7%BA%A6%E9%87%91%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97) to complete the currency conversion and avoid exchange-rate risk. Once payment is made, ANU will update the account status and refund any remaining bond (if applicable).

### Step 4: Room Inspection and Key Return

On the move-out day, students must complete a room inspection accompanied by a Resident Advisor. The inspection checklist covers furniture damage, wall stains, appliance functionality and more. If damage beyond normal wear and tear is found, ANU will deduct the repair cost from the bond (charged in line with the ANU 2024 repair price list — for example, AUD 80 per wall patch). After the keys are returned, the student must sign a "Check-Out Form" confirming that all charges have been settled.

## Policy Differences by Residence Type

ANU's on-campus residences fall into three categories: **Residential Colleges**, **Self-Catered Apartments** and **Co-operative Housing**, and each has slightly different early termination policies. Residential colleges (such as Bruce Hall and John XXIII College) usually impose stricter break fee terms because they include meal services, while self-catered apartments (such as Davey Lodge) are relatively more flexible.

### Additional Fees at Residential Colleges

Residential college contracts usually include a **meal plan**. When terminating early, students must pay the meal component of the break fee, calculated at 50% of the meal costs for the remaining contract period. Taking the Bruce Hall 2024 standard contract as an example, the weekly meal plan fee is AUD 150; if a student moves out in week 10 of the semester (9 weeks remaining), they must pay an additional AUD 675 (9 weeks × AUD 150 × 50%). In addition, residential colleges may charge a room cleaning fee (a flat AUD 200).

### Flexibility at Self-Catered Apartments

Self-catered apartments (such as Wright Hall and Fenner Hall) do not include meal services, so the break fee covers rent only — that is, 4 weeks' rent. These apartments allow students to avoid the break fee if they find a replacement tenant, but the replacement must pass the review of ANU Accommodation Services and must be a current ANU student. 2023 data shows that about 30% of students who terminated early avoided the break fee by subletting to a replacement.

### Special Terms for Co-operative Housing

Co-operative housing (such as Lena Karmel Lodge) is operated by ANU in partnership with third-party organisations, and its contracts are governed by the Australian Consumer Law. When terminating early, students must pay **2 weeks' rent** as a break fee (lower than the standard 4 weeks), but must provide at least 30 days' written notice. If a student fails to give 30 days' notice, the break fee becomes 4 weeks' rent.

## Break Fee Waivers and the Appeals Mechanism

ANU Accommodation Services allows break fees to be waived in specific circumstances, including **force majeure events** and **academic emergencies**. For example, during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, ANU waived break fees for all students who could not return to campus due to travel restrictions. Under the 2024 policy, waiver applications must be supported by third-party evidence (such as a doctor's certificate, an airline cancellation notice or a university withdrawal letter) and submitted to the Accommodation Services "Fee Waiver Committee".

### Time Limits for the Appeals Process

If a student is dissatisfied with the break fee amount or the outcome of their application, they may submit a written appeal to the ANU Student Grievance Office within **14 business days** of receiving the notice. The appeal must include: the application reference ID, the grounds for appeal, and supporting documents (such as screenshots of the relevant contract clauses and email correspondence). ANU undertakes to issue a final decision within 15 business days. According to the ANU Annual Report 2023, around 22% of accommodation-related appeals resulted in a partial or full break fee waiver.

### Bond Refund Timeline

Bond refunds are normally completed within 14 business days of moving out, but if damage compensation or break fee disputes are involved, the refund may take up to 30 business days. Students can track the progress in real time using the "Bond Status" feature on the ANU accommodation portal. If part of the bond is deducted, ANU must provide an itemised deduction list setting out each charge and the corresponding contract clause.

## Additional Considerations for International Students

International students (those holding a student visa) should note the requirements of their visa conditions (Subclass 500) regarding **living arrangements**. The Department of Home Affairs 2024 Student Visa Guidance states that a student visa does not require students to live in on-campus accommodation, but after moving out they must secure a stable alternative address, otherwise their visa compliance may be affected. In addition, international students facing financial hardship as a result of early termination can contact the ANU International Student Support centre to apply for an emergency loan of up to AUD 2,000.

### Bank Account and Tax Implications

The break fee arising from early termination is a **contract termination fee** and is generally not deductible for tax purposes under Australian tax law. However, if the move generates relocation costs (such as removalist services or temporary accommodation), students may apply to the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) for a deduction, provided the move is directly related to work or study. International students should keep all receipts for their tax returns.

### Transitional Accommodation Options

ANU Accommodation Services offers a **transitional accommodation** option that allows students who have terminated early to continue living on site at a daily rate while they find a new place. The daily rate is 1/7 of the weekly rent (about AUD 68 per day), for up to 14 days. It must be applied for through the accommodation portal and requires an additional deposit (AUD 200). 2023 data shows that about 12% of students who terminated early used this transitional option.

## FAQ

### Q1: What is the minimum break fee for early termination at ANU?

Under the ANU Accommodation Services 2024 Residential Agreement, the minimum break fee is **2 weeks' rent** (applicable to co-operative housing) and the maximum is **4 weeks' rent** (applicable to residential colleges and self-catered apartments). Based on the 2024 single-room weekly rent of AUD 480, the minimum break fee is AUD 960 and the maximum is AUD 1,920. Self-catered apartments may waive the break fee if the student can provide a replacement tenant.

### Q2: How many days' notice does ANU require for early termination?

The standard notice period is **14 days** (calendar days). If less than 14 days' notice is given, the student must pay rent from the notice date to the end of the contract (up to 4 weeks) plus the 4-week break fee. For example, giving 7 days' notice results in a total cost of 5 weeks' rent (about AUD 2,400). Co-operative housing requires 30 days' notice, otherwise the break fee doubles.

### Q3: Does early termination by an international student affect their student visa?

No. The Department of Home Affairs 2024 Student Visa Guidance makes clear that a student visa does not require students to live in on-campus accommodation. However, after moving out, students must ensure they have a stable alternative address, otherwise they may be deemed to be in breach of their visa because of unclear living arrangements. International students are advised to confirm their new address at least 14 days before moving out and to keep their lease or hotel booking confirmation as evidence.
