The Australian National University (ANU) awards more than A$20 million in scholarships to international students every year, covering undergraduate through to PhD level. Under ANU's official 2024 Scholarships Terms and Conditions document, all continuing scholarships require recipients to maintain a **minimum weighted average (GPA) of 4.0/7.0** (equivalent to a Distinction level) as well as **at least a 75% full-time study load each semester** (i.e., at least 18 units per semester). Falling short in any semester triggers scholarship suspension or termination procedures. With ANU ranked 30th in the 2023 QS World University Rankings, this policy has a direct impact on the academic planning of the roughly 8,000 international students enrolled each year (Australian Department of Education, 2023 International Student Data).

## The Core Renewal Metrics: GPA and Study Load

The two hard requirements for renewing an ANU international scholarship are the **GPA threshold** and the **minimum enrolment load**. According to the Scholarship Holder Handbook published by the ANU Scholarships Office in 2024, undergraduate scholarships require a GPA of at least 4.0/7.0 each semester, while postgraduate scholarships require at least 5.0/7.0 (Credit level). On study load, all scholarship holders must enrol in **at least 18 units per semester** (a standard full-time load is 24 units) — that is, a 75% full-time load. Falling below this threshold immediately triggers the warning mechanism.

### Credit Conversion and Exemptions

ANU uses the **ANU credit system**: a standard course is worth 6 units, so 3 courses make up the 18-unit floor. Students seeking a **credit reduction** for medical or family emergencies must submit evidence to the ANU Academic Board in advance; if approved, they keep their scholarship eligibility. However, under section 5.2 of ANU's 2023 Academic Progress Policy, an unapproved credit reduction leads to automatic scholarship suspension.

### How GPA Is Calculated and Reviewed

**GPA** is calculated on the ANU 7-point scale, weighting each course by its grade band (HD=7, D=6, CR=5, P=4, Fail=0). Students who believe a grade is wrong can request a recheck through the **ANU grade review system** within 20 business days of results release; the review costs 50 Australian dollars, refunded if it succeeds. A review does not change your scholarship status, but if your GPA is still below the threshold after review, the scholarship is suspended from the following semester.

## Suspension and Termination Procedures

Failing to meet the renewal conditions triggers a **three-tier process**. Tier 1 is a **warning letter**: within 2 weeks of results release, the ANU Scholarships Office emails a warning and grants a 1-semester probation period. Tier 2 is **scholarship suspension**: if you still fall short after the probation period, the scholarship is suspended for 1 semester and no payments are made during that time. Tier 3 is **permanent termination**: after two consecutive semesters below standard or a total of three warnings, the scholarship is permanently cancelled and cannot be appealed.

### How to Be Reinstated After Suspension

During suspension, students can apply for reinstatement through an **academic improvement plan**, which requires a written statement, a proposed course schedule adjustment and a supervisor's reference letter. According to ANU's 2024 Scholarship Reinstatement Guide, about 35% of suspended students are successfully reinstated the following semester. After reinstatement, you must hold a GPA of at least 4.0 for two consecutive semesters to return to the normal renewal track.

### Force Majeure Provisions

ANU recognises **special circumstances exemptions**, covering serious illness, the death of a family member or natural disasters. Students must submit a **special circumstances application form** with third-party evidence (such as a hospital diagnosis or death certificate) within 4 weeks of results release. If approved, the semester is excluded from GPA assessment and the scholarship renews automatically. According to ANU Student Services 2023 data, about 12% of scholarship suspension cases were exempted under this provision.

## Renewal Differences by Scholarship Type

ANU's main international scholarships differ slightly in renewal conditions, chiefly in the **GPA threshold** and **renewal frequency**. For example, the **ANU Chancellor's International Scholarship** requires a GPA of at least 5.5/7.0 each semester and renews once a year, while the **ANU International Merit Scholarship** requires a GPA of at least 4.5/7.0 and is assessed every semester. The **ANU Research Scholarship**, meanwhile, requires a research progress report approved by the supervisor, with GPA used only as a reference indicator.

### Undergraduate vs Postgraduate Scholarships

**Undergraduate scholarships** usually require a GPA of at least 4.0 each semester and a **study load** of no less than 18 units. **Postgraduate coursework scholarships** require a GPA of at least 5.0 and completion of at least 3 courses (18 units) per semester. **Postgraduate research scholarships** have no fixed GPA requirement but require passing an **annual research progress review** assessed jointly by the supervisor and the college. According to ANU's 2024 Scholarship Handbook, the renewal success rate for postgraduate research scholarships is about 88%, above the 72% for coursework scholarships.

### Coordinating External and ANU Scholarships

If a student holds both an **external scholarship** (such as the Australian Government's Endeavour Scholarship) and an **ANU scholarship**, the renewal conditions of each must be followed. ANU allows students to hold both, but the combined amount must not exceed 150% of tuition plus living costs. Students must submit a **dual scholarship declaration** to the ANU Scholarships Office; failing to do so can lead to the ANU scholarship being suspended.

## Alternatives If Renewal Fails

If a scholarship is terminated, students still have several **funding support pathways**. First, they can apply for the **ANU temporary hardship grant** of up to 3,000 Australian dollars per year, which has no GPA requirement. Second, they can switch to the **ANU tuition instalment plan**, paying tuition in 4 instalments to avoid the pressure of a single payment. In addition, some colleges offer **academic achievement awards** — for example, the ANU College of Business and Economics pays students with a GPA of at least 5.5 a 1,000 Australian dollar award each semester.

### Academic Support Resources

To help students meet the targets, ANU offers **free academic tutoring**, including the writing centre, the maths learning centre and peer tutoring programs. According to a 2023 survey by the ANU Student Experience Office, students who used academic support raised their GPA by an average of 0.8. Students can also adjust their course selection strategy through the **ANU course planning system**, prioritising courses with high pass rates. Some families use dedicated channels such as [Flywire tuition payments](https://go.compares.cheap/flywire-edu-payments-2376?p=anu-wiki/articles/anu-international-scholarship-renewal-conditions-maintaining-a-satisfactory-gpa) for cross-border fee transfers.

### Transferring and Credit Transfer

If the scholarship cannot be restored, students may consider **transferring to another Australian university**. According to the Australian Department of Education's 2023 transfer data report, about 15% of ANU students whose scholarships were terminated transferred to UNSW or the University of Sydney the following semester. ANU offers a **credit transfer assessment service** to ensure completed credits are partly recognised. After transferring, students can apply for scholarships at their new university, but must satisfy its renewal conditions afresh.

## Renewal Timeline and Notifications

Scholarship renewal assessments are completed **within 4 weeks of results release each semester**. The ANU Scholarships Office sends notifications through the **student portal system**, including transcripts, GPA calculations and scholarship status. Students who fall short receive a formal warning letter or suspension notice **within 8 weeks**. They must reply to confirm **within 14 days**, otherwise the decision is deemed accepted.

### Appeals

Students have the right to appeal **within 20 business days** of receiving a suspension notice. Appeals go through the **ANU Academic Appeals Committee** with written grounds and evidence. According to ANU's 2024 Appeals Policy, the appeal success rate is about 18%. Common grounds include calculation errors, special circumstances not being considered, or procedural breaches. Scholarship payments stay suspended during the appeal, and are not back-paid.

### Impact on International Student Visas

Failing to renew a scholarship can affect your **student visa** status. Under the Australian Department of Home Affairs' 2023 student visa provisions, students whose academic progress lags due to low GPA or insufficient credits may breach **visa condition 8202** (maintaining enrolment in a registered course with satisfactory attendance). ANU is obliged to report students who are not meeting academic requirements to the Department of Home Affairs. Students must provide an explanation within 28 days, or the visa may be cancelled. Students are advised to also consult the ANU international student support team.

## FAQ

### Q1: What is the minimum GPA for ANU international scholarships?

The minimum GPA requirement for ANU international scholarships is **4.0/7.0** (undergraduate) or **5.0/7.0** (postgraduate). Specific scholarship types may demand more — the Chancellor's Scholarship, for instance, requires 5.5/7.0. GPA is calculated as the weighted average each semester, and failed courses (0 points) drag the average down significantly.

### Q2: Will my scholarship be terminated immediately if my GPA falls short?

Not immediately. ANU uses a three-tier mechanism: the first miss earns a **warning letter** and a 1-semester probation period; a second miss results in **suspension for 1 semester**; and two consecutive misses or three warnings in total lead to **permanent termination**. During suspension you can apply for reinstatement through an academic improvement plan.

### Q3: Can scholarship renewal failure affect my student visa?

It can. Under **visa condition 8202** of the Australian Department of Home Affairs, students must maintain enrolment in a registered course and satisfactory academic progress. If low GPA or insufficient credits slow your progress, ANU will report it to the Department. You must provide an explanation within 28 days, or the visa may be cancelled. Contact the ANU international student support team for help.
