The Australian National University (ANU) main campus sits in the Acton district of Australia's capital, Canberra, and the choice between on-campus accommodation and off-campus renting directly affects students' annual budgets and daily commute efficiency. According to data released by ANU Accommodation Services in 2024, the annual rent for a standard on-campus single room (including meals) is approximately AUD 24,000 to 28,000, while the median annual rent for a single room in a shared off-campus flat is roughly AUD 11,000 to 16,000 (excluding meals) — a difference of more than double. Meanwhile, the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) 2023 housing and commuting survey found that the average one-way commute for Canberra residents is 28 minutes, but public transport travel time from the main off-campus rental areas (such as Belconnen and Kingston) to campus is typically 25 to 45 minutes for ANU students. Drawing on ANU's official accommodation guide, ACT government transport data, and third-party rental platform statistics, this article systematically compares the room types, cost breakdowns, and commuting characteristics of the two options, offering quantifiable decision-making reference points for students planning to study at ANU.

## On-Campus Housing: Room Types and Cost Structure

ANU's on-campus accommodation system comprises 22 residential halls and colleges, located mainly on the Acton main campus and the nearby Daley Road area. According to the Accommodation Guide published by ANU Accommodation Services (2024), on-campus housing comes in three core room types: **Standard Single Room**, **Ensuite Single Room**, and **Studio/Flat**. A standard single room is roughly 12 to 15 square metres and comes with a single bed, desk, wardrobe, and shared bathroom and kitchen facilities; annual rent (including a meal plan covering 20 meals per week) is AUD 24,000 to 26,000. An ensuite single room is about 15 to 18 square metres with a private bathroom, priced at AUD 26,000 to 28,000 per year. A studio/flat is approximately 20 to 30 square metres with its own kitchen and bathroom, costing AUD 28,000 to 32,000 per year.

### Meal Plans and Additional Fees

All on-campus residences come with a compulsory meal plan, and the cost is already included in the rent. Data from ANU Accommodation Services (2024) shows that the meal plan accounts for about 35% to 40% of the annual rent. In addition, students pay a one-off application fee (AUD 50 to 100) and a deposit (usually equivalent to 4 weeks' rent). **Internet fees** are included in the rent, but laundry services cost extra (around AUD 4 to 6 per load). On-campus contracts typically run 44 to 48 weeks (from the start of Semester 1 to the end of Semester 2) and do not cover the summer break (mid-December to mid-February), so students need to arrange alternative accommodation for that period.

### Application Competition and Allocation

ANU allocates on-campus housing under a policy that gives priority to new international students. According to the annual report of the ANU Student Accommodation Association (2024), a total of 4,200 applications were received for Semester 1, 2024, of which 3,100 places were allocated — an acceptance rate of about 73.8%. **Application deadlines** are usually 31 October (for entry in the following year's Semester 1) or 31 May (for Semester 2 entry). Students who miss the deadline are placed on a waitlist, and the probability of waitlisted students securing accommodation is below 30% (ANU Accommodation Services, 2024). Students planning to live on campus should therefore start the application process as soon as they receive their offer letter.

## Off-Campus Housing: Room Types and Cost Structure

The off-campus rental market is concentrated in northern Canberra (Belconnen, Gungahlin), the south (Kingston, Manuka), and the inner city (Braddon, Dickson). According to the rental report from Australian property data platform CoreLogic (2024), the median weekly rent in Canberra is AUD 550 for a one-bedroom apartment, AUD 720 for a two-bedroom apartment, and AUD 950 for a three-bedroom apartment. For ANU students, the most common room types are **shared apartments** and **studios**. In a shared apartment, a single bedroom rents for AUD 250 to 400 per week (excluding utilities and internet), for an annual total of roughly AUD 13,000 to 20,800. A studio rents for AUD 400 to 550 per week, for an annual total of roughly AUD 20,800 to 28,600.

### Deposits, Bills, and Contract Flexibility

Off-campus leases typically require a bond of 4 weeks' rent (held in trust by the New South Wales rental bond authority) plus 2 weeks' rent paid in advance. **Utility bills** (electricity, gas, water, internet) come to about AUD 150 to 250 per month, split among the tenants. Compared with on-campus housing, off-campus contracts are more flexible: common lease terms are 6 or 12 months, and some landlords allow early termination (subject to a penalty of 2 to 4 weeks' rent). Students can also sublet their room during the summer break (December to February) to reduce the cost of an empty room.

### Key Rental Areas and Rent Differences

Rents vary significantly from area to area. **Belconnen** is about 8 km from ANU, roughly 30 minutes by bus, and the median weekly rent for a one-bedroom apartment there is AUD 480 (CoreLogic, 2024). **Kingston** is about 4 km from ANU, roughly 20 minutes by bus, with a median weekly rent of AUD 620 for a one-bedroom. **Braddon** is right next to campus and within walking distance, with a median weekly rent of AUD 650 for a one-bedroom. Students need to choose an area based on their budget and commute preferences.

## Commute Times and Transport Costs Compared

Commute efficiency is a key variable in choosing where to live. According to the Public Transport Network Report published by Transport Canberra in 2023, the ANU main campus (Acton) is served by multiple bus routes, including R2, R3, R4, and R6. **Average commute times** from the main off-campus residential areas to campus are: Belconnen (bus, 30 to 40 minutes), Kingston (bus, 20 to 30 minutes), and Braddon (walking, 10 to 15 minutes). Students living on campus typically walk 5 to 10 minutes to their teaching buildings.

### Public Transport Fares and Concessions

Canberra's public transport uses the MyWay card system. ANU students can apply for concession fares, with a single trip costing AUD 2.40 (the adult fare is AUD 4.80). **Annual transport budget**: assuming 10 bus trips per week (5 days of round trips) over 40 weeks a year, the total comes to about AUD 960. If you prefer cycling, the ACT government offers a free bike-share service (Canberra Bike Share), but you need to buy your own helmet (about AUD 30). Students living on campus have no transport costs, but should factor in walking or cycling time.

### The Cost of Driving

Some students choose to drive to campus. According to the Australian Automobile Association (AAA) 2024 Vehicle Operating Cost Report, running a private car in Canberra costs about AUD 0.75 per kilometre (including fuel, insurance, and maintenance). Assuming a daily round trip of 16 km (e.g., from Belconnen) over 40 weeks a year, that works out to roughly 3,200 km in total, for an annual running cost of about AUD 2,400. On top of that, an ANU campus parking permit costs AUD 1,200 per year (ANU Transport and Parking Services, 2024). Driving to campus can therefore cost more than AUD 3,600 a year including parking — significantly more than taking the bus.

## Convenience and Social Opportunities

On-campus housing offers a highly integrated living experience. ANU Accommodation Services (2024) notes that every residential hall and college is equipped with **common rooms, study rooms, laundry facilities, and a small supermarket**. Some colleges (such as Bruce Hall and Burgmann College) also have a gym, music room, and squash courts. On the social side, each college organises at least 3 group activities a week, including academic talks, movie nights, and outdoor trips. For international students, living on campus is an efficient way to build a social network — a survey by ANU International Student Services (2024) found that 78% of international students in on-campus housing had met at least 5 fellow students within 4 weeks of enrolment.

### Off-Campus Amenities and Community Environment

Off-campus convenience varies by area. **Braddon** has a dense cluster of cafés, restaurants, and supermarkets, and is just a 10-minute walk from ANU. **Kingston** is known for its waterfront restaurants and weekend markets, but it is 2.5 km from campus, so you will need to rely on the bus or a bike. **Belconnen** has a large shopping centre (Westfield Belconnen), but the pace of life leans more towards a family suburb. Students need to sort out their own meals — weekly grocery costs run about AUD 80 to 120 (based on 2024 average prices at Canberra supermarkets Coles and Woolworths). Social opportunities off campus also depend more on personal initiative, such as joining student clubs or taking part in community events.

## Visa Compliance and Proof of Accommodation Requirements

The Australian Department of Home Affairs does not impose any mandatory accommodation requirement on students, but applicants must provide **proof of accommodation arrangements** (such as an on-campus accommodation confirmation letter or an off-campus lease) when applying for a student visa. According to the Department of Home Affairs' 2024 Student Visa Guidelines, proof of accommodation is a non-mandatory but recommended document on the visa checklist. In addition, international students on a student visa must satisfy the Genuine Student Requirement, and a stable accommodation arrangement can serve as supporting evidence of your ability to live in Australia.

### Visa Assistance for On-Campus Residents

ANU Accommodation Services (2024) provides visa-support documents for students in on-campus housing, including a Confirmation of Accommodation that can be submitted together with the visa application. For students under 18, ANU requires them to live in on-campus accommodation, with a guardian appointed by the university (ANU Welfare and Support Services, 2024). This policy is based on the Department of Home Affairs' guardianship requirements for minor international students (Migration Regulations, clause 2.25).

### Contract Risks in Off-Campus Leases

Off-campus leases deserve close attention to their terms. Australian Capital Territory (ACT) tenancy law requires landlords to provide a written contract and to state the conditions for bond refunds clearly. Students should search for properties through reputable platforms (such as Realestate.com.au and Domain) and avoid private, informal deals. The ANU Student Legal Service (2024) reports handling 218 disputes involving off-campus rentals in 2023, of which 32% concerned bonds not refunded in full and 28% concerned property maintenance issues. Before signing, students should ask the landlord for a Condition Report and keep photographic evidence.

## Annual Total Cost Comparison: On-Campus vs Off-Campus

Based on the data above, here is a comparison of annual total costs for the two options (at 2024 prices). **On-campus** (standard single room, meals included): annual rent AUD 24,000 to 26,000, no extra transport costs, no food spending — an annual total of about AUD 24,000 to 26,000. **Off-campus shared room** (Belconnen, AUD 320 per week): annual rent AUD 16,640, utilities and internet AUD 2,400, food AUD 4,160, transport AUD 960 — an annual total of about AUD 24,160. **Off-campus studio** (Braddon, AUD 600 per week): annual rent AUD 31,200, utilities and internet AUD 2,400, food AUD 4,160, transport AUD 0 (walking) — an annual total of about AUD 37,760. In short, **sharing off campus** can cost roughly the same as living on campus, but you will need to manage your own meals and bills; a **studio** costs significantly more than on-campus housing.

### Hidden Costs and the Cost of Time

Hidden costs of on-campus living include the compulsory meal plan — if you eat little or prefer to cook for yourself, the meal plan can go to waste. Hidden costs off campus include furniture (a one-bedroom furniture package costs about AUD 1,500 to 3,000), internet installation (around AUD 100), and lost interest on the bond. Commuting time also carries a cost that can be converted into dollars: assuming an hourly wage of AUD 20 (part-time work), a 30-minute daily commute over 40 weeks a year works out to about AUD 2,000. The commute time saved by living on campus can be converted into study or part-time work time.

## FAQ

### Q1: Does ANU guarantee on-campus rooms for all new students?

No. According to ANU Accommodation Services (2024), 4,200 applications were received for Semester 1, 2024, with 3,100 places allocated — an acceptance rate of 73.8%. New international students who submit their application before 31 October get priority, but places are still limited. It is wise to prepare an off-campus backup plan at the same time.

### Q2: How far in advance should I start looking for off-campus housing?

Start 6 to 8 weeks in advance. Canberra's rental market is highly competitive in January and February (enrolment season), with vacancy rates below 1.5% (CoreLogic, 2024). You can set alerts on Realestate.com.au and prepare application documents such as your passport, visa, and bank statements in advance. Booking early helps you avoid paying inflated last-minute rents.

### Q3: Which area near the ANU campus offers the best value for money?

Belconnen is the best value option. According to CoreLogic (2024), the median weekly rent for a shared room in the area is AUD 320, the bus ride to campus takes about 30 minutes, and the annual total cost (including transport) is around AUD 24,160 — close to on-campus costs but with more flexibility on meals. Braddon is within walking distance, but rents there are about 50% higher.
